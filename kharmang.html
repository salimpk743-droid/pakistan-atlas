Kharmang District: Quick Answers

Kharmang District is one of the 10 districts for which Gilgit-Baltistan's Planning & Development Department publishes statistics. The 2023 census counted 61,304 people on 6,144 km², and 71.09% of residents were literate.

Population and growth

Kharmang District had 61,304 people in the 2023 census, up from 54,613 in 2017, an average annual growth rate of 1.94%. It covers 6,144 km². The census counted 8,819 households, with an average of 7 people each, and 115 males for every 100 females (GB at a Glance 2025).

How Kharmang compares within Gilgit-Baltistan

Among the 10 districts in the GB statistics, Kharmang ranks 10th by population, 6th by area and 4th by literacy (71.09% in the 2023 census). Gilgit-Baltistan as a whole counted 1,709,049 people on 72,496 km² (GB at a Glance 2025).

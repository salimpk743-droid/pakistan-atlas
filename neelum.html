Neelum District: Quick Answers

Neelum District is one of the 10 districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, in Muzaffarabad Division. The 2017 census counted 189,785 people on 3,621 km², in 2 tehsils: Athmuqam and Sharda.

Tehsils of Neelum District

The 2017 census tables published by the AJ&K Bureau of Statistics list 2 tehsils in Neelum District: Athmuqam and Sharda (Statistical Year Book 2023, Table 15.15). Athmuqam is the most populous, with 129,215 people, and Sharda is the largest by area (2,329 km²). AJK at a Glance 2024 counts 32 tehsils across Azad Kashmir in 2023, against 30 in the 2017 census tables, so tehsil boundaries may since have changed (AJK at a Glance 2024).

Population and area

Neelum District had 189,785 people in the 2017 census, with average annual growth of 2.19% since 1998, 4.12% of them in urban areas and an average household of 7.01 people (Table 15.6). It covers 3,621 km² (Table 15.1). Of the 10 AJK districts it ranks 9th by population and 1st by area. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects about 0.216 million people for 2023 (AJK at a Glance 2024); these are projections, not census counts.

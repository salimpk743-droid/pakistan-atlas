Khyber District

Peshawar Division · former FATA agency · headquarters Landi Kotal / Jamrud · 4 tehsils · 1,146,267 people (2023 census)

Khyber District: Quick Answers

Khyber District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Landi Kotal / Jamrud. It has 4 tehsils: Bara, Jamrud, Landi Kotal and Mulla Gori. The 2023 census counted 1,146,267 people on 2,576 km², and 38.45% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

From tribal agency to district

Khyber was one of the seven agencies of the Federally Administered Tribal Areas (Xinhua). FATA was merged into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa when the 25th Constitutional Amendment was signed on 31 May 2018 (Dawn), and the Board of Revenue placed Khyber District in Peshawar Division (Dawn).

The Khyber Pass

The Khyber Pass connects Peshawar with Kabul. It enters the Khyber Hills a few kilometres west of Jamrud and runs northwest for about 53 km to the Afghan border at Torkham. Fort Ali Masjid stands at 967 metres, and the fort and cantonment of Landi Kotal lie about 16 km beyond it at 1,072 metres (Britannica).

Tehsils of Khyber District

The 2023 census counted 4 tehsils in Khyber District: Bara, Jamrud, Landi Kotal and Mulla Gori. Bara is the most populous, with 548,084 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,430 km²). Literacy ranges from 34.72% in Bara to 46.18% in Jamrud.

Population and growth

Khyber District had 1,146,267 people in the 2023 census, up from 984,246 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.58%). It covers 2,576 km². Of the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the census, it ranks 15th by population and 15th by area. 8.26% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 109.18 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Jamrud (56,642) and Landi Kotal (38,065). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.7%).

Literacy and schooling

38.45% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 24th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 58.08% and female literacy 17.16%; the urban rate was 60.44% and the rural rate 36.37%. The census counted 213,510 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (132,756 girls and 80,749 boys).

Khyber District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Khyber District?

Khyber District has 4 tehsils: Bara, Jamrud, Landi Kotal and Mulla Gori.

What is the population of Khyber District?

The 2023 census counted 1,146,267 people in Khyber District, up from 984,246 in 2017 (annual growth 2.58%). 8.26% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Khyber District?

Khyber District covers 2,576 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 444.98 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Khyber District?

The 2023 census lists 2 urban localities in Khyber District: Jamrud (56,642, town committee) and Landi Kotal (38,065, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Khyber District?

38.45% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 58.08%, female 17.16%).

Which languages are spoken in Khyber District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.7% of the 1,145,982 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Khyber District in?

Khyber District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Landi Kotal / Jamrud.

When did Khyber Agency become a district?

Khyber was a FATA agency until the 25th Constitutional Amendment, signed on 31 May 2018, merged FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It is now a district of Peshawar Division. (Dawn)

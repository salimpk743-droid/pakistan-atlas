Nagar District: Quick Answers

Nagar District is one of the 10 districts for which Gilgit-Baltistan's Planning & Development Department publishes statistics. The 2023 census counted 87,410 people on 4,137 km², and 77.62% of residents were literate.

Population and growth

Nagar District had 87,410 people in the 2023 census, up from 71,746 in 2017, an average annual growth rate of 3.35%. It covers 4,137 km². The census counted 13,162 households, with an average of 7 people each, and 105 males for every 100 females (GB at a Glance 2025).

How Nagar compares within Gilgit-Baltistan

Among the 10 districts in the GB statistics, Nagar ranks 7th by population, 10th by area and 2nd by literacy (77.62% in the 2023 census). Gilgit-Baltistan as a whole counted 1,709,049 people on 72,496 km² (GB at a Glance 2025).

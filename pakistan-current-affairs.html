Pakistan Current Affairs 2026: Weekly News Updates (Politics, Economy, Provinces)

A weekly round-up of Pakistan news this week, written for students and for PPSC, FPSC and CSS current affairs preparation.

By mybook.pk Editorial · Last updated 4 October 2026

Every week we publish a new page of Pakistan current affairs: the main national politics and government news, parliament and court decisions, the economy (IMF, State Bank, budget and the petrol price in Pakistan), news from Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Kashmir, foreign affairs, security, sports, weather and education. Each week ends with key facts and ten current affairs MCQs.

Latest news (4 October 2026): PTI goes ahead with Islamabad march after talks with the government end in deadlock. Every Sunday we publish one detailed news article; see the weekly news archive.

This Week: 1–4 October 2026 (updated 4 October)

The main Pakistan news of this week so far, as reported up to the afternoon of Sunday 4 October 2026. Each item has been checked against dated reports, which are listed after it. For the full story of the week, read our article: PTI goes ahead with Islamabad march after talks with the government end in deadlock.

  • Government–PTI talks end in deadlock. Two rounds of government–opposition talks were held at Parliament House on 2 and 3 October, chaired by National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq. Dawn described them as the first formal talks between the federal government and the PTI in almost three years. The second round ran for more than eight hours and ended in a “deadlock” shortly before 3am on 4 October. The sticking points named were meetings with jailed PTI founder Imran Khan, his access to his personal physicians, and the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government’s assurance of cooperation against terrorism. Sources: Dawn, 3–4 Oct; Business Recorder, 4 Oct; Geo News, 3 Oct.
  • PTI march to Islamabad. The PTI said its long march, led by KP Chief Minister Sohail Afridi, would begin at 1pm on 4 October from Gandi Chowk in Lakki Marwat. The announced route has overnight stops near Karak, Kohat, Mattani and Charsadda Interchange, and ends at Khairabad on 8 October, where the next phase is to be announced. The Prime Minister’s Adviser on Political Affairs, Rana Sanaullah, said “the long march will not be allowed to enter the limits of Islamabad”, but that talks could resume if the KP government was ready to cooperate against terrorism. Sources: Dawn, 4 Oct; Geo News, 4 Oct.
  • Governor’s rule or emergency in KP debated. PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari said in Karachi on 3 October that the federal government had been ready to impose an emergency or governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, but that the PPP wanted a political solution first. He called for a written agreement between the federal and KP governments on fighting terrorism. No emergency or governor’s rule had been imposed as of 4 October. Sources: Dawn, 3 Oct; The News, 4 Oct.
  • Corps Commanders’ Conference warning. The 277th Corps Commanders’ Conference, chaired by Chief of Defence Forces and Army Chief Field Marshal Asim Munir in Rawalpindi on 2 October, resolved that any attempt to cause agitation or create a “May 9-like situation” would be dealt with “decisive force”. It also affirmed Pakistan’s right to self-defence and to targeted action against terrorism emanating from Afghanistan. Sources: Dawn, 3 Oct; APP, 2 Oct; Radio Pakistan, 2 Oct.
  • Inflation eases to 10.3%. The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics reported on 1 October that CPI inflation was 10.26% year on year in September 2026, down from 11.1% in August. Prices rose 1.27% month on month, and inflation averaged 10.20% for July–September (the first quarter of 2026-27). Sources: PBS press release, 1 Oct; Business Recorder, 2 Oct.
  • Petrol price rises to Rs392.76. The government raised petrol by Rs2.10 to Rs392.76 per litre and high-speed diesel by Re0.30 to Rs399.64 per litre, for 3 to 5 October. Sources: Dawn, 2 Oct; Radio Pakistan, 3 Oct; Business Recorder, 3 Oct.
  • IMF review talks continue. The IMF mission led by Iva Petrova, in Islamabad for the fourth review of the $7bn EFF and the third review of the RSF (about $1.2bn in total if completed), shared its first draft of the Memorandum of Economic and Financial Policies (MEFP). The talks also covered replacing tariff-based electricity subsidies with targeted cash transfers through BISP by January 2027. The mission was expected to stay until about the middle of next week. Sources: Dawn, 1 Oct; Business Recorder, 2 Oct; Geo News, 3 Oct.
  • Asian Games medals on 3 October. Pakistan won silver in men’s cricket after losing the final to India by 19 runs in Nisshin, Japan; Hasan Nawaz scored 96 off 51 balls. The hockey team won bronze by beating South Korea 5–3, a day after losing 4–3 to India in the semi-final. The volleyball team beat China 3–0 for bronze, Pakistan’s first Asian Games volleyball medal since 1962. Sources: Dawn (cricket), 3 Oct; Dawn (hockey and volleyball), 3 Oct; Olympics.com.

How These Weekly Updates Are Written

  • Every news item is checked against at least two reliable outlets (for example Dawn, The News, Express Tribune, Geo News, APP, Radio Pakistan or official bodies such as the PBS and State Bank). Anything we cannot confirm is left out.
  • Summaries are written in our own words and in a neutral tone. We do not take sides between political parties.
  • Each item links to its sources with the outlet name and date, and each weekly page ends with a full list of sources.
  • Pages are prepared by mybook.pk Editorial and show when they were published and last updated. Corrections are welcome through our contact page.

FAQ: Pakistan Current Affairs 2026

How often is this Pakistan current affairs page updated?

Which sources are used for the weekly news?

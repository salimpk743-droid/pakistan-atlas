Tehsils of Peshawar District

The 2023 census lists 7 administrative units in Peshawar District: Badhber (tehsil), Cham Kani (tehsil), Hassan Khel (sub-division), Mathra (tehsil), Peshawar (tehsil), Pishta Khara (tehsil) and Shah Alam (tehsil). Peshawar is the most populous, with 2,113,596 people in the 2023 census, and Badhber is the largest by area (357 km²).

How the tehsils compare

Peshawar tehsil has the most people (2,113,596) and Hassan Khel the fewest (72,557). Badhber covers the largest area (357 km²). Peshawar is the most urban (90.18% of residents in urban areas). Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 38.66% in Badhber to 60.88% in Peshawar.

How many tehsils are in Peshawar District?

The 2023 census lists 7 administrative units: Badhber, Cham Kani, Hassan Khel, Mathra, Peshawar, Pishta Khara and Shah Alam. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which is the largest tehsil of Peshawar District by population?

Peshawar, with 2,113,596 people in the 2023 census. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which is the largest tehsil of Peshawar District by area?

Badhber, at 357 km². (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which tehsil of Peshawar District has the highest literacy rate?

Peshawar, where 60.88% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Peshawar District administrative structure

The 2023 census lists 7 administrative units in Peshawar District: Badhber (tehsil), Cham Kani (tehsil), Hassan Khel (sub-division), Mathra (tehsil), Peshawar (tehsil), Pishta Khara (tehsil) and Shah Alam (tehsil). The district is part of Peshawar Division.

Badhber Tehsil

Population (2023): 439,912

Area: 357 km² · 1,232.25 people per km²

Urban share: no urban population recorded

Cham Kani Tehsil

Population (2023): 624,354

Area: 226 km² · 2,762.63 people per km²

Urban share: no urban population recorded

Hassan Khel Sub-division

Population (2023): 72,557

Area: 261 km² · 278 people per km²

Urban share: no urban population recorded

Mathra Tehsil

Population (2023): 495,059

Urban share: no urban population recorded

Peshawar Tehsil

Population (2023): 2,113,596

Urban share: 90.18%

Pishta Khara Tehsil

Population (2023): 480,436

Shah Alam Tehsil

Mohabbat Drama: Cast and Story, Episode Guide and OST

Mohabbat (محبت) is a family drama on Geo Entertainment (Har Pal Geo), written by Shafia Khan and directed by Asad Jabal, starring Feroze Khan, Nawal Saeed.

Mohabbat Story

Mohabbat (Love) is a new Geo Entertainment and 7th Sky Entertainment serial. Its story revolves around Yahya and Zarrar, two brothers from different generations whose bond of love is put to the test.

The dreams and hopes of Asiya and Mehr Maah become entangled under the same roof, putting relationships and family ties under pressure as the brothers' relationship is tested by circumstances and changing family dynamics. The premiere report did not say which actor plays which character, so this page lists the cast without roles.

Har Pal Geo's official episode uploads have since named the characters, and the cast table below now gives them.

Mohabbat Drama Story So Far (Episodes 1 and 2)

Mohabbat sets two love stories inside one wealthy family. Yahya Jamal (Feroze Khan), the hot-headed elder son of Saleha Nizami and Jamal, runs the family company and is in love with Asiya Majeed (Nawal Saeed), a middle-class woman who works there as head of strategy. His younger brother Zarrar (Shahvir Kadwani) is a university student whose closest friend since convent school is Meherma (Aina Asif), a scholarship student who lives in rented homes with her widowed mother Fehmida, a tailor.

The first episode is mostly about pride. Yahya takes on the police after they raid his office, his father Jamal is furious with him, and Meherma publicly shames a young man who had been harassing her friend. At the same time she and her mother are being pushed out of their home, and a broker tells them that landlords do not want to rent to women living without a man.

Episode 2 turns the story into a class war. Yahya proposes to Asiya. His mother says she will only send a proposal if Asiya loses her job. Then Saleha goes behind his back: she offers Asiya money to walk away, slaps her, and has the police raid the Majeed home on a theft complaint, giving Asiya ten minutes to cut Yahya off. Zarrar is the one family member who tells his mother she went too far.

Feroze Khan and Nawal Saeed Drama: The Lead Pair

Mohabbat is built around Feroze Khan as Yahya and Nawal Saeed as Asiya, with Shahvir Kadwani and Aina Asif as the second couple, Zarrar and Meherma. Every Har Pal Geo episode title is billed "Feroze Khan - Nawal Saeed", and the trailers add Shahvir Kadwani and Aina Asif. The official synopsis suggests the two stories will collide. Asiya is pushed out of Yahya's life by his mother and later sets out to challenge Saleha, while Meherma proposes to her friend Zarrar to escape her own troubles, and the marriage that follows begins as a practical decision.

Mohabbat Premiere, Timing and Episode Count

Har Pal Geo began running promos on 25 September 2026 ("Starting from 28th September, 8:00 PM"), and the drama premiered on Geo Entertainment on Monday 28 September 2026.

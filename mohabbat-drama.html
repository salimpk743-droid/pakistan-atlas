Mohabbat Drama: Cast and Story

Mohabbat (محبت) is a family drama on Geo Entertainment (Har Pal Geo), written by Shafia Khan and directed by Asad Jabal, starring Feroze Khan, Nawal Saeed.

Mohabbat Story

Mohabbat (Love) is a new Geo Entertainment and 7th Sky Entertainment serial. Its story revolves around Yahya and Zarrar, two brothers from different generations whose bond of love is put to the test.

The dreams and hopes of Asiya and Mehr Maah become entangled under the same roof, putting relationships and family ties under pressure as the brothers' relationship is tested by circumstances and changing family dynamics. Writer, Director and Production of Mohabbat

Mohabbat is written by Shafia Khan and directed by Asad Jabal, and produced by Abdullah Kadwani and Asad Qureshi.

Producer / production house: Abdullah Kadwani and Asad Qureshi (7th Sky Entertainment).

Mohabbat OST

The OST is sung and written by Shani Haider, with the music arranged together with Aliyan. See the Pakistani drama OSTs guide for more soundtracks.

When and Where to Watch Mohabbat

Mohabbat airs on Geo Entertainment (Har Pal Geo), Monday and Tuesday, 8:00 pm (Pakistan time). It premiered on Monday 28 September 2026. Schedules and repeat timings change, so check the official Geo Entertainment website for the latest episode and timing. Channels also upload episodes to their official YouTube channels.

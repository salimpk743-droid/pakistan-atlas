Upper Chitral District: Quick Answers

Upper Chitral District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Booni. It has 1 sub-divisions: Mastuj. The 2023 census counted 195,528 people on 8,392 km², and 73.83% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

If Lower Chitral is the town under Trich Mir, Upper Chitral is the long up-valley: Mastuj, Buni, Torkhow, Mulkhow, and the road that climbs toward Shandur. The district is huge on a map and thin on the ground. Twenty-three people per square kilometre is the 2023 density. The Bureau printed the whole unit as rural.

Households: 26,365. Population: 195,528. Literacy: 73.83 percent — 84.87 percent for men, 62.11 percent for women. That female figure is higher than most of southern KP. Winter still decides whether a girl in a side valley actually reaches class in January.

Khowar is the language of almost every counted household (the census “other language” row is essentially Khowar here). Wakhi is spoken toward the northern passes. Pashto is rare in this table, unlike Drosh down-valley.

How the books split the land

PBS Table 1 for 2023 lists Upper Chitral as one block: Mastuj sub-division, 8,392 km², same population as the district. Local administration also uses valley names that are not always given their own census line: Buni, Torkhow (Torkoh), Mulkhow (Mulkoh). When a later tehsil book prints separate numbers, this page should follow that book. Until then, treat Mastuj as the official row and the others as living valley names.

Places people actually say

Mastuj is the old fort-and-bazaar name of the upper river. Buni is the large settlement many families treat as their town. Torkhow and Mulkhow are the inhabited side valleys, not empty labels. Shandur pass is the polo-ground story the rest of the country already knows; it is a seasonal road, not a third city.

Do not copy the Kalash valley list onto this page. Those valleys sit with Lower Chitral / Ayun.

College, clinic, and the assembly seat

Degree work still points down to Chitral town or out through Lowari. Upper Chitral keeps government colleges and high schools in the main settlements; specialist hospital beds are not in Mastuj. A DHQ-style facility is what people ask for when they talk about the new district, not a second university.

On population alone, Upper Chitral did not clear the 2023 provincial-seat threshold. Coverage has been discussed as a combined Upper-and-Lower seat. That is an Election Commission arithmetic note, not a local insult.

Upper Chitral District: FAQ

How many sub-divisions are in Upper Chitral District?

Upper Chitral District has 1 sub-divisions: Mastuj.

What is the population of Upper Chitral District?

The 2023 census counted 195,528 people in Upper Chitral District, up from 169,297 in 2017 (annual growth 2.44%). 0% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Upper Chitral District?

Upper Chitral District covers 8,392 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 23.3 people per km².

Is Upper Chitral District urban or rural?

Entirely rural: the 2023 census counted no urban population in Upper Chitral District, so all 195,528 residents were enumerated in rural areas.

What is the literacy rate of Upper Chitral District?

73.83% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 84.87%, female 62.11%).

Which province is Upper Chitral District in?

Upper Chitral District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Booni.

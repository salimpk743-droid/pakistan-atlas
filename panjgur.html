Panjgur District

On the Rakhshan River · headquarters Panjgur · 5 census units · 509,781 people (2023 census)

Panjgur District: Quick Answers

Panjgur District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Makuran Division, with its headquarters at Panjgur. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Gichk, Gowargo, Jaheen Parome, Kallag and Panjgur. The 2023 census counted 509,781 people on 16,891 km², and 42.07% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Panjgur town

Panjgur stands on the south bank of the Rakhshan River in the Siahan Range. It is a market centre and is linked by road with Turbat and Pasni to the southwest. The surrounding semi-arid region is mostly mountainous (Britannica).

Administrative units of Panjgur District

The 2023 census counted 5 administrative units in Panjgur District: Gichk (sub-tehsil), Gowargo (sub-division), Jaheen Parome (tehsil), Kallag (sub-tehsil) and Panjgur (sub-division). Panjgur is the most populous, with 392,277 people, and Gichk is the largest by area (8,387 km²). Literacy ranges from 20.15% in Gichk to 46.19% in Panjgur.

Population and growth

Panjgur District had 509,781 people in the 2023 census, up from 315,353 in 2017 (average annual growth 8.36%). It covers 16,891 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 8th by population and 6th by area. 30.93% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 112.09 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 3 urban localities: Tasp (66,030), Chitkan (47,034) and Washbood (44,629). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (99.63%).

Literacy and schooling

42.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 11th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 45.8% and female literacy 37.8%; the urban rate was 36.43% and the rural rate 44.81%. The census counted 90,815 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (44,824 girls and 45,983 boys).

Panjgur District: FAQ

What is the population of Panjgur District?

The 2023 census counted 509,781 people in Panjgur District, up from 315,353 in 2017 (annual growth 8.36%). 30.93% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Panjgur District?

Panjgur District covers 16,891 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 30.18 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Panjgur District?

The 2023 census lists 3 urban localities in Panjgur District: Tasp (66,030, municipal committee), Chitkan (47,034, municipal corporation) and Washbood (44,629, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Panjgur District?

42.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 45.8%, female 37.8%).

Which languages are spoken in Panjgur District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (99.63% of the 509,781 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Panjgur District in?

Panjgur District is in Balochistan, in Makuran Division. Its headquarters is Panjgur.

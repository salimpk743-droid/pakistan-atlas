Battagram District

Headquarters Battagram · One tehsil after Allai became its own district · 2023 population 335,984, all rural

Battagram District: Quick Answers

Battagram District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Battagram. It has 2 tehsils: Allai and Batagram. The 2023 census counted 554,133 people on 1,301 km², and 39.09% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Battagram is a hill district of Hazara on the Karakoram Highway, between Mansehra and the Kohistan / Shangla side. It was a tehsil of Mansehra until 1993. The 8 October 2005 earthquake hit this belt hard; that date is still how older people mark time, not a tourist line.

PBS 2023 for the present district (Battagram tehsil only): 335,984 people, 51,366 households, area 497 km². Urban column: zero. Literacy 39.09 percent overall — 54.69 percent for men, 23.34 percent for women. Phone code 0997. Postal code often listed as 21040. District site: battagram.kp.gov.pk.

Older books add Allai and quote half a million people. Allai was raised to a separate district in 2022 (218,149 people in its 2023 tehsil table). This page does not glue those two books back together.

Battagram: One tehsil, twelve union councils

Public lists for Battagram tehsil name these twelve councils:

Ajmera, Banian, Battagram, Batamori, Gijbori, Kuza Banda, Paimal Sharif, Peshora, Rajdhari, Shumlai, Thakot, Trand.

Thakot is the Indus-side name many drivers already know from the highway. Battagram bazaar is the headquarters. The rest are valley councils, not municipal towns.

Pashto is the main speech. Gujari and some Hindko / Kohistani speech show up in older language notes. Do not treat those percentages as a fresh PBS table unless the Bureau reprints them for this slimmer district.

Schools and the hospital in Battagram District

Government colleges sit in Battagram. Degree students still leave for Mansehra, Abbottabad or Peshawar. DHQ Battagram is the first hospital. After 2005 the rebuilt schools and the DHQ are part of the district story, not extras. Female literacy at 23 percent is why a girls’ high school in a far union council matters more on this page than a scenery paragraph.

Winter and monsoon both close the side roads off the KKH. That is ordinary administration here.

Sources: PBS 2023; KP District Battagram; union-council list published with the current one-tehsil district. Last checked September 2026.

Battagram District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Battagram District?

Battagram District has 2 tehsils: Allai and Batagram.

What is the population of Battagram District?

The 2023 census counted 554,133 people in Battagram District, up from 476,749 in 2017 (annual growth 2.55%). 0% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Battagram District?

Battagram District covers 1,301 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 425.93 people per km².

Is Battagram District urban or rural?

Entirely rural: the 2023 census counted no urban population in Battagram District, so all 554,133 residents were enumerated in rural areas.

What is the literacy rate of Battagram District?

39.09% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 54.69%, female 23.34%).

Which languages are spoken in Battagram District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (81.94% of the 553,285 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Hindko (3.85%), Kohistani (2.11%).

Which province is Battagram District in?

Battagram District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Battagram.

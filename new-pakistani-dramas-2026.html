New Pakistani Dramas 2026

The latest and upcoming Pakistani dramas of 2025–2026 on HUM TV, Geo Entertainment, ARY Digital, Green Entertainment and Express Entertainment, with cast, story, writer, director and timings from official and newspaper sources.

New Pakistani Dramas 2026 at a Glance

This guide lists new and latest Pakistani dramas of 2025–2026 by channel, with cast, story, writer, director and broadcast slot. Every entry is taken from an official channel page or a report in Dawn, The Express Tribune, The News, ARY News or another named Pakistani outlet, and each source is linked. Latest HUM TV Dramas 2026 (HUM TV Dramas List)

Zanjeerain

Zanjeerain (Chains) is a HUM TV romance drama set against the mountains of Azad Kashmir. Its story is built around love, power and a long feud between rival families, with Sajal Aly as Rabiya, known as Biya, at the centre.

Cast: Sajal Aly (Rabiya (Biya)), Danyal Zafar (Sar Buland), Ameer Gilani (Mudassir), Ahsan Khan (Sher Dil), Adnan Siddiqui (Zafran Khan)

Writer: Farhat Ishtiaq · Director: Shehzad Kashmiri · Premiere: 29 April 2026

Zanjeerain cast and story

Aap Ki Izzat

Aap Ki Izzat (Your Honour) is a 2026 HUM TV serial about a marriage tested by love, dignity, trust and the pressures that enter a relationship from outside. Ahsan Khan plays Tabish and Urwa Hocane plays Beena.

Cast: Ahsan Khan (Tabish), Urwa Hocane (Beena), Sania Saeed, Saif Ali Khan, Nauman Masood

Writer: Umera Ahmed · Director: Saife Hassan · Slot: Friday and Saturday, 8:00 pm (repeat Saturday and Sunday, 9:00 am)

Aap Ki Izzat cast and story

Gulbahar

Gulbahar is a new HUM TV family drama about Sara (Sanam Saeed) and Sheheryar (Emmad Irfani), a couple deeply in love whose marriage comes under pressure because Sara cannot have children.

Cast: Sanam Saeed (Sara), Emmad Irfani (Sheheryar), Komal Meer (Diya), Shahood Alvi, Bakhtawar Mazhar

Writer: Aliya Bukhari · Director: Mehreen Jabbar · Slot: Wednesday and Thursday, 8:00 pm (repeat Thursday and Friday, 9:00 am)

Gulbahar cast and story

Khushi

Daily serial (Monday to Sunday, 7:00 pm). Writer Hina Huma Nafees; director Haseeb Ali; cast Marium Ejaz, Summiya Buksh, Ahin Babar, Arsalan Khan, Behroz Sabzwari and Seemi Pasha.

Source: hum.tv

Mohabbat Mukhtasir

Sunday, 8:00 pm. Writer Noor ul Huda Shah; director Meer Sikandar; cast Usama Khan, Syeda Madiha Imam, Aly Khan and Atiqa Odho.

Zehar-e-Mohabbat

Saturday and Sunday, 9:00 pm. Writer Shahid Dogar; director Saqib Zafar; cast Zoha Tauqeer, Umer Shahzad, Ammara Malik and Arez Ahmed.

Source: hum.tv

Writer Radain Shah; director Asad Mumtaz Malik; cast Ali Raza, Kinza, Farhan Tahir and Ali Safina.

Muamma

Writer Imran Nazir; director Shaqielle Khan; cast Saba Qamar, Usman Mukhtar and Maria Wasti.

Musafat

New Geo Entertainment Dramas 2026 (Har Pal Geo)

Nashtar

Nashtar (also spelt Nishter) is a Geo Entertainment drama about Shah Dawood, the proud heir of a powerful landowning family who is used to getting his way, and Bareera Aijaz, a principled university student who refuses to be intimidated by his status.

Cast: Danish Taimoor (Shah Dawood), Anmol Baloch (Bareera Aijaz), Naeema Butt (Tamseel), Usmaan Peerzada (Dastoor Ali Khan), Haroon Shahid (Arbaz)

Writer: Maha Malik · Director: Seraj ul Haq · Premiere: 21 August 2026 · Slot: Friday and Saturday, 8:00 pm

Nashtar cast and story

Mohabbat

Mohabbat (Love) is a new Geo Entertainment and 7th Sky Entertainment serial. Its story revolves around Yahya and Zarrar, two brothers from different generations whose bond of love is put to the test.

Cast: Feroze Khan, Nawal Saeed, Shahveer Kadwani, Aina Asif, Saira Nadeem

Writer: Shafia Khan · Director: Asad Jabal · Premiere: Monday 28 September 2026 · Slot: Monday and Tuesday, 8:00 pm

Mohabbat cast and story

Uswah

Daily at 7:00 pm from 23 September 2026. Written by Hina Huma Nafees and directed by Ali Akbar for Geo and 7th Sky Entertainment. Uswah's cheerful life changes completely because of what first looks like a harmless joke, and the serial explores how small matters among young people can turn serious. Music by Azaan Zair Baga with vocals by Afshan Fawad; the OST's poetry, composition and voice are by Sahir Ali Bagga.

Source: www.thenews.pk

Official schedule: Har Pal Geo.

New ARY Digital Dramas 2026

Dar-e-Nijaat

Dar-e-Nijaat (spelt Dar-i-Nijaat by Dawn) is an ARY Digital drama written by Umera Ahmed and one of the most discussed shows on air in 2026. Its lead, Zaryab (Durefishan Saleem), is a grey, secular heroine, a break from the writer's usual morally upright heroine.

Cast: Durefishan Saleem (Zaryab), Nameer Khan (Zamil), Sheheryar Munawar (Abu Bakar)

Writer: Umera Ahmed · Slot: Friday and Saturday, 8:00 pm

Dar-e-Nijaat cast and story

Hey Fam

Hey Fam is an ARY Digital drama about viral fame, social media and online personas, aimed at a younger audience. It stars Zaviyar Nauman Ejaz, Zarrar Khan and Dua Zahra.

Cast: Zaviyar Nauman Ejaz, Zarrar Khan, Dua Zahra

Writer: Awais Ahmad Joyia · Director: Zeshan Ansari · Slot: Wednesday and Thursday, 8:00 pm (ARY Digital and ARY Plus)

Hey Fam cast and story

Official schedule: ARY Digital.

New Green Entertainment Dramas 2026

Mitti De Baway

Mitti De Baway is a large-scale Green TV drama set in rural Punjab. It marks Mahira Khan's return to television after several years focused mainly on films, and pairs her with Wahaj Ali.

Cast: Mahira Khan (Billo), Wahaj Ali (Bala), Sohail Ahmed, Amar Khan, Hina Afridi

Writer: Faiza Iftikhar · Director: Syed Wajahat Hussain · Premiere: 23 September 2026 · Slot: Wednesday and Thursday, 8:00 pm

Mitti De Baway cast and story

Nizaam

Saturday and Sunday, 8:00 pm. Written by Fatima Faizan. Hoor (Momina Iqbal) dreams of studying abroad, but her plans collapse when her brother Shahzaib (Daniyal Aamir) marries YouTuber Zoya (Zunairah Rafi), alarming their conservative father Fareed (Ayub Khosa). Naumaan Ijaz plays Shafiq, a stern, principled father, and Usama Khan plays his son Ahsan, who strikes up a fragile acquaintance with Hoor.

Source: www.dawn.com

Official schedule: Green Entertainment.

Mere Paas Raho Tum

Missing Darling

Writers and Directors Behind the 2026 Dramas

Umera Ahmed has three high-profile 2026 projects: Aap Ki Izzat on HUM TV, Dar-e-Nijaat on ARY Digital and the upcoming Rutba on ARY. Farhat Ishtiaq wrote Zanjeerain, and Hina Huma Nafees wrote both Uswah (Geo) and Khushi (HUM). Among directors, Mehreen Jabbar returned with Gulbahar, Saife Hassan directed Aap Ki Izzat, Shehzad Kashmiri directed Zanjeerain, Seraj ul Haq directed Nashtar and Syed Wajahat Hussain directed Mitti De Baway. New Pakistani Dramas 2026 FAQ

What are the new Pakistani dramas of 2026?

New 2026 dramas include Zanjeerain, Aap Ki Izzat and Gulbahar on HUM TV; Nashtar, Uswah and Mohabbat on Geo Entertainment; Dar-e-Nijaat and Hey Fam on ARY Digital; Mitti De Baway and Nizaam on Green Entertainment; and Ranjish and Shaandaar on Express Entertainment.

Which upcoming Pakistani dramas have been announced?

Rutba, an ARY Digital drama by Umera Ahmed with Talha Chahour, Zahid Ahmed, Hiba Bukhari and Mehwish Hayat, was listed as coming soon by Dawn in September 2026. The Express Tribune also reported an Express Entertainment serial pairing Bilal Abbas Khan and Sarah Khan.

Which new drama stars Mahira Khan?

Mahira Khan stars as Billo in Mitti De Baway on Green TV, opposite Wahaj Ali as Bala. It premiered on 23 September 2026.

Who wrote Zanjeerain and who is in its cast?

Zanjeerain is written by Farhat Ishtiaq and directed by Shehzad Kashmiri. Sajal Aly, Danyal Zafar, Ameer Gilani, Ahsan Khan and Adnan Siddiqui lead the cast.

Where can I check today's Pakistani drama timings?

Use the official schedules on hum.tv, harpalgeo.tv, arydigital.tv, greenentertainment.tv and expressentertainment.tv, because slots and repeat timings change often.

Mohmand District

Peshawar Division · former FATA agency · headquarters Ghalanai · 7 tehsils · 553,933 people (2023 census)

Mohmand District: Quick Answers

Mohmand District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Ghalanai. It has 7 tehsils: Ambar Utman Khel, Halim Zai, Pindiali, Prang Ghar, Safi, Upper Mohmand and Yake Ghund. The 2023 census counted 553,933 people on 2,296 km², and 31.28% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

From tribal agency to district

Mohmand was one of the seven agencies of the Federally Administered Tribal Areas (FATA) (Xinhua). FATA was merged into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa when President Mamnoon Hussain signed the 25th Constitutional Amendment on 31 May 2018 (Dawn). The provincial Board of Revenue then placed Mohmand District in Peshawar Division (Dawn).

Tehsils of Mohmand District

The 2023 census counted 7 tehsils in Mohmand District: Ambar Utman Khel, Halim Zai, Pindiali, Prang Ghar, Safi, Upper Mohmand and Yake Ghund. Pindiali is the most populous, with 112,247 people, and Upper Mohmand is the largest by area (513 km²). Literacy ranges from 21.62% in Upper Mohmand to 45.18% in Halim Zai.

Population and growth

Mohmand District had 553,933 people in the 2023 census, up from 474,345 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.63%). Among the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the PBS 2023 census tables, it ranks 26th by population and 15th by area (2,296 km²). PBS counted no urban population in the district. There were 103.24 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.8%).

Literacy and schooling

31.28% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 30th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 46.85% and female literacy 15.1%. The census counted 123,082 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (76,387 girls and 46,695 boys).

Mohmand District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Mohmand District?

Mohmand District has 7 tehsils: Ambar Utman Khel, Halim Zai, Pindiali, Prang Ghar, Safi, Upper Mohmand and Yake Ghund.

What is the population of Mohmand District?

The 2023 census counted 553,933 people in Mohmand District, up from 474,345 in 2017 (annual growth 2.63%). 0% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Mohmand District?

Mohmand District covers 2,296 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 241.26 people per km².

Is Mohmand District urban or rural?

Entirely rural: the 2023 census counted no urban population in Mohmand District, so all 553,933 residents were enumerated in rural areas.

What is the literacy rate of Mohmand District?

31.28% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 46.85%, female 15.1%).

Which languages are spoken in Mohmand District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.8% of the 553,933 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Mohmand District in?

Mohmand District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Ghalanai.

When did Mohmand become a district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa?

Mohmand was a FATA tribal agency until the 25th Constitutional Amendment, signed on 31 May 2018, merged FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It is now a district of Peshawar Division. (Dawn)

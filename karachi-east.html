Karachi East District: Quick Answers

Karachi East District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, in Karachi Division. It has 4 sub-divisions: Ferozabad, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Gulzar-e-Hijri and Jamshed Quarters. The 2023 census counted 3,921,742 people on 139 km², and 80.07% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Karachi

Karachi, the capital of Sindh, is Pakistan's largest city and principal seaport, on the Arabian Sea just north-west of the Indus delta (Britannica).

Administrative units of Karachi East District

The 2023 census counted 4 administrative units in Karachi East District: Ferozabad (sub-division), Gulshan-e-Iqbal (sub-division), Gulzar-e-Hijri (sub-division) and Jamshed Quarters (sub-division). Ferozabad is the most populous, with 1,167,692 people, and Gulzar-e-Hijri is the largest by area (79 km²). Literacy ranges from 64.74% in Gulzar-e-Hijri to 89.18% in Gulshan-e-Iqbal.

Population and growth

Karachi East District had 3,921,742 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,875,315 in 2017 (average annual growth 5.32%). It covers 139 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 1st by population and 27th by area. 100% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 113.51 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Urdu was the most common mother tongue (48.98%), followed by Sindhi (12.81%), Pashto (11.59%) and Punjabi (10.41%).

Literacy and schooling

80.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 2nd of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 81.31% and female literacy 78.65%. The census counted 286,039 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (119,632 girls and 166,377 boys).

Karachi East District: FAQ

How many sub-divisions are in Karachi East District?

Karachi East District has 4 sub-divisions: Ferozabad, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Gulzar-e-Hijri and Jamshed Quarters.

What is the population of Karachi East District?

The 2023 census counted 3,921,742 people in Karachi East District, up from 2,875,315 in 2017 (annual growth 5.32%). 100% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Karachi East District?

Karachi East District covers 139 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 28,213.97 people per km².

What is the literacy rate of Karachi East District?

80.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 81.31%, female 78.65%).

Which languages are spoken in Karachi East District?

Which province is Karachi East District in?

Karachi East District is in Sindh, in Karachi Division.

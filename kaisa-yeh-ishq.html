Kaisa Yeh Ishq Story

Kaisa Yeh Ishq is the story of Sila and Ali, who have loved each other since childhood, when their engagement was arranged. Sila (Zoha Tauqeer) is a gentle, innocent young woman brought up by her strict grandfather, Ayaz Durrani (Usman Peerzada).

Still hurt by his own daughter's love marriage, Ayaz Durrani keeps Sila under tight restrictions to protect the family's honour, and Ali (Hashaam Khan) becomes her comfort and her source of happiness. A misunderstanding then leads her grandfather to break the engagement.

Sila and Ali refuse to give up and Ali plans a secret marriage, but Sara (Saima Hussyn), who is in love with Ali, tells Ayaz Durrani. In his anger he quickly marries Sila off to a stranger, and she is pushed into an unhappy life, while a heartbroken Ali goes on to marry Sara but cannot forget Sila. The drama asks whether fate will give the two another chance and whether Sila can take charge of her own life.

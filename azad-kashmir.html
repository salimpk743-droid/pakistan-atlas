Azad Jammu and Kashmir آزاد جموں و کشمیر

Azad Jammu and Kashmir is administered from Muzaffarabad and has 10 districts in 3 divisions. The 2017 census counted 4,032,363 people on 13,297 km²; the AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects 4,460,971 for 2023.

About Azad Jammu and Kashmir

The administration of Azad Kashmir is headquartered at Muzaffarabad; Mirpur is the major town in the south of the territory. In 2005 a strong earthquake devastated Muzaffarabad and the surrounding region (Britannica).

Culture and Geography of Azad Jammu and Kashmir

Kashmiri, Pahari and Gojri heritage. Neelum and Jhelum valleys, traditional wooden architecture and mountain crafts.

Education and Health in Azad Jammu and Kashmir

University of AJK and several public colleges in each district.

CMH and DHQ hospitals in Muzaffarabad, Mirpur and Rawalakot.

Public Issues in Azad Jammu and Kashmir

Terrain, winter access, and earthquake-resilient construction after 2005.

Notable Places in Azad Jammu and Kashmir

Muzaffarabad, Mirpur, Neelum, Rawalakot

Districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir

Bagh

Bagh District

Poonch Division district of Azad Kashmir with 3 tehsils (Bagh, Dhirkot and Harighel); the 2017 census counted 371,901 people on 770 km².

Bhimber

Bhimber District

Mirpur Division district of Azad Kashmir with 3 tehsils (Bhimber, Barnala and Samahni); the 2017 census counted 418,543 people on 1,516 km².

Forward Kahuta

Haveli District

Poonch Division district of Azad Kashmir with 2 tehsils (Haveli/Mumtazabad and Khurshidabad); the 2017 census counted 146,220 people on 598 km².

Hattian Bala

Jhelum Valley District

Muzaffarabad Division district of Azad Kashmir with 3 tehsils (Hattian, Chikar and Leepa); the 2017 census counted 226,496 people on 854 km².

Kotli

Kotli District

Mirpur Division district of Azad Kashmir with 5 tehsils (Kotli, Khuiratta, Charhoi/Darlia Jattan, Sehnsa and Fatehpur Thakyala); the 2017 census counted 774,017 people on 1,862 km².

Mirpur

Mirpur District

Mirpur Division district of Azad Kashmir with 2 tehsils (Mirpur and Dudyal); the 2017 census counted 456,541 people on 1,010 km².

Muzaffarabad

Muzaffarabad District

Muzaffarabad Division district of Azad Kashmir with 2 tehsils (Muzaffarabad and Naseerabad); the 2017 census counted 651,154 people on 1,642 km².

Athmuqam

Neelum District

Muzaffarabad Division district of Azad Kashmir with 2 tehsils (Athmuqam and Sharda); the 2017 census counted 189,785 people on 3,621 km².

Rawalakot

Poonch (Rawalakot) District

Poonch Division district of Azad Kashmir with 4 tehsils (Rawalakot, Hajira, Abbaspur and Thorar); the 2017 census counted 499,826 people on 855 km².

Pallandri

Sudhanoti District

Poonch Division district of Azad Kashmir with 4 tehsils (Sudhnoti, Mong, Tararkhal and Baloch); the 2017 census counted 297,880 people on 569 km².

Azad Jammu and Kashmir: FAQ

What is the capital of Azad Jammu and Kashmir?

The administration of Azad Kashmir is headquartered at Muzaffarabad. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

What is the population of Azad Kashmir?

The 2017 census counted 4,032,363 people. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects 4,460,971 for 2023; this is a projection, not a census count. (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

What is the area of Azad Kashmir?

13,297 km². (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

How many tehsils does Azad Kashmir have?

32 sub-divisions (tehsils) in 2023, with 278 union councils, in 10 districts and 3 divisions. (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

How many districts does Azad Jammu and Kashmir have?

10 districts in 3 divisions, as of September 2026. (Planning & Development Department, Government of AJK)

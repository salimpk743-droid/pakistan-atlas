Azad Jammu and Kashmir آزاد جموں و کشمیر

Kashmiri, Pahari and Gojri heritage. Neelum and Jhelum valleys, traditional wooden architecture and mountain crafts.

Azad Jammu and Kashmir facts

Capital: Muzaffarabad · Population: 4,045,366 (2023 census) · Area: 13,297 km² · Districts: 10.

Full list: districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir → · All provinces and territories →

Culture and geography

Kashmiri, Pahari and Gojri heritage. Neelum and Jhelum valleys, traditional wooden architecture and mountain crafts.

Education and health

University of AJK and several public colleges in each district.

CMH and DHQ hospitals in Muzaffarabad, Mirpur and Rawalakot.

Tehsils of Swat District

Swat District has 4 tehsils: Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot. Babuzai is the most populous, with 696,697 people in the 2023 census, and Kabal is the largest by area (485 km²).

Swat District administrative structure

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Malakand Division at the 2023 census. Each card gives the 2023 census population, area, urban share and literacy of one tehsil.

The 2023 census counted Swat and Bar Swat (Upper Swat) as one district (Swat). Since the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government notification of 27 January 2026, Swat District consists of Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot; the population and area shown are the sum of those units' 2023 census rows.

Current tehsil list: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government notification of 27 January 2026, reported by Dawn on 28 January 2026.

How the tehsils compare

Babuzai tehsil has the most people (696,697) and Charbagh the fewest (159,358). Kabal covers the largest area (485 km²). Barikot is the most urban (52.26% of residents in urban areas). Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 49.26% in Kabal to 56.06% in Babuzai.

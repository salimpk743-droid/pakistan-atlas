Malakand Division · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa · 2023 census figures

Swat District administrative structure

Swat District has 4 tehsils: Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot. The district is part of Malakand Division.

The 2023 census counted Swat and Bar Swat (Upper Swat) as one district (Swat). Since the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government notification of 27 January 2026, Swat District consists of Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot; the population and area shown are the sum of those units' 2023 census rows.

Current tehsil list: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government notification of 27 January 2026, reported by Dawn on 28 January 2026 (https://www.dawn.com/news/1969386).

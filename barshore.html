Barshore District

Pishin Division · Balochistan

Barshore District: Quick Answers

Created
Balochistan Revenue Department notification of 8 July 2026, from Pishin District
2023 census
The census counted Barshore tehsil under Pishin District: 141,994 people on 2,288 km². No separate district total was published.
Headquarters
Barshore
Division
Pishin Division
Province / territory
Balochistan

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Census 2023, Table 1.

Administrative status: ProPakistani, 2026-02-25; Dawn, 2026-07-12; Dawn, 2026-07-11.

Barshore (Barshor) District was approved by the Balochistan cabinet in early 2026 and is part of the new Pishin Division. The 8 July 2026 notification upgraded Barshore to a tehsil and created the Toba Kakari sub-division and tehsil in the district.

Barshore: How the district was created

Created by the Balochistan Revenue Department notification of 8 July 2026. It was formed from Pishin District.

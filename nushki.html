Nushki District: Quick Answers

Nushki District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Rakhshan Division, with its headquarters at Nushki. The 2023 census lists 2 administrative units in it: Dak and Nushki. The 2023 census counted 207,834 people on 5,797 km², and 57.12% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Nushki District

The 2023 census counted 2 administrative units in Nushki District: Dak (sub-tehsil) and Nushki (sub-division). Nushki is the most populous, with 190,905 people, and it is also the largest by area (3,731 km²). Literacy ranges from 36.29% in Dak to 59.04% in Nushki.

Population and growth

Nushki District had 207,834 people in the 2023 census, up from 178,947 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.53%). It covers 5,797 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 27th by population and 17th by area. 23.37% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 108.75 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Nushki (48,572). Brahui was the most common mother tongue (56.87%), followed by Balochi (38.46%) and Pashto (4.34%).

Literacy and schooling

57.12% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 1st of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 69.24% and female literacy 44.16%; the urban rate was 66.33% and the rural rate 54.16%. The census counted 31,069 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (16,327 girls and 14,742 boys).

Nushki District: FAQ

What is the population of Nushki District?

The 2023 census counted 207,834 people in Nushki District, up from 178,947 in 2017 (annual growth 2.53%). 23.37% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Nushki District?

Nushki District covers 5,797 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 35.85 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Nushki District?

What is the literacy rate of Nushki District?

Which languages are spoken in Nushki District?

Which province is Nushki District in?

Nushki District is in Balochistan, in Rakhshan Division. Its headquarters is Nushki.

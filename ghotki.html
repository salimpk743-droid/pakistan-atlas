Ghotki District: Quick Answers

Ghotki District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Ghotki. It has 5 talukas: Daharki, Ghotki, Khangarh, Mirpur Mathelo and Ubauro. The 2023 census counted 1,772,609 people on 6,083 km², and 41.38% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Ghotki District

The 2023 census counted 5 talukas in Ghotki District: Daharki, Ghotki, Khangarh, Mirpur Mathelo and Ubauro. Ghotki is the most populous, with 540,939 people, and Daharki is the largest by area (2,088 km²). Literacy ranges from 36.84% in Daharki to 45.96% in Ghotki.

Population and growth

Ghotki District had 1,772,609 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,648,708 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.22%). It covers 6,083 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 16th by population and 9th by area. 21.4% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 107.99 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 6 urban localities; the largest are Ghotki (119,879), Daharki (90,177), Mirpur Mathelo (74,651) and Ubauro (50,981). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (95.79%), followed by Urdu (1.79%).

Literacy and schooling

41.38% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 21st of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 55.32% and female literacy 26.27%; the urban rate was 57.69% and the rural rate 36.53%. The census counted 320,479 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (185,128 girls and 135,342 boys).

Ghotki District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Ghotki District?

Ghotki District has 5 talukas: Daharki, Ghotki, Khangarh, Mirpur Mathelo and Ubauro.

What is the population of Ghotki District?

The 2023 census counted 1,772,609 people in Ghotki District, up from 1,648,708 in 2017 (annual growth 1.22%). 21.4% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Ghotki District?

Ghotki District covers 6,083 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 291.4 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Ghotki District?

The 2023 census lists 6 urban localities in Ghotki District: Ghotki (119,879, municipal committee), Daharki (90,177, town committee), Mirpur Mathelo (74,651, municipal committee), Ubauro (50,981, town committee), Khangarh (32,648, town committee) and Adilpur (11,046, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Ghotki District?

41.38% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 55.32%, female 26.27%).

Which languages are spoken in Ghotki District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (95.79% of the 1,760,761 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (1.79%).

Which province is Ghotki District in?

Ghotki District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Ghotki.

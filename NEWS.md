# Weekly Sunday news: how to publish

Every Sunday mybook.pk publishes **one** detailed article on the most important Pakistan news of the past week.

- Article pages: `news-<yyyy-mm-dd>-<slug>.html`, at the site root like every other page (relative `css/` and `js/` paths).
- Archive, newest first: `pakistan-weekly-news.html`, live at <https://mybook.pk/pakistan-weekly-news.html>.
- One "Latest news" line appears on `pakistan-current-affairs.html`, above the current week's news. Only the text between `<!-- mb:latest-news:start -->` and `<!-- mb:latest-news:end -->` changes, and it links to the new article and to the archive.
- **The homepage (`index.html`) is never changed by this routine.** Don't add news links, headlines or dates to it. `build-news.js` refuses to write it. (This is the owner's instruction from 4 Oct 2026.)

All of these files are generated by `scripts/build-news.js` from data files. Don't hand-edit the generated HTML, because the next build overwrites it.

## Every Sunday (about 15 minutes plus research)

1. **Pick the story.** Choose the most important Pakistan news of the seven days up to Sunday. Check it against at least two reliable, dated sources: Dawn, The Express Tribune, Geo News, APP, Reuters, Business Recorder, The News, or official sites (PID, NA, Senate, ECP, ISPR, SBP).
2. **Copy last week's data file.** For example:
   `cp data/news/2026-10-04-pti-march-talks-deadlock.json data/news/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<slug>.json`
   The slug must be lowercase letters, digits and hyphens. Then edit the new file:
   - `date`, `slug`: must match the file name.
   - `published` and `modified`: full ISO time with the PKT offset, e.g. `2026-10-11T14:30:00+05:00`.
   - `updated_label`: e.g. `11 October 2026`.
   - `headline`: the H1, and the NewsArticle headline (110 characters max).
   - `short_headline`: about 60 characters max. Used for "More Pakistan news" links on later articles.
   - `title`: 65 characters max. `description`: 70–170 characters.
   - `summary`: shown under the H1 and in the archive. `lead`: first paragraph; HTML is allowed.
   - `key_facts`, `sections[]` (`id`, `heading`, `paragraphs[]` and/or `list[]`; HTML links are allowed), `urdu` (`heading`, `points[]`) and `sources[]` (`outlet`, `date`, `title`, `url`).
3. **Add the entry to the top of `data/news.json`** (`{"date": "...", "slug": "..."}`). Keep it newest first and **never delete old entries**. Every page listed there stays published and in the archive.
4. **Build and check:**
   ```bash
   node scripts/build-news.js      # writes the article, the archive and the latest-news line on pakistan-current-affairs.html
   node scripts/build-site.js      # adds head tags, header, footer, breadcrumbs (CI also runs this)
   node scripts/generate-sitemap.js
   node scripts/audit-html.js && node scripts/audit-internal-links.js && node scripts/audit-sitemap.js
   ```
   Or run the whole CI pipeline (see `.github/workflows/generate-sitemap.yml`). It already includes `build-news.js`.
5. **Bring `pakistan-current-affairs.html` up to date by hand.** Update the "This Week" section (verified, dated items, each with its sources) and keep every older week. When a week ends, rename its heading to "Previous Week" and leave its items in place. Never delete or shorten them.
6. **Branch, then PR.** Wait for the Vercel checks, then merge. After deployment, open the article, the archive and `pakistan-current-affairs.html`, and confirm the "Latest news" line links to the new page. Also check that the homepage has not changed. The sitemap picks up the new page automatically.

## Editorial rules

- Use verified facts only. Every number, date and quotation must come from a listed, dated source. Never invent or "tidy up" quotes. If something is unconfirmed at publication time (for example, whether an event has actually started), say so, e.g. "was scheduled to".
- State the time the reporting reflects, e.g. "reflects reports available by 2:30pm PKT on 4 October 2026".
- Cover what happened, who was involved, the dates, the background, reactions and what comes next. List the sources at the end. Include a short, accurate Urdu summary (`urdu.points`).
- Images: use only free-licensed images (record them in `scripts/lib/image-credits.json`), or none. By default the page uses the site's own OG image in its structured data.
- If you correct an old article later, edit its data file, update `modified` and `updated_label`, and never shorten or remove content. Corrections go to zainkhanpk742@gmail.com.
- Don't touch the AdSense (`ca-pub-3672700167787763`) or GA4 (`G-3QCG16GN6M`) tags. `build-news.js` writes them exactly as every other page has them, and `build-site.js` keeps one copy of each.

## What the build adds automatically

- `NewsArticle` JSON-LD with `datePublished` and `dateModified`, a canonical URL, `og:type=article`, and `article:published_time`/`modified_time`. `build-site.js` adds the Open Graph and Twitter tags, the breadcrumbs (Home › Weekly News › article) and the footer.
- The archive page gets `CollectionPage` + `ItemList` structured data, built from `<ol class="news-archive">`.
- Sitemap entries for the article and the archive.

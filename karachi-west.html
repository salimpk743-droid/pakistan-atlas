Karachi West District: Quick Answers

Karachi West District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, in Karachi Division. It has 3 sub-divisions: Manghopir, Mominabad and Orangi. The 2023 census counted 2,679,380 people on 370 km², and 67.43% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Karachi

Karachi, the capital of Sindh, is Pakistan's largest city and principal seaport, on the Arabian Sea just north-west of the Indus delta (Britannica).

Administrative units of Karachi West District

The 2023 census counted 3 administrative units in Karachi West District: Manghopir (sub-division), Mominabad (sub-division) and Orangi (sub-division). Manghopir is the most populous, with 1,081,753 people, and it is also the largest by area (342 km²). Literacy ranges from 60.76% in Orangi to 76.07% in Mominabad.

Population and growth

Karachi West District had 2,679,380 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,077,228 in 2017 (average annual growth 4.35%). It covers 370 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 4th by population and 26th by area. 90.71% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 112 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Urdu was the most common mother tongue (55.4%), followed by Pashto (24.38%), Punjabi (4.87%) and Sindhi (3.88%).

Literacy and schooling

67.43% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 5th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 69.75% and female literacy 64.79%; the urban rate was 68.98% and the rural rate 52.22%. The census counted 298,918 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (148,107 girls and 150,805 boys).

Karachi West District: FAQ

How many sub-divisions are in Karachi West District?

Karachi West District has 3 sub-divisions: Manghopir, Mominabad and Orangi.

What is the population of Karachi West District?

The 2023 census counted 2,679,380 people in Karachi West District, up from 2,077,228 in 2017 (annual growth 4.35%). 90.71% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Karachi West District?

Karachi West District covers 370 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 7,241.57 people per km².

What is the literacy rate of Karachi West District?

67.43% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 69.75%, female 64.79%).

Which languages are spoken in Karachi West District?

Which province is Karachi West District in?

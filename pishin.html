Pishin District

Founded 1883 as a British station · headquarters Pishin · 7 census units · 835,482 people (2023 census)

Pishin District: Quick Answers

Pishin District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Pishin Division, with its headquarters at Pishin. The 2023 census lists 7 administrative units in it: Barshore, Bostan, Hurram Zai, Karezat, Nana Sahib, Pishin and Saranan. The 2023 census counted 835,482 people (pre-split boundaries) on 6,218 km², and 51.07% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Pishin town and valley

The present town of Pishin was founded by the British as a military and civil station in 1883 and is linked by road with Quetta. The district lies north of Quetta and is made up of long valleys at 1,370–1,680 metres, drained by the Pishin Lora and its tributaries. Grapes, apples, apricots and peaches are important crops (Britannica).

Administrative units of Pishin District

The 2023 census counted 7 administrative units in Pishin District: Barshore (tehsil), Bostan (tehsil), Hurram Zai (sub-division), Karezat (sub-division), Nana Sahib (tehsil), Pishin (sub-division) and Saranan (tehsil). Pishin is the most populous, with 325,641 people, and Barshore is the largest by area (2,288 km²). Literacy ranges from 39.67% in Hurram Zai to 74.18% in Karezat.

Population and growth

Pishin District had 835,482 people in the 2023 census, up from 736,903 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.12%). It covers 6,218 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 4th by population and 16th by area. 29.18% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.34 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 4 urban localities: Pishin (107,646), Khanozai (46,682), Saranan (46,512) and Hurram Zai (42,945). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.07%).

Literacy and schooling

51.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 3rd of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 65.85% and female literacy 36.05%; the urban rate was 56.1% and the rural rate 48.94%. The census counted 170,453 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (93,336 girls and 77,106 boys).

Pishin District: FAQ

What is the population of Pishin District?

The 2023 census counted 835,482 people in Pishin District (pre-split boundaries), up from 736,903 in 2017 (annual growth 2.12%). 29.18% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Pishin District?

Pishin District covers 6,218 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 134.37 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Pishin District?

The 2023 census lists 4 urban localities in Pishin District: Pishin (107,646, municipal corporation), Khanozai (46,682, municipal committee), Saranan (46,512, municipal committee) and Hurram Zai (42,945, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Pishin District?

51.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 65.85%, female 36.05%).

Which languages are spoken in Pishin District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.07% of the 835,482 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Pishin District in?

Pishin District is in Balochistan, in Pishin Division. Its headquarters is Pishin.

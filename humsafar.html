Humsafar — ہم سفر

Humsafar (ہم سفر) is a landmark HUM TV Pakistani drama starring Fawad Khan and Mahira Khan. Explore its story, cast, writer, director and OST context.

Story

Humsafar follows Ashar and Khirad, whose arranged marriage is tested by family expectations, misunderstandings and separation. HUM TV describes the drama as a major part of its legacy and highlights the performances of Fawad Khan and Mahira Khan as Ashar and Khirad. citeturn0search3

Humsafar — ہم سفر

Humsafar (ہم سفر) is a landmark HUM TV Pakistani drama starring Fawad Khan and Mahira Khan. Explore its story, cast, writer, director and OST context.

ChannelHUM TV
WriterFarhat Ishtiaq
DirectorSarmad Khoosat
LeadsFawad Khan · Mahira Khan

Story

Humsafar follows Ashar and Khirad, whose arranged marriage is tested by family expectations, misunderstandings and separation. HUM TV describes the drama as a major part of its legacy and highlights the performances of Fawad Khan and Mahira Khan as Ashar and Khirad.

OST, Episodes & Ending Context

The drama is based on Farhat Ishtiaq's novel and is associated with Sarmad Khoosat as director. The page should treat creator credits as production facts, separate from audience interpretation.

Bilal Abbas Khan biography

Bilal Abbas Khan is a Pakistani actor who works in Urdu television and film. He was born on 4 June 1993 in Karachi, Sindh, according to an ACE News profile cited by Wikipedia. His father, Sohail Abbas, is a civil servant who earlier served as a DSP in the Sindh Police, and his mother, Farhana Sohail, is a fashion designer. He has two brothers and an elder sister.

He grew up in Karachi and was later sent to Lawrence College, Murree, a boarding school where school plays first got him interested in acting. He studied marketing at the Shaheed Zulfikar Ali Bhutto Institute of Science and Technology (SZABIST) in Karachi, graduating in 2015, and trained in acting at the National Academy of Performing Arts (NAPA). He started in 2016 with commercial modelling before auditioning for television roles.

Within three years he went from a small part in the ARY Digital soap Khushaal Susral to the lead in Cheekh (2019), and he has since won three Lux Style Awards. In 2020 the UK weekly Eastern Eye named him among its "Top 50 Asian celebrities", and in 2021 it listed him in its "30 under 30 Global Asian Stars".

Bilal Abbas Khan's biggest hits

O Rangreza (HUM TV, 2017)

His breakout role: Qasim, an introvert raised by a strict uncle (Noman Ijaz) who falls for the uncle's daughter (Sajal Aly). It earned him a HUM Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor.

Cheekh (ARY Digital, 2019)

As the antagonist Wajih opposite Saba Qamar he played against his usual image. Daily Times called him "scary as the coldblooded murderer", and the role brought a Lux Style Award nomination.

Pyar Ke Sadqay (HUM TV, 2020)

A naive maths genius opposite Yumna Zaidi. The role won him the Lux Style Award for Best TV Actor (Critics' Choice).

Ishq Murshid (HUM TV, 2023–2024)

As Shahmeer opposite Durefishan Saleem's Shibra. The Express Tribune reported that the final episode was screened in cinemas, and the pair won Best On-Screen Couple at the 10th HUM Awards in 2025. Our Durefishan Saleem page records that the official first-episode upload had passed 94 million YouTube views as of 2 October 2026.

Meri Zindagi Hai Tu (ARY Digital, 2025–2026)

His first pairing with Hania Aamir, as Kamyar Sohail. The serial ran from 7 November 2025 to 22 March 2026 (34 episodes); ARY Digital uploaded the last episode on 22 March 2026. See our Meri Zindagi Hai Tu guide for cast and story.

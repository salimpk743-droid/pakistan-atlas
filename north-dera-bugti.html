North Dera Bugti District

Koh-i-Suleman Division · Balochistan

North Dera Bugti District: Quick Answers

Created
Created as Upper Dera Bugti in February 2026; renamed North Dera Bugti by the 8 July 2026 notification, from Dera Bugti District
2023 census
The census counted Baiker sub-tehsil under Dera Bugti District: 33,410 people on 258 km². No separate district total was published.
Headquarters
Baiker (Bekhar)
Division
Koh-i-Suleman Division
Province / territory
Balochistan

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Census 2023, Table 1.

Administrative status: Balochistan Express, 2026-02-26; Dawn, 2026-07-12; Dawn, 2026-07-11.

North Dera Bugti was created as Upper Dera Bugti when Dera Bugti District was divided in February 2026, with its headquarters at Baiker (Bekhar), and was renamed North Dera Bugti by the 8 July 2026 notification. It is part of the new Koh-i-Suleman Division.

North Dera Bugti: How the district was created

Created as Upper Dera Bugti in February 2026; renamed North Dera Bugti by the 8 July 2026 notification. It was formed from Dera Bugti District.

Tehsils of Lahore District

Lahore District has 5 tehsils: Lahore Cantt, Lahore City, Model Town, Raiwind and Shalimar. Lahore City is the most populous, with 4,123,354 people in the 2023 census, and Raiwind is the largest by area (467 km²).

How the tehsils compare

Lahore City tehsil has the most people (4,123,354) and Raiwind the fewest (1,080,637). Raiwind covers the largest area (467 km²). All of them were counted as fully urban. Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 72.35% in Raiwind to 81.21% in Shalimar.

Tehsils of Lahore District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Lahore District?

5: Lahore Cantt, Lahore City, Model Town, Raiwind and Shalimar. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which is the largest tehsil of Lahore District by population?

Lahore City, with 4,123,354 people in the 2023 census. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which is the largest tehsil of Lahore District by area?

Raiwind, at 467 km². (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which tehsil of Lahore District has the highest literacy rate?

Shalimar, where 81.21% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

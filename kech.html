Kech District

Tump separated in 2026 · headquarters Turbat · 6 census units · 857,118 people (2023 census)

Kech District: Quick Answers

Kech District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Makuran Division, with its headquarters at Turbat. The 2023 census lists 6 administrative units in it: Turbat, Bulaida, Dasht, Zamoran, Balnigore and Hoshab. The 2023 census counted 857,118 people on 19,138 km², and 49.76% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

The 2023 census counted Kech and Tump as one district (Kech). Since the Balochistan Revenue Department notification of 24 February 2026, Kech District consists of Turbat, Bulaida, Dasht, Zamoran, Balnigore and Hoshab; the population and area shown are the sum of those units' 2023 census rows.

Turbat and the Kech valley

Turbat, the district headquarters, lies on the left bank of the Kech River, a tributary of the Dasht River. It is a market for dates grown in the surrounding valley; sorghum, barley, wheat and rice are also grown. Roads link Turbat with Panjgur and Kalat to the northwest and with Pasni to the southeast (Britannica).

A Revenue Department notification of 24 February 2026 separated the Tump and Mand units from Kech as the new Tump District (Baldiyat Times; Radio Pakistan). The census figures below are for the units that remain in Kech, so the district is not ranked against the 2023 census districts.

Administrative units of Kech District

Kech District is made up of 6 administrative units that the 2023 census counted before the district took its present shape: Turbat (sub-division), Bulaida (sub-division), Dasht (sub-division), Zamoran (sub-tehsil), Balnigore (sub-tehsil) and Hoshab (sub-tehsil). Turbat is the most populous, with 470,605 people, and it is also the largest by area (9,742 km²). Literacy ranges from 28.97% in Zamoran to 63.65% in Turbat.

Population and growth

Kech District had 857,118 people in the 2023 census. It covers 19,138 km².

Towns

The census lists 2 urban localities: Turbat (268,625) and Bulaida (68,752).

Kech District: FAQ

What is the population of Kech District?

The 2023 census counted 857,118 people in Kech District.

What is the area of Kech District?

Kech District covers 19,138 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 44.79 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Kech District?

The 2023 census lists 2 urban localities in Kech District: Turbat (268,625, municipal corporation) and Bulaida (68,752, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Kech District?

49.76% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census. Computed from the 2023 Table 12 rows of its 6 units (literate persons aged 10+ ÷ population aged 10+).

Which province is Kech District in?

Kech District is in Balochistan, in Makuran Division. Its headquarters is Turbat.

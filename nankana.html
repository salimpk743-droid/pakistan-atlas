Birthplace of Guru Nanak. A district created from Sheikhupura.

Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is Nankana Sahib. The available public data note is Census 2023 — see PBS. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Education

Public colleges; students also go to Lahore and Sheikhupura.

Hospitals and healthcare

DHQ Nankana Sahib.

Culture and geography

Sikh heritage tourism and Punjabi farms.

Public issues

Managing pilgrimage infrastructure with village needs.

Villages and local places

Sangla Hill, Shah Kot villages

Sources: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics census material and public provincial or district sources. Statistics are not presented where the source dataset does not provide them.

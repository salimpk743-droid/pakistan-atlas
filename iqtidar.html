Iqtidar — Pakistani Drama

Iqtidar (اقتدار) is a Pakistani Urdu drama associated with Anmol Baloch and Ali Raza, with a story shaped around power, family and social conflict.

LanguageUrdu

Story & Context

The drama stars Anmol Baloch as Mehr-un-Nisa and Ali Raza as Shahnawaz, alongside a supporting cast including Javed Sheikh and Rubina Ashraf. Reference production information lists its release date as 19 September 2024 and identifies Hina Huma Nafees as writer and Fahim Burney as director.

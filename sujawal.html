headquarters Sujawal · 5 talukas · 839,292 people (2023 census)

Sujawal District: Quick Answers

Sujawal District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Sujawal. It has 5 talukas: Jati, Kharo Chan, Mirpur Bathoro, Shah Bunder and Sujawal. The 2023 census counted 839,292 people on 8,785 km², and 27.02% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Sujawal District

The 2023 census counted 5 talukas in Sujawal District: Jati, Kharo Chan, Mirpur Bathoro, Shah Bunder and Sujawal. Mirpur Bathoro is the most populous, with 231,735 people, and Jati is the largest by area (3,489 km²). Literacy ranges from 6.98% in Kharo Chan to 35.02% in Sujawal.

Population and growth

Sujawal District had 839,292 people in the 2023 census, up from 779,062 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.25%). It covers 8,785 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 29th by population and 5th by area. 10.59% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 108.71 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 5 urban localities; the largest are Sujawal (38,736), Chuhar Jamali (18,354), Daro (10,847) and Mirpur Bathoro (10,462). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (99.4%).

Literacy and schooling

27.02% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 29th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 33.7% and female literacy 19.66%; the urban rate was 65.4% and the rural rate 22%. The census counted 207,764 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (105,531 girls and 102,230 boys).

What is the population of Sujawal District?

What is the area of Sujawal District?

Sujawal District covers 8,785 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 95.54 people per km².

Which languages are spoken in Sujawal District?

Which province is Sujawal District in?

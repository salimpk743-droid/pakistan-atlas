Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum — کبھی میں کبھی تم

Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum (کبھی میں کبھی تم) is an ARY Digital drama starring Fahad Mustafa and Hania Aamir, written by Farhat Ishtiaq and directed by Badar Mehmood.

ChannelARY Digital
WriterFarhat Ishtiaq
DirectorBadar Mehmood
LeadsFahad Mustafa · Hania Aamir

Story

The story follows Mustafa, a relaxed and carefree younger son, and Sharjeena, a practical high-achieving woman. ARY Digital describes the drama as an “opposites attract” relationship story while also exploring Adeel and Rubab's power dynamics.

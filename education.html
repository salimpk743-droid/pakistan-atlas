Universities & colleges

Use HEC and provincial higher-education department lists as the source of truth.

Pakistan’s public university map is uneven. Punjab and Sindh hold the oldest campuses. KP expanded quickly after 2010. Balochistan, AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan have fewer degree-awarding institutions relative to distance. A useful atlas does not invent campus counts; it copies the latest Higher Education Commission recognised list and then adds public degree colleges run by each province.

Anchor universities students already know

  • Punjab: University of the Punjab, UET Lahore, Agriculture University Faisalabad, BZU Multan, IUB Bahawalpur.
  • Sindh: University of Karachi, NED, IBA Karachi, University of Sindh Jamshoro, Liaquat University of Medical & Health Sciences.
  • KP: University of Peshawar, UET Peshawar, Abdul Wali Khan University Mardan, Gomal University, University of Swat.
  • Balochistan: University of Balochistan, BUITEMS Quetta, Lasbela University.
  • ICT: Quaid-i-Azam University, NUST, COMSATS, IIUI.
  • AJK & GB: University of Azad Jammu & Kashmir, Karakoram International University.

How to fill a district education box

  1. Public university campus or sub-campus, if any.
  2. Government postgraduate and degree colleges (men / women).
  3. Cadet college or medical college if present.
  4. Literacy rate from the latest PBS table, with the year written beside it.

Never publish fake rankings. Link to HEC and the provincial education dashboard instead.

Tehsils of Rawalpindi District

The 2023 census lists 7 administrative units in Rawalpindi District: Gujar Khan (tehsil), Kahuta (tehsil), Kallar Sayaddan (tehsil), Kotli Sattian (tehsil), Murree (tehsil), Rawalpindi (tehsil) and Taxila (tehsil). Rawalpindi is the most populous, with 3,744,590 people in the 2023 census, and Rawalpindi is the largest by area (1,682 km²).

How the tehsils compare

Rawalpindi tehsil has the most people (3,744,590) and Kotli Sattian the fewest (120,421). Rawalpindi covers the largest area (1,682 km²). Rawalpindi is the most urban (89.67% of residents in urban areas). Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 79.72% in Gujar Khan to 88.2% in Kotli Sattian.

Haveli District: Quick Answers

Haveli District is one of the 10 districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, in Poonch Division. The 2017 census counted 146,220 people on 598 km², in 2 tehsils: Haveli/Mumtazabad and Khurshidabad.

Tehsils of Haveli District

The 2017 census tables published by the AJ&K Bureau of Statistics list 2 tehsils in Haveli District: Haveli/Mumtazabad and Khurshidabad (Statistical Year Book 2023, Table 15.15). Haveli/Mumtazabad is the most populous, with 118,956 people, and it is also the largest by area (391 km²). AJK at a Glance 2024 counts 32 tehsils across Azad Kashmir in 2023, against 30 in the 2017 census tables, so tehsil boundaries may since have changed (AJK at a Glance 2024).

Population and area

Haveli District had 146,220 people in the 2017 census, with average annual growth of 1.43% since 1998, 3.49% of them in urban areas and an average household of 6.78 people (Table 15.6). It covers 598 km² (Table 15.1). Of the 10 AJK districts it ranks 10th by population and 9th by area. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects about 0.159 million people for 2023 (AJK at a Glance 2024); these are projections, not census counts.

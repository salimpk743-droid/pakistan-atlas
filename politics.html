Politics

Four provincial assemblies, Parliament, ICT administration, AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan governments.

A district page on this site is also a civic map. The hospital and the degree college sit under a provincial department. The census sits under PBS. The capital territory sits under the federal government. Mixing those three into one “government” sentence hides who the reader should write to.

National Assembly and Senate seats are federal. Provincial assembly seats are where school and health budgets are argued. ICT has no provincial assembly; it has a federal administration and a metropolitan corporation. AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan have their own elected houses. This atlas names those units so a student can find the right door.

Opinion on this site stays tied to public services: whether a girls’ high school in Jandola has a teacher, whether a DHQ in Judba can take a night case. Party slogans without a district fact do not belong here.

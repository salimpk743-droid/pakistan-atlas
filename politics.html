News note

How Pakistan’s map is governed

Four provincial assemblies, the National Assembly and Senate, the ICT administration, and the governments of AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan. When you write politics here, say which house passed the thing you are describing.

Opinion

Why a district atlas is also a civic lesson

Young readers understand parliament faster when they can find their own DHQ hospital and degree college on a page. Opinion pieces on this site should stay tied to public services — schools, water, hospitals — not personality wars. Sign every opinion with a name.

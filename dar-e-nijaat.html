Dar-e-Nijaat Drama: Cast and Story

Dar-e-Nijaat (درِ نجات) is a relationship drama on ARY Digital, written by Umera Ahmed, starring Durefishan Saleem, Nameer Khan.

Dar-e-Nijaat Story

Dar-e-Nijaat (spelt Dar-i-Nijaat by Dawn) is an ARY Digital drama written by Umera Ahmed and one of the most discussed shows on air in 2026. Its lead, Zaryab (Durefishan Saleem), is a grey, secular heroine, a break from the writer's usual morally upright heroine.

Zaryab begins in a relationship with her childhood sweetheart Zamil (Nameer Khan), but Zamil turns to religion and rejects her as a bad influence. Abu Bakar (Sheheryar Munawar), the man Zaryab once mocked as too religious and provincial, turns out to be the kinder and more compassionate person. The drama has prompted wide social-media debate about contemporary relationships, and it underlines that women often pay a heavier price than men for moral slips.

Writer, Director and Production of Dar-e-Nijaat

The serial is written by Umera Ahmed. Dawn praised Durefishan Saleem's performance as a woman at breaking point, Nameer Khan's range and Sheheryar Munawar's warmth in the role of Abu Bakar.

When and Where to Watch Dar-e-Nijaat

Dar-e-Nijaat airs on ARY Digital, Friday and Saturday, 8:00 pm (Pakistan time). Schedules and repeat timings change, so check the official ARY Digital website for the latest episode and timing. Channels also upload episodes to their official YouTube channels.

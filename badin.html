Badin District: Quick Answers

Badin District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Badin. It has 5 talukas: Badin, Golarchi (S.F.Rahu), Matli, Talhar and Tando Bago. The 2023 census counted 1,947,081 people on 6,858 km², and 36.65% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Badin town

Badin was founded in 1750 and lies in swampy deltaic land east of the Indus River. Rice is the region's major crop; the town has sugar and rice mills and is the terminus of the Hyderabad–Badin railway. Oil and natural gas production in the area began in the mid-1990s (Britannica).

Talukas of Badin District

The 2023 census counted 5 talukas in Badin District: Badin, Golarchi (S.F.Rahu), Matli, Talhar and Tando Bago. Badin is the most populous, with 490,386 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,816 km²). Literacy ranges from 33.5% in Golarchi (S.F.Rahu) to 39.32% in Matli.

Population and growth

Badin District had 1,947,081 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,804,958 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.27%). It covers 6,858 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 11th by population and 8th by area. 22.08% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 108.42 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 12 urban localities; the largest are Badin (117,455), Matli (74,402), Tando Ghulam Ali (36,961) and Shaheed Fazal Rahu (35,313). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (93.85%), followed by Punjabi (2.75%) and Hindko (1.11%).

Literacy and schooling

36.65% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 25th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 46.46% and female literacy 26%; the urban rate was 53.67% and the rural rate 31.52%. The census counted 381,384 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (204,686 girls and 176,681 boys).

Badin District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Badin District?

Badin District has 5 talukas: Badin, Golarchi (S.F.Rahu), Matli, Talhar and Tando Bago.

What is the population of Badin District?

The 2023 census counted 1,947,081 people in Badin District, up from 1,804,958 in 2017 (annual growth 1.27%). 22.08% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Badin District?

Badin District covers 6,858 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 283.91 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Badin District?

The 2023 census lists 12 urban localities in Badin District; the ten largest are Badin (117,455, municipal committee), Matli (74,402, municipal committee), Tando Ghulam Ali (36,961, town committee), Shaheed Fazal Rahu (35,313, town committee), Khoski (29,324, town committee), Talhar (27,231, town committee), Tando Bago (22,786, municipal committee), Kario Ghanwar (21,775, town committee), Rajo Khanani (21,513, town committee) and Pangrio (17,982, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Badin District?

36.65% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 46.46%, female 26%).

Which languages are spoken in Badin District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (93.85% of the 1,945,417 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Punjabi (2.75%), Hindko (1.11%).

Which province is Badin District in?

Badin District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Badin.

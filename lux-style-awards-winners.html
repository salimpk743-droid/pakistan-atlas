Lux Style Awards 2025 Winners

The full winners list of the 24th Lux Style Awards, held at the Mohatta Palace in Karachi on 11 December 2025, from the Dawn and Geo News ceremony reports.

24th Lux Style Awards 2025: Ceremony

The 24th Lux Style Awards (LSA), one of Pakistan's oldest entertainment and fashion awards, were held on the night of Thursday 11 December 2025 in the gardens of the Mohatta Palace in Karachi. Sheheryar Munawar and Sanam Saeed hosted. The ceremony opened with the national anthem sung by students of Shehzad Roy's Zindagi Trust, and closed with a performance by Abida Parveen after she received the Lifetime Achievement Award to a standing ovation.

In television, Zard Patton Ka Bunn won the most trophies (play, writer and director), while Fahad Mustafa and Hania Aamir won the drama acting awards for Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum. In film, Nayab won the most awards (director and actress).

What the 2025 Results Mean for Pakistani Dramas

The results rewarded two very different dramas. Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum, the ARY Digital romance by Farhat Ishtiaq and director Badar Mehmood, took both lead-acting awards for Fahad Mustafa and Hania Aamir. Zard Patton Ka Bunn, written by Mustafa Afridi and directed by Saife Hasan, swept the critics' awards for play, writing and direction; the same serial also won big at the 10th HUM Awards in October 2025, where Sajal Aly was named best actress. Saife Hasan went on to direct Aap Ki Izzat for HUM TV in 2026.

Lux Style Awards FAQ

Who won best actress at the Lux Style Awards 2025?

Hania Aamir won the drama best actress award for Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum, and Yumna Zaidi won Film Actress of the Year for Nayab.

Who won best actor at the Lux Style Awards 2025?

Fahad Mustafa won the drama best actor award for Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum, and Samar Jafri won Film Actor of the Year for Na Baligh Afraad.

Which drama won the most Lux Style Awards in 2025?

Zard Patton Ka Bunn won TV Play of the Year, TV Drama Writer of the Year (Mustafa Afridi) and TV Director of the Year (Saife Hasan).

Where were the 24th Lux Style Awards held?

At the Mohatta Palace in Karachi on 11 December 2025.

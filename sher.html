Sher — Pakistani Drama

Sher (شیر) was a 2025 ARY Digital drama starring Danish Taimoor and Sarah Khan. It became a high-interest Pakistani drama search topic during its run.

Story & Context

The drama centres on Sher Zaman, played by Danish Taimoor, and Dr Fajar, played by Sarah Khan, amid family conflict, betrayal and competing interests. ARY News reported strong YouTube trending performance during the show's run. The available production credits identify Zanjabeel Asim Shah as writer and Aehsun Talish as director.

The 2023 census counted Tump and Kech as one district (Kech). Since the Balochistan Revenue Department notification of 24 February 2026, Tump District consists of Tump and Mand; the population and area shown are the sum of those units' 2023 census rows.

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Census 2023, Table 1. Checked 2026-09-29.

Administrative status: Baldiyat Times, 2026-02-27; Radio Pakistan, 2026-05-12; Daily Pakistan (via The Patriot), 2026-07-12.

Tump District was carved out of Kech by a Revenue Department notification of 24 February 2026, with its headquarters at Tump. It consists of the Tump and Mand sub-divisions and tehsils, in Makuran (Makran) Division.

How the district was created

Created by the Balochistan Revenue Department notification of 24 February 2026. It was formed from Kech District.

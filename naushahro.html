headquarters Naushahro Feroze · 5 talukas · 1,777,082 people (2023 census)

Naushahro Feroze District: Quick Answers

Naushahro Feroze District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Naushahro Feroze. It has 5 talukas: Bhiria, Kandiaro, Mehrabpur, Moro and Naushahro Feroze. The 2023 census counted 1,777,082 people on 2,946 km², and 57.15% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Naushahro Feroze District

The 2023 census counted 5 talukas in Naushahro Feroze District: Bhiria, Kandiaro, Mehrabpur, Moro and Naushahro Feroze. Naushahro Feroze is the most populous, with 408,356 people, and Kandiaro is the largest by area (771 km²). Literacy ranges from 51.24% in Naushahro Feroze to 64.04% in Kandiaro.

Population and growth

Naushahro Feroze District had 1,777,082 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,612,047 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.64%). It covers 2,946 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 15th by population and 14th by area. 28.54% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 11 urban localities; the largest are Moro (142,685), Naushahro Feroze (87,416), Mehrabpur (57,978) and Kandiaro (44,382). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (92.13%), followed by Urdu (3.98%) and Punjabi (2.13%).

Literacy and schooling

57.15% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 10th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 67.58% and female literacy 46.36%; the urban rate was 66.43% and the rural rate 53.2%. The census counted 214,663 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (115,160 girls and 99,496 boys).

How many talukas are in Naushahro Feroze District?

Naushahro Feroze District has 5 talukas: Bhiria, Kandiaro, Mehrabpur, Moro and Naushahro Feroze.

What is the population of Naushahro Feroze District?

The 2023 census counted 1,777,082 people in Naushahro Feroze District, up from 1,612,047 in 2017 (annual growth 1.64%). 28.54% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Naushahro Feroze District?

Naushahro Feroze District covers 2,946 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 603.22 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Naushahro Feroze District?

The 2023 census lists 11 urban localities in Naushahro Feroze District; the ten largest are Moro (142,685, municipal committee), Naushahro Feroze (87,416, town committee), Mehrabpur (57,978, town committee), Kandiaro (44,382, town committee), Mithiani (31,936, town committee), Bhiria Road (30,527, town committee), Tharu Shah (27,651, town committee), Padidan (27,092, town committee), Halani (25,317, town committee) and Darya Khan Marri (17,890, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Naushahro Feroze District?

Which languages are spoken in Naushahro Feroze District?

Which province is Naushahro Feroze District in?

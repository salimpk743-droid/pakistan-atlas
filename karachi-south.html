Karachi South District: Quick Answers

Karachi South District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, in Karachi Division. It has 5 sub-divisions: Aram Bagh, Civil Lines, Garden, Lyari and Saddar. The 2023 census counted 2,329,764 people on 122 km², and 78.57% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Karachi city

Karachi, the capital of Sindh, is Pakistan's largest city and principal seaport, on the Arabian Sea just north-west of the Indus delta (Britannica). Karachi's four arterial roads, among them M.A. Jinnah Road and I.I. Chundrigar Road, begin near Merewether Tower by the port. The old town lies north of M.A. Jinnah Road; east of it are the Civil Lines and Saddar Bazar, laid out on a grid (Britannica).

Administrative units of Karachi South District

The 2023 census counted 5 administrative units in Karachi South District: Aram Bagh (sub-division), Civil Lines (sub-division), Garden (sub-division), Lyari (sub-division) and Saddar (sub-division). Lyari is the most populous, with 949,878 people, and Civil Lines is the largest by area (73 km²). Literacy ranges from 68.4% in Lyari to 88.56% in Saddar.

Population and growth

Karachi South District had 2,329,764 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,769,230 in 2017 (average annual growth 4.71%). It covers 122 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 8th by population and 28th by area. 100% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 114 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Urdu was the most common mother tongue (34.7%), followed by Sindhi (15.62%), Balochi (11.58%) and Pashto (9.42%).

Literacy and schooling

78.57% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 4th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 80.76% and female literacy 76.05%. The census counted 155,238 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (77,946 girls and 77,287 boys).

Karachi South District: FAQ

How many sub-divisions are in Karachi South District?

Karachi South District has 5 sub-divisions: Aram Bagh, Civil Lines, Garden, Lyari and Saddar.

What is the population of Karachi South District?

The 2023 census counted 2,329,764 people in Karachi South District, up from 1,769,230 in 2017 (annual growth 4.71%). 100% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Karachi South District?

Karachi South District covers 122 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 19,096.43 people per km².

What is the literacy rate of Karachi South District?

Which languages are spoken in Karachi South District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Urdu (34.7% of the 2,329,764 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Sindhi (15.62%), Balochi (11.58%), Pashto (9.42%).

Which province is Karachi South District in?

Karachi South District is in Sindh, in Karachi Division.

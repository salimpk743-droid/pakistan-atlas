Skardu District: Quick Answers

Skardu District is one of the 10 districts for which Gilgit-Baltistan's Planning & Development Department publishes statistics. The 2023 census counted 278,885 people on 10,168 km², and 70.62% of residents were literate.

Population and growth

Skardu District had 278,885 people in the 2023 census, up from 260,836 in 2017, an average annual growth rate of 1.12%. It covers 10,168 km². The census counted 37,699 households, with an average of 7 people each, and 107 males for every 100 females (GB at a Glance 2025).

How Skardu compares within Gilgit-Baltistan

Among the 10 districts in the GB statistics, Skardu ranks 3rd by population, 2nd by area and 5th by literacy (70.62% in the 2023 census). Gilgit-Baltistan as a whole counted 1,709,049 people on 72,496 km² (GB at a Glance 2025).

New districts notified in 2019

In June 2019 the Gilgit-Baltistan government notified four sub-divisions (Gupis-Yasin, Rondu, Darel and Tangir) as separate districts with the same boundaries they had as sub-divisions (Dawn). The GB statistics for 2023 still report 10 districts, so any figures for Roundu are included in the numbers above.

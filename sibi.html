Sibi District: Quick Answers

Sibi District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Sevi Division, with its headquarters at Sibi. The 2023 census lists 4 administrative units in it: Kot Mandai, Lehri, Sangan and Sibi. The 2023 census counted 224,148 people on 7,121 km², and 47.41% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Sibi District

The 2023 census counted 4 administrative units in Sibi District: Kot Mandai (sub-tehsil), Lehri (sub-division), Sangan (sub-tehsil) and Sibi (sub-division). Sibi is the most populous, with 154,970 people, and Kot Mandai is the largest by area (1,977 km²). Literacy ranges from 8.93% in Kot Mandai to 52.4% in Sibi.

Population and growth

Sibi District had 224,148 people in the 2023 census, up from 179,751 in 2017 (average annual growth 3.76%). It covers 7,121 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 25th by population and 14th by area. 30.92% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 105.43 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Sibi (69,300). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (44.15%), followed by Sindhi (23.5%), Pashto (13.4%) and Saraiki (13.36%).

Literacy and schooling

47.41% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 6th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 55.72% and female literacy 38.63%; the urban rate was 68.33% and the rural rate 36.61%. The census counted 47,202 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (24,033 girls and 23,169 boys).

Sibi District: FAQ

What is the population of Sibi District?

The 2023 census counted 224,148 people in Sibi District, up from 179,751 in 2017 (annual growth 3.76%). 30.92% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Sibi District?

Sibi District covers 7,121 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 31.48 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Sibi District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Sibi District: Sibi (69,300, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Sibi District?

47.41% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 55.72%, female 38.63%).

Which languages are spoken in Sibi District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (44.15% of the 224,148 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Sindhi (23.5%), Pashto (13.4%), Saraiki (13.36%).

Which province is Sibi District in?

Sibi District is in Balochistan, in Sevi Division. Its headquarters is Sibi.

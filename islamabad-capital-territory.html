Islamabad Capital Territory اسلام آباد

Islamabad Capital Territory is the federal capital territory of Pakistan with its capital at Islamabad. The 2023 census counted 2,363,863 people (0.98% of Pakistan) on 906 km², and 83.97% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

About Islamabad Capital Territory

Islamabad is the capital of Pakistan, on the Potwar Plateau 14 km north-east of Rawalpindi. A commission chose the site in 1959 and construction began in 1961; the planned capital area covers 906 km² (Britannica).

Culture and Geography of Islamabad Capital Territory

Planned capital designed by Constantinos Doxiadis. Faisal Mosque, Margalla Hills and a mix of communities from every province.

Education and Health in Islamabad Capital Territory

QAU, NUST, COMSATS, IIUI and many federal colleges.

PIMS, Polyclinic and private teaching hospitals serve the capital and northern Punjab.

Public Issues in Islamabad Capital Territory

Rapid housing growth, traffic and pressure on the Margalla ecosystem.

Notable Places in Islamabad Capital Territory

Islamabad, Bari Imam, Saidpur Village

Islamabad Capital Territory: FAQ

What is the capital of Islamabad Capital Territory?

Islamabad is the capital of Pakistan; the territory covers the city and its surroundings. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

What is the population of Islamabad Capital Territory?

The 2023 census counted 2,363,863 people, up from 2,003,368 in 2017 (annual growth 2.80%). (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

What is the area of Islamabad Capital Territory?

906 km², with 2609.12 people per km² in 2023. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

What is the literacy rate of Islamabad Capital Territory?

83.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 88.23%, female 79.13%). (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

How many districts does Islamabad Capital Territory have?

1 district, as of September 2026. (ICT Administration)

QAU, NUST, COMSATS, IIUI and many federal colleges.

PIMS, Polyclinic and private teaching hospitals serve the capital and northern Punjab.

Islamabad

Islamabad District

Population: 2,363,863 (2023 census)

Islamabad was designed in the 1960s as a planned garden capital at the foot of the Margalla Hills. The Faisal Mosque, Daman-e-Koh viewpoint, Saidpur Village and Bari Imam shrine sit alongside the government sector belt and a ring of fast-growing housing societies.

Public issues

Rapid housing growth, traffic and pressure on the Margalla ecosystem.

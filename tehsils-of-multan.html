Tehsils of Multan District

Multan District has 4 tehsils: Jalalpur Pirwala, Multan City, Multan Saddar and Shujabad. Multan City is the most populous, with 2,555,486 people in the 2023 census, and Multan Saddar is the largest by area (1,632 km²).

How the tehsils compare

Multan City tehsil has the most people (2,555,486) and Jalalpur Pirwala the fewest (608,488). Multan Saddar covers the largest area (1,632 km²). Multan City is the most urban (84.91% of residents in urban areas). Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 38.5% in Jalalpur Pirwala to 73.65% in Multan City.

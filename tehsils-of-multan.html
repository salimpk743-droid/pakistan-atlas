Tehsils of Multan District

Multan Division · Punjab · 2023 census figures

Multan District administrative structure

Multan District has 4 tehsils: Jalalpur Pirwala, Multan City, Multan Saddar and Shujabad. The district is part of Multan Division.

Jalalpur Pirwala Tehsil

Population (2023): 608,488

Area: 978 km² · 622.18 people per km²

Urban share: 14.74%

Multan City Tehsil

Population (2023): 2,555,486

Area: 304 km² · 8,406.2 people per km²

Urban share: 84.91%

Multan Saddar Tehsil

Population (2023): 1,516,004

Area: 1,632 km² · 928.92 people per km²

Urban share: 5.88%

Shujabad Tehsil

Population (2023): 682,327

Area: 806 km² · 846.56 people per km²

Urban share: 22.15%

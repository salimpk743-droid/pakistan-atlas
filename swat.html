Swat District

Malakand Division · headquarters Gulkada (Babuzai tehsil) · 4 tehsils since January 2026 · 1,557,030 people in those tehsils (2023 census)

Swat District: Quick Answers

Swat District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, in Malakand Division, with its headquarters at Gulkada (Babuzai tehsil). It has 4 tehsils: Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot. The 2023 census counted 1,557,030 people on 1,362 km², and 52.63% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Swat District

Swat District has four tehsils: Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot. The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government notified the split of the old district on 27 January 2026. The other three tehsils, Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela, became Bar Swat District, with its headquarters at Matta (Dawn, 28 January 2026). The headquarters of Swat District is Gulkada, in Babuzai tehsil.

The 2023 census was taken before the split. The population and area shown on this page are the sum of the 2023 rows of the four tehsils that are now in Swat District. The census table above lists each tehsil separately.

Population and literacy

The four tehsils counted 1,557,030 people on 1,362 km² in 2023. Babuzai is the most populous, with 696,697 people. The largest urban locality is Mingora municipal committee (361,112), followed by Kabal (132,549) and Barri Kot (115,045). 52.63% of residents aged 10 and over in the four tehsils were literate.

Before the split, the census counted the whole old district, all seven tehsils, at 2,687,384 people. Its literacy rate was 48.13%: 61.83% for males and 33.95% for females.

History

Swat was a princely state ruled by the Wali of Swat. Miangul Abdul Haq Jahanzeb, the last Wali, was enthroned on 12 December 1949. He left office on 28 July 1969, when the state was merged with Pakistan (The Express Tribune).

Geography: the Swat River

The Swat River forms at Kalam, where the Gabral and Ushu rivers meet. Kalam is now in Bar Swat District. The river drains the whole valley, flowing south and then west until the Panjkora River joins it. It meets the Kabul River at Nisatta after a course of about 320 km. Canals from the river irrigate about 65,000 hectares, mainly of sugarcane and wheat (Britannica).

Notable person

Malala Yousafzai was born in Mingora on 12 July 1997. She shared the 2014 Nobel Peace Prize (The Nobel Prize).

How many tehsils are in Swat District?

What is the population of Swat District?

What is the area of Swat District?

What are the main towns and cities in Swat District?

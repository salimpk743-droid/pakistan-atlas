Muzaffarabad District: Quick Answers

Muzaffarabad District is one of the 10 districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, in Muzaffarabad Division. The 2017 census counted 651,154 people on 1,642 km², in 2 tehsils: Muzaffarabad and Naseerabad.

Tehsils of Muzaffarabad District

The 2017 census tables published by the AJ&K Bureau of Statistics list 2 tehsils in Muzaffarabad District: Muzaffarabad and Naseerabad (Statistical Year Book 2023, Table 15.15). Muzaffarabad is the most populous, with 499,798 people, and it is also the largest by area (904 km²). AJK at a Glance 2024 counts 32 tehsils across Azad Kashmir in 2023, against 30 in the 2017 census tables, so tehsil boundaries may since have changed (AJK at a Glance 2024).

Population and area

Muzaffarabad District had 651,154 people in the 2017 census, with average annual growth of 1.91% since 1998, 26.49% of them in urban areas and an average household of 6.01 people (Table 15.6). It covers 1,642 km² (Table 15.1). Of the 10 AJK districts it ranks 2nd by population and 3rd by area. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects about 0.733 million people for 2023 (AJK at a Glance 2024); these are projections, not census counts.

headquarters Matiari · 3 talukas · 849,383 people (2023 census)

Matiari District: Quick Answers

Matiari District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Matiari. It has 3 talukas: Hala, Matiari and Saeedabad. The 2023 census counted 849,383 people on 1,417 km², and 45.88% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Matiari District

The 2023 census counted 3 talukas in Matiari District: Hala, Matiari and Saeedabad. Matiari is the most populous, with 377,945 people, and it is also the largest by area (568 km²). Literacy ranges from 39.64% in Matiari to 52.58% in Hala.

Population and growth

Matiari District had 849,383 people in the 2023 census, up from 770,040 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.65%). It covers 1,417 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 28th by population and 23rd by area. 23.86% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 102.58 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 7 urban localities; the largest are Hala (71,094), Bhit Shah (45,345), New Saeedabad (24,084) and Matiari (22,517). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (92.95%), followed by Urdu (2.66%), Balochi (1.33%) and Hindko (1.31%).

Literacy and schooling

45.88% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 16th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 56.34% and female literacy 35.12%; the urban rate was 62.03% and the rural rate 40.61%. The census counted 129,690 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (70,261 girls and 59,424 boys).

Matiari District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Matiari District?

Matiari District has 3 talukas: Hala, Matiari and Saeedabad.

What is the population of Matiari District?

The 2023 census counted 849,383 people in Matiari District, up from 770,040 in 2017 (annual growth 1.65%). 23.86% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Matiari District?

Matiari District covers 1,417 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 599.42 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Matiari District?

The 2023 census lists 7 urban localities in Matiari District: Hala (71,094, municipal committee), Bhit Shah (45,345, town committee), New Saeedabad (24,084, town committee), Matiari (22,517, town committee), Hala Old (13,992, town committee), Khyber (12,937, town committee) and Odero Lal Station (12,704, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Matiari District?

45.88% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 56.34%, female 35.12%).

Which languages are spoken in Matiari District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (92.95% of the 849,351 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (2.66%), Balochi (1.33%), Hindko (1.31%).

Which province is Matiari District in?

Matiari District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Matiari.

Attock District: Quick Answers

Attock District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Attock. It has 6 tehsils: Attock, Fateh Jang, Hasan Abdal, Hazro, Jand and Pindi Gheb. The 2023 census counted 2,170,423 people on 6,857 km², and 70.22% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Attock District

The 2023 census counted 6 tehsils in Attock District: Attock, Fateh Jang, Hasan Abdal, Hazro, Jand and Pindi Gheb. Attock is the most populous, with 516,277 people, and Jand is the largest by area (2,043 km²). Literacy ranges from 66.45% in Hazro to 74.8% in Attock.

Population and growth

Attock District had 2,170,423 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,886,378 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.37%). It covers 6,857 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 23rd by population and 8th by area. 28.75% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 100.83 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 11 urban localities; the largest are Attock (142,826), Fateh Jang (81,321), Hasan Abdal (69,529) and Kamra Cantonment (67,425). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (65.23%), followed by Pashto (15.59%), Hindko (14.41%) and Urdu (2.68%).

Literacy and schooling

70.22% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 12th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 79.69% and female literacy 60.66%; the urban rate was 78.18% and the rural rate 67.22%. The census counted 96,795 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (50,879 girls and 45,913 boys).

Attock District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Attock District?

Attock District has 6 tehsils: Attock, Fateh Jang, Hasan Abdal, Hazro, Jand and Pindi Gheb.

What is the population of Attock District?

The 2023 census counted 2,170,423 people in Attock District, up from 1,886,378 in 2017 (annual growth 2.37%). 28.75% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Attock District?

Attock District covers 6,857 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 316.53 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Attock District?

The 2023 census lists 11 urban localities in Attock District; the ten largest are Attock (142,826, municipal committee), Fateh Jang (81,321, municipal committee), Hasan Abdal (69,529, municipal committee), Kamra Cantonment (67,425, cantonment), Pindi Gheb (63,810, municipal committee), Jand (56,254, municipal committee), Hazro (44,242, municipal committee), Mirza (34,378, town committee), Attock Cantonment (33,718, cantonment) and Ghur Ghushti (18,730, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Attock District?

70.22% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 79.69%, female 60.66%).

Which languages are spoken in Attock District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (65.23% of the 2,133,005 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Pashto (15.59%), Hindko (14.41%), Urdu (2.68%).

Which province is Attock District in?

Attock District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Attock.

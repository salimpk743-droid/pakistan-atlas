Administrative status: Dawn, 14 October 2022.

South Waziristan Upper is the northern, Mehsud-majority half of the former South Waziristan tribal district. The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa cabinet approved dividing South Waziristan on 12 April 2022 (Dawn). The formal notification, under the Land Revenue Act 1967, came in October 2022, and it took Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to 36 districts (Dawn). The notification gave Upper two sub-divisions, Sarwakai and Ladha, with Spinkai Raghazai as headquarters, and seven tehsils: Tiarza, Serwakai, Shawal, Ladha, Makin, Shaktui and Sararogha (Dawn).

On 30 September 2024 the provincial assembly passed a resolution to rename the district, saying the Mehsud tribe made up 90% of its population (APP). On 31 January 2025 Chief Minister Ali Amin Gandapur announced the new name Mahsud Waziristan, a third sub-division and tehsil status for Kaniguram (The News). This page keeps the notified name because we have not found a renaming notification.

2023 census rows

PBS counted the whole of South Waziristan as one district, with no urban population (PBS Table 1). The six census tehsils that are now in Upper add up to 488,438 people on 2,815 km². Shawal has no separate row. Sararogha tehsil: 813 km², 145,118 people, literacy 35.07%. Ladha tehsil: 289 km², 108,344 people, literacy 47.95%. Makin tehsil: 404 km², 66,042 people, literacy 48.71%. Tiarza tehsil: 734 km², 65,798 people, literacy 27.31%. Serwekai tehsil: 398 km², 58,804 people, literacy 37.67%. Shaktoi tehsil: 177 km², 44,332 people, literacy 32.93%.

Across these six tehsils, 38.88% of people aged 10 and over were literate (PBS Table 12). For the combined figures and the Lower district, see South Waziristan.

South Waziristan Upper District: FAQ

When was South Waziristan divided?

The KP cabinet approved the division on 12 April 2022; the notification creating South Waziristan Upper and Lower came in October 2022. (Dawn)

What is the headquarters of South Waziristan Upper?

Spinkai Raghazai. (Dawn)

Has South Waziristan Upper been renamed Mahsud Waziristan?

The chief minister announced the name Mahsud Waziristan on 31 January 2025, after an assembly resolution in September 2024. We have not found a renaming notification. (The News)

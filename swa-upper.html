South Waziristan Upper was created in April 2022, when the former South Waziristan district was divided in two, with Spinkai Raghazai as its headquarters.

Population and headquarters in South Waziristan Upper District

The district headquarters is Spinkai Raghazai. The available public data note is About 367,000 (2017 census). Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Culture and geography of South Waziristan Upper

Rebuilding markets, schools and roads after years of displacement remains an ongoing task.

South Waziristan Upper District: FAQ

When was South Waziristan divided?

The KP cabinet approved the division on 12 April 2022; the notification creating South Waziristan Upper and Lower came in October 2022. (Dawn)

What is the headquarters of South Waziristan Upper?

Spinkai Raghazai. (Dawn)

Has South Waziristan Upper been renamed Mahsud Waziristan?

The chief minister announced the name Mahsud Waziristan on 31 January 2025, after an assembly resolution in September 2024. We have not found a renaming notification. (The News)

Musakhel District

headquarters Musakhel · 6 census units · 182,275 people (2023 census)

Musakhel District: Quick Answers

Musakhel District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Loralai Division, with its headquarters at Musakhel. The 2023 census lists 6 administrative units in it: Drug, Kingri, Musakhel, Tiyar Essot, Toisar and Zimri Plaseen. The 2023 census counted 182,275 people on 5,728 km², and 36.6% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Musakhel District

The 2023 census counted 6 administrative units in Musakhel District: Drug (tehsil), Kingri (tehsil), Musakhel (tehsil), Tiyar Essot (sub-tehsil), Toisar (tehsil) and Zimri Plaseen (sub-tehsil). Toisar is the most populous, with 56,818 people, and Kingri is the largest by area (3,683 km²). Literacy ranges from 15.02% in Zimri Plaseen to 65.37% in Drug.

Population and growth

Musakhel District had 182,275 people in the 2023 census, up from 167,243 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.45%). It covers 5,728 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 32nd by population and 18th by area. 8.67% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 113.02 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Musakhel (15,805). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (85.83%), followed by Balochi (12%).

Literacy and schooling

36.6% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 20th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 47.5% and female literacy 24.72%; the urban rate was 60.61% and the rural rate 34.35%. The census counted 35,220 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (17,710 girls and 17,509 boys).

Musakhel District: FAQ

What is the population of Musakhel District?

The 2023 census counted 182,275 people in Musakhel District, up from 167,243 in 2017 (annual growth 1.45%). 8.67% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Musakhel District?

Musakhel District covers 5,728 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 31.82 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Musakhel District?

What is the literacy rate of Musakhel District?

Which languages are spoken in Musakhel District?

Which province is Musakhel District in?

headquarters Mithi · 7 talukas · 1,778,407 people (2023 census)

Tharparkar District: Quick Answers

Tharparkar District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Mithi. It has 7 talukas: Chachro, Dahli, Diplo, Islamkot, Kaloi, Mithi and Nagar Parkar. The 2023 census counted 1,778,407 people on 19,637 km², and 36.39% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Tharparkar District

The 2023 census counted 7 talukas in Tharparkar District: Chachro, Dahli, Diplo, Islamkot, Kaloi, Mithi and Nagar Parkar. Chachro is the most populous, with 371,769 people, and Nagar Parkar is the largest by area (3,862 km²). Literacy ranges from 29.49% in Nagar Parkar to 49.84% in Mithi.

Population and growth

Tharparkar District had 1,778,407 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,647,036 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.29%). It covers 19,637 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 14th by population and 1st by area. 8.12% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 109.21 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 7 urban localities; the largest are Mithi (52,376), Chachro (25,309), Islamkot (19,064) and Diplo (13,257). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (99.36%).

Literacy and schooling

36.39% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 26th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 48.5% and female literacy 23.49%; the urban rate was 63% and the rural rate 33.73%. The census counted 372,976 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (203,718 girls and 169,251 boys).

Tharparkar District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Tharparkar District?

Tharparkar District has 7 talukas: Chachro, Dahli, Diplo, Islamkot, Kaloi, Mithi and Nagar Parkar.

What is the population of Tharparkar District?

The 2023 census counted 1,778,407 people in Tharparkar District, up from 1,647,036 in 2017 (annual growth 1.29%). 8.12% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Tharparkar District?

Tharparkar District covers 19,637 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 90.56 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Tharparkar District?

The 2023 census lists 7 urban localities in Tharparkar District: Mithi (52,376, municipal committee), Chachro (25,309, town committee), Islamkot (19,064, town committee), Diplo (13,257, town committee), Chelhar (12,489, town committee), Nagar Parkar (12,239, town committee) and Kheme-Jo-Par (9,671, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Tharparkar District?

36.39% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 48.5%, female 23.49%).

Which languages are spoken in Tharparkar District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (99.36% of the 1,778,123 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Tharparkar District in?

Tharparkar District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Mithi.

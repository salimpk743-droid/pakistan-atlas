Explainers for students — what a public event means for provinces and districts, not a shouting match.

What belongs on this page

Floods, census notes, education policy, energy and climate, and how a federal decision lands in Turbat or Hunza. Each item should answer: what happened, which official body said it, which districts feel it first.

Starter explainer — why districts keep splitting

Pakistan’s district map is not frozen. Balochistan, Sindh and parts of Punjab have notified new districts so that a headquarters is closer to citizens. For this website that means a list can be “complete for this month” and still need a footnote next year. When you add a new district, copy the gazette date and leave the old parent district page with a line that says “X tehsil is now its own district”.

Starter explainer — 18th Amendment in one paragraph

After 2010, education and health became provincial subjects. That is why a DHQ hospital in Khuzdar and a DHQ hospital in Gujrat are funded through different cabinets. Current-affairs pieces should name the province that actually runs the school or ward.

Current affairs

The map changed. The census is catching up in pieces.

The 25th Amendment in 2018 ended FATA as a separate federal belt and put the former agencies inside Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Bajaur, Mohmand, Khyber, Orakzai, Kurram and the two Waziristans are now districts with deputy commissioners. Schools and police lines followed on paper. Female literacy rows on those district pages show how uneven the follow-up has been.

Kohistan was cut into Lower, Upper and then Kolai-Palas. Dir was cut into Lower and Upper in 1996; Central Dir with a seat at Wari was notified in 2023. South Waziristan was cut in April 2022. Torghar left Mansehra in 2011. A student who uses a 2010 map will miss half the offices.

Islamabad is not a province. It is a capital territory with its own 2023 count. Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Kashmir are listed on this atlas as separate units because that is how readers look for them. They are not counted inside the 241.5 million figure PBS prints for the four provinces plus ICT.

Source: 25th Amendment; provincial district notifications; PBS 2023 national report. 2 September 2026.

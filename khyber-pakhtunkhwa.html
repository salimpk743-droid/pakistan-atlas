Khyber Pakhtunkhwa خیبر پختونخوا

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is a province of Pakistan with its capital at Peshawar. The 2023 census counted 40,856,097 people (16.92% of Pakistan) on 101,741 km², and 51.09% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

About Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, formerly the North-West Frontier Province, has Peshawar as its capital and is divided by the Kabul River, which runs east to west (Britannica). The Federally Administered Tribal Areas were merged into the province when the 25th Constitutional Amendment was signed on 31 May 2018 (Dawn).

Culture and Geography of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Pashtunwali hospitality, Attan dance, rabab music and historic passes of Khyber and Malakand. Former FATA agencies are now districts of KP.

Education and Health in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

University of Peshawar, UET Peshawar, AWKUM and Gomal University.

Lady Reading Hospital Peshawar is a major referral centre; remote districts rely on DHQ hospitals.

Public Issues in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Mountain access, climate shocks, and integrating newly merged districts into the provincial service system.

Notable Places in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Peshawar, Abbottabad, Swat, Mardan, Dera Ismail Khan

Largest districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Among the 32 current districts with their own 2023 census row, the most populous are Peshawar District (4,758,762), Mardan District (2,744,898) and Swabi District (1,894,600). The largest by area are Upper Chitral District (8,392 km²), Lower Chitral District (6,458 km²) and Upper Kohistan District (5,440 km²). Literacy ranges from 18.8% in Kolai-Palas District to 77.34% in Abbottabad District. Figures are from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Tables 1 and 12; districts split after the census are left out of this comparison.

How many districts does Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have?

38 districts in 7 divisions, as of September 2026. 35 districts in the PBS 2023 census tables (South Waziristan counted as one), plus the 2022 split of South Waziristan into Upper and Lower South Waziristan, Paharpur (notified 26 November 2025) and Bar Swat (notified 27 January 2026).

Press totals disagree (The News: 37 after Bar Swat; MM News: 40). Allai is not counted: a January 2023 notification was never implemented, and KP chief ministers announced district status again in May 2025, February 2026 and August 2026 without a new notification.

Sources: APP, 2025-11-26; The News, 2026-01-28; Dawn, 2026-01-28; APP, 2025-05-23; Dawn, 2026-02-25; The Public Purview, 2026-08-29.

announced, not yet notified

Allai District

Allai is a tehsil of Battagram District. A January 2023 notification giving it district status was not implemented; KP chief ministers announced district status again in May 2025 and February 2026, and in August 2026 it was still described as a tehsil to be upgraded. MyBook.Pk does not count it as a district until a notification is issued.

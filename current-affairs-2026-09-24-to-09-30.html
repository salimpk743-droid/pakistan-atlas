Pakistan Current Affairs: 24–30 September 2026

Weekly current affairs Pakistan: the main politics, economy, provincial, foreign affairs, security and sports news of the week, each checked against two outlets, with key facts and MCQs for PPSC, FPSC and CSS.

By mybook.pk Editorial · Published 30 September 2026 · Last updated 30 September 2026 · Part of Pakistan current affairs 2026

The week’s biggest Pakistan news: the IMF opened its fourth review of the $7 billion loan programme, the prime minister opened a London Stock Exchange session, Dr Muhammad Najeeb Naqi became president of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, and PTI moved its Islamabad march to 4 October amid talk of governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. A suicide attack in Darazinda killed 14 people, petrol fell to Rs387.54 a litre and Abrar Ahmed became the world’s top-ranked T20I bowler.

Pakistan News This Week: Top Headlines

  • IMF staff mission starts the fourth review of Pakistan’s $7bn EFF; about $1.2bn could follow if it is completed.
  • PM Shehbaz Sharif becomes the first Pakistani prime minister to open trading at the London Stock Exchange.
  • PML-N’s Dr Muhammad Najeeb Naqi is elected president of Azad Jammu and Kashmir with 32 votes.
  • PTI moves its Islamabad march to 4 October while federal ministers talk of governor’s rule in KP.
  • Suicide attack on a police and customs checkpoint in Darazinda, DI Khan, kills 14 people.
  • Petrol price in Pakistan cut to Rs387.54 per litre for 30 September; Abrar Ahmed is No. 1 in ICC T20I bowling.

Each summary below is written by our editors from at least two reliable outlets; the links after every item go to the original reports. Dates are the dates of the event or announcement (Pakistan time).

National Politics & Government: Pakistan Politics News

PTI moves Islamabad march to 4 October

25–29 Sep: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi said on 25 September that PTI’s political committee had pushed the party’s march on Islamabad back from 27 September to 4 October, with Peshawar as the starting point. On 29 September he said the march would still go ahead on 4 October in two stages: first to Swabi, then on to Islamabad. Sources: Dawn, 26 Sep 2026; Express Tribune, 26 Sep 2026; Dawn, 30 Sep 2026.

Governor’s rule in KP discussed by federal ministers

28 Sep: Federal minister Amir Muqam said on 28 September that, in his view, the conditions for imposing governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa had been met and that consultations were taking place. KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi also issued a warning to the provincial government. No such order had been issued by the end of the week. Sources: Geo News, 28 Sep 2026; Dawn, 29 Sep 2026.

Over 5.8 million register for PM’s Fuel Relief Scheme

25 Sep: IT Minister Shaza Fatima Khawaja said more than 5.8 million people had signed up for the Prime Minister’s Fuel Relief Scheme by 25 September. The scheme is meant for owners of motorcycles, rickshaws, Qingqis and cars of up to 800cc. Sources: Radio Pakistan, 25 Sep 2026; Dawn, 26 Sep 2026.

Parliament & Laws

IMF seeks 174 legal amendments

24 Sep: Finance Secretary Imdadullah Bosal told the National Assembly Standing Committee on Finance, chaired by Syed Naveed Qamar, on 24 September that the IMF wants 174 changes to Pakistan’s laws. He said the amendments would be placed before parliament for approval. Sources: Dawn, 25 Sep 2026; The News, 25 Sep 2026.

IHC strikes down part of Passport Rules on the control list

27 Sep: The Islamabad High Court held that the second part of Rule 22(2)(b) of the Passport Rules 2021 is unconstitutional. That clause had allowed agencies to have a person’s name put on the Passport Control List without a decision by a competent authority or proper safeguards. The judgment was written by Justice Raja Inaam Ameen Minhas. Sources: Dawn, 27 Sep 2026; Express Tribune, 27 Sep 2026.

Economy: IMF, State Bank and Petrol Price in Pakistan

IMF review talks begin in Islamabad

28–29 Sep: An IMF staff mission headed by Iva Petrova began formal talks on 28 September on the fourth review of Pakistan’s $7 billion Extended Fund Facility (EFF) and the third review of the Resilience and Sustainability Facility (RSF). Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb led the opening session on 29 September. A successful review could release about $1.2 billion ($1bn under the EFF and $200m under the RSF); the mission is due to stay until the first week of October. Sources: Dawn, 28 Sep 2026; The News, 29 Sep 2026.

Petrol price in Pakistan cut twice

29–30 Sep: For 29 September the government lowered petrol by Rs2.27 to Rs389.03 a litre and high-speed diesel by Rs3.56 to Rs404.97. For 30 September petrol fell a further Rs1.49 to Rs387.54 and diesel Rs2.73 to Rs402.24. Prices are now set daily by the Petroleum Division using a rolling seven-working-day average calculated by OGRA. Sources: Radio Pakistan, 29 Sep 2026; Dawn, 30 Sep 2026; Express Tribune, 29 Sep 2026.

SBP reserves at a record $21.4 billion

24 Sep: The State Bank said on 24 September that its own foreign reserves rose by $11 million to a record $21.4 billion in the week ending 18 September. The country’s total liquid reserves stood at $26.81 billion, of which commercial banks held $5.41 billion. Sources: Dawn, 25 Sep 2026; APP, 24 Sep 2026.

Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme launched

25 Sep: SBP Governor Jameel Ahmad launched the bank-funded Pasban Remittance Reward Scheme on 25 September, with Rs16 billion in prizes a year (Rs4 billion each quarter for 2,521 winners, top prize Rs100 million). People who receive at least $100 in each of three months through formal channels qualify; cash-over-counter and Roshan Digital Account remittances are excluded. The first draw is set for 15 January 2027. Sources: Dawn, 26 Sep 2026; APP, 25 Sep 2026.

Weekly inflation (SPI) at 11.92%

25 Sep: The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics reported that the Sensitive Price Indicator for the week ended 24 September was 11.92% higher than a year earlier and 0.99% higher than the previous week. Sources: PBS, 25 Sep 2026; Dawn, 26 Sep 2026.

Public debt reaches Rs86.7 trillion

29 Sep: The Annual Borrowing Plan for FY27, released on 29 September, put total public debt at Rs86.7 trillion at the end of June 2026 (Rs59.4tr domestic and Rs27.3tr external), about 76% more than the Rs49.3tr of June 2022. It puts this year’s gross financing needs at Rs28.647tr and the fiscal deficit at Rs7.02tr. Sources: Dawn, 30 Sep 2026; Business Recorder, 30 Sep 2026.

PM opens London Stock Exchange session

29 Sep: On 29 September Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif became the first Pakistani prime minister to open a trading session at the London Stock Exchange. He also marked the listing of Pakistan’s $3 billion two-tranche Eurobond. Sources: Geo News, 29 Sep 2026; APP, 29 Sep 2026; Dawn, 30 Sep 2026.

Plan to open government bonds to retail investors

29 Sep: The Finance Ministry announced a strategic action plan, linked to the IMF programme, to develop the local-currency bond market and make government securities easier for ordinary savers to buy through the stock exchange and banks. Sources: Dawn, 30 Sep 2026; Business Recorder, 30 Sep 2026.

Punjab

PML-N wins NA-168 Bahawalpur by-election

27–28 Sep: In the 27 September by-election for NA-168 Bahawalpur-V, the provisional Form-47 result gave PML-N’s Malik Munir Iqbal Channar 87,789 votes against 39,888 for independent candidate Wali Dad Cheema, on a turnout of 29.46%. The seat had fallen vacant after the death of MNA Malik Muhammad Iqbal Channar in August; PTI stayed out of the contest. Sources: ARY News, 28 Sep 2026; Dawn, 28 Sep 2026.

Sindh

Summary sent for 500 electric buses

23 Sep: Senior Minister Sharjeel Inam Memon said the Sindh Transport Department had sent the provincial cabinet a summary for buying 500 electric buses. He added that 50 double-decker buses are expected by 30 November. Sources: APP, 23 Sep 2026; Dawn, 24 Sep 2026.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Fifth polio case of 2026 reported from Bannu

29 Sep: A 17-month-old girl from union council Khandar Khankhel in Bannu became Pakistan’s fifth confirmed polio case of 2026. Four of this year’s five cases are from Bannu division and one from Sindh. Pakistan recorded 31 cases in 2025 and 74 in 2024. Sources: Geo News, 29 Sep 2026; Dawn, 30 Sep 2026.

Balochistan

Section 144 imposed across Balochistan for 30 days

23 Sep: The Balochistan government imposed Section 144 across the province for 30 days from 23 September. It bars gatherings of five or more people, the display of weapons and covering the face in public. Sources: Dawn, 25 Sep 2026; Express Tribune, 24 Sep 2026.

Jinnah Peoples Train launched in Quetta

29 Sep: Balochistan’s first mass-transit service, the Jinnah Peoples Train, runs 32 km between Kuchlak and Sariab with seven stations. It was built for about Rs3 billion in 120 days and was launched by Railways Minister Hanif Abbasi and Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti. Sources: Dawn, 26 Sep 2026; The Nation, 29 Sep 2026.

Gilgit-Baltistan & Azad Kashmir

Dr Najeeb Naqi elected AJK president

24 Sep: PML-N’s Dr Muhammad Najeeb Naqi was elected President of Azad Jammu and Kashmir on 24 September, winning 32 votes to 13 for PPP’s Chaudhry Latif Akbar. AJK Chief Justice Raja Saeed Akram Khan administered the oath the same day. Sources: Radio Pakistan, 24 Sep 2026; The News, 25 Sep 2026; Dawn, 25 Sep 2026.

Wider consultation on GB constitutional status

24 Sep: A prime minister’s subcommittee on Gilgit-Baltistan, chaired by Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar, decided on 24 September to set up a larger committee to widen consultations on a constitutional amendment giving GB provisional-province status and representation in parliament. GB Chief Minister Amjad Hussain and opposition leader Hafiz Hafeezur Rehman attended. Sources: Dawn, 25 Sep 2026; APP, 24 Sep 2026.

Foreign Affairs

PM addresses the 81st UN General Assembly

25 Sep: In his UNGA speech on 25 September, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif called for the Strait of Hormuz and Bab al-Mandab to be kept open, pointed to Pakistan’s mediation between the United States and Iran, and cautioned India against diverting water meant for Pakistan. Sources: Dawn, 26 Sep 2026; The News, 25 Sep 2026.

SCO national coordinators meet in Islamabad

28–30 Sep: Islamabad hosted the first meeting of the SCO Council of National Coordinators held under Pakistan’s 2026–27 chairmanship of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation, from 28 to 30 September. Sources: Radio Pakistan, 28 Sep 2026; Dawn, 28 Sep 2026.

Security

Drones downed, strikes inside Afghanistan

23–24 Sep: Security forces shot down eight drones near Torkham and Kohat on 23 September. The next day Information Minister Attaullah Tarar announced precision strikes on 10 locations inside Afghanistan. Sources: Dawn, 24 Sep 2026; Radio Pakistan, 24 Sep 2026; Dawn, 25 Sep 2026.

Darazinda checkpoint suicide attack kills 14

26 Sep: A suicide bomber driving an explosives-laden tractor-trolley struck the Aman Mela joint police and customs checkpoint in Darazinda, Dera Ismail Khan, on 26 September, and an ambulance that came to help was attacked afterwards. Fourteen people, including rescue workers, were killed and 35 injured; the CTD registered a case. Sources: Dawn, 27 Sep 2026; Dawn, 29 Sep 2026; Pakistan Today, 27 Sep 2026.

33 militants killed in Balochistan operations

25 Sep: ISPR said security forces killed 33 militants in Balochistan between 21 and 25 September: 22 in Operation Shaban and 11 in Nushki. Sources: Radio Pakistan, 25 Sep 2026; Dawn, 26 Sep 2026.

Three traffic police killed in Tank

28 Sep: Gunmen attacked a traffic police van in Wazirabad Bazaar, Tank, on 28 September, killing three policemen. Sources: Dawn, 29 Sep 2026; The News, 28 Sep 2026.

Sports

Abrar Ahmed tops ICC T20I bowling rankings

24 Sep: Leg-spinner Abrar Ahmed moved to No. 1 in the ICC Men’s T20I bowling rankings with 736 rating points, passing Rashid Khan. He is the fifth Pakistani to hold the top spot, after Umar Gul, Saeed Ajmal, Imad Wasim and Shahid Afridi. Sources: Dawn, 24 Sep 2026; APP, 24 Sep 2026.

Asian Games: five bronze medals so far

28–29 Sep: By 29 September Pakistan had won five bronze medals at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games: the women’s cricket team, Asim Khan (MMA), squash players Noor Zaman and Ashab Irfan, and the PUBG Mobile esports team. Javelin thrower Arshad Nadeem finished fourth with 80.29m on 28 September. The hockey team meets India and the men’s cricket team meets Bangladesh in semi-finals on 1 October. Sources: Dawn, 30 Sep 2026; Geo News, 29 Sep 2026; Dawn, 29 Sep 2026.

First point at FIFA ASEAN Cup

29 Sep: After a 4–0 defeat to Thailand on their debut in the FIFA ASEAN Cup, Pakistan came back to draw 2–2 with the Philippines in Bandung on 29 September, with goals from Ali Akbar Khan and Abdul Samad, for their first point. Sources: Dawn, 30 Sep 2026; Geo News, 29 Sep 2026.

Weather, Water & Disasters

About 25% water shortage expected for Rabi

28 Sep: Water managers expect a shortage of about 25% for the Rabi season, as reservoir carryover storage is below 9.5 million acre-feet; IRSA is to finalise provincial shares. Sources: Dawn, 28 Sep 2026; ProPakistani, 28 Sep 2026.

Unfrozen glacial lakes rise to 105

28 Sep: Suparco reported that the number of unfrozen glacial lakes in Pakistan’s north went up from 40 in July to 105 in August, increasing the risk of glacial lake outburst floods (GLOFs). Sources: Dawn, 28 Sep 2026; Express Tribune, 28 Sep 2026.

Education & Exams

KPPSC starts computer-based tests

24 Sep: The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Public Service Commission began computer-based testing with ETEA, holding its first tests on 24 September at the KPPSC IT Lab in Peshawar. Sources: Dawn, 26 Sep 2026; KP DGIPR, 25 Sep 2026.

Punjab extends 45,000 School Teacher Interns

25 Sep: The Punjab School Education Department extended about 45,000 School Teacher Interns until 31 May 2027 through fresh agreements effective from 24 August 2026, according to a notification issued on 25 September. Sources: Dawn, 26 Sep 2026; ProPakistani, 25 Sep 2026.

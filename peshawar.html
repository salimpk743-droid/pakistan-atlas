Peshawar District

Capital of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa · headquarters Peshawar · 7 census units · 4,758,762 people (2023 census)

Peshawar District: Quick Answers

Peshawar District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, in Peshawar Division, with its headquarters at Peshawar. The 2023 census lists 7 administrative units in it: Badhber, Cham Kani, Hassan Khel, Mathra, Peshawar, Pishta Khara and Shah Alam. The 2023 census counted 4,758,762 people on 1,518 km², and 53.28% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Peshawar city: history and landmarks

Peshawar lies just west of the Bara River, a tributary of the Kabul, near the Khyber Pass. It was once the capital of the Buddhist kingdom of Gandhara and was known as Purusapura; the present name, meaning "frontier town", is ascribed to the Mughal emperor Akbar. The Shahji-ki Dheri mounds to the east cover the ruins of the largest Buddhist stupa in the subcontinent (2nd century CE). Historic buildings include the Bala Hissar fort, Gor Khatri, the Mahabat Khan Mosque (1630) and the old Qissa Khwani Bazaar, the "street of storytellers" (Britannica).

Administrative units of Peshawar District

The 2023 census counted 7 administrative units in Peshawar District: Badhber (tehsil), Cham Kani (tehsil), Hassan Khel (sub-division), Mathra (tehsil), Peshawar (tehsil), Pishta Khara (tehsil) and Shah Alam (tehsil). Peshawar is the most populous, with 2,113,596 people, and Badhber is the largest by area (357 km²). Literacy ranges from 38.66% in Badhber to 60.88% in Peshawar.

Population and growth

Peshawar District had 4,758,762 people in the 2023 census, up from 4,331,959 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.58%). It covers 1,518 km². Of the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the census, it ranks 1st by population and 27th by area. 40.05% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.99 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 3 urban localities: Peshawar (1,837,741), Peshawar Cantonment (63,978) and Peshawar University (4,256). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (93.07%), followed by Hindko (3.79%) and Urdu (1.8%).

Literacy and schooling

53.28% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 14th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 64.91% and female literacy 41.09%; the urban rate was 61.82% and the rural rate 47.2%. The census counted 519,928 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (319,767 girls and 200,142 boys).

What is the population of Peshawar District?

What is the area of Peshawar District?

Peshawar District covers 1,518 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 3,134.89 people per km².

The 2023 census lists 3 urban localities in Peshawar District: Peshawar (1,837,741, municipal corporation), Peshawar Cantonment (63,978, cantonment) and Peshawar University (4,256, town committee).

Bar Swat (Upper Swat)

Headquarters Matta · notified after the 19 December 2025 cabinet decision · three tehsils from old Swat

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District: Quick Answers

Population
1,130,354 (2023 census)
Area
3,975 km² · 284.37 people per km²
Tehsils
3 – Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela
Headquarters
Matta
Province / territory
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
Known for
Bar Swat (Upper Swat) was notified as a separate district in January 2026, following a provincial cabinet decision of 19 December 2025. Its headquarters is Matta and it has three tehsils: Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela.
Culture
The district covers the upper Swat valley including Kalam, Bahrain and Matiltan, long known as one of Pakistan's most scenic tourist areas.
Education
Most higher education still runs through University of Swat's main campus in the remaining Swat district.
Healthcare
New district health facilities are being organised in Matta; patients also use hospitals in Saidu Sharif.
Villages / local areas
Kalam, Bahrain and Matiltan.

The 2023 census counted Bar Swat (Upper Swat) and Swat as one district (Swat). Since the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government notification of 27 January 2026, Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District consists of Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela; the population and area shown are the sum of those units' 2023 census rows. Current tehsils: Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government notification of 27 January 2026, reported by Dawn on 28 January 2026.

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Census 2023, Table 1. Checked 2026-09-29.

Bar Swat is the official name on the January 2026 revenue notification. Upper Swat is the English name people already use. Headquarters is Matta. The three tehsils taken out of old Swat are Matta, Bahrain (Behrain) and Khwazakhela. Mingora, Saidu Sharif and Gulkada stay in Swat district.

PBS has not published a new district book. The figures below are the 2023 tehsil rows added together. That sum is about 1,130,354 people (Matta 552,431 + Khwazakhela 307,300 + Bahrain 270,623).

Matta — 684 km², 552,431 people. Headquarters town, east-bank bazaar.

Khwazakhela — 392 km², 307,300 people. Mid-valley town on the road toward Bahrain.

Bahrain — 2,899 km², 270,623 people. Largest land, thinnest settlement. Madyan and Kalam sit in this tehsil.

University of Swat and Saidu Group of Teaching Hospitals remain in the other district. Matta is where the new offices are supposed to sit. Tourism in Kalam and Bahrain is the cash and the pressure: crowds, riverbed building, and a thin winter road.

Sources: KP cabinet 19 December 2025; Board of Revenue notification January 2026 (Dawn, 28 January 2026); PBS 2023 tehsil rows. September 2026.

Bar Swat (Upper Swat)

Created January 2026 from Swat District · headquarters Matta · 3 tehsils · 1,130,354 people (2023 census)

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District: Quick Answers

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Matta. It has 3 tehsils: Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela. The 2023 census counted 1,130,354 people on 3,975 km², and 41.78% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

How Bar Swat District was created

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government notified the division of Swat on 27 January 2026. Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela tehsils became Bar Swat District, with its headquarters at Matta, while Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot remained in Swat District (Dawn, 28 January 2026).

The 2023 census was taken before the split, so the figures below are the sums of the census rows for the three tehsils. Because Bar Swat did not exist as a district in the census, it is not ranked against other districts.

Tehsils of Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District is made up of 3 tehsils that the 2023 census counted before the district took its present shape: Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela. Matta is the most populous, with 552,431 people, and Bahrain is the largest by area (2,899 km²). Literacy ranges from 39.26% in Bahrain to 42.63% in Khwazakhela.

Population and growth

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District had 1,130,354 people in the 2023 census. It covers 3,975 km².

Towns

The census lists 3 urban localities: Behrain (76,728), Khawaza Khela (57,113) and Matta (51,821).

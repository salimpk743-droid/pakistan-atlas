Ghizer District, Gilgit-Baltistan

200,069 people (2023 census) · 12,381 km² · literacy 70.28%

Ghizer District: Quick Answers

Ghizer District is one of the 10 districts for which Gilgit-Baltistan's Planning & Development Department publishes statistics. The 2023 census counted 200,069 people on 12,381 km², and 70.28% of residents were literate.

Population and growth

Ghizer District had 200,069 people in the 2023 census, up from 172,696 in 2017, an average annual growth rate of 2.48%. It covers 12,381 km². The census counted 27,862 households, with an average of 7 people each, and 101 males for every 100 females (GB at a Glance 2025).

How Ghizer compares within Gilgit-Baltistan

Among the 10 districts in the GB statistics, Ghizer ranks 4th by population, 1st by area and 6th by literacy (70.28% in the 2023 census). Gilgit-Baltistan as a whole counted 1,709,049 people on 72,496 km² (GB at a Glance 2025).

New districts notified in 2019

In June 2019 the Gilgit-Baltistan government notified four sub-divisions (Gupis-Yasin, Rondu, Darel and Tangir) as separate districts with the same boundaries they had as sub-divisions (Dawn). The GB statistics for 2023 still report 10 districts, so any figures for Gupis-Yasin are included in the numbers above.

Capital of Punjab · headquarters Lahore · 5 tehsils · 13,004,135 people (2023 census)

Lahore District: Quick Answers

Lahore District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, in Lahore Division, with its headquarters at Lahore. It has 5 tehsils: Lahore Cantt, Lahore City, Model Town, Raiwind and Shalimar. The 2023 census counted 13,004,135 people on 1,772 km², and 79.62% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

History of Lahore

Lahore lies on the Ravi River in the upper Indus plain. It was captured by Babur's troops in 1524, which began its golden age under the Mughals, when it was often a royal residence; it was greatly expanded under Shah Jahan (1628–58). It was the seat of Ranjit Singh's government (1799–1839) and passed under British rule in 1849. At independence in 1947 it became the capital of West Punjab; in 1955 it was made the capital of West Pakistan, which was reconstituted as Punjab province in 1970 (Britannica).

Landmarks

The walled old city is entered through 13 gates. Its landmarks include the Wazir Khan Mosque (1634), the Lahore Fort, begun under Akbar, and the Badshahi Mosque, built by Aurangzeb. Shah Jahan laid out the Shalimar Garden east of the city in 1642. The University of the Punjab (1882) is the oldest university in Pakistan, and the Lahore Museum dates from 1864 (Britannica). The Lahore Fort and Shalamar Gardens were inscribed together as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1981 (UNESCO).

Tehsils of Lahore District

The 2023 census counted 5 tehsils in Lahore District: Lahore Cantt, Lahore City, Model Town, Raiwind and Shalimar. Lahore City is the most populous, with 4,123,354 people, and Raiwind is the largest by area (467 km²). Literacy ranges from 72.35% in Raiwind to 81.21% in Shalimar.

Population and growth

Lahore District had 13,004,135 people in the 2023 census, up from 11,119,985 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.65%). It covers 1,772 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 1st by population and 36th by area. 100% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 112.47 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (73.58%), followed by Urdu (21.13%), Pashto (2.06%) and Mewati (2.01%).

Literacy and schooling

79.62% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 4th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 81.41% and female literacy 77.59%. The census counted 718,199 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (327,913 girls and 390,124 boys).

Lahore District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Lahore District?

Lahore District has 5 tehsils: Lahore Cantt, Lahore City, Model Town, Raiwind and Shalimar.

What is the population of Lahore District?

The 2023 census counted 13,004,135 people in Lahore District, up from 11,119,985 in 2017 (annual growth 2.65%). 100% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Lahore District?

Lahore District covers 1,772 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 7,338.68 people per km².

What is the literacy rate of Lahore District?

79.62% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 81.41%, female 77.59%).

Which languages are spoken in Lahore District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (73.58% of the 12,978,661 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (21.13%), Pashto (2.06%), Mewati (2.01%).

Which province is Lahore District in?

Lahore District is in Punjab, in Lahore Division. Its headquarters is Lahore.

Which Lahore sites are UNESCO World Heritage Sites?

The Lahore Fort and the Shalamar (Shalimar) Gardens, inscribed together in 1981. (UNESCO World Heritage Centre)

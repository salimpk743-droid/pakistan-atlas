Sudhanoti District: Quick Answers

Sudhanoti District is one of the 10 districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, in Poonch Division. The 2017 census counted 297,880 people on 569 km², in 4 tehsils: Sudhnoti, Mong, Tararkhal and Baloch.

Tehsils of Sudhanoti District

The 2017 census tables published by the AJ&K Bureau of Statistics (which spell the district Sudhnoti) list 4 tehsils in Sudhanoti District: Sudhnoti, Mong, Tararkhal and Baloch (Statistical Year Book 2023, Table 15.15). Sudhnoti is the most populous, with 124,179 people, and it is also the largest by area (256 km²). AJK at a Glance 2024 counts 32 tehsils across Azad Kashmir in 2023, against 30 in the 2017 census tables, so tehsil boundaries may since have changed (AJK at a Glance 2024).

Population and area

Sudhanoti District had 297,880 people in the 2017 census, with average annual growth of 1.51% since 1998, 7.81% of them in urban areas and an average household of 6.41 people (Table 15.6). It covers 569 km² (Table 15.1). Of the 10 AJK districts it ranks 7th by population and 10th by area. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects about 0.326 million people for 2023 (AJK at a Glance 2024); these are projections, not census counts.

Sudhanoti District: FAQ

What is the population of Sudhanoti District?

The 2017 census counted 297,880 people. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects about 0.326 million for 2023. (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

How many tehsils are in Sudhanoti District?

The 2017 census tables list 4: Sudhnoti, Mong, Tararkhal and Baloch. (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

Which division is Sudhanoti District in?

Poonch Division. AJK has three divisions (Muzaffarabad, Poonch and Mirpur) and 10 districts. (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

Sajal Aly: The Complete Dramas List

سجل علی

More than forty television serials since 2011. She is the actress Pakistani directors go to for characters with an inner life, from Yakeen Ka Safar to Zanjeerain.

Sajal Aly biography

Sajal Aly has worked steadily since 2011, and her filmography is long: our verified table below has 38 television serials. Very little of it is filler. Even early soaps such as Mehmoodabad Ki Malkain led to Nanhi (2013), the thriller that brought her first Lux Style Awards nomination.

Geo News marked her 26th birthday in January 2020, which matches the widely reported birth date of 17 January 1994. Images Dawn gives Lahore as her hometown and notes that her sister, Saboor Aly, is also an actress.

The mid-2010s made her a lead: Chup Raho, Gul-e-Rana (her first HUM Award) and then Yakeen Ka Safar (2017), which Images Dawn and The Express Tribune both rank among her best work. She also acted outside Pakistan, playing Sridevi's daughter in the Hindi film Mom (2017), and won the Lux Style Award for film actress for Khel Khel Mein (2021).

Sajal Aly's biggest hits

Yakeen Ka Safar (HUM TV, 2017)

As Dr Zubiya, a medical student rebuilding her life after a trauma, opposite Ahad Raza Mir, Sajal won both the popular and jury best-actress awards at the 6th HUM Awards (Daily Times). Episode 1 on HUM TV's channel has 27.8 million views (as of 2 October 2026).

Kuch Ankahi (ARY Digital, 2023)

A warm ensemble comedy-drama with Bilal Abbas Khan. She plays Aaliya Agha. The official episode 1 upload (7 January 2023) has 22.8 million views.

Zard Patton Ka Bunn (HUM TV, 2024)

Written by Mustafa Afridi, this was the jury favourite of the 2025 awards season. The Express Tribune's report on the 10th HUM Awards (October 2025) names Sajal Aly Best Actress, and our drama awards page has the full results. Episode 1 has 19.3 million views on HUM TV's channel.

Zanjeerain (HUM TV, 2026)

Her 2026 serial casts her as Rabiya, alongside Ameer Gilani and Danyal Zafar. Episode 1 (29 April 2026) has 32.7 million views on HUM TV's channel. See our Zanjeerain guide.

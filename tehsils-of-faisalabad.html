Tehsils of Faisalabad District

Faisalabad District has 6 tehsils: Chak Jhumra, Faisalabad City, Faisalabad Sadar, Jaranwala, Sammundri and Tandlian Wala. Faisalabad City is the most populous, with 3,691,999 people in the 2023 census, and Jaranwala is the largest by area (1,811 km²).

How the tehsils compare

Faisalabad City tehsil has the most people (3,691,999) and Chak Jhumra the fewest (385,169). Jaranwala covers the largest area (1,811 km²). Faisalabad City is the most urban (100% of residents in urban areas). Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 52.83% in Tandlian Wala to 81.59% in Faisalabad City.

Tehsils of Faisalabad District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Faisalabad District?

6: Chak Jhumra, Faisalabad City, Faisalabad Sadar, Jaranwala, Sammundri and Tandlian Wala. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which is the largest tehsil of Faisalabad District by population?

Faisalabad City, with 3,691,999 people in the 2023 census. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which is the largest tehsil of Faisalabad District by area?

Jaranwala, at 1,811 km². (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Which tehsil of Faisalabad District has the highest literacy rate?

Faisalabad City, where 81.59% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Tehsils of Faisalabad District

Faisalabad Division · Punjab · 2023 census figures

Faisalabad District administrative structure

Faisalabad District has 6 tehsils: Chak Jhumra, Faisalabad City, Faisalabad Sadar, Jaranwala, Sammundri and Tandlian Wala. The district is part of Faisalabad Division.

Chak Jhumra Tehsil

Population (2023): 385,169

Area: 654 km² · 588.94 people per km²

Urban share: 15.61%

Faisalabad City Tehsil

Population (2023): 3,691,999

Area: 168 km² · 21,976.18 people per km²

Urban share: 100%

Faisalabad Sadar Tehsil

Population (2023): 1,742,958

Area: 1,186 km² · 1,469.61 people per km²

Urban share: 5.56%

Jaranwala Tehsil

Population (2023): 1,731,148

Area: 1,811 km² · 955.91 people per km²

Urban share: 15.46%

Sammundri Tehsil

Population (2023): 729,672

Area: 754 km² · 967.73 people per km²

Urban share: 25.54%

Tandlian Wala Tehsil

Population (2023): 794,873

Area: 1,284 km² · 619.06 people per km²

Urban share: 11.31%

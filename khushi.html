Khushi Drama: Cast Name, Story and Timing

Khushi (خوشی) is a daily family drama on HUM TV, written by Hina Huma Nafees and directed by Haseeb Ali, starring Sumaiyya Bukhsh, Mariam Ejaz. It premiered on 13 September 2026.

Khushi Story

Khushi is a daily HUM TV serial that the channel describes as a story of love, relationships and the choices that shape destiny. As life unfolds, each decision brings new challenges and unexpected turns that test love and family bonds.

HUM TV has not published a detailed synopsis or the characters' names in its official material, so this page does not guess them; the cast below is the channel's own list.

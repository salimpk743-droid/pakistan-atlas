Nankana Sahib District: Quick Answers

Nankana Sahib District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Nankana Sahib. It has 3 tehsils: Nankana Sahib, Sangla Hill and Shah Kot. The 2023 census counted 1,634,871 people on 2,216 km², and 63.12% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Birthplace of Guru Nanak

Guru Nanak, the founder and first Guru of Sikhism, was born in 1469 at Rai Bhoi di Talvandi, the town now called Nankana Sahib (Britannica).

Tehsils of Nankana Sahib District

The 2023 census counted 3 tehsils in Nankana Sahib District: Nankana Sahib, Sangla Hill and Shah Kot. Nankana Sahib is the most populous, with 1,065,063 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,662 km²). Literacy ranges from 59.02% in Nankana Sahib to 72.08% in Sangla Hill.

Population and growth

Nankana Sahib District had 1,634,871 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,354,986 in 2017 (average annual growth 3.19%). It covers 2,216 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 32nd by population and 35th by area. 19.78% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.62 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 4 urban localities: Sangla Hill (103,709), Nankana (94,988), Shahkot (89,638) and Warburton (35,053). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (97.24%), followed by Urdu (1.88%).

Literacy and schooling

63.12% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 17th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 68.38% and female literacy 57.63%; the urban rate was 75.86% and the rural rate 59.86%. The census counted 108,439 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (55,036 girls and 53,401 boys).

Nankana Sahib District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Nankana Sahib District?

Nankana Sahib District has 3 tehsils: Nankana Sahib, Sangla Hill and Shah Kot.

What is the population of Nankana Sahib District?

The 2023 census counted 1,634,871 people in Nankana Sahib District, up from 1,354,986 in 2017 (annual growth 3.19%). 19.78% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Nankana Sahib District?

Nankana Sahib District covers 2,216 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 737.76 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Nankana Sahib District?

The 2023 census lists 4 urban localities in Nankana Sahib District: Sangla Hill (103,709, municipal committee), Nankana (94,988, municipal committee), Shahkot (89,638, municipal committee) and Warburton (35,053, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Nankana Sahib District?

63.12% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 68.38%, female 57.63%).

Which languages are spoken in Nankana Sahib District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (97.24% of the 1,634,871 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (1.88%).

Which province is Nankana Sahib District in?

Nankana Sahib District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Nankana Sahib.

What was the old name of Nankana Sahib?

Sindh سندھ

Sindh is a province of Pakistan with its capital at Karachi. The 2023 census counted 55,696,147 people (23.06% of Pakistan) on 140,914 km², and 57.54% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

About Sindh

Sindh is essentially part of the Indus River delta, from which it takes its name. The province was established in 1970, and its capital is Karachi (Britannica).

Largest districts of Sindh

Among the 30 current districts with their own 2023 census row, the most populous are Karachi East District (3,921,742), Karachi Central District (3,822,325) and Korangi District (3,128,971). The largest by area are Tharparkar District (19,637 km²), Khairpur District (15,910 km²) and Jamshoro District (11,204 km²). Literacy ranges from 26.88% in Thatta District to 83.55% in Karachi Central District. Figures are from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Tables 1 and 12; districts split after the census are left out of this comparison.

Sindh: FAQ

What is the capital of Sindh?

Karachi. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

What is the population of Sindh?

The 2023 census counted 55,696,147 people, up from 47,854,510 in 2017 (annual growth 2.57%). (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

What is the area of Sindh?

140,914 km², with 395.25 people per km² in 2023. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

What is the literacy rate of Sindh?

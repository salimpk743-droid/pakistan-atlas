Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is Toba Tek Singh. The available public data note is Census 2023 — see PBS. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Education

Government colleges in TTS, Gojra and Kamalia.

Hospitals and healthcare

DHQ Toba Tek Singh.

Culture and geography

Punjabi Christian and Muslim towns together in Gojra and Kamalia.

Public issues

College seats versus student numbers.

Villages and local places

Gojra, Kamalia villages

Sports

A ground has a district. This page keeps that link.

Jansher Khan is the Peshawar squash name the world already uses. Anwar Ali and Ihsanullah are the Swat cricket names on current lists. Shandur polo sits on the Chitral side of the Lowari road. Sialkot’s workshops, when this atlas reaches Punjab in the same depth, will belong on a district page as well as on a sports page.

School grounds matter more than a talk-show argument. If a district page lists colleges and no playable ground, that is a sports fact. Girls’ teams need the same ground hours as boys’ teams. DHQ towns that have a stadium and empty school yards have the balance wrong.

Match scores change every week. District homes do not. This page will name the venue and the district when a national event is recorded here.

North Waziristan District

Bannu Division · former FATA agency · headquarters Miran Shah · 9 tehsils · 693,332 people (2023 census)

North Waziristan District: Quick Answers

North Waziristan District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Miran Shah. It has 9 tehsils: Datta Khel, Dossali, Gharyum, Ghulam Khan, Mir Ali, Miran Shah, Razmak, Shewa and Spinwam. The 2023 census counted 693,332 people on 4,707 km², and 32.82% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

From tribal agency to district

North Waziristan was one of the seven agencies of the Federally Administered Tribal Areas (FATA) (Xinhua). FATA was merged into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa when President Mamnoon Hussain signed the 25th Constitutional Amendment on 31 May 2018 (Dawn). The provincial Board of Revenue then placed North Waziristan District in Bannu Division (Dawn).

Tehsils of North Waziristan District

The 2023 census counted 9 tehsils in North Waziristan District: Datta Khel, Dossali, Gharyum, Ghulam Khan, Mir Ali, Miran Shah, Razmak, Shewa and Spinwam. Mir Ali is the most populous, with 229,647 people, and Datta Khel is the largest by area (1,807 km²). Literacy ranges from 8.44% in Ghulam Khan to 38.36% in Shewa.

Population and growth

North Waziristan District had 693,332 people in the 2023 census, up from 540,546 in 2017 (average annual growth 4.25%). Among the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the PBS 2023 census tables, it ranks 24th by population and 5th by area (4,707 km²). 0.6% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.71 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Miran Shah (4,131). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.66%).

Literacy and schooling

32.82% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 29th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 46.94% and female literacy 18.03%; the urban rate was 64.44% and the rural rate 32.65%. The census counted 160,200 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (93,165 girls and 67,028 boys).

North Waziristan District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in North Waziristan District?

North Waziristan District has 9 tehsils: Datta Khel, Dossali, Gharyum, Ghulam Khan, Mir Ali, Miran Shah, Razmak, Shewa and Spinwam.

What is the population of North Waziristan District?

The 2023 census counted 693,332 people in North Waziristan District, up from 540,546 in 2017 (annual growth 4.25%). 0.6% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of North Waziristan District?

North Waziristan District covers 4,707 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 147.3 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in North Waziristan District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in North Waziristan District: Miran Shah (4,131, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of North Waziristan District?

32.82% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 46.94%, female 18.03%).

Which languages are spoken in North Waziristan District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.66% of the 693,137 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is North Waziristan District in?

North Waziristan District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Miran Shah.

When did North Waziristan become a district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa?

North Waziristan was a FATA tribal agency until the 25th Constitutional Amendment, signed on 31 May 2018, merged FATA into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It is now a district of Bannu Division. (Dawn)

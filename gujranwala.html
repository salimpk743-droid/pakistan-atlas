Gujranwala District

On the Grand Trunk Road · headquarters Gujranwala · 5 tehsils · 5,959,750 people (2023 census)

Gujranwala District: Quick Answers

Gujranwala District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, in Gujranwala Division, with its headquarters at Gujranwala. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Gujranwala City, Gujranwala Saddar, Kamoke, Nowshera Virkan and Wazirabad. The 2023 census counted 5,959,750 people (pre-split boundaries) on 3,622 km², and 76.77% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Gujranwala city: history

Gujranwala is connected by rail and the Grand Trunk Road with Peshawar and Lahore. It was founded by a pastoral people called Gujars and became a municipality in 1867. The city was the birthplace of the Sikh ruler Ranjit Singh and remained his capital until he took Lahore in 1799. It is an agricultural market and an industrial centre known for ceramics, iron safes and metal utensils (Britannica).

Tehsils of Gujranwala District

The 2023 census counted 5 tehsils in Gujranwala District: Gujranwala City, Gujranwala Saddar, Kamoke, Nowshera Virkan and Wazirabad. Gujranwala City is the most populous, with 2,511,118 people, and Wazirabad is the largest by area (1,196 km²). Literacy ranges from 71.36% in Nowshera Virkan to 79.39% in Gujranwala City.

Population and growth

Gujranwala District had 5,959,750 people in the 2023 census, up from 5,011,066 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.94%). It covers 3,622 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 4th by population and 23rd by area. 60.3% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.32 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 13 urban localities; the largest are Gujranwala (2,511,118), Kamoke (292,023), Gujranwala Cantonment (156,929) and Wazirabad (152,624). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (92.32%), followed by Urdu (6.21%).

Literacy and schooling

76.77% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 7th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 78.14% and female literacy 75.36%; the urban rate was 79.35% and the rural rate 72.84%. The census counted 321,967 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (142,762 girls and 179,171 boys).

Gujranwala District: FAQ

What is the population of Gujranwala District?

The 2023 census counted 5,959,750 people in Gujranwala District (pre-split boundaries), up from 5,011,066 in 2017 (annual growth 2.94%). 60.3% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Gujranwala District?

Gujranwala District covers 3,622 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 1,645.43 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Gujranwala District?

The 2023 census lists 13 urban localities in Gujranwala District; the ten largest are Gujranwala (2,511,118, metropolitan corporation), Kamoke (292,023, municipal corporation), Gujranwala Cantonment (156,929, cantonment), Wazirabad (152,624, municipal committee), Ludhewala Waraich (100,331, municipal committee), Ghakkhar (80,320, municipal committee), Ali Pur Chatha (76,964, municipal committee), Qila Didar Singh (74,523, municipal committee), Nowshera Virkan (59,639, municipal committee) and Eminabad (30,399, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Gujranwala District?

76.77% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 78.14%, female 75.36%).

Which languages are spoken in Gujranwala District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (92.32% of the 5,940,173 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (6.21%).

Which province is Gujranwala District in?

Gujranwala District is in Punjab, in Gujranwala Division. Its headquarters is Gujranwala.

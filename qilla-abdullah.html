Qilla Abdullah District: Quick Answers

Qilla Abdullah District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Pishin Division. The 2023 census lists 3 administrative units in it: Dobandi, Gulistan and Killa Abdullah. The 2023 census counted 361,971 people on 3,553 km², and 36.4% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Qilla Abdullah and Chaman

In June 2021 the Balochistan Board of Revenue split Qilla Abdullah into two districts. The new Chaman District took Chaman city and Saddar Chaman tehsil, while Qilla Abdullah, Gulistan and Dobandi tehsils stayed in Qilla Abdullah District (Dawn).

Administrative units of Qilla Abdullah District

The 2023 census counted 3 administrative units in Qilla Abdullah District: Dobandi (sub-tehsil), Gulistan (sub-division) and Killa Abdullah (sub-division). Killa Abdullah is the most populous, with 165,738 people, and Dobandi is the largest by area (1,604 km²). Literacy ranges from 18.1% in Dobandi to 44% in Killa Abdullah.

Population and growth

Qilla Abdullah District had 361,971 people in the 2023 census, up from 323,793 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.88%). It covers 3,553 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 12th by population and 24th by area. 9.78% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 109.49 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Killa Abdullah (35,384). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.24%).

Literacy and schooling

36.4% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 22nd of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 47.28% and female literacy 24.7%; the urban rate was 34.72% and the rural rate 36.59%. The census counted 92,276 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (48,070 girls and 44,199 boys).

Qilla Abdullah District: FAQ

What is the population of Qilla Abdullah District?

The 2023 census counted 361,971 people in Qilla Abdullah District, up from 323,793 in 2017 (annual growth 1.88%). 9.78% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Qilla Abdullah District?

Qilla Abdullah District covers 3,553 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 101.88 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Qilla Abdullah District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Qilla Abdullah District: Killa Abdullah (35,384, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Qilla Abdullah District?

36.4% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 47.28%, female 24.7%).

Which languages are spoken in Qilla Abdullah District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.24% of the 361,971 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Qilla Abdullah District in?

Qilla Abdullah District is in Balochistan, in Pishin Division.

When was Chaman separated from Qilla Abdullah?

In June 2021, when the Balochistan Board of Revenue split Qilla Abdullah into Qilla Abdullah District (Qilla Abdullah, Gulistan and Dobandi tehsils) and Chaman District (Chaman city and Saddar Chaman). (Dawn)

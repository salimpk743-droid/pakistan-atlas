Uswah Drama: Cast Name, Story and Timing

Uswah (اسوہ) is a family and romance drama on Geo Entertainment, written by Hina Huma Nafees and directed by Ali Akbar, starring Saniya Shamshad, Omer Shahzad. It premiered on 23 September 2026.

Uswah Story

Uswah (Saniya Shamshad) is a cheerful, clever and ambitious young woman from a middle-class family who works part-time to pay for her studies and help at home. Her cousin Mahabir (Haroon Shahid) admires her confidence, and the two grow fond of each other.

At university she meets Essa (Omer Shahzad), a wealthy, self-assured student who becomes her academic rival. A playful moment between them seems trivial, but it changes both their lives: just as Uswah is about to marry Mahabir, a sudden revelation traps her in a relationship she never wanted.

Inside Essa's world their bond is full of resentment, misunderstandings and conflict, yet Uswah's patience, strength and deepening faith begin to challenge his outlook. New suspicions and family tensions then put the relationship to its hardest test, and the story asks whether Essa's actions will push Uswah back towards Mahabir.

Story summarised in our own words from the channel's official synopsis listed under Sources; it covers only what has been published or aired.

Uswah Premise and Themes

Geo’s launch report in The News (23 September 2026) describes the central idea: Uswah’s cheerful, happy life changes completely because of what at first looks like a harmless joke. The serial, the report says, explores how seemingly small matters among the younger generation can turn into something much more serious.

The official synopsis on Har Pal Geo builds the drama around three pulls on its heroine: family expectations, love and circumstances beyond her control. Once Uswah is forced into a relationship she never wanted, the story follows how her patience, strength and growing understanding of faith begin to challenge Essa’s outlook, before fresh suspicions and family tensions test them again.

The channel itself frames the story around three open questions: whether Uswah and Essa will ever find common ground, whether Uswah will be able to clear the air with Essa, and whether Essa’s actions will make Uswah turn back to Mahabir. Mahabir is spelled “Mahbir” in the synopsis and “Mahabir” in the episode cast credits.

Uswah Main Characters

  • Uswah (Saniya Shamshad): the title character. In the channel’s description she is a cheerful, intelligent and ambitious young woman from a middle-class family who works part-time to pay for her education and help her family.
  • Essa (Omer Shahzad): a wealthy and confident university student who becomes Uswah’s academic rival. A playful moment between the two sets the main story in motion.
  • Mahabir (Haroon Shahid): Uswah’s cousin, who admires her confidence and strong character. The two develop feelings for each other, and Uswah is preparing to marry him when an unexpected revelation changes everything.

Har Pal Geo credits nine more actors by character name: Ammara Malik (Fariya), Junaid Akhtar (Zaid), Rabia Noreen (Naheed), Hira Soomro (Abeera), Hareem Sohail (Anaya), Akbar Islam (Ibrahim), Huma Tahir (Farheen), Syed Mohsin Gillani (Ehtisham) and Saima Qureshi (Tabassum). The channel has not published descriptions of these roles, so we do not guess at their relationships to the leads. Akbar Islam also appears in Geo’s 2026 serial Mohabbat.

Uswah OST

The Uswah OST is sung by Sahir Ali Bagga and Afshan Fawad; Sahir Ali Bagga is credited as composer and lyricist. Listen on the official Har Pal Geo upload. See the Pakistani drama OSTs guide for more soundtracks.

According to The News (23 September 2026), the music for the serial was composed by Azaan Zair Baga, with vocals by Afshan Fawad, and Sahir Ali Bagga contributed the poetry, composition and his own voice to the OST, which is meant to bring out the emotional pain of Uswah’s journey. The official upload is titled “Uswah | Full OST | Sahir Ali Bagga & Afshan Fawad | Ft. Omer Shahzad, Saniya Shamshad”.

Writer, Director and Production of Uswah

Uswah is written by Hina Huma Nafees and directed by Ali Akbar. Production: Abdullah Kadwani and Asad Qureshi (7th Sky Entertainment).

The News (23 September 2026) describes Uswah as another new production from Geo and 7th Sky Entertainment, with Hina Huma Nafees as writer, Ali Akbar as director and Abdullah Kadwani and Asad Qureshi as producers. Har Pal Geo’s episode descriptions open with the same credit line: “7th Sky Entertainment Presentation”.

Uswah Timing: When and Where to Watch

Uswah airs on Geo Entertainment: 7:00 pm (Pakistan time). Geo's OST upload announces Uswah at 7:00 pm; Episodes 1 to 8 were uploaded on eight consecutive days, 23 to 30 September 2026. Episodes are also uploaded to the official Har Pal Geo YouTube channel. Schedules change, so check the channel's official website or YouTube channel for the latest timing.

Geo announced the daily slot before launch. The News (23 September 2026) said viewers could watch Uswah “daily at 7pm on Geo TV from September 23”, and Har Pal Geo’s launch promos were titled “Starting From Tomorrow | 7:00 PM” and “Starting From Tonight | 7:00 PM”. Later promos say “Daily at 7:00 PM”.

For viewers outside Pakistan, 7:00 pm Pakistan time (UTC+5) is 6:00 pm in the UAE, 5:00 pm in Saudi Arabia, 7:30 pm in India and 3:00 pm in the UK while British Summer Time lasts (until 25 October 2026). Full episodes with English subtitles appear on the official HAR PAL GEO YouTube channel, and the Har Pal Geo programme page lists episodes and teasers.

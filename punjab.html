Punjab پنجاب

Punjab is a province of Pakistan with its capital at Lahore. The 2023 census counted 127,688,922 people (52.87% of Pakistan) on 205,345 km², and 66.25% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

About Punjab

Punjab is Pakistan's second-largest and most densely populated province; Lahore, the provincial capital, lies near the Indian border. The present provincial boundaries were established in 1970 (Britannica).

Culture and Geography of Punjab

Punjab is the cultural heartland of Sufi poetry, Qawwali, Bhangra and harvest festivals such as Basant. Major languages are Punjabi and Saraiki. Historic cities include Lahore, Multan and Taxila.

Education and Health in Punjab

Highest number of universities and colleges in the country, including University of the Punjab, UET Lahore and UVAS.

Dense public hospital network in northern and central districts; southern Punjab still needs more specialists.

Public Issues in Punjab

Urban crowding in Lahore and Faisalabad, smog, groundwater stress, and the need to expand technical education in southern districts.

Notable Places in Punjab

Lahore, Multan, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi, Bahawalpur

Largest districts of Punjab

Among the 31 current districts with their own 2023 census row, the most populous are Lahore District (13,004,135), Faisalabad District (9,075,819) and Rahim Yar Khan District (5,564,703). The largest by area are Bahawalpur District (24,830 km²), Rajanpur District (12,318 km²) and Rahim Yar Khan District (11,880 km²). Literacy ranges from 36.09% in Rajanpur District to 81.37% in Gujrat District. Figures are from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Tables 1 and 12; districts split after the census are left out of this comparison.

Punjab: FAQ

What is the capital of Punjab?

Lahore. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

What is the population of Punjab?

The 2023 census counted 127,688,922 people, up from 109,989,655 in 2017 (annual growth 2.53%). (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

What is the area of Punjab?

205,345 km², with 621.83 people per km² in 2023. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

What is the literacy rate of Punjab?

66.25% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 71.98%, female 60.19%). (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Punjab facts

Capital: Lahore · Population: 127,688,922 (2023 baseline) · Area: 205,344 km² · Administrative units: 41 district pages.

Culture and geography

Punjab is the cultural heartland of Sufi poetry, Qawwali, Bhangra and harvest festivals such as Basant. Major languages are Punjabi and Saraiki. Historic cities include Lahore, Multan and Taxila.

Education and health

Highest number of universities and colleges in the country, including University of the Punjab, UET Lahore and UVAS.

Dense public hospital network in northern and central districts; southern Punjab still needs more specialists.

Punjab پنجاب

Punjab is the cultural heartland of Sufi poetry, Qawwali, Bhangra and harvest festivals such as Basant. Major languages are Punjabi and Saraiki. Historic cities include Lahore, Multan and Taxila.

Punjab facts

Capital: Lahore · Population: 127,688,922 (2023 census) · Area: 205,344 km² · District pages on MyBook.Pk: 41.

Full list: districts of Punjab → · All provinces and territories →

Culture and geography

Punjab is the cultural heartland of Sufi poetry, Qawwali, Bhangra and harvest festivals such as Basant. Major languages are Punjabi and Saraiki. Historic cities include Lahore, Multan and Taxila.

Education and health

Highest number of universities and colleges in the country, including University of the Punjab, UET Lahore and UVAS.

Dense public hospital network in northern and central districts; southern Punjab still needs more specialists.

Districts of Punjab

Every district page below has its headquarters, population and local information; population figures are from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Table 1.

Attock

Attock District

Population: 2,170,423 (2023 census)

6 tehsils: Attock, Fateh Jang, Hasan Abdal, Hazro, Jand and Pindi Gheb

North-western Punjab on the Indus. Attock Fort looks over the river crossing toward KP.

Bahawalnagar

Bahawalnagar District

Population: 3,550,342 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Bahawalnagar, Chishtian, Fort Abbas, Haroonabad and Minchinabad

South-eastern Punjab on the Indian border, once part of Bahawalpur State.

Bahawalpur

Bahawalpur District

Population: 4,284,964 (2023 census)

6 tehsils: Ahmadpur East, Bahawalpur City, Bahawalpur Saddar, Hasilpur, Khairpur Tamewali and Yazman

Former princely state. Palaces, the Noor Mahal area and the gateway to Cholistan Desert.

Bhakkar

Bhakkar District

Population: 1,957,470 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Bhakkar, Darya Khan, Kalur Kot and Mankera

Thal desert district on the left bank of the Indus.

Chakwal

Chakwal District

Population: 1,734,854 (2023 census, pre-split)

Salt Range and Potohar plateau. Many families have a long military tradition.

Chiniot

Chiniot District

Population: 1,563,024 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Bhawana, Chiniot and Lalian

Famous for wood carving and furniture on the Chenab.

D.G. Khan

Dera Ghazi Khan District

Population: 3,393,705 (2023 census, pre-split)

Gateway between southern Punjab and the Sulaiman mountains.

Faisalabad

Faisalabad District

Population: 9,075,819 (2023 census)

6 tehsils: Chak Jhumra, Faisalabad City, Faisalabad Sadar, Jaranwala, Sammundri and Tandlian Wala

Textile capital of Pakistan, laid out as Lyallpur in British canal-colony years.

Gujranwala

Gujranwala District

Population: 5,959,750 (2023 census, pre-split)

Industrial city of Punjab’s north-east, known for electrical goods and food.

Gujrat

Gujrat District

Population: 3,219,375 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Gujrat, Kharian and Sarai Alamgir

Chenab district known for furniture, ceramics and overseas workers.

Hafizabad

Hafizabad District

Population: 1,319,909 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Hafizabad and Pindi Bhattian

Rice belt of central Punjab.

Jhang

Jhang District

Population: 3,065,639 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: 18-Hazari, Ahmadpur Sial, Jhang and Shorkot

Land of the Heer-Ranjha story, at the meeting of Chenab and Jhelum systems.

Jhelum

Jhelum District

Population: 1,382,308 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Dina, Jhelum, Pind Dadan Khan and Sohawa

Grand Trunk Road and the river Jhelum. Rohtas Fort is nearby in this historic belt.

Kasur

Kasur District

Population: 4,084,286 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Chunian, Kasur, Kot Radha Kishan and Pattoki

South of Lahore, shrine of Baba Bullhe Shah and a busy border district.

Khanewal

Khanewal District

Population: 3,364,077 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Jahanian, Kabirwala, Khanewal and Mian Channu

Railway junction district in the Multan division.

Jauharabad

Khushab District

Population: 1,501,089 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Khushab, Noor Pur, Nowshera and Quaidabad

Salt Range and Soon Valley on one side, Thal on the other.

Kot Addu

Kot Addu District

Separated from Muzaffargarh on the Indus plain.

Lahore

Lahore District

Population: 13,004,135 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Lahore Cantt, Lahore City, Model Town, Raiwind and Shalimar

Provincial capital. Walled City, Badshahi Mosque, Lahore Fort, and Pakistan’s densest university cluster.

Layyah

Layyah District

Population: 2,102,386 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Choubara, Karor Lal Esan and Layyah

Thal district between the Indus and Chenab systems.

Lodhran

Lodhran District

Population: 1,928,299 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Dunyapur, Kahror Pacca and Lodhran

Compact cotton-wheat district south of Multan.

Mandi Bahauddin

Mandi Bahauddin District

Population: 1,829,486 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Malakwal, Mandi Bahauddin and Phalia

Between Jhelum and Chenab, known as a clean planned mandi town.

Mianwali

Mianwali District

Population: 1,798,268 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Isakhel, Mianwali and Piplan

Western Punjab on the Salt Range and Indus. Lakes and namak-era geology.

Multan

Multan District

Population: 5,362,305 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Jalalpur Pirwala, Multan City, Multan Saddar and Shujabad

City of saints. One of the oldest urban centres in South Asia, now a south-Punjab hub.

Murree

Murree District

Hill district separated from Rawalpindi. Colonial hill station and the road to Kashmir.

Muzaffargarh

Muzaffargarh District

Population: 5,015,325 (2023 census, pre-split)

Between Chenab and Indus. Repeatedly hit by river floods.

Nankana Sahib

Nankana Sahib District

Population: 1,634,871 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Nankana Sahib, Sangla Hill and Shah Kot

Birthplace of Guru Nanak. A district created from Sheikhupura.

Narowal

Narowal District

Population: 1,950,954 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal

North-east Punjab on the Indian border, including the town of Shakargarh.

Okara

Okara District

Population: 3,515,490 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Depalpur, Okara and Renala Khurd

Canal-colony dairy and military-farm district.

Pakpattan

Pakpattan District

Population: 2,136,170 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Arif Wala and Pakpattan

Shrine of Baba Farid Ganjshakar on the old river route.

Rahim Yar Khan

Rahim Yar Khan District

Population: 5,564,703 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Khanpur, Liaquat Pur, Rahim Yar Khan and Sadiqabad

Southern-most large district of Punjab, on the road to Sindh.

Rajanpur

Rajanpur District

Population: 2,381,049 (2023 census)

Foot of the Sulaiman Range. Hill torrents can cut the district in the monsoon.

Rawalpindi

Rawalpindi District

Population: 6,118,911 (2023 census, pre-split)

Twin city of Islamabad. Old bazaars, cantonment, and the road to Murree.

Sahiwal

Sahiwal District

Population: 2,881,811 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Chichawatni and Sahiwal

Canal-colony city formerly Montgomery. Cattle breed named after the district.

Sargodha

Sargodha District

Population: 4,334,448 (2023 census)

7 tehsils: Bhalwal, Bhera, Kot Momin, Sahiwal, Sargodha, Shahpur and Sillanwali

Citrus capital of Pakistan and a large air-base city.

Sheikhupura

Sheikhupura District

Population: 4,049,418 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Ferozewala, Muridke, Safdarabad, Sharak Pur and Sheikhupura

Hiran Minar and industrial belt west of Lahore.

Sialkot

Sialkot District

Population: 4,499,394 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Daska, Pasrur, Sambrial and Sialkot

Export city of sports goods, surgical instruments and leather. Birthplace of Allama Iqbal.

Talagang

Talagang District

Created from Chakwal. Dry Potohar and salt-range west.

Taunsa Sharif

Taunsa District

Separated from D.G. Khan. Shrine town and the road toward Fort Munro.

Toba Tek Singh

Toba Tek Singh District

Population: 2,524,044 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Gojra, Kamalia, Pirmahal and Toba Tek Singh

Canal-colony district made famous in literature, now a farm and small-industry belt.

Vehari

Vehari District

Population: 3,430,421 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Burewala, Mailsi and Vehari

Cotton-wheat district created from Multan.

Wazirabad

Wazirabad District

Chenab industrial town, cut out of Gujranwala. Known for cutlery.

Public issues

Urban crowding in Lahore and Faisalabad, smog, groundwater stress, and the need to expand technical education in southern districts.

Sources: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census and provincial or district sources noted on individual pages.

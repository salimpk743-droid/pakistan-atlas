Districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir

An alphabetical list of the 10 district pages for Azad Jammu and Kashmir on MyBook.Pk, with headquarters, 2023 census population and tehsils where the census publishes them.

Bagh

Bagh District

Hill district badly hit in 2005, since rebuilt as a district headquarters.

Bhimber

Bhimber District

Southern-most AJK district toward the Punjab plain.

Hattian Bala

Hattian Bala District

Jhelum valley district created after 2005.

Forward Kahuta

Haveli District

Eastern district toward the Line of Control.

Kotli

Kotli District

Central-south AJK on the Poonch river system.

Mirpur

Mirpur District

Mangla lake city with a large overseas Kashmiri link to Britain.

Muzaffarabad

Muzaffarabad District

Capital at the meeting of Neelum and Jhelum. Rebuilt after the 2005 earthquake.

Athmuqam

Neelum District

Long north-east valley along the Neelum River.

Rawalakot

Poonch (Rawalakot) District

Hill town of Rawalakot, often called the Pearl Valley.

Pallandri

Sudhanoti District

Hill district of Pallandri.

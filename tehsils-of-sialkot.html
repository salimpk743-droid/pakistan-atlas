Tehsils of Sialkot District

Gujranwala Division · Punjab · 2023 census figures

Sialkot District administrative structure

Sialkot District has 4 tehsils: Daska, Pasrur, Sambrial and Sialkot. The district is part of Gujranwala Division.

Daska Tehsil

Population (2023): 980,547

Area: 690 km² · 1,421.08 people per km²

Urban share: 26.45%

Pasrur Tehsil

Population (2023): 970,366

Area: 975 km² · 995.25 people per km²

Urban share: 13.59%

Sambrial Tehsil

Population (2023): 460,280

Area: 450 km² · 1,022.84 people per km²

Urban share: 30.83%

Sialkot Tehsil

Population (2023): 2,088,201

Area: 901 km² · 2,317.65 people per km²

Urban share: 45.44%

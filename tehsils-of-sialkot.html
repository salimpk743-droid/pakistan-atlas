Tehsils of Sialkot District

Sialkot District has 4 tehsils: Daska, Pasrur, Sambrial and Sialkot. Sialkot is the most populous, with 2,088,201 people in the 2023 census, and Pasrur is the largest by area (975 km²).

How the tehsils compare

Sialkot tehsil has the most people (2,088,201) and Sambrial the fewest (460,280). Pasrur covers the largest area (975 km²). Sialkot is the most urban (45.44% of residents in urban areas). Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 74.52% in Pasrur to 79.89% in Sambrial.

Gilgit District: Quick Answers

Gilgit District is one of the 10 districts for which Gilgit-Baltistan's Planning & Development Department publishes statistics. The 2023 census counted 324,552 people on 4,208 km², and 73.13% of residents were literate.

Population and growth

Gilgit District had 324,552 people in the 2023 census, up from 285,236 in 2017, an average annual growth rate of 2.18%. It covers 4,208 km². The census counted 47,496 households, with an average of 7 people each, and 117 males for every 100 females (GB at a Glance 2025).

How Gilgit compares within Gilgit-Baltistan

Among the 10 districts in the GB statistics, Gilgit ranks 2nd by population, 8th by area and 3rd by literacy (73.13% in the 2023 census). Gilgit-Baltistan as a whole counted 1,709,049 people on 72,496 km² (GB at a Glance 2025).

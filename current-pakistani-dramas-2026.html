Current Pakistani Dramas: Story of Each Drama

HUM TV Dramas 2026: Latest Dramas on Air

  • Khushi (HUM TV; started 13 September 2026): Khushi is a daily HUM TV serial that the channel describes as a story of love, relationships and the choices that shape destiny. As life unfolds, each decision brings new challenges and unexpected turns that test love and family bonds. HUM TV has not published a detailed synopsis or the characters' names in its official material, so this page does not guess them; the cast below is the channel's own list. Episodes so far: 18 episodes had been uploaded to the official HUM TV YouTube channel by 30 September 2026 (Episode 18 on 30 September); HUM released the Episode 19 teaser the same day.
  • Tark-e-Tamanna (HUM TV; started 28 September 2026): HUM TV sums it up as a story of two people whose hearts are consumed by hatred, where the strongest feelings may be hiding behind the deepest resentment, and asks whether they can find their way to love. Produced by Momina Duraid Productions. Episodes so far: 3 episodes uploaded by 30 September 2026.
  • Zehar-e-Mohabbat (HUM TV; started 22 August 2026): Dawn describes a Karachi story: middle-class Arham, desperate for money, becomes a witness to the murder of Seth Irtiza and is pressured into silence by the criminal mastermind behind the city's crime wave. He later saves Seth Irtiza's daughter Umamaa, gets a job through her and is weighed down by guilt on learning he works in her father's office, while Waqar, married to Umamaa's elder sister Anzala, plots against him. Produced by Moomal Productions and MD Productions. Episodes so far: Episode 11 was uploaded on 26 September 2026.
  • Mohabbat Mukhtasir (HUM TV; started 27 September 2026): HUM TV presents it as a drama about the complexities of relationships, the power of emotions and life's unexpected turns, in which some stories are written by fate and others are shaped by love, sacrifice and choices. Dawn calls it a light romance. Produced by Momina Duraid Productions. Episodes so far: Episode 1 was uploaded on 27 September 2026.

Har Pal Geo Dramas List 2026: Latest on Geo Entertainment

  • Kaisa Yeh Ishq (Geo Entertainment; started 12 August 2026): Kaisa Yeh Ishq is the story of Sila and Ali, who have loved each other since childhood, when their engagement was arranged. Sila (Zoha Tauqeer) is a gentle, innocent young woman brought up by her strict grandfather, Ayaz Durrani (Usman Peerzada). Still hurt by his own daughter's love marriage, Ayaz Durrani keeps Sila under tight restrictions to protect the family's honour, and Ali (Hashaam Khan) becomes her comfort and her source of happiness. A misunderstanding then leads her grandfather to break the engagement. Episodes so far: 50 episodes had been uploaded to the official Har Pal Geo YouTube channel by 30 September 2026 (Episode 50 on 30 September); Geo promoted Episode 51 for 1 October.
  • Uswah (Geo Entertainment; started 23 September 2026): Uswah (Saniya Shamshad) is a cheerful, clever and ambitious young woman from a middle-class family who works part-time to pay for her studies and help at home. Her cousin Mahabir (Haroon Shahid) admires her confidence, and the two grow fond of each other. At university she meets Essa (Omer Shahzad), a wealthy, self-assured student who becomes her academic rival. A playful moment between them seems trivial, but it changes both their lives: just as Uswah is about to marry Mahabir, a sudden revelation traps her in a relationship she never wanted. Episodes so far: 8 episodes had been uploaded to the official Har Pal Geo YouTube channel by 30 September 2026 (Episode 8 on 30 September); Geo released the Episode 9 teaser the same day.
  • Khafa (Geo Entertainment; started 5 August 2026): Khafa follows Tazeen (Hira Mani), a caring schoolteacher who has given her life to raising her younger siblings since her father died, putting her own dreams aside to meet every family responsibility. Mohtashim (Mikaal Zulfiqar), son of Raza, is born into wealth, but his stepmother Shehla and Rayan make his life miserable, and he chooses a simple, humble life over luxury. When Tazeen is falsely accused of theft, she loses her family's trust and the man she loves; Mohtashim marries her to protect her. Episodes so far: 17 episodes had been uploaded to the official Har Pal Geo YouTube channel by 30 September 2026 (Episode 17 on 30 September); Geo promoted Episode 18 for 1 October.

ARY Digital New Dramas: Latest on Air

  • Main Tera (ARY Digital; started 22 July 2026): Main Tera is the story of Hassan, a devoted son whose life revolves around caring for his four sisters. At 40 he is still unmarried, because his mother believes marriage would pull him away from his duty to see his sisters settled first, even though he has long been engaged to Kashmala. Episodes so far: Episode 50 was uploaded on 30 September 2026.
  • Izzat (ARY Digital; started 20 September 2026): ARY News describes the teaser: Rutba (Merub Ali) is deeply in love with a young man played by Atabik Mohsin, and the couple seem happy as they prepare to marry, until Rutba suddenly calls off the wedding and Hashaam Khan's character enters with aggressive intentions, setting up an intense conflict. Episodes so far: Episode 11 was uploaded on 30 September 2026.
  • Bas Tera Saath Ho (ARY Digital; started 4 May 2026): A story of resilience born from loss: after his parents die in an accident, young Anas grows up in a home that gives him shelter but not warmth. Dawn's reviews follow the grown-up Anas (Farhan Saeed), his wife Ansa (Sana Javed), his cousin Shireen (Zoya Nasir), her brother Shayan (Haris Waheed) and his uncle Mursaleen (Faran Tahir), whose family has long used deceit against Anas. Episodes so far: Episode 44, billed as the second-last episode, was uploaded on 29 September 2026; ARY's promos bill Episode 45 as the last episode.

Green Entertainment Dramas 2026: Latest on Air

  • Raqaas (Green Entertainment; started 7 September 2026): Green Entertainment presents Raqaas as an intense psychological tale about the fragile space between love and obsession, freedom and restraint, and memory and reality, where every relationship carries an unspoken weight and every truth may have another side. Dawn's review sets out the early story: to fulfil her conservative father Dilawar Khan's wish, Urooj (Komal Meer) becomes a doctor and buries her love of art; after the freedom of university she longs to draw and perhaps dance, but her strict family objects even to an anklet. Her easy friendship with her cousin Fawad (Hunain Shahid) changes when she learns her father has chosen Fawad as her husband. Episodes so far: 8 episodes had been uploaded to the official Green TV Entertainment YouTube channel by 29 September 2026 (Episode 8 on 29 September).
  • Madaar (Green Entertainment; started 23 September 2026): After his father's death, Samar takes responsibility for his mother, brothers and sisters, supporting them through his growing IT business. His generosity turns into pressure as relatives become dependent and entitled. Amid the chaos he meets Zoha (spelt Zoya in the synopsis), an independent young woman wary of marriage, and their early suspicion turns into love even as greed and jealousy tear at his family. Presented by Multiverse Entertainment. Episodes so far: Episode 6 was uploaded on 30 September 2026.
  • Dastoor (Green Entertainment; started 22 August 2026): A family drama of love, sacrifice, family conflict and secrets that follows a mother and her daughters as they face hard choices, broken relationships and unexpected turns of fate. Presented by Multiverse Entertainment. Episodes so far: Episode 40 was uploaded on 30 September 2026.

Express Entertainment Dramas 2026: Latest on Air

  • Hum Bhi Ji Lain (Express Entertainment; started 24 September 2026): An emotional family drama about Sadia, who becomes a mother to her siblings after losing their parents. Her love and sacrifices hold the family together, but as her siblings grow up her protection starts to feel like control. Presented by IVYStar Media and Full Frame Productions; produced by M. Riaz Ilyas. Episodes so far: Episode 1 was uploaded on 24 September 2026.
  • Mein Kahani Hun (Season 3) (Express Entertainment; started 27 September 2026): Express describes a limited, complete story about a relationship built on love, trust and understanding that is put at risk by a hidden truth, asking whether love can survive betrayal. Presented by TNI Entertainment and Express Entertainment. Episodes so far: Episode 1 was uploaded on 27 September 2026.
  • Picnic Fund (Express Entertainment; started 21 September 2026): A light-hearted family drama of fun, emotions and everyday family moments, from small misunderstandings to memorable moments. Written, directed and produced by Shariq Yousuf Thara. Episodes so far: Episode 1 was uploaded on 21 September 2026.

Current Pakistani Dramas FAQ: October 2026

Which Pakistani dramas air daily?

Khushi (HUM TV, 7:00 pm every day), Kaisa Yeh Ishq (Geo, 9:00 pm) and Uswah (Geo, 7:00 pm) have been airing on consecutive days; Main Tera (ARY) and Tark-e-Tamanna (HUM) air Monday to Friday at 9:00 pm, and Picnic Fund (Express) Monday to Thursday at 9:00 pm.

Which drama started most recently?

Tark-e-Tamanna (HUM TV) and Mohabbat (Geo) began on 28 September 2026, and Mohabbat Mukhtasir (HUM TV) and Mein Kahani Hun Season 3 (Express) on 27 September 2026.

What is the Har Pal Geo dramas list 2026 at night?

Districts of Balochistan

An alphabetical list of the 35 district pages for Balochistan on MyBook.Pk, with headquarters, 2023 census population and tehsils where the census publishes them.

Balochistan province guide → · All districts of Pakistan →

Awaran

Awaran District

Population: 178,958 (2023 census)

Sparsely populated southern district.

Barkhan

Barkhan District

Population: 210,249 (2023 census)

Eastern Balochistan toward the Punjab border.

Dalbandin

Chagai District

Population: 269,192 (2023 census)

Huge western desert district, including Taftan border logic.

Chaman

Chaman District

Population: 466,218 (2023 census)

Busy border city district toward Spin Boldak.

Dera Bugti

Dera Bugti District

Population: 355,274 (2023 census)

Eastern gas-field district.

Duki

Duki District

Population: 205,044 (2023 census)

Coal-field district created from Loralai.

Gwadar

Gwadar District

Population: 305,160 (2023 census)

Deep-sea port on the Makran coast. Fast-changing town with a very old fishing history.

Harnai

Harnai District

Population: 127,571 (2023 census)

Railway-pass district in north-east Balochistan.

Hub

Hub District

Industrial town district on the Sindh border, created from Lasbela.

Dera Allah Yar

Jafarabad District

Population: 594,558 (2023 census, pre-split)

Irrigated plains of Nasirabad division.

Gandawa

Jhal Magsi District

Population: 203,368 (2023 census)

Desert-plain district known for a desert rally in some years.

Kalat

Kalat District

Population: 271,560 (2023 census)

Historic khanate highlands.

Turbat

Kech District

Population: 1,060,931 (2023 census)

Kech river oasis; Turbat is the largest city of Makran after the Quetta comparison.

Kharan

Kharan District

Population: 260,352 (2023 census)

Old desert state, now a district of huge empty space.

Khuzdar

Khuzdar District

Population: 997,214 (2023 census)

Central highland city on the RCD road.

Kohlu

Kohlu District

Population: 260,220 (2023 census)

Eastern highland district.

Uthal

Lasbela District

Population: 680,977 (2023 census, pre-split)

Coastal-plain district toward Hub and the Sindh border, including Sonmiani.

Loralai

Loralai District

Population: 272,432 (2023 census)

Almond and highland town of north-east Balochistan.

Mastung

Mastung District

Population: 313,271 (2023 census)

Valley south of Quetta on the way to Kalat.

Musakhel

Musakhel District

Population: 182,275 (2023 census)

North-eastern district toward Punjab.

Dera Murad Jamali

Nasirabad District

Population: 563,315 (2023 census)

Canal plain of the Pat Feeder command.

Nushki

Nushki District

Population: 207,834 (2023 census)

Desert town on the old railway toward Dalbandin.

Panjgur

Panjgur District

Population: 509,781 (2023 census)

Date-palm oasis of western Makran.

Pishin

Pishin District

Population: 835,482 (2023 census)

Valley north of Quetta, fruit and karez country.

Qilla Abdullah District

Population: 361,971 (2023 census)

Border highlands west of Pishin. Chaman was later treated as its own district.

Qilla Saifullah

Qilla Saifullah District

Population: 380,200 (2023 census)

Apple country of north-east Balochistan.

Quetta

Quetta District

Population: 2,595,492 (2023 census)

Provincial capital in a high dry bowl. Fruit markets, cantonment and university city. Some tables now split Quetta East / West — confirm before citing a count.

Sherani

Sherani District

Population: 191,687 (2023 census)

Sulaiman mountain district created from Zhob.

Sibi

Sibi District

Population: 224,148 (2023 census)

Famous for Sibi Mela and a winter heat record reputation.

Sohbatpur

Sohbatpur District

Population: 240,106 (2023 census)

Plains district created from Jafarabad.

Surab

Surab District

Population: 279,038 (2023 census)

Created from Kalat highlands.

Usta Muhammad

Usta Muhammad District

Plains town district near the Sindh border.

Washuk

Washuk District

Population: 302,623 (2023 census)

Vast western district split from Kharan.

Zhob

Zhob District

Population: 355,692 (2023 census)

Northern gateway toward D.I. Khan.

Ziarat

Ziarat District

Population: 189,535 (2023 census)

Juniper forest hill station and Quaid-e-Azam Residency.

Sources and editorial method

Population, area and tehsil figures are taken from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Table 1. Districts created after the census are marked, and no figure is shown where no official number exists.

Districts of Balochistan

Balochistan has 41 districts (September 2026). An alphabetical list with headquarters, 2023 census population and tehsils where the census publishes them.

Balochistan districts, A–Z

Awaran

Awaran District

Population: 178,958 (2023 census)

Awaran District, Balochistan: 178,958 people in the 2023 census, 5 administrative units, headquarters Awaran.

Barkhan

Barkhan District

Population: 210,249 (2023 census)

Single-tehsil district of Koh-i-Suleman Division, separated from Loralai on 31 December 1991; 210,249 people in the 2023 census.

Barshore

Barshore District

Barshore (Barshor) District was approved by the Balochistan cabinet in early 2026 and is part of the new Pishin Division. The 8 July 2026 notification upgraded Barshore to a tehsil and created the Toba Kakari sub-division and tehsil in the district.

Dalbandin

Chagai District

Population: 269,192 (2023 census)

Largest district of Pakistan by area (44,748 km²), in north-west Balochistan; home of the Saindak and Reko Diq copper-gold projects.

Chaman

Chaman District

Population: 466,218 (2023 census)

Balochistan border district created in June 2021 from Qilla Abdullah, made up of Chaman city and Saddar Chaman.

Duki

Duki District

Population: 205,044 (2023 census)

Coal-mining district of northern Balochistan, upgraded from a sub-division of Loralai to a district with effect from 1 August 2017.

Gwadar

Gwadar District

Population: 305,160 (2023 census)

Balochistan coastal district; Gwadar town, on the Arabian Sea, was part of the Sultanate of Muscat and Oman from 1797 until 1958.

Harnai

Harnai District

Population: 127,571 (2023 census)

Harnai District, Balochistan: 127,571 people in the 2023 census, 3 administrative units, headquarters Harnai.

Hub

Hub District

Lasbela Division district notified in September 2022 from Lasbela, with Hub, Dureji and Sonmiani (Winder) sub-divisions.

Dera Allah Yar

Jafarabad District

Population: 594,558 (2023 census, pre-split)

Jafarabad District, Balochistan: 594,558 people in the 2023 census, 3 administrative units, headquarters Dera Allah Yar.

Gandawa

Jhal Magsi District

Population: 203,368 (2023 census)

Jhal Magsi District, Balochistan: 203,368 people in the 2023 census, 3 administrative units, headquarters Gandawa.

Dhadar

Kachhi District

Population: 442,674 (2023 census)

Kachhi District, Balochistan: 442,674 people in the 2023 census, 6 administrative units, headquarters Dhadar.

Kalat

Kalat District

Population: 271,560 (2023 census)

Balochistan district whose headquarters was the capital of the Khans of Kalat; the region became part of Pakistan in 1948.

Turbat

Kech District

Population: 857,118 (2023 census)

Balochistan district whose headquarters, Turbat, lies on the Kech River; Tump and Mand were separated as Tump District in 2026.

Kharan

Kharan District

Population: 260,352 (2023 census)

Balochistan desert district; Kharan town, a former caravan depot, lies about 10 km from the Baddo River.

Khuzdar

Khuzdar District

Population: 997,214 (2023 census, 2023)

Balochistan district whose headquarters lies on the Kolachi River, on the main road between Kalat and Karachi.

Kohlu

Kohlu District

Population: 260,220 (2023 census)

Kohlu District, Balochistan: 260,220 people in the 2023 census, 5 administrative units, headquarters Kohlu.

Uthal

Lasbela District

Population: 680,977 (2023 census, pre-split)

Lasbela District, Balochistan: 680,977 people in the 2023 census, 9 administrative units, headquarters Uthal.

Loralai

Loralai District

Population: 272,432 (2023 census)

Balochistan district headquartered at Loralai town (founded 1886); headquarters of Loralai Division since 2021.

Mastung

Mastung District

Population: 313,271 (2023 census)

Mastung District, Balochistan: 313,271 people in the 2023 census, 4 administrative units, headquarters Mastung.

Musakhel

Musakhel District

Population: 182,275 (2023 census)

Musakhel District, Balochistan: 182,275 people in the 2023 census, 6 administrative units, headquarters Musakhel.

Dera Murad Jamali

Nasirabad District

Population: 563,315 (2023 census)

Nasirabad District, Balochistan: 563,315 people in the 2023 census, 6 administrative units, headquarters Dera Murad Jamali.

Baiker (Bekhar)

North Dera Bugti District

North Dera Bugti was created as Upper Dera Bugti when Dera Bugti District was divided in February 2026, with its headquarters at Baiker (Bekhar), and was renamed North Dera Bugti by the 8 July 2026 notification. It is part of the new Koh-i-Suleman Division.

Nushki

Nushki District

Population: 207,834 (2023 census)

Nushki District, Balochistan: 207,834 people in the 2023 census, 2 administrative units, headquarters Nushki.

Panjgur

Panjgur District

Population: 509,781 (2023 census)

Balochistan district whose headquarters lies on the south bank of the Rakhshan River in the Siahan Range.

Pishin

Pishin District

Population: 835,482 (2023 census, pre-split)

Balochistan district north of Quetta, drained by the Pishin Lora; Pishin town was founded by the British in 1883.

Qilla Abdullah District

Population: 361,971 (2023 census)

Balochistan district of Qilla Abdullah, Gulistan and Dobandi tehsils; Chaman was separated from it as a district in 2021.

Qilla Saifullah

Qilla Saifullah District

Population: 380,200 (2023 census)

Zhob Division district with headquarters at Qilla Saifullah; the 2023 census counted 380,200 people on 6,831 km².

Quetta East District

Quetta East is one of the two districts formed when Quetta District was divided along the railway line by the 8 July 2026 notification. It comprises the Saddar, City and Sariab sub-divisions.

Quetta West District

Quetta West is one of the two districts formed when Quetta District was divided along the railway line by the 8 July 2026 notification. It comprises the Kuchlak sub-division and the newly created Brewery and Panjpai sub-divisions, with a new Brewery tehsil.

Sherani

Sherani District

Population: 191,687 (2023 census)

Zhob Division district with headquarters at Sherani; the 2023 census counted 191,687 people on 4,310 km².

Sibi

Sibi District

Population: 224,148 (2023 census)

Sevi Division district with headquarters at Sibi; the 2023 census counted 224,148 people on 7,121 km².

Sohbatpur

Sohbatpur District

Population: 240,106 (2023 census)

Naseerabad Division district with headquarters at Sohbatpur; the 2023 census counted 240,106 people on 802 km².

South Dera Bugti District

South Dera Bugti is the remainder of the former Dera Bugti District after Upper (now North) Dera Bugti was separated in 2026. The 8 July 2026 notification renamed it from Lower Dera Bugti and placed it in Sevi (Sibi) Division.

Surab

Surab District

Population: 279,038 (2023 census, 2023)

Khuzdar Division district, made a district with effect from 1 August 2017.

Tump

Tump District

Population: 203,813 (2023 census)

Tump District was carved out of Kech by a Revenue Department notification of 24 February 2026, with its headquarters at Tump. It consists of the Tump and Mand sub-divisions and tehsils, in Makuran (Makran) Division.

Usta Muhammad

Usta Muhammad District

Naseerabad Division district formed from Jafarabad; the Balochistan cabinet approved it in June 2022.

Nal

Wadh District

Wadh District was separated from Khuzdar by the 8 July 2026 notification and contains the Wadh, Ornach and Nal sub-divisions. It is part of the new Khuzdar Division.

Washuk

Washuk District

Population: 302,623 (2023 census)

Rakhshan Division district with headquarters at Washuk; the 2023 census counted 302,623 people on 33,093 km².

Zhob

Zhob District

Population: 355,692 (2023 census)

Zhob Division district on the Zhob River; its headquarters, originally Apozai, was called Fort Sandeman from 1889 until the 1970s.

Ziarat

Ziarat District

Population: 189,535 (2023 census)

Loralai Division district with headquarters at Ziarat; the 2023 census counted 189,535 people on 3,301 km².

How many districts does Balochistan have?

41 districts in 11 divisions, as of September 2026. Balochistan Revenue Department notification of 8 July 2026: 11 divisions and 41 districts (up from 8 divisions and 36 districts).

The five added districts are Quetta West (Quetta split into Quetta East and Quetta West), Wadh (from Khuzdar), Tump (from Kech, notified 24 February 2026), Barshore (from Pishin) and North Dera Bugti (Dera Bugti split into North and South Dera Bugti). The division of each district follows the only published full list (Daily Pakistan); Musakhel appears under both Zhob and Loralai in the February 2026 cabinet summary.

Sources: Dawn, 2026-07-12; Dawn, 2026-07-11; ProPakistani, 2026-07-12; ARY News, 2026-07; Daily Pakistan (via The Patriot), 2026-07-12; ProPakistani, 2026-02-25; Baldiyat Times, 2026-02-27.

Quetta District

Quetta District was divided into Quetta East and Quetta West along the railway line by the Balochistan Revenue Department notification of 8 July 2026. This page covers the undivided district; the 2023 census figures below are for the whole of it.

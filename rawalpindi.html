Rawalpindi

Potohar · code 051 · the garrison that outgrew the parade

Rawalpindi is not Islamabad’s suburb, though the highway pretends it is. It is the older city on the Potohar plateau: Gakhar restoration, Sikh market, British Northern Command, Pakistan’s General Headquarters, a bazaar that still smells of open cloth and bus exhaust, and a literacy row that looks like another country from Liaquatpur. The new capital was poured onto its northern fields in the 1960s. The old streets did not move. They thickened.

This page walks the district as a long civic essay — history, tehsils, cantonment, Taxila, the assassination park, the writers, the fast bowlers, the crush.

The 2023 sheet

After Murree and Kotli Sattian were cut away in 2022, Rawalpindi District is 5,745,964 people on about 4,547 km². Urban about seventy percent. Literacy near 83 percent (women about 78). Phone 051. Pothwari Punjabi first. Urdu thick in the city. Pashto a real minority. Hindko from the hills.

Rawalpindi tehsil — 3,744,590 people, literacy 83.97 percent. Taxila — 739,244, 81.98 percent. Gujar Khan — 781,578, 79.72 percent. Kahuta — 237,843, 84.05 percent. Kallar Syedan — 242,709, 82.23 percent.

Before the garrison

Taxila is the first city on this plateau — Bhir Mound, Sirkap, Sirsukh, Jaulian; UNESCO. Fatehpur Baori decayed after Mongol weather. In 1493 Jhanda Khan Gakhar restored it as Rawalpindi. Sikh misls took it in the 1760s; Milkha Singh invited traders. On 14 March 1849 the Sikh army laid down arms here after Gujrat. The British made a garrison.

The cantonment century

Northern Command, municipality 1867, railway to Peshawar. Partition emptied Hindu and Sikh mohallas — Kartarpura, Amarpura, Mohanpura, Arya Mohalla — and filled them from the other side of the new line. Pakistan kept GHQ here. Islamabad was designed next door. On 16 October 1951 Liaquat Ali Khan was shot in Company Bagh. The park took his name. The Rawalpindi Conspiracy sits in the same year. Ayub used this town as the real capital while the new one was scaffolding.

Streets

Saddar is the cantonment grid. Raja Bazaar is the other lung. Murree Road swallowed its gardens. Liaquat Bagh is the political ground. The cricket stadium holds Tests. Committee Chowk and Sixth Road are the working names. The Leh remembers it is a stream. Twin-city commute is the class map.

People

Jhanda Khan. Milkha Singh. Liaquat’s death. Mumtaz Mufti, Syed Zamir Jafri, Anwar Masood (Gujrat-born, Pindi stage), Colonel Muhammad Khan, Shafiq-ur-Rahman. Sheikh Rashid and Lal Haveli. Shoaib Akhtar, the Rawalpindi Express. Gordon College. Fatima Jinnah Women University. A boy from Gujar Khan who takes a commission is as typical as a boy from Raja Bazaar who takes a shop.

Issues

Congestion, winter air, June tankers, the Leh in monsoon, societies eating fields toward Gujar Khan, protocol lanes. Not katcha dacoity — ordinary city crime. Same province as Rojhan. Different century, apparently.

Islamabad was designed. Rawalpindi accumulated. Census 2023. Gazette, 1849 surrender, 1951 park, streets that still use the old mohalla names.

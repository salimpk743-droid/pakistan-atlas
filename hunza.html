Hunza District: Quick Answers

Hunza District is one of the 10 districts for which Gilgit-Baltistan's Planning & Development Department publishes statistics. The 2023 census counted 65,497 people on 10,109 km², and 86.62% of residents were literate.

Population and growth

Hunza District had 65,497 people in the 2023 census, up from 51,372 in 2017, an average annual growth rate of 4.13%. It covers 10,109 km². The census counted 11,186 households, with an average of 6 people each, and 106 males for every 100 females (GB at a Glance 2025).

How Hunza compares within Gilgit-Baltistan

Among the 10 districts in the GB statistics, Hunza ranks 9th by population, 3rd by area and 1st by literacy (86.62% in the 2023 census). Gilgit-Baltistan as a whole counted 1,709,049 people on 72,496 km² (GB at a Glance 2025).

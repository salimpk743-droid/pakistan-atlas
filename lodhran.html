Lodhran District

Multan Division · north bank of the Sutlej · phone 0608

Lodhran District: Quick Answers

Lodhran District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Lodhran. It has 3 tehsils: Dunyapur, Kahror Pacca and Lodhran. The 2023 census counted 1,928,299 people on 2,778 km², and 51.68% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Lodhran is Multan’s younger file. Until 1991 it was a tehsil of Multan District. The river on the south is the Sutlej. The name is a tribe: Lodhra. When the British cut a tehsil here on 5 May 1883, zaildar Malik Ghulam Hussain Lodhra offered the word and it stuck.

PBS 2023: 1,928,299 people on 2,778 km². Urban only 17 percent. Literacy 51.68 percent (men 60.63, women 42.12). Saraiki about 74 percent. Official: lodhran.punjab.gov.pk. Phone 0608.

Three tehsils of Lodhran District (List)

Lodhran — 1,111 km², 809,205 people, literacy 50.10 percent. City about 144,512.

Kahror Pakka — 778 km², 547,761 people, literacy 49.81 percent.

Dunyapur — 889 km², 571,333 people, literacy 55.66 percent.

Cotton and mango (including white Chaunsa). Wheat and cane fill the year.

Lodhran: Political houses

Siddique Khan Kanju — minister of state for foreign affairs under Nawaz Sharif; killed at his Kahror Pakka farmhouse in July 2001. Abdul Rehman Khan Kanju has held the NA seat and served as minister of state for overseas Pakistanis.

Jahangir Khan Tareen — agribusiness, federal industries minister, later PTI organiser; Lodhran by-elections in 2015 and 2018 became national stories. Ali Tareen has carried the surname into later races.

Siddique Khan Baloch — PML-N, 2018 by-election winner, 2024 winner from Lodhran-II.

Also on the sheet: Nawab Amanullah Khan (2002), Mirza Nasir Baig (PPP), Mian Muhammad Shafiq Arain, Zawar Hussain Warraich, Rao Sardar Ali Khan (later IGP Punjab). Yousaf Raza Gillani won Lodhran in 1985; the Gillani house remains Multan’s.

Biradari still writes the first draft of a ticket. Parties rent the draft.

Lodhran: Towns

Lodhran city, Kahror Pakka, Dunyapur. Specialist care and degrees still mean Multan.

Lodhran District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Lodhran District?

Lodhran District has 3 tehsils: Dunyapur, Kahror Pacca and Lodhran.

What is the population of Lodhran District?

The 2023 census counted 1,928,299 people in Lodhran District, up from 1,699,693 in 2017 (annual growth 2.13%). 16.86% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Lodhran District?

Lodhran District covers 2,778 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 694.13 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Lodhran District?

The 2023 census lists 4 urban localities in Lodhran District: Lodhran (144,512, municipal committee), Kahror Pacca (98,325, municipal committee), Dunyapur (51,888, municipal committee) and Dhanote (30,328, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Lodhran District?

51.68% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 60.63%, female 42.12%).

Which languages are spoken in Lodhran District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Saraiki (73.8% of the 1,926,357 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Punjabi (15.23%), Urdu (6.41%), Mewati (2.19%).

Which province is Lodhran District in?

Lodhran District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Lodhran.

Iqra Aziz biography

Iqra Aziz started young. A talent agency signed her for commercials as a teenager, and producer Momina Duraid cast her in Kissey Apna Kahein (HUM TV, 2014). Dawn's 2018 Icon interview gives Karachi as her hometown and describes how her mother raised the family alone. In a November 2017 Instagram post on her official account she said she had "turned 20", which matches the widely reported birth date of 24 November 1997.

Her lead in Muqaddas (2015) won her the HUM Award for Best TV Sensation. Through 2016–17 she worked constantly, in Choti Si Zindagi, Laaj and Khamoshi. 2018 was her breakthrough year: Suno Chanda, a Ramadan comedy opposite Farhan Saeed, and then Ranjha Ranjha Kardi, in which she plays Noori opposite Imran Ashraf. The second won her the Lux Style Award for Best TV Actress (Critics' Choice), announced in December 2020 (Images Dawn).

In 2021 she starred in two very different serials: the social drama Raqeeb Se, which won her the 8th HUM Awards jury prize, and Geo's Khuda Aur Mohabbat Season 3, opposite Feroze Khan. The second was a huge hit on YouTube, though critics were less kind.

Iqra Aziz's biggest hits

Suno Chanda (HUM TV, 2018)

As Ajiya "Jiya" Nazakat, a young woman set against an arranged marriage, she found her audience. The Lux Style Award (Viewers' Choice) followed in 2019 (The News), and so did a sequel. HUM TV's episode 1 has 92.1 million views (as of 2 October 2026).

Ranjha Ranjha Kardi (HUM TV, 2018–2019)

In Faiza Iftikhar's story she plays Noori, a woman from a family the village looks down on, opposite Imran Ashraf's Bhola. It won her the LSA Critics' Choice award (Images Dawn).

Khuda Aur Mohabbat Season 3 (Geo Entertainment, 2021)

With Feroze Khan. Its first episode has 144.8 million views on HAR PAL GEO, the largest figure for any upload checked for this guide.

Iqra Aziz new drama 2026 and upcoming projects

Her most recent serial is Paradise (Express Entertainment, 2025), with Shuja Asad. She is also in the cast of Netflix's Jo Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo. In July 2026 reports said it would be released in late 2026, but Netflix has not confirmed a date. In January 2026 ARY News reported that she and Yasir Hussain had welcomed a daughter, Sophia.

Personal life

Iqra Aziz married the actor and writer Yasir Hussain on 28 December 2019. They had got engaged in public at the 18th Lux Style Awards that July (The Express Tribune). They have a son, Kabir, born in July 2021, and a daughter, Sophia, born in January 2026.

Thatta District

Makli necropolis · UNESCO World Heritage Site · headquarters Thatta · 4 talukas · 1,083,191 people (2023 census)

Thatta District: Quick Answers

Thatta District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Thatta. It has 4 talukas: Ghorabari, Keti Bunder, Mirpur Sakro and Thatta. The 2023 census counted 1,083,191 people on 8,570 km², and 26.88% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Thatta and Makli

Thatta lies just west of the Indus River, inland from Karachi. It was the capital of the Samma dynasty of Lower Sindh. The Jama Mosque (1647–49) was built by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan, and the nearby Makli Hills are a vast necropolis with thousands of graves. The town became a municipality in 1854 (Britannica). The Historical Monuments at Makli, Thatta, were inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1981 (UNESCO).

Talukas of Thatta District

The 2023 census counted 4 talukas in Thatta District: Ghorabari, Keti Bunder, Mirpur Sakro and Thatta. Thatta is the most populous, with 444,976 people, and it is also the largest by area (3,823 km²). Literacy ranges from 12.86% in Keti Bunder to 30.9% in Thatta.

Population and growth

Thatta District had 1,083,191 people in the 2023 census, up from 982,138 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.65%). It covers 8,570 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 26th by population and 6th by area. 17.88% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 105.18 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 6 urban localities; the largest are Makli (54,137), Thatta (51,723), Gharo (40,220) and Garho (19,961). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (95.01%), followed by Urdu (1.26%) and Pashto (1.12%).

Literacy and schooling

26.88% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 30th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 33.23% and female literacy 20.12%; the urban rate was 44.2% and the rural rate 22.99%. The census counted 268,486 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (136,853 girls and 131,620 boys).

Thatta District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Thatta District?

Thatta District has 4 talukas: Ghorabari, Keti Bunder, Mirpur Sakro and Thatta.

What is the population of Thatta District?

The 2023 census counted 1,083,191 people in Thatta District, up from 982,138 in 2017 (annual growth 1.65%). 17.88% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Thatta District?

Thatta District covers 8,570 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 126.39 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Thatta District?

The 2023 census lists 6 urban localities in Thatta District: Makli (54,137, town committee), Thatta (51,723, municipal committee), Gharo (40,220, town committee), Garho (19,961, town committee), Ghorabari (15,184, town committee) and Mirpur Sakro (12,454, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Thatta District?

26.88% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 33.23%, female 20.12%).

Which languages are spoken in Thatta District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (95.01% of the 1,083,191 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (1.26%), Pashto (1.12%).

Which province is Thatta District in?

Thatta District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Thatta.

Is Makli a UNESCO World Heritage Site?

Yes. The Historical Monuments at Makli, Thatta, were inscribed on the World Heritage List in 1981. (UNESCO World Heritage Centre)

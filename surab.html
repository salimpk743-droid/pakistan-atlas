Surab District: Quick Answers

Surab District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Khuzdar Division, with its headquarters at Surab. The 2023 census lists 4 administrative units in it: Dasht E Goran, Gidder, Shaheed Meharabad Zehri and Surab. The 2023 census counted 279,038 people (2023 boundaries) on 762 km², and 37.44% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

District status

The Balochistan government notified Surab (named Shaheed Sikandarabad) and Duki as new districts, effective from 1 August 2017 (Daily Azadi).

Administrative units of Surab District

The 2023 census counted 4 administrative units in Surab District: Dasht E Goran (sub-division), Gidder (sub-division), Shaheed Meharabad Zehri (sub-division) and Surab (sub-division). Surab is the most populous, with 95,469 people, and it is also the largest by area (233 km²). Literacy ranges from 34.34% in Surab to 42.85% in Shaheed Meharabad Zehri.

Population and growth

Surab District had 279,038 people in the 2023 census, up from 208,493 in 2017 (average annual growth 4.99%). It covers 762 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 18th by population and 34th by area. 13.07% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.11 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Surab (36,468). Brahui was the most common mother tongue (96.75%), followed by Balochi (2.9%).

Literacy and schooling

37.44% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 18th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 45.16% and female literacy 29.58%; the urban rate was 47.4% and the rural rate 35.97%. The census counted 69,427 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (36,491 girls and 32,933 boys).

Surab District: FAQ

What is the population of Surab District?

The 2023 census counted 279,038 people in Surab District (2023 boundaries), up from 208,493 in 2017 (annual growth 4.99%). 13.07% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Surab District?

Surab District covers 762 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 366.19 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Surab District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Surab District: Surab (36,468, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Surab District?

37.44% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 45.16%, female 29.58%).

Which languages are spoken in Surab District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Brahui (96.75% of the 279,038 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Balochi (2.9%).

Which province is Surab District in?

Surab District is in Balochistan, in Khuzdar Division. Its headquarters is Surab.

When did Surab become a district?

It was notified as a district, named Shaheed Sikandarabad, effective 1 August 2017. (Daily Azadi)

Hospitals & public health

Every district should list its DHQ, THQ and teaching hospitals — with no medical advice on this site.

Health is a provincial subject after the 18th Amendment. That is why service quality looks different in Lahore, Turbat and Hunza. For the atlas, keep three layers:

  • Teaching hospitals attached to medical universities (Mayo, Jinnah, Lady Reading, Civil Hospital Quetta, PIMS).
  • DHQ hospitals at district headquarters.
  • THQ / RHC / BHU closer to villages.

What the data already shows

Punjab has the thickest hospital network. Sindh’s capacity is concentrated in Karachi and Hyderabad. KP improved immunisation but mountain districts still send serious cases to Peshawar. Balochistan’s distances mean an ambulance ride can last half a day. These are planning facts, not insults.

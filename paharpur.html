Paharpur District

New district (November 2025) · carved out of Dera Ismail Khan · headquarters Paharpur · tehsils Paharpur and Paniyala

Paharpur District: Quick Answers

2023 census
The census counted Paharpur tehsil under Dera Ismail Khan District: 406,467 people on 1,657 km². No separate district total was published.

How Paharpur District was created

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government created Paharpur District by dividing Dera Ismail Khan District. According to the notification, the new district is made up of Paharpur and Paniyala tehsils, which had previously been a sub-division of Dera Ismail Khan, and Paharpur town is the district headquarters. The provincial cabinet approved the district at its 39th meeting on 2 October 2025, and APP reported the notification on 26 November 2025 (APP).

Population (2023 census)

The 2023 census was taken before the district existed and has no Paniyala tehsil. It counted Paharpur tehsil, then part of Dera Ismail Khan, at 406,467 people on 1,657 km², and 21.58% of them were urban. PBS has not published a separate total for the new district.

Paharpur District: FAQ

When was Paharpur District created?

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa cabinet approved Paharpur District on 2 October 2025, and APP reported the notification on 26 November 2025. The district was carved out of Dera Ismail Khan. (Associated Press of Pakistan)

How many tehsils does Paharpur District have?

Two: Paharpur and Paniyala. The headquarters is Paharpur town. (Associated Press of Pakistan)

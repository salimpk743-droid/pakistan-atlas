Washuk District: Quick Answers

Washuk District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Rakhshan Division, with its headquarters at Washuk. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Besima, Mashkhel, Nag, Shahoo Garhi and Washuk. The 2023 census counted 302,623 people on 33,093 km², and 21.58% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Washuk District

The 2023 census counted 5 administrative units in Washuk District: Besima (sub-division), Mashkhel (sub-division), Nag (sub-tehsil), Shahoo Garhi (sub-tehsil) and Washuk (sub-division). Mashkhel is the most populous, with 67,142 people, and it is also the largest by area (11,663 km²). Literacy ranges from 16.33% in Mashkhel to 31.83% in Besima.

Population and growth

Washuk District had 302,623 people in the 2023 census, up from 175,712 in 2017 (average annual growth 9.51%). It covers 33,093 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 17th by population and 3rd by area. 13.58% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 116.63 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Washuk (41,107). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (68.29%), followed by Brahui (31.47%).

Literacy and schooling

21.58% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 34th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 26.76% and female literacy 15.76%; the urban rate was 19.65% and the rural rate 21.86%. The census counted 84,010 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (39,772 girls and 44,230 boys).

Washuk District: FAQ

What is the population of Washuk District?

The 2023 census counted 302,623 people in Washuk District, up from 175,712 in 2017 (annual growth 9.51%). 13.58% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Washuk District?

Washuk District covers 33,093 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 9.14 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Washuk District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Washuk District: Washuk (41,107, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Washuk District?

21.58% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 26.76%, female 15.76%).

Which languages are spoken in Washuk District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (68.29% of the 302,367 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Brahui (31.47%).

Which province is Washuk District in?

Washuk District is in Balochistan, in Rakhshan Division. Its headquarters is Washuk.

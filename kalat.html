Kalat District

Seat of the Khans of Kalat · headquarters Kalat · 4 census units · 271,560 people (2023 census)

Kalat District: Quick Answers

Kalat District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Khuzdar Division, with its headquarters at Kalat. The 2023 census lists 4 administrative units in it: Gazg, Johan, Kalat and Mangochar. The 2023 census counted 271,560 people on 7,654 km², and 39.7% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Kalat town and the Khanate

Kalat stands on a spur of the Shas-i-Mardan hill at the western end of the Kalat valley. Captured by the Mirwaris in the 15th century, it was the capital of the Ahmadzai khans until the British occupation of 1839; the town was returned to the khan in 1842. The old town inside the citadel, with the khans' palace, was partly destroyed by the 1935 earthquake. The region became part of Pakistan in 1948 (Britannica).

Administrative units of Kalat District

The 2023 census counted 4 administrative units in Kalat District: Gazg (sub-tehsil), Johan (sub-tehsil), Kalat (sub-division) and Mangochar (sub-division). Kalat is the most populous, with 167,405 people, and it is also the largest by area (3,788 km²). Literacy ranges from 18.75% in Gazg to 44.19% in Kalat.

Population and growth

Kalat District had 271,560 people in the 2023 census, up from 203,565 in 2017 (average annual growth 4.93%). It covers 7,654 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 20th by population and 12th by area. 16.36% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.26 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Kalat (44,440). Brahui was the most common mother tongue (73.22%), followed by Balochi (25.53%).

Literacy and schooling

39.7% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 16th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 49.54% and female literacy 29.41%; the urban rate was 68.49% and the rural rate 33.94%. The census counted 52,253 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (31,092 girls and 21,157 boys).

Kalat District: FAQ

What is the population of Kalat District?

The 2023 census counted 271,560 people in Kalat District, up from 203,565 in 2017 (annual growth 4.93%). 16.36% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Kalat District?

Kalat District covers 7,654 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 35.48 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Kalat District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Kalat District: Kalat (44,440, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Kalat District?

39.7% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 49.54%, female 29.41%).

Which languages are spoken in Kalat District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Brahui (73.22% of the 271,560 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Balochi (25.53%).

Which province is Kalat District in?

Kalat District is in Balochistan, in Khuzdar Division. Its headquarters is Kalat.

When did Kalat join Pakistan?

The Kalat region became part of Pakistan in 1948. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

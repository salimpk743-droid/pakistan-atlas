Aap Ki Izzat Drama: Cast and Story

Aap Ki Izzat (آپ کی عزت) is a family and marriage drama on HUM TV, written by Umera Ahmed and directed by Saife Hassan, starring Ahsan Khan, Urwa Hocane.

ChannelHUM TV

Year2026

Aap Ki Izzat Story

Aap Ki Izzat (Your Honour) is a 2026 HUM TV serial about a marriage tested by love, dignity, trust and the pressures that enter a relationship from outside. Ahsan Khan plays Tabish and Urwa Hocane plays Beena.

The story asks what happens when affection collides with questions of honour and respect within a family. Senior actors Sania Saeed and Nauman Masood lead the supporting cast, and the serial also features Saif Ali Khan, the Pakistani television personality who shares the name of the Indian film star.

Writer, Director and Production of Aap Ki Izzat

The drama is written by Umera Ahmed, the novelist and screenwriter behind Zindagi Gulzar Hai, and directed by Saife Hassan, whose credits include Zard Patton Ka Bunn. It is produced by Nina Kashif and Momina Duraid Productions.

Producer / production house: Nina Kashif and Momina Duraid Productions.

When and Where to Watch Aap Ki Izzat

Aap Ki Izzat airs on HUM TV, Friday and Saturday, 8:00 pm (repeat Saturday and Sunday, 9:00 am) (Pakistan time). Schedules and repeat timings change, so check the official HUM TV website for the latest episode and timing. Channels also upload episodes to their official YouTube channels.

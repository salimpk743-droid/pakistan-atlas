Districts of Gilgit-Baltistan

Gilgit-Baltistan has 14 districts (September 2026). An alphabetical list with headquarters, 2023 census population and tehsils where the census publishes them.

Gilgit-Baltistan districts, A–Z

Astore District

Gilgit-Baltistan district; the 2023 census counted 111,573 people on 5,411 km², with literacy of 66.4%.

Darel District

Diamer division district notified in June 2019 from the Darel sub-division, with unchanged boundaries.

Diamer District

Gilgit-Baltistan district; the 2023 census counted 337,329 people on 7,234 km², with literacy of 30.95%.

Ghanche District

Gilgit-Baltistan district; the 2023 census counted 157,822 people on 8,531 km², with literacy of 69.57%.

Ghizer District

Gilgit-Baltistan district; the 2023 census counted 200,069 people on 12,381 km², with literacy of 70.28%.

Gilgit District

Gilgit-Baltistan district; the 2023 census counted 324,552 people on 4,208 km², with literacy of 73.13%.

Gupis-Yasin District

Gilgit division district notified in June 2019 from the Gupis-Yasin sub-division, with unchanged boundaries.

Hunza District

Gilgit-Baltistan district; the 2023 census counted 65,497 people on 10,109 km², with literacy of 86.62%.

Kharmang District

Nagar District

Roundu District

Baltistan division district notified in June 2019 from the Rondu sub-division, with unchanged boundaries.

Shigar District

Skardu District

Tangir District

Diamer division district notified in June 2019 from the Tangir sub-division, with unchanged boundaries.

How many districts does Gilgit-Baltistan have?

14 districts in 3 divisions, as of September 2026. Gilgit-Baltistan government notification of June 2019 (four new districts, 14 in total).

Sources: Dawn, 2019-06.

Sources and editorial method

Population, area and tehsil figures are taken from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Table 1. Districts created after the census are marked, and no figure is shown where no official number exists.

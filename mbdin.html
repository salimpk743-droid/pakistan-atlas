Mandi Bahauddin District: Quick Answers

Mandi Bahauddin District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Mandi Bahauddin. It has 3 tehsils: Malakwal, Mandi Bahauddin and Phalia. The 2023 census counted 1,829,486 people on 2,673 km², and 70.27% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Mandi Bahauddin District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Mandi Bahauddin District?

Mandi Bahauddin District has 3 tehsils: Malakwal, Mandi Bahauddin and Phalia.

What is the population of Mandi Bahauddin District?

The 2023 census counted 1,829,486 people in Mandi Bahauddin District, up from 1,594,039 in 2017 (annual growth 2.33%). 18.92% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Mandi Bahauddin District?

Mandi Bahauddin District covers 2,673 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 684.43 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Mandi Bahauddin District?

The 2023 census lists 3 urban localities in Mandi Bahauddin District: Mandi Bahauddin (232,361, municipal committee), Phalia (62,453, municipal committee) and Malakwal (51,327, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Mandi Bahauddin District?

70.27% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 74.89%, female 65.7%).

Which languages are spoken in Mandi Bahauddin District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (93.2% of the 1,829,152 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (4.57%), Pashto (1.77%).

Which province is Mandi Bahauddin District in?

Mandi Bahauddin District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Mandi Bahauddin.

List of Prime Ministers of Pakistan

From Liaquat Ali Khan in 1947 to Shehbaz Sharif today: every term, with official dates.

Pakistan has had 28 prime ministers since 1947, counting caretakers. The National Assembly's official list has 33 entries, because several people held the office more than once. The first prime minister was Liaquat Ali Khan (from 15 August 1947). The current prime minister is Shehbaz Sharif, in office since 3 March 2024.

Gaps in the list

The list has three long gaps. There was no prime minister from 7 October 1958 to 7 December 1971, from 5 July 1977 to 23 March 1985, or from 12 October 1999 to 23 November 2002. During the first gap the country was led by Presidents Ayub Khan and Yahya Khan. The second gap began when Zulfikar Ali Bhutto's term ended on 5 July 1977, and Zia-ul-Haq was president from 1978. The third gap began when Nawaz Sharif's term ended on 12 October 1999. See the list of presidents for these periods.

Caretaker prime ministers

Caretaker prime ministers run the government between the dissolution of the National Assembly and the formation of a new government after an election. 8 caretakers have served: Ghulam Mustafa Jatoi, Balakh Sher Mazari, Moeenuddin Ahmad Qureshi (Moin Qureshi), Malik Meraj Khalid, Muhammad Mian Soomro, Mir Hazar Khan Khoso, Nasirul Mulk, Anwaar-ul-Haq Kakar. The National Assembly list marks the first seven as caretakers. Anwaar-ul-Haq Kakar was sworn in as the eighth caretaker prime minister on 14 August 2023 (Dawn).

FAQ

Who was the first prime minister of Pakistan?

Liaquat Ali Khan, who took office on 15 August 1947 and served until 16 October 1951.

Who is the current prime minister of Pakistan?

Shehbaz Sharif. His second term began on 3 March 2024. His first term ran from 11 April 2022 to 14 August 2023.

Who was the first woman prime minister of Pakistan?

Benazir Bhutto, who took office on 2 December 1988. She served a second term from 19 October 1993 to 5 November 1996.

How many times has Nawaz Sharif been prime minister?

Nawaz Sharif appears 4 times in the National Assembly's list: 1990–1993, May–July 1993, 1997–1999 and 2013–2017.

How many prime ministers has Pakistan had?

28 different people, including caretakers, across 33 terms listed by the National Assembly. Excluding caretakers, 20 people have held the office.

Qambar Shahdadkot District: Quick Answers

Qambar Shahdadkot District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Qambar. It has 7 talukas: Kambar Ali Khan, Miro Khan, Nasirabad, Qubo Saeed Khan, Shahdad Kot, Sujawal Junejo and Warah. The 2023 census counted 1,514,869 people on 5,475 km², and 40.02% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Qambar Shahdadkot District

The 2023 census counted 7 talukas in Qambar Shahdadkot District: Kambar Ali Khan, Miro Khan, Nasirabad, Qubo Saeed Khan, Shahdad Kot, Sujawal Junejo and Warah. Kambar Ali Khan is the most populous, with 448,990 people, and it is also the largest by area (2,260 km²). Literacy ranges from 26.46% in Qubo Saeed Khan to 47.06% in Shahdad Kot.

Population and growth

Qambar Shahdadkot District had 1,514,869 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,338,035 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.1%). It covers 5,475 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 20th by population and 11th by area. 27.85% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 109.31 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 9 urban localities; the largest are Shahdad Kot (120,687), Kambar (112,313), Warah (38,394) and Nasirabad (38,105). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (96.21%), followed by Brahui (2.58%).

Literacy and schooling

40.02% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 22nd of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 48.94% and female literacy 30.35%; the urban rate was 50.85% and the rural rate 35.65%. The census counted 280,556 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (144,692 girls and 135,855 boys).

Qambar Shahdadkot District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Qambar Shahdadkot District?

Qambar Shahdadkot District has 7 talukas: Kambar Ali Khan, Miro Khan, Nasirabad, Qubo Saeed Khan, Shahdad Kot, Sujawal Junejo and Warah.

What is the population of Qambar Shahdadkot District?

The 2023 census counted 1,514,869 people in Qambar Shahdadkot District, up from 1,338,035 in 2017 (annual growth 2.1%). 27.85% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Qambar Shahdadkot District?

What are the main towns and cities in Qambar Shahdadkot District?

Which languages are spoken in Qambar Shahdadkot District?

Which province is Qambar Shahdadkot District in?

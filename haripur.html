Haripur District: Quick Answers

Haripur District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Haripur. It has 3 tehsils: Ghazi, Haripur and Khanpur. The 2023 census counted 1,174,783 people on 1,725 km², and 74.88% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Haripur District

The 2023 census counted 3 tehsils in Haripur District: Ghazi, Haripur and Khanpur. Haripur is the most populous, with 836,058 people, and it is also the largest by area (834 km²). Literacy ranges from 69.69% in Ghazi to 76.07% in Haripur.

Population and growth

Haripur District had 1,174,783 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,001,515 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.7%). It covers 1,725 km². Of the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the census, it ranks 14th by population and 21st by area. 12.58% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 101.43 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Haripur (91,915) and Khalabat (55,850). Hindko was the most common mother tongue (80.32%), followed by Pashto (14.7%), Urdu (2%) and Punjabi (1.01%).

Literacy and schooling

74.88% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 2nd of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 84.13% and female literacy 65.61%; the urban rate was 85.39% and the rural rate 73.31%. The census counted 43,838 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (24,148 girls and 19,690 boys).

How many tehsils are in Haripur District?

What is the population of Haripur District?

The 2023 census counted 1,174,783 people in Haripur District, up from 1,001,515 in 2017 (annual growth 2.7%). 12.58% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Haripur District?

What are the main towns and cities in Haripur District?

What is the literacy rate of Haripur District?

Which languages are spoken in Haripur District?

Which province is Haripur District in?

Haripur District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Haripur.

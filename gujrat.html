Gujrat District

Between the Chenab and Jhelum · headquarters Gujrat · 3 tehsils · 3,219,375 people (2023 census)

Gujrat District: Quick Answers

Gujrat District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Gujrat. It has 3 tehsils: Gujrat, Kharian and Sarai Alamgir. The 2023 census counted 3,219,375 people on 3,192 km², and 81.37% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Gujrat city: history

Gujrat lies just north of the Chenab River on the Grand Trunk Road. The present city grew around a fort built by the Mughal emperor Akbar in 1580 and became a municipality in 1867. The Battle of Gujrat in 1849 broke Sikh power and allowed the British to annex the Punjab. The area lies between the Chenab and Jhelum rivers, and a mound at Mong has been identified as the site of Alexandria Nicaea, founded by Alexander the Great after his victory over Porus (Britannica).

Tehsils of Gujrat District

The 2023 census counted 3 tehsils in Gujrat District: Gujrat, Kharian and Sarai Alamgir. Gujrat is the most populous, with 1,746,173 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,463 km²). Literacy ranges from 79.69% in Kharian to 82.48% in Gujrat.

Population and growth

Gujrat District had 3,219,375 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,756,289 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.63%). It covers 3,192 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 18th by population and 27th by area. 41.13% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 99.83 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (93.47%), followed by Urdu (3.48%) and Pashto (2.16%).

Literacy and schooling

81.37% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 2nd of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 84.81% and female literacy 77.95%; the urban rate was 84.19% and the rural rate 79.39%. The census counted 118,204 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (50,567 girls and 67,627 boys).

Rahim Yar Khan District

Headquarters: Rahim Yar Khan · Census 2023 — see PBS · Punjab

Southern-most large district of Punjab, on the road to Sindh.

Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is Rahim Yar Khan. The available public data note is Census 2023 — see PBS. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Education

Khawaja Fareed University of Engineering and Information Technology and medical college.

Hospitals and healthcare

Sheikh Zayed Medical College / Hospital.

Culture and geography

Saraiki and desert-edge towns including Sadiqabad and Khanpur.

Public issues

Heat, and stretching specialists across a long district.

Villages and local places

Liaquatpur, Khanpur villages

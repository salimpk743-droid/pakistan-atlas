Pakistani Drama Writers

A reference directory connecting Pakistani drama writers with the serials, stories and characters they created.

Featured Writers

Farhat Ishtiaq

Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum — ARY Digital. Also known for Humsafar and other television work.

Hashim Nadeem

Parizaad — based on his novel. HUM TV identifies him as the writer.

Nooran Makhdoom

Tere Bin — Geo's official programme page credits Nooran Makhdoom as writer.

Umera Ahmed

Zindagi Gulzar Hai — based on her novel and associated with one of HUM TV's landmark dramas.

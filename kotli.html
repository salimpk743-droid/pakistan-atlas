Kotli

A land of green hills, warm springs, quiet valleys and historic routes — where the softer landscapes of southern Kashmir meet forests, streams and mountain horizons.

Kotli District • Azad Jammu & Kashmir • Pakistan

Region Azad Jammu & Kashmir
District HQ Kotli
Area 1,862 km²
2017 Population 774,000

Kotli District is a beautiful hilly district in the southern part of Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Its landscape rises gradually from the lower country toward the higher mountains of Poonch, creating a transition of green hills, forests, valleys, streams and mountain viewpoints.

The district is especially known for Tatta Pani, famous for its sulphur hot springs, as well as Khoi Ratta, Teenda and a number of scenic hill and valley locations. (ajkcs.gov.pk)

According to the AJK District Profiles 2022, Kotli covers approximately 1,862 square kilometres and had a 2017 census population of approximately 774,000. The district contains six tehsils and 36 union councils. (pndajk.gov.pk)

“Kotli is Kashmir in a quieter mood — green hills, warm springs, winding roads and peaceful valleys stretching toward the mountains.”

Southern Kashmir

Kotli: Where the Hills Rise Toward Kashmir's Mountains

The official AJK Tourism Department describes Kotli as a hilly area that rises gradually toward the higher mountains of Poonch. Its sub-mountainous topography also gives it a more moderate climate than neighbouring Mirpur. (ajkcs.gov.pk)

This gradual rise is one of the most interesting features of the district. The landscape does not suddenly become alpine. Instead, the hills slowly become higher and more rugged as the journey continues toward the interior of AJK.

Green slopes, villages, agricultural land, wooded hills and winding roads create the visual character of Kotli.

For travellers who prefer peaceful landscapes rather than crowded tourist centres, this slower rhythm is part of Kotli's charm.

Natural Wonder

Kotli: Tatta Pani — The Warm Springs of Kashmir

Tatta Pani is one of the best-known natural attractions associated with Kotli and the wider southern AJK region.

The name literally refers to hot water, and the destination is famous for its naturally warm sulphur springs. The springs are situated beside the River Poonch and are easily accessible from Kotli. (ajkcs.gov.pk)

The AJK Tourism Department records Tatta Pani as approximately 26 kilometres from Kotli and notes its popularity for hot-water bathing. It also records tourist facilities including a Tourist Inn and bathing rooms. (ajkcs.gov.pk)

The combination of naturally warm water, river scenery and surrounding hills gives Tatta Pani a character unlike ordinary mountain viewpoints.

Kotli: Why Tatta Pani Is So Special

Many mountain destinations are defined by peaks, lakes or waterfalls. Tatta Pani is different.

Here, the attraction comes from the earth itself. Warm sulphur water emerges naturally in a landscape shaped by the River Poonch and surrounding hills.

This creates a memorable travel experience: visitors can experience the warmth of the springs while being surrounded by the green mountain environment of Kashmir.

A Natural Wellness Tradition

The AJK Tourism Department notes that Tatta Pani has traditionally attracted visitors for hot-water bathing and records claims of its use for skin and rheumatic ailments. Such traditional wellness claims should not be treated as a substitute for medical treatment. (ajkcs.gov.pk)

Kotli: Khoi Ratta — A Mountain Escape

Khoi Ratta is another important tourism destination of Kotli District.

The official AJK Tourism Department describes it as a remarkable attraction and records a Tourism Department rest house for visitors. The older official tourism profile places the destination at approximately 2,570 metres above sea level. (ajkcs.gov.pk)

At this elevation, the landscape becomes significantly more mountainous, offering a different experience from the lower parts of Kotli.

The surrounding hills, open views and cooler mountain environment make Khoi Ratta an attractive destination for travellers looking for a quieter highland experience.

Viewpoint

Kotli: Teenda — A View Across the Hills

Teenda is a scenic viewpoint close to Kotli city.

The official tourism department describes Teenda as a viewpoint connected by a metalled road and located approximately six kilometres from Kotli. A Tourism Department rest house is also listed there. (ajkcs.gov.pk)

Its importance is simple: it provides an elevated perspective over the surrounding landscape without requiring a long journey into the mountains.

Kotli City — The Heart of the District

Kotli is the district headquarters and the main urban centre of the region.

The city serves as an important connection point for travellers moving toward different parts of southern and central AJK.

The official tourism department records basic visitor facilities in Kotli, including accommodation and services for travellers. (ajkcs.gov.pk)

From the city, routes extend toward Tatta Pani, Teenda, Khoi Ratta and other mountain destinations.

Kotli on the Historic Routes of Kashmir

Kotli's geographical importance is not only modern.

The AJK Archaeology Department describes an ancient route from the Jhelum region through Mirpur, Chaumukh, Bihari, Sansar, Kotli, Saria and onward toward Poonch. (archaeology.ajk.gov.pk)

This places Kotli within the wider historical network of routes connecting the plains with the mountain regions of Kashmir.

Long-distance movement through these landscapes helped shape the cultural geography of southern AJK.

A Landscape of Movement

Roads and mountain passes were essential to Kashmir's history. Kotli's position along one of these historic routes helps explain why the district developed as an important settlement and connection point.

Kotli: Forests & Green Mountains

One of Kotli's greatest visual strengths is its greenery.

As the land rises toward the mountain areas, wooded slopes and vegetation become increasingly prominent. The green landscape gives Kotli the soft, peaceful appearance associated with the lower and middle mountain regions of Kashmir.

Forests are also important ecological assets. They help protect slopes, regulate water systems and provide habitat for wildlife.

For visitors, they create shade, texture and depth across the landscape — especially when viewed from elevated roads and viewpoints.

The River Poonch & Kotli's Water Landscapes

Water is an important part of Kotli's identity, particularly around Tatta Pani and the River Poonch.

The river creates a natural corridor through the hills and gives the landscape movement. In combination with green slopes and settlements, it creates some of the most peaceful scenery in southern AJK.

The relationship between river and hills is especially noticeable around Tatta Pani, where the hot springs add another geological dimension to the landscape.

Culture & Life in Kotli

Kotli is a predominantly rural district in which village life, agriculture, family networks and local traditions remain important.

The 2022 AJK District Profile records approximately 89.8 percent of the district's 2017 population as rural, highlighting the strong rural character of Kotli. (pndajk.gov.pk)

This rural character is visible in the landscape: cultivated fields, scattered homes, trees and small settlements spread across the hills.

Kotli for Photography

Kotli is ideal for photographers who enjoy natural landscapes without the overwhelming scale of high alpine scenery.

The district offers layers: green foreground hills, wooded slopes, distant mountains, villages and winding roads.

Tatta Pani adds the River Poonch and hot-spring environment, while Khoi Ratta offers higher mountain perspectives. Teenda provides an accessible viewpoint close to Kotli city.

In spring and summer, the landscape becomes intensely green. During rainy weather, clouds can settle over the hills and create a dramatic atmosphere.

Kotli Through the Seasons

Spring — Fresh Green Hills

Spring brings fresh vegetation and renewed colour to the valleys and hills. The landscape becomes particularly attractive for scenic drives and outdoor exploration.

Summer — The Green Season

Summer brings lush vegetation to the hills and is a popular period for exploring the greener landscapes of Kotli.

Monsoon — Streams Come Alive

Rain can strengthen the flow of streams and waterfalls and cover the hills in deeper shades of green.

Autumn — Softer Mountain Colours

Autumn offers pleasant scenery and can be especially attractive for photography and exploring the countryside.

Winter — Warm Springs & Cool Hills

Cooler weather makes the contrast at Tatta Pani especially interesting: warm spring water within a cool mountain environment.

Why Kotli Matters

Kotli may not be the first name people mention when they think of Kashmir, but that is exactly what makes it worth discovering.

It represents a quieter and greener side of Azad Jammu & Kashmir: rolling hills, forests, villages, streams and mountain roads gradually rising toward the higher country of Poonch.

Its greatest natural attraction, Tatta Pani, offers something completely different from the usual Kashmir experience — naturally warm sulphur springs beside the River Poonch.

Higher destinations such as Khoi Ratta reveal another side of the district, while Teenda provides a scenic viewpoint close to Kotli city.

Beneath all of this lies a much older story. Historic routes connecting the Jhelum region, Mirpur, Kotli and Poonch demonstrate that this landscape has long been a corridor through Kashmir. (archaeology.ajk.gov.pk)

Kotli is therefore more than a district on the map. It is green southern Kashmir — a place of hills, hot springs, rivers, villages and historic journeys.

کوٹلی کے موسم

بہار

بہار میں پہاڑ اور وادیاں تازہ سبز رنگ اختیار کر لیتے ہیں اور قدرتی مناظر بہت خوبصورت ہو جاتے ہیں۔

گرمی

گرمیوں میں سبزہ نمایاں ہوتا ہے اور پہاڑی علاقوں کی طرف سفر خوشگوار منظر پیش کرتا ہے۔

برسات

بارش کے بعد ندی نالے زیادہ خوبصورت دکھائی دیتے ہیں اور پہاڑ مزید گہرے سبز رنگ میں نظر آ سکتے ہیں۔

خزاں

خزاں میں موسم نسبتاً خوشگوار اور دیہی علاقوں کی سیر کے لیے موزوں ہو سکتا ہے۔

سردی

سرد موسم میں تتہ پانی کے گرم چشموں کا تجربہ خاص طور پر منفرد محسوس ہو سکتا ہے۔

کوٹلی کیوں خاص ہے؟

کوٹلی شاید کشمیر کا پہلا نام نہ ہو جو ہر شخص کے ذہن میں آئے، لیکن یہی چیز اسے دریافت کرنے کے قابل بناتی ہے۔

یہ آزاد کشمیر کا ایک ایسا سبز اور پُرسکون حصہ ہے جہاں نچلے پہاڑ آہستہ آہستہ بلند علاقوں کی طرف بڑھتے ہیں، راستے گاؤں اور جنگلات سے گزرتے ہیں اور دریا وادیوں کو زندگی دیتے ہیں۔

تتہ پانی اسے ایک منفرد قدرتی شناخت دیتا ہے، جبکہ کھوئی رٹہ اور ٹینڈہ اس کے پہاڑی حسن کو نمایاں کرتے ہیں۔

اس کے نیچے ایک اور بڑی کہانی موجود ہے — قدیم راستے جو میرپور، کوٹلی اور پونچھ کو کشمیر کے وسیع تاریخی جغرافیے سے جوڑتے تھے۔ (archaeology.ajk.gov.pk)

اسی لیے کوٹلی صرف ایک ضلع نہیں بلکہ سبز جنوبی کشمیر، گرم چشموں، پہاڑی راستوں، دریاؤں اور پرسکون دیہی زندگی کی ایک خوبصورت تصویر ہے۔

ذمہ دار سیاحت

  • قدرتی چشموں اور دریاؤں کو صاف رکھیں۔
  • کچرا پہاڑوں، جنگلات یا ندی نالوں میں نہ پھینکیں۔
  • جنگلات اور قدرتی ماحول کو نقصان نہ پہنچائیں۔
  • مقامی لوگوں اور نجی زمین کا احترام کریں۔
  • مذہبی اور ثقافتی مقامات کا احترام کریں۔
  • مقامی گائیڈز، ہوٹلوں اور کاروبار کو سپورٹ کریں۔
  • بارش اور پہاڑی موسم کے دوران سفر سے پہلے سڑکوں اور موسم کی تازہ صورتحال ضرور معلوم کریں۔

Kotli District: Quick Answers

Kotli District is one of the 10 districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, in Mirpur Division. The 2017 census counted 774,017 people on 1,862 km², in 5 tehsils: Kotli, Khuiratta, Charhoi/Darlia Jattan, Sehnsa and Fatehpur Thakyala.

Kotli District: FAQ

What is the population of Kotli District?

The 2017 census counted 774,017 people. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects about 0.856 million for 2023. (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

How many tehsils are in Kotli District?

The 2017 census tables list 5: Kotli, Khuiratta, Charhoi/Darlia Jattan, Sehnsa and Fatehpur Thakyala. (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

Which division is Kotli District in?

Mirpur Division. AJK has three divisions (Muzaffarabad, Poonch and Mirpur) and 10 districts. (AJ&K Bureau of Statistics)

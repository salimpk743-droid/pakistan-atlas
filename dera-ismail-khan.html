Dera Ismail Khan District

Division headquarters on the Indus · 2023 census: 1,829,811 people in 6 tehsils · Paharpur made a separate district in November 2025

Dera Ismail Khan District: Quick Answers

Dera Ismail Khan District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at D.I. Khan. The 2023 census lists 6 administrative units in it: Daraban, Dera Ismail Khan, Drazanda, Kulachi, Paharpur and Paroa. The 2023 census counted 1,829,811 people (pre-split boundaries) on 9,334 km², and 46.58% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Dera Ismail Khan District

The 2023 census counted six tehsils in Dera Ismail Khan District: Daraban, Dera Ismail Khan, Drazanda, Kulachi, Paharpur and Paroa. The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government then created Paharpur District out of Dera Ismail Khan. The new district is made up of Paharpur and Paniyala tehsils, with Paharpur town as its headquarters. The provincial cabinet approved the move on 2 October 2025, and APP reported the notification on 26 November 2025 (APP). In 2023, Paharpur tehsil had 406,467 people, so the district's current population is lower than the 2023 total shown on this page.

Population and literacy (2023 census, pre-split boundaries)

The district as enumerated in 2023 had 1,829,811 people on 9,334 km². Its literacy rate was 46.58%: 58.14% for males and 33.71% for females. Literacy by tehsil is shown in the census table above.

History of Dera Ismail Khan

The town is named after Ismail Khan, son of the 15th-century Baloch chief who founded it. The Indus River washed away the old town, 6 km to the east, in 1823. The present town was laid out by Durrani chiefs and became a municipality in 1867 (Britannica).

Geography, economy and how to reach

Dera Ismail Khan lies just west of the Indus River. A bridge across the river connects it to Darya Khan, 19 km to the east, and the town is a transport junction. The main crops of the surrounding area are wheat, millet, gram and sorghum, and camels and sheep are bred widely. Gomal University opened in the town in 1974 (Britannica).

Dera Ismail Khan District: FAQ

What is the population of Dera Ismail Khan District?

The 2023 census counted 1,829,811 people in Dera Ismail Khan District (pre-split boundaries), up from 1,693,594 in 2017 (annual growth 1.3%). 20.48% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Dera Ismail Khan District?

Dera Ismail Khan District covers 9,334 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 196.04 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Dera Ismail Khan District?

The 2023 census lists 6 urban localities in Dera Ismail Khan District: Dera Ismail Khan (213,756, municipal committee), Paharpur (76,027, municipal committee), Paroa (42,005, municipal committee), Kulachi (24,481, municipal committee), Panyala (11,669, town committee) and Dera Ismail Khan Cantonment (6,819, cantonment).

What is the literacy rate of Dera Ismail Khan District?

46.58% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 58.14%, female 33.71%).

Which languages are spoken in Dera Ismail Khan District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Saraiki (65.77% of the 1,822,916 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Pashto (31.97%), Urdu (1.71%).

Which province is Dera Ismail Khan District in?

Dera Ismail Khan District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is D.I. Khan.

Floodplain district · two rivers · three tehsils left on the file

Muzaffargarh District: Quick Answers

Muzaffargarh District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Muzaffargarh. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Alipur, Chowk Sarwar Shaheed, Jatoi, Kot Addu and Muzaffargarh. The 2023 census counted 5,015,325 people (pre-split boundaries) on 8,249 km², and 47.99% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Write this district as a water year, not as a slogan. The Chenab holds one shoulder. The Indus holds the other. In a quiet year the canals look obedient. In a loud year the same fields become a lake. That is Muzaffargarh after the map-makers cut Kot Addu away into its own district.

The 2023 count is 3,528,567 people in 552,926 households on about 4,778 km². Urban only 663,000. Literacy 43.74 percent — 51.38 among men, 35.74 among women. One resident in three is under ten. Saraiki is the kitchen language. The town takes the name Nawab Muzaffar Khan left it: the fort of Muzaffar.

What the new map kept

Muzaffargarh tehsil — 2,377 km², 1,905,995 people, reading rate 46.84 percent.

Jatoi — 1,010 km², 862,046 people, reading rate 40.67 percent. Town about 155,000.

Alipur — 1,391 km², 760,526 people, reading rate 39.15 percent. The southern wet end. Sitpur sits this way.

Kot Addu and Chowk Sarwar Shaheed keep their own commissioner now.

Wheat, cotton, the orchard that floods

Wheat first, then cotton, cane, mango on better loam, buffalo on the rest. The Recep Tayyip Erdoğan Hospital sits on the public health list beside the ordinary DHQ and THQ row.

The houses that keep getting elected

Ghulam Mustafa Khar, born 1937 at Sanawan, was governor and chief minister of Punjab in the Bhutto years. His niece Hina Rabbani Khar was foreign minister — the first woman in that chair. Ghulam Noor Rabbani and Raza Rabbani Khar keep the surname on later rolls. Jatoi is a tehsil and a house: Abdul Qayyum Khan Jatoi, the Dasti names, Nawabzada Nasrullah Khan, Jamshed Dasti. Mushtaq Ahmed Gurmani governed West Pakistan from an earlier generation.

The voice that left the katchi

Pathanay Khan took a Saraiki kaafi out of this belt onto Radio Pakistan. Tauqeer Nasir took the later screen. Wrestler Azhar Hussain took a medal line.

How a flood year reads

2010 taught the newsroom the name. Later high-water years taught it again. Then the same field is sown. DG Khan is west. Multan is east. This strip is the argument both cities drive through.

Muzaffargarh District: FAQ

What is the population of Muzaffargarh District?

The 2023 census counted 5,015,325 people in Muzaffargarh District (pre-split boundaries), up from 4,328,549 in 2017 (annual growth 2.49%). 18.88% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Muzaffargarh District?

Muzaffargarh District covers 8,249 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 607.99 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Muzaffargarh District?

The 2023 census lists 12 urban localities in Muzaffargarh District; the ten largest are Muzaffargarh (235,541, municipal committee), Jatoi (155,196, municipal committee), Kot Addu (142,161, municipal committee), Alipur (74,095, municipal committee), Chowk Sarwar Shaheed (63,421, municipal committee), Shehr Sultan (62,184, municipal committee), Ghalwan (49,137, town committee), Sinawan (40,593, municipal committee), Daira Din Panah (37,644, town committee) and Khangarh (32,161, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Muzaffargarh District?

47.99% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 55.95%, female 39.72%).

Which languages are spoken in Muzaffargarh District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Saraiki (89.37% of the 5,012,124 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Punjabi (5.2%), Urdu (3.99%).

Which province is Muzaffargarh District in?

Muzaffargarh District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Muzaffargarh.

Jafarabad District: Quick Answers

Jafarabad District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Naseerabad Division, with its headquarters at Dera Allah Yar. The 2023 census lists 3 administrative units in it: Gandakha, Jhat Pat and Usta Muhammad. The 2023 census counted 594,558 people (pre-split boundaries) on 1,643 km², and 35.53% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Jafarabad District

The 2023 census counted 3 administrative units in Jafarabad District: Gandakha (tehsil), Jhat Pat (sub-division) and Usta Muhammad (sub-division). Jhat Pat is the most populous, with 302,498 people, and it is also the largest by area (690 km²). Literacy ranges from 24.59% in Gandakha to 38.17% in Usta Muhammad.

Population and growth

Jafarabad District had 594,558 people in the 2023 census, up from 513,972 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.46%). It covers 1,643 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 6th by population and 31st by area. 27.48% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 102.79 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Dera Allah Yar (98,761) and Usta Muhammad (64,632). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (51.91%), followed by Sindhi (19.33%), Saraiki (15.45%) and Brahui (12.07%).

Literacy and schooling

35.53% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 24th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 44.43% and female literacy 26.37%; the urban rate was 49.84% and the rural rate 29.68%. The census counted 137,885 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (73,476 girls and 64,408 boys).

Jafarabad District: FAQ

What is the population of Jafarabad District?

The 2023 census counted 594,558 people in Jafarabad District (pre-split boundaries), up from 513,972 in 2017 (annual growth 2.46%). 27.48% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Jafarabad District?

Jafarabad District covers 1,643 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 361.87 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Jafarabad District?

The 2023 census lists 2 urban localities in Jafarabad District: Dera Allah Yar (98,761, municipal corporation) and Usta Muhammad (64,632, municipal corporation).

What is the literacy rate of Jafarabad District?

35.53% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 44.43%, female 26.37%).

Which languages are spoken in Jafarabad District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (51.91% of the 594,558 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Sindhi (19.33%), Saraiki (15.45%), Brahui (12.07%).

Which province is Jafarabad District in?

Jafarabad District is in Balochistan, in Naseerabad Division. Its headquarters is Dera Allah Yar.

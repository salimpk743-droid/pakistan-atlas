Lower Chitral District

Formed in 2018 from Chitral District · headquarters Chitral · 2 census units · 320,407 people (2023 census)

Lower Chitral District: Quick Answers

Lower Chitral District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Chitral. It has 2 sub-divisions: Chitral and Drosh. The 2023 census counted 320,407 people on 6,458 km², and 66.1% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

How Lower Chitral was formed

Before 2018, Chitral was the largest district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, covering 14,850 km². On 20 November 2018 the Board of Revenue notified Upper Chitral as a district with its headquarters at Booni, made up of the Mastuj and Torkhow/Mulkhow sub-divisions (Dawn). A further notification on 21 December 2018 formally divided Chitral into Lower Chitral and Upper Chitral districts (APP via The Nation).

Chitral town

Chitral town lies along the Kunar River, also called the Chitral River, in a valley about 3 km wide at around 1,490 metres. It is reached by several mountain passes and is linked with Peshawar by air. Khowar is the lingua franca, and a significant portion of the people are Ismailis (Britannica).

Administrative units of Lower Chitral District

The 2023 census counted 2 administrative units in Lower Chitral District: Chitral (sub-division) and Drosh (sub-division). Chitral is the most populous, with 211,374 people, and it is also the largest by area (6,127 km²). Literacy ranges from 57.38% in Drosh to 70.2% in Chitral.

Population and growth

Lower Chitral District had 320,407 people in the 2023 census, up from 278,328 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.38%). It covers 6,458 km². Of the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the census, it ranks 32nd by population and 4th by area. 17.84% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.31 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Chitral (57,157). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (9.36%), followed by Kalasha (1.59%).

Literacy and schooling

66.1% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 4th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 76.81% and female literacy 54.77%; the urban rate was 77.17% and the rural rate 63.64%. The census counted 17,282 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (9,589 girls and 7,693 boys).

Lower Chitral District: FAQ

How many sub-divisions are in Lower Chitral District?

Lower Chitral District has 2 sub-divisions: Chitral and Drosh.

What is the population of Lower Chitral District?

The 2023 census counted 320,407 people in Lower Chitral District, up from 278,328 in 2017 (annual growth 2.38%). 17.84% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Lower Chitral District?

Lower Chitral District covers 6,458 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 49.61 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Lower Chitral District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Lower Chitral District: Chitral (57,157, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Lower Chitral District?

66.1% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 76.81%, female 54.77%).

Which languages are spoken in Lower Chitral District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (9.36% of the 318,234 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Kalasha (1.59%).

Which province is Lower Chitral District in?

Lower Chitral District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Chitral.

When was Chitral divided into Upper and Lower Chitral?

Upper Chitral was notified as a district on 20 November 2018, and a notification on 21 December 2018 formally divided Chitral into Lower Chitral and Upper Chitral districts. (APP via The Nation)

Lower Chitral District

Headquarters: Chitral · Census 2023 · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Lower Chitral covers the Kalash valleys and Chitral town, sitting in the shadow of Trich Mir, Pakistan's highest peak outside the Karakoram.

Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is Chitral. The available public data note is Census 2023. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Education

The University of Chitral and a number of colleges serve the district.

Hospitals and healthcare

DHQ Chitral is the main hospital.

Culture and geography

Khowar is the dominant language, and the Kalash festivals and polo matches draw visitors from across the country.

Public issues

The Lowari Tunnel has cut winter isolation sharply, though side valleys still lose road access in heavy snow.

Villages and local places

Ayun and the Kalash valleys.

Sialkot District: Quick Answers

Sialkot District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, in Gujranwala Division, with its headquarters at Sialkot. It has 4 tehsils: Daska, Pasrur, Sambrial and Sialkot. The 2023 census counted 4,499,394 people on 3,016 km², and 78.37% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Sialkot city

Sialkot lies just north of the Aik stream and south of the Jammu hills, and is linked by rail with Wazirabad and by road with Lahore and Gujranwala. It may be the site of ancient Sakala (Sagul), capital of the Indo-Greek king Menander. It became a municipality in 1867 and is known for making sporting goods. Sialkot was the birthplace of the poet-philosopher Muhammad Iqbal (Britannica).

Tehsils of Sialkot District

The 2023 census counted 4 tehsils in Sialkot District: Daska, Pasrur, Sambrial and Sialkot. Sialkot is the most populous, with 2,088,201 people, and Pasrur is the largest by area (975 km²). Literacy ranges from 74.52% in Pasrur to 79.89% in Sambrial.

Population and growth

Sialkot District had 4,499,394 people in the 2023 census, up from 3,894,938 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.44%). It covers 3,016 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 8th by population and 28th by area. 32.94% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 102.58 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 9 urban localities; the largest are Sialkot (835,337), Daska (228,626), Sambrial (119,571) and Pasrur (102,717). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (92.01%), followed by Urdu (4.86%), Mewati (1.27%) and Pashto (1.22%).

Literacy and schooling

78.37% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 5th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 80.24% and female literacy 76.5%; the urban rate was 83.31% and the rural rate 75.94%. The census counted 153,560 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (65,510 girls and 87,658 boys).

Sialkot District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Sialkot District?

Sialkot District has 4 tehsils: Daska, Pasrur, Sambrial and Sialkot.

What is the population of Sialkot District?

The 2023 census counted 4,499,394 people in Sialkot District, up from 3,894,938 in 2017 (annual growth 2.44%). 32.94% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Sialkot District?

Sialkot District covers 3,016 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 1,491.84 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Sialkot District?

The 2023 census lists 9 urban localities in Sialkot District: Sialkot (835,337, municipal corporation), Daska (228,626, municipal committee), Sambrial (119,571, municipal committee), Pasrur (102,717, municipal committee), Sialkot Cantonment (76,480, cantonment), Kotli Loharan (37,063, town committee), Jamke Cheema (30,698, town committee), Chawinda (29,140, town committee) and Bhopalwala (22,336, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Sialkot District?

78.37% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 80.24%, female 76.5%).

Which languages are spoken in Sialkot District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (92.01% of the 4,472,462 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (4.86%), Mewati (1.27%), Pashto (1.22%).

Which province is Sialkot District in?

Sialkot District is in Punjab, in Gujranwala Division. Its headquarters is Sialkot.

Was Allama Iqbal born in Sialkot?

Yes. Sialkot was the birthplace of the poet-philosopher Muhammad Iqbal. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

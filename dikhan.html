Dera Ismail Khan District

Headquarters: D.I. Khan · Census 2023 · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Dera Ismail Khan is a Saraiki-Pashtun city on the Indus in southern KP; it lost its northern Paharpur and Paniala tehsils to a new district in October 2025.

Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is D.I. Khan. The available public data note is Census 2023. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Education

Gomal University and a medical college serve the district, alongside public colleges in the remaining tehsils.

Hospitals and healthcare

Mufti Mehmood Teaching Hospital and the DHQ hospital handle most public healthcare.

Culture and geography

The district sits where southern KP culture meets South Punjab, with Saraiki widely spoken alongside Pashto.

Public issues

Summer heat, dust storms and long rural distances to services remain a challenge.

Villages and local places

Kulachi and Paroa villages.

Sohbatpur District: Quick Answers

Sohbatpur District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Naseerabad Division, with its headquarters at Sohbatpur. The 2023 census lists 6 administrative units in it: Faridabad, Hair Din, Manjhipur, Panhwar, Saeed Muhammad Kanrani and Sohbatpur. The 2023 census counted 240,106 people on 802 km², and 41.02% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Sohbatpur District

The 2023 census counted 6 administrative units in Sohbatpur District: Faridabad (tehsil), Hair Din (tehsil), Manjhipur (tehsil), Panhwar (tehsil), Saeed Muhammad Kanrani (tehsil) and Sohbatpur (sub-division). Faridabad is the most populous, with 68,948 people, and Sohbatpur is the largest by area (334 km²). Literacy ranges from 35.38% in Panhwar to 45.9% in Saeed Muhammad Kanrani.

Population and growth

Sohbatpur District had 240,106 people in the 2023 census, up from 200,426 in 2017 (average annual growth 3.06%). It covers 802 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 24th by population and 33rd by area. 6.13% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.06 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Sohbatpur (14,728). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (67.39%), followed by Sindhi (17.63%), Brahui (7.92%) and Saraiki (6.38%).

Literacy and schooling

41.02% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 13th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 53.26% and female literacy 28.42%; the urban rate was 52.11% and the rural rate 40.27%. The census counted 48,360 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (26,588 girls and 21,771 boys).

Sohbatpur District: FAQ

What is the population of Sohbatpur District?

The 2023 census counted 240,106 people in Sohbatpur District, up from 200,426 in 2017 (annual growth 3.06%). 6.13% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Sohbatpur District?

Sohbatpur District covers 802 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 299.38 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Sohbatpur District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Sohbatpur District: Sohbatpur (14,728, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Sohbatpur District?

41.02% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 53.26%, female 28.42%).

Which languages are spoken in Sohbatpur District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (67.39% of the 240,106 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Sindhi (17.63%), Brahui (7.92%), Saraiki (6.38%).

Which province is Sohbatpur District in?

Sohbatpur District is in Balochistan, in Naseerabad Division. Its headquarters is Sohbatpur.

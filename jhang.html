Jhang District

Twin towns of Jhang and Maghiana · headquarters Jhang · 4 tehsils · 3,065,639 people (2023 census)

Jhang District: Quick Answers

Jhang District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Jhang. It has 4 tehsils: 18-Hazari, Ahmadpur Sial, Jhang and Shorkot. The 2023 census counted 3,065,639 people on 6,166 km², and 59.45% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Jhang Sadar

Jhang Sadar, just east of the Chenab River, joins the twin towns of Maghiana, on the edge of the highlands, and Jhang, in the lowlands below. Jhang was founded in 1462 and re-established in 1688 after a flood destroyed the first settlement; the twin towns became a municipality in 1867. The Shorkot ruins to the south may be a city captured by Alexander the Great in 325 BCE (Britannica).

Tehsils of Jhang District

The 2023 census counted 4 tehsils in Jhang District: 18-Hazari, Ahmadpur Sial, Jhang and Shorkot. Jhang is the most populous, with 1,640,676 people, and it is also the largest by area (2,591 km²). Literacy ranges from 56.87% in Ahmadpur Sial to 60.96% in Jhang.

Population and growth

Jhang District had 3,065,639 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,743,526 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.87%). It covers 6,166 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 19th by population and 12th by area. 26.13% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 107.15 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (93.33%), followed by Urdu (4.71%).

Literacy and schooling

59.45% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 25th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 69.09% and female literacy 49.14%; the urban rate was 73.98% and the rural rate 54.05%. The census counted 245,763 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (129,482 girls and 116,272 boys).

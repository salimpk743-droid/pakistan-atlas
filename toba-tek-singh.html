Toba Tek Singh District: Quick Answers

Toba Tek Singh District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Toba Tek Singh. It has 4 tehsils: Gojra, Kamalia, Pirmahal and Toba Tek Singh. The 2023 census counted 2,524,044 people on 3,252 km², and 71.38% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Toba Tek Singh District

The 2023 census counted 4 tehsils in Toba Tek Singh District: Gojra, Kamalia, Pirmahal and Toba Tek Singh. Toba Tek Singh is the most populous, with 849,352 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,141 km²). Literacy ranges from 63.55% in Kamalia to 74.45% in Toba Tek Singh.

Population and growth

Toba Tek Singh District had 2,524,044 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,190,602 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.4%). It covers 3,252 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 21st by population and 25th by area. 22.33% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 105.49 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (96.2%), followed by Urdu (2.48%).

Literacy and schooling

71.38% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 10th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 76.37% and female literacy 66.13%; the urban rate was 81.82% and the rural rate 68.3%. The census counted 142,390 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (66,948 girls and 75,438 boys).

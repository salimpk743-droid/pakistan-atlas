Tehsils of Gujranwala District

The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in Gujranwala District: Gujranwala City (tehsil), Gujranwala Saddar (tehsil), Kamoke (tehsil), Nowshera Virkan (tehsil) and Wazirabad (tehsil). Gujranwala City is the most populous, with 2,511,118 people in the 2023 census, and Wazirabad is the largest by area (1,196 km²).

Gujranwala District administrative structure

How the tehsils compare

Gujranwala City tehsil has the most people (2,511,118) and Nowshera Virkan the fewest (640,780). Wazirabad covers the largest area (1,196 km²). Gujranwala City is the most urban (100% of residents in urban areas). Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 71.36% in Nowshera Virkan to 79.39% in Gujranwala City.

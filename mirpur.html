Mirpur District: Quick Answers

Mirpur District is one of the 10 districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, in Mirpur Division. The 2017 census counted 456,541 people on 1,010 km², in 2 tehsils: Mirpur and Dudyal.

Tehsils of Mirpur District

The 2017 census tables published by the AJ&K Bureau of Statistics list 2 tehsils in Mirpur District: Mirpur and Dudyal (Statistical Year Book 2023, Table 15.15). Mirpur is the most populous, with 343,184 people, and it is also the largest by area (627 km²). AJK at a Glance 2024 counts 32 tehsils across Azad Kashmir in 2023, against 30 in the 2017 census tables, so tehsil boundaries may since have changed (AJK at a Glance 2024).

Population and area

Mirpur District had 456,541 people in the 2017 census, with average annual growth of 1.66% since 1998, 39.78% of them in urban areas and an average household of 6.27 people (Table 15.6). It covers 1,010 km² (Table 15.1). Of the 10 AJK districts it ranks 4th by population and 5th by area. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects about 0.505 million people for 2023 (AJK at a Glance 2024); these are projections, not census counts.

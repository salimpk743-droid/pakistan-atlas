Mirpur District: Quick Answers

Where is Mirpur District? Mirpur lies in southern Azad Jammu and Kashmir near the Mangla reservoir and the Punjab border. Mirpur city is the district headquarters and a major commercial centre.

What is Mirpur famous for? Mirpur is known for Mangla Dam and Lake, its large overseas diaspora, commercial activity, Pahari/Potohari cultural connections and its position between AJK's hill country and the Punjab plain.

History and geography: Mirpur's landscape combines rolling Potohar-edge terrain with the large Mangla reservoir. The construction of Mangla Dam transformed the physical and economic geography of the district and displaced many historic settlements.

Education and healthcare: Mirpur has universities, colleges, schools and hospitals serving the district and nearby parts of AJK. Its institutions also support a population with strong links to overseas communities.

Important places and local areas: Mirpur city, Mangla, Mangla Lake, Mangla Fort, Ramkot Fort and Khari Sharif are central reference points.

Mirpur and AJK: Mirpur is one of the principal commercial and population centres of southern AJK and has strong economic and social links with Punjab. Continue to the Azad Jammu and Kashmir guide.

Mirpur District occupies a distinctive place in Azad Jammu & Kashmir. Unlike the high mountain landscapes of Neelum or the forested hill stations farther north, Mirpur is shaped by a remarkable combination of water, history, agriculture, urban life and cultural heritage.

The enormous Mangla reservoir, the historic Mangla Fort, the dramatic Ramkot Fort, the sacred landscape of Khari Sharif and the modern city of Mirpur give the district a character unlike anywhere else in AJK.

Official AJK material lists Mirpur at approximately 1,010 square kilometres and records a 2017 population of approximately 456,200. The older AJK administrative profile also identifies Mirpur as one of the principal districts of the region. ([ajkcs.gov.pk](https://ajkcs.gov.pk/ajkadmin.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

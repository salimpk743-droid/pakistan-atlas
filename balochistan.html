Balochistan بلوچستان

Largest province by area. Baloch and Brahui traditions, juniper forests of Ziarat, Makran coast and the historic Quetta valley.

Balochistan facts

Capital: Quetta · Population: 14,894,402 (2023 baseline) · Area: 347,190 km² · Administrative units: 35 district pages.

Culture and geography

Largest province by area. Baloch and Brahui traditions, juniper forests of Ziarat, Makran coast and the historic Quetta valley.

Education and health

University of Balochistan and BUITEMS in Quetta are the main campuses; more district colleges are being added.

BMC Quetta is the main teaching hospital. Many districts still have limited specialist cover.

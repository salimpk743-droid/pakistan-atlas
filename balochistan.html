Balochistan بلوچستان

Balochistan is a province of Pakistan with its capital at Quetta. The 2023 census counted 14,894,402 people (6.17% of Pakistan) on 347,190 km², and 42.01% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

About Balochistan

Balochistan became a separate province in its present form in 1970. It is the largest and most sparsely populated province of Pakistan, and its capital is Quetta (Britannica).

Culture and Geography of Balochistan

Largest province by area. Baloch and Brahui traditions, juniper forests of Ziarat, Makran coast and the historic Quetta valley.

Education and Health in Balochistan

University of Balochistan and BUITEMS in Quetta are the main campuses; more district colleges are being added.

BMC Quetta is the main teaching hospital. Many districts still have limited specialist cover.

Public Issues in Balochistan

Low population density, water scarcity, school access for girls in remote tehsils, and long travel times to hospitals.

Notable Places in Balochistan

Quetta, Gwadar, Turbat, Khuzdar, Ziarat

Largest districts of Balochistan

Among the 26 current districts with their own 2023 census row, the most populous are Nasirabad District (563,315), Panjgur District (509,781) and Chaman District (466,218). The largest by area are Chagai District (44,748 km²), Washuk District (33,093 km²) and Awaran District (29,510 km²). Literacy ranges from 21.58% in Washuk District to 57.12% in Nushki District. Figures are from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Tables 1 and 12; districts split after the census are left out of this comparison.

How many districts does Balochistan have?

41 districts in 11 divisions, as of September 2026. Balochistan Revenue Department notification of 8 July 2026: 11 divisions and 41 districts (up from 8 divisions and 36 districts).

The five added districts are Quetta West (Quetta split into Quetta East and Quetta West), Wadh (from Khuzdar), Tump (from Kech, notified 24 February 2026), Barshore (from Pishin) and North Dera Bugti (Dera Bugti split into North and South Dera Bugti). The division of each district follows the only published full list (Daily Pakistan); Musakhel appears under both Zhob and Loralai in the February 2026 cabinet summary.

Sources: Dawn, 2026-07-12; Dawn, 2026-07-11; ProPakistani, 2026-07-12; ARY News, 2026-07; Daily Pakistan (via The Patriot), 2026-07-12; ProPakistani, 2026-02-25; Baldiyat Times, 2026-02-27.

Balochistan: FAQ

What is the capital of Balochistan?

Quetta. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

What is the population of Balochistan?

The 2023 census counted 14,894,402 people, up from 12,335,129 in 2017 (annual growth 3.20%). (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

What is the area of Balochistan?

347,190 km², with 42.90 people per km² in 2023. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

What is the literacy rate of Balochistan?

42.01% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 50.50%, female 32.80%). (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

How many districts does Balochistan have?

41 districts in 11 divisions, as of September 2026. (Dawn)

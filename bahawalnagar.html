Bahawalnagar District

On the Sutlej · formerly part of Bahawalpur State · headquarters Bahawalnagar · 5 tehsils · 3,550,342 people (2023 census)

Bahawalnagar District: Quick Answers

Bahawalnagar District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Bahawalnagar. It has 5 tehsils: Bahawalnagar, Chishtian, Fort Abbas, Haroonabad and Minchinabad. The 2023 census counted 3,550,342 people on 8,878 km², and 57.01% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Bahawalnagar town and the Sutlej valley

Bahawalnagar lies just east of the Sutlej River and is a market town linked by road with Multan and Bahawalpur, and by rail with Bahawalpur. The surrounding region, once part of the princely state of Bahawalpur, is mainly an alluvial tract in the lowlands of the Sutlej valley. Wheat, rice, sorghum and cotton are the chief crops (Britannica).

Tehsils of Bahawalnagar District

The 2023 census counted 5 tehsils in Bahawalnagar District: Bahawalnagar, Chishtian, Fort Abbas, Haroonabad and Minchinabad. Bahawalnagar is the most populous, with 976,049 people, and Fort Abbas is the largest by area (2,536 km²). Literacy ranges from 44.05% in Minchinabad to 66.28% in Haroonabad.

Population and growth

Bahawalnagar District had 3,550,342 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,975,656 in 2017 (average annual growth 3%). It covers 8,878 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 13th by population and 5th by area. 27.44% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 108.27 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 12 urban localities; the largest are Bahawalnagar (241,873), Chishtian (192,403), Haroonabad (149,679) and Fort Abbas (83,192). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (94.08%), followed by Urdu (3.35%) and Saraiki (1.74%).

Literacy and schooling

57.01% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 28th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 63.55% and female literacy 49.95%; the urban rate was 70.84% and the rural rate 51.51%. The census counted 344,881 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (164,457 girls and 180,401 boys).

Bahawalnagar District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Bahawalnagar District?

Bahawalnagar District has 5 tehsils: Bahawalnagar, Chishtian, Fort Abbas, Haroonabad and Minchinabad.

What is the population of Bahawalnagar District?

The 2023 census counted 3,550,342 people in Bahawalnagar District, up from 2,975,656 in 2017 (annual growth 3%). 27.44% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Bahawalnagar District?

Bahawalnagar District covers 8,878 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 399.9 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Bahawalnagar District?

The 2023 census lists 12 urban localities in Bahawalnagar District; the ten largest are Bahawalnagar (241,873, municipal committee), Chishtian (192,403, municipal committee), Haroonabad (149,679, municipal committee), Fort Abbas (83,192, municipal committee), Minchinabad (67,164, municipal committee), Faqir Wali (61,586, town committee), Donga Bonga (39,150, municipal committee), Daharanwala (37,126, town committee), Khichi Wala (26,545, town committee) and Ahmed Pur Mecleod Gunj (25,997, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Bahawalnagar District?

57.01% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 63.55%, female 49.95%).

Which languages are spoken in Bahawalnagar District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (94.08% of the 3,544,911 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (3.35%), Saraiki (1.74%).

Which province is Bahawalnagar District in?

Bahawalnagar District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Bahawalnagar.

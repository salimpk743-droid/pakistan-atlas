More recent HUM TV serials

  • Khushi: Daily serial (Monday to Sunday, 7:00 pm). Writer Hina Huma Nafees; director Haseeb Ali; cast Marium Ejaz, Summiya Buksh, Ahin Babar, Arsalan Khan, Behroz Sabzwari and Seemi Pasha.
  • Mohabbat Mukhtasir: Sunday, 8:00 pm. Writer Noor ul Huda Shah; director Meer Sikandar; cast Usama Khan, Syeda Madiha Imam, Aly Khan and Atiqa Odho.
  • Zehar-e-Mohabbat: Saturday and Sunday, 9:00 pm. Writer Shahid Dogar; director Saqib Zafar; cast Zoha Tauqeer, Umer Shahzad, Ammara Malik and Arez Ahmed.
  • Sirf Shabana: Writer Sanam Mehdi; director Syed Abis Raza; cast Sohai Ali Abro, Adeel Hussain and Durab Khalil.
  • Leader: Writer Radain Shah; director Asad Mumtaz Malik; cast Ali Raza, Kinza, Farhan Tahir and Ali Safina.
  • Muamma: Writer Imran Nazir; director Shaqielle Khan; cast Saba Qamar, Usman Mukhtar and Maria Wasti.
  • Musafat: Writer Shah Bano; director Abdullah Badini; cast Mohsin Abbas, Alishba Khan and Imran Aslam.
  • Ek Jhooti Kahani: Writer Amna Mufti; director Shehrazade Sheikh; cast Mohib Mirza, Zara Noor Abbas and Uzma Beg.
  • Hadd: Writer Qaisera Hayat; director Zahid Iqbal; cast Nabeel Zuberi, Minsa Malik and Arsalan Asad Butt.

HUM TV Dramas at the 10th HUM Awards

The 10th HUM Awards were held in Houston, Texas, in October 2025, hosted by Hamza Sohail and Dananeer Mobeen. Sajal Aly won best actress for Zard Patton Ka Bunn in both the popular and jury categories, and Bilal Abbas Khan and Durefishan Saleem won best on-screen couple for Ishq Murshid, which The Express Tribune reported as the leading winner of the night.

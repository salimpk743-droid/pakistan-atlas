Kasur District

On the Indian border south of Lahore · headquarters Kasur · 4 tehsils · 4,084,286 people (2023 census)

Kasur District: Quick Answers

Kasur District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Kasur. It has 4 tehsils: Chunian, Kasur, Kot Radha Kishan and Pattoki. The 2023 census counted 4,084,286 people on 3,995 km², and 62.85% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Kasur city

Kasur lies on the border with India about 50 km south of Lahore. Tradition says it was founded by Kusa, son of Rama. It was settled by a Pashtun colony in the Mughal period, captured by the Sikhs in 1807, and became a municipality in 1867. The city is made up of 26 fortified hamlets (kots) overlooking the valleys of the Beas and Sutlej. The Chhanga Manga forest plantation was started in 1864 (Britannica).

Tehsils of Kasur District

The 2023 census counted 4 tehsils in Kasur District: Chunian, Kasur, Kot Radha Kishan and Pattoki. Kasur is the most populous, with 1,603,658 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,493 km²). Literacy ranges from 60.64% in Chunian to 64.66% in Kot Radha Kishan.

Population and growth

Kasur District had 4,084,286 people in the 2023 census, up from 3,454,881 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.84%). It covers 3,995 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 11th by population and 20th by area. 30.46% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.53 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 10 urban localities; the largest are Kasur (510,875), Phool Nagar (114,530), Pattoki (113,735) and Kot Radha Kishan (102,057). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (84.29%), followed by Mewati (11.53%) and Urdu (3.36%).

Literacy and schooling

62.85% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 19th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 67.97% and female literacy 57.44%; the urban rate was 68.6% and the rural rate 60.27%. The census counted 274,362 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (133,792 girls and 140,530 boys).

Kasur District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Kasur District?

Kasur District has 4 tehsils: Chunian, Kasur, Kot Radha Kishan and Pattoki.

What is the population of Kasur District?

The 2023 census counted 4,084,286 people in Kasur District, up from 3,454,881 in 2017 (annual growth 2.84%). 30.46% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Kasur District?

Kasur District covers 3,995 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 1,022.35 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Kasur District?

The 2023 census lists 10 urban localities in Kasur District: Kasur (510,875, municipal committee), Phool Nagar (114,530, municipal committee), Pattoki (113,735, municipal committee), Kot Radha Kishan (102,057, municipal committee), Chunian (87,547, municipal committee), Mustafabad (81,550, municipal committee), Alahabad (80,097, municipal committee), Raja Jang (66,404, municipal committee), Khadian (48,519, municipal committee) and Kanganpur (38,568, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Kasur District?

62.85% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 67.97%, female 57.44%).

Which languages are spoken in Kasur District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (84.29% of the 4,081,568 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Mewati (11.53%), Urdu (3.36%).

Which province is Kasur District in?

Kasur District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Kasur.

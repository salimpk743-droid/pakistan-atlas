Talagang District: FAQ

When did Talagang become a district?

Talagang was approved as a district on 14 October 2022 and is listed as a district of Rawalpindi Division in the Punjab notification of December 2024. (ProPakistani)

Which tehsils are in Talagang District?

Two: Talagang and Lawa, both formerly in Chakwal District. (The Express Tribune)

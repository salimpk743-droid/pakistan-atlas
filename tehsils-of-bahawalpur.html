Tehsils of Bahawalpur District

Bahawalpur Division · Punjab · 2023 census figures

Bahawalpur District administrative structure

Bahawalpur District has 6 tehsils: Ahmadpur East, Bahawalpur City, Bahawalpur Saddar, Hasilpur, Khairpur Tamewali and Yazman. The district is part of Bahawalpur Division.

Ahmadpur East Tehsil

Population (2023): 1,307,578

Area: 1,738 km² · 752.35 people per km²

Urban share: 26.67%

Bahawalpur City Tehsil

Population (2023): 815,202

Area: 1,490 km² · 547.12 people per km²

Urban share: 100%

Bahawalpur Saddar Tehsil

Population (2023): 675,950

Area: 745 km² · 907.32 people per km²

Urban share: 22.33%

Hasilpur Tehsil

Population (2023): 508,415

Area: 1,490 km² · 341.22 people per km²

Urban share: 38.64%

Khairpur Tamewali Tehsil

Population (2023): 290,582

Area: 993 km² · 292.63 people per km²

Urban share: 16.27%

Yazman Tehsil

Population (2023): 687,237

Area: 18,374 km² · 37.4 people per km²

Urban share: 8.84%

How many districts does Punjab have?

41 districts in 10 divisions, as of September 2026. Punjab government notification of the administrative structure (reported 1 January 2026): 10 divisions, 41 districts, 156 tehsils.

Sources: SUNO News, 2026-01-01.

Sources and editorial method

Population, area and tehsil figures are taken from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Table 1. Districts created after the census are marked, and no figure is shown where no official number exists.

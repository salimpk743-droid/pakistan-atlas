Districts of Punjab

Punjab has 41 districts (September 2026). An alphabetical list with headquarters, 2023 census population and tehsils where the census publishes them.

Punjab province guide → · All districts of Pakistan → · Original Punjab districts page →

Punjab districts, A–Z

Attock

Attock District

Population: 2,170,423 (2023 census)

6 tehsils: Attock, Fateh Jang, Hasan Abdal, Hazro, Jand and Pindi Gheb

Attock District, Punjab: 2,170,423 people in the 2023 census, 6 tehsils, headquarters Attock.

Bahawalnagar

Bahawalnagar District

Population: 3,550,342 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Bahawalnagar, Chishtian, Fort Abbas, Haroonabad and Minchinabad

Punjab district east of the Sutlej River, once part of the princely state of Bahawalpur.

Bahawalpur

Bahawalpur District

Population: 4,284,964 (2023 census)

6 tehsils: Ahmadpur East, Bahawalpur City, Bahawalpur Saddar, Hasilpur, Khairpur Tamewali and Yazman

Largest district of Punjab by area; its headquarters city was founded in 1748 by Muhammad Bahawal Khan and was the capital of the nawabs of Bahawalpur.

Bhakkar

Bhakkar District

Population: 1,957,470 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Bhakkar, Darya Khan, Kalur Kot and Mankera

Bhakkar District, Punjab: 1,957,470 people in the 2023 census, 4 tehsils, headquarters Bhakkar.

Chakwal

Chakwal District

Population: 1,734,854 (2023 census, pre-split)

Chakwal District, Punjab: 1,734,854 people in the 2023 census, 5 tehsils, headquarters Chakwal.

Chiniot

Chiniot District

Population: 1,563,024 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Bhawana, Chiniot and Lalian

Chiniot District, Punjab: 1,563,024 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Chiniot.

D.G. Khan

Dera Ghazi Khan District

Population: 3,393,705 (2023 census, pre-split)

Punjab district whose headquarters, founded by Ghazi Khan, was rebuilt in 1911 after an Indus flood.

Faisalabad

Faisalabad District

Population: 9,075,819 (2023 census)

6 tehsils: Chak Jhumra, Faisalabad City, Faisalabad Sadar, Jaranwala, Sammundri and Tandlian Wala

Punjab district whose headquarters, founded in 1890 and named after Sir Charles James Lyall, was formerly called Lyallpur.

Gujranwala

Gujranwala District

Population: 5,959,750 (2023 census, pre-split)

Punjab district on the Grand Trunk Road; Gujranwala city was the birthplace and early capital of Ranjit Singh.

Gujrat

Gujrat District

Population: 3,219,375 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Gujrat, Kharian and Sarai Alamgir

Punjab district between the Chenab and Jhelum rivers; Gujrat city grew around a fort built by Akbar in 1580.

Hafizabad

Hafizabad District

Population: 1,319,909 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Hafizabad and Pindi Bhattian

Hafizabad District, Punjab: 1,319,909 people in the 2023 census, 2 tehsils, headquarters Hafizabad.

Jhang

Jhang District

Population: 3,065,639 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: 18-Hazari, Ahmadpur Sial, Jhang and Shorkot

Punjab district whose headquarters, Jhang Sadar, combines the historic twin towns of Jhang and Maghiana east of the Chenab.

Jhelum

Jhelum District

Population: 1,382,308 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Dina, Jhelum, Pind Dadan Khan and Sohawa

Punjab district on the Jhelum River; the old town across the river may have been Bucephala, founded by Alexander the Great.

Kasur

Kasur District

Population: 4,084,286 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Chunian, Kasur, Kot Radha Kishan and Pattoki

Punjab district on the Indian border about 50 km south of Lahore; Kasur city grew from 26 fortified hamlets.

Khanewal

Khanewal District

Population: 3,364,077 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Jahanian, Kabirwala, Khanewal and Mian Channu

Khanewal District, Punjab: 3,364,077 people in the 2023 census, 4 tehsils, headquarters Khanewal.

Jauharabad

Khushab District

Population: 1,501,089 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Khushab, Noor Pur, Nowshera and Quaidabad

Khushab District, Punjab: 1,501,089 people in the 2023 census, 4 tehsils, headquarters Jauharabad.

Kot Addu

Kot Addu District

Dera Ghazi Khan Division district formed from Kot Addu and Chowk Sarwar Shaheed tehsils of Muzaffargarh; notified in December 2024.

Lahore

Lahore District

Population: 13,004,135 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Lahore Cantt, Lahore City, Model Town, Raiwind and Shalimar

Capital of Punjab and the second-largest city of Pakistan, on the Ravi River; home to the Lahore Fort and Shalimar Gardens, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1981.

Layyah

Layyah District

Population: 2,102,386 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Choubara, Karor Lal Esan and Layyah

Layyah District, Punjab: 2,102,386 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Layyah.

Lodhran

Lodhran District

Population: 1,928,299 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Dunyapur, Kahror Pacca and Lodhran

Lodhran District, Punjab: 1,928,299 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Lodhran.

Mandi Bahauddin

Mandi Bahauddin District

Population: 1,829,486 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Malakwal, Mandi Bahauddin and Phalia

Mandi Bahauddin District, Punjab: 1,829,486 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Mandi Bahauddin.

Mianwali

Mianwali District

Population: 1,798,268 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Isakhel, Mianwali and Piplan

Punjab district whose headquarters, founded in 1868, lies just east of the Indus River.

Multan

Multan District

Population: 5,362,305 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Jalalpur Pirwala, Multan City, Multan Saddar and Shujabad

Punjab district whose headquarters, one of the oldest cities of the subcontinent, is known for its shrines, including that of Shah Rukn-e-Alam.

Murree

Murree District

Hill district of Rawalpindi Division formed from Murree and Kotli Sattian tehsils of Rawalpindi; confirmed in the December 2024 notification.

Muzaffargarh

Muzaffargarh District

Population: 5,015,325 (2023 census, pre-split)

Muzaffargarh District, Punjab: 5,015,325 people in the 2023 census, 5 tehsils, headquarters Muzaffargarh.

Nankana Sahib

Nankana Sahib District

Population: 1,634,871 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Nankana Sahib, Sangla Hill and Shah Kot

Punjab district whose headquarters, Nankana Sahib (formerly Rai Bhoi di Talvandi), is the birthplace of Guru Nanak, founder of Sikhism.

Narowal

Narowal District

Population: 1,950,954 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Narowal, Shakargarh and Zafarwal

Narowal District, Punjab: 1,950,954 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Narowal.

Okara

Okara District

Population: 3,515,490 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Depalpur, Okara and Renala Khurd

Okara District, Punjab: 3,515,490 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Okara.

Pakpattan

Pakpattan District

Population: 2,136,170 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Arif Wala and Pakpattan

Pakpattan District, Punjab: 2,136,170 people in the 2023 census, 2 tehsils, headquarters Pakpattan.

Rahim Yar Khan

Rahim Yar Khan District

Population: 5,564,703 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Khanpur, Liaquat Pur, Rahim Yar Khan and Sadiqabad

Punjab district whose headquarters was founded in 1751 as Naushehra and renamed Rahim Yar Khan in 1881.

Rajanpur

Rajanpur District

Population: 2,381,049 (2023 census)

Rajanpur District, Punjab: 2,381,049 people in the 2023 census, 4 administrative units, headquarters Rajanpur.

Rawalpindi

Rawalpindi District

Population: 6,118,911 (2023 census, pre-split)

Punjab district whose headquarters, Rawalpindi, was the capital of Pakistan from 1959 to 1969 and is the army headquarters.

Sahiwal

Sahiwal District

Population: 2,881,811 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Chichawatni and Sahiwal

Punjab district whose headquarters, founded in 1865 as Montgomery, was renamed Sahiwal in 1969; Harappa lies to its southwest.

Sargodha

Sargodha District

Population: 4,334,448 (2023 census)

7 tehsils: Bhalwal, Bhera, Kot Momin, Sahiwal, Sargodha, Shahpur and Sillanwali

Punjab district whose headquarters was founded in 1903 as the headquarters of the Lower Jhelum Canal colony.

Sheikhupura

Sheikhupura District

Population: 4,049,418 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Ferozewala, Muridke, Safdarabad, Sharak Pur and Sheikhupura

Sheikhupura District, Punjab: 4,049,418 people in the 2023 census, 5 tehsils, headquarters Sheikhupura.

Sialkot

Sialkot District

Population: 4,499,394 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Daska, Pasrur, Sambrial and Sialkot

Punjab district whose headquarters, Sialkot, is the birthplace of Muhammad Iqbal and a centre of sporting-goods manufacture.

Talagang

Talagang District

Rawalpindi Division district formed from Talagang and Lawa tehsils of Chakwal; approved in October 2022 and confirmed in December 2024.

Taunsa Sharif

Taunsa District

Dera Ghazi Khan Division district with Taunsa, Wehova and Koh-e-Suleman tehsils, formed from Dera Ghazi Khan; notified in December 2024.

Toba Tek Singh

Toba Tek Singh District

Population: 2,524,044 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Gojra, Kamalia, Pirmahal and Toba Tek Singh

Toba Tek Singh District, Punjab: 2,524,044 people in the 2023 census, 4 tehsils, headquarters Toba Tek Singh.

Vehari

Vehari District

Population: 3,430,421 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Burewala, Mailsi and Vehari

Vehari District, Punjab: 3,430,421 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Vehari.

Wazirabad

Wazirabad District

Gujrat Division district formed from Wazirabad tehsil of Gujranwala in October 2022, with Wazirabad and Alipur Chatha tehsils.

How many districts does Punjab have?

41 districts in 10 divisions, as of September 2026. Punjab government notification of the administrative structure (reported 1 January 2026): 10 divisions, 41 districts, 156 tehsils.

Sources: SUNO News, 2026-01-01.

Sources and editorial method

Population, area and tehsil figures are taken from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Table 1. Districts created after the census are marked, and no figure is shown where no official number exists.

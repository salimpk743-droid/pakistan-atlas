Bahawalpur District

Bahawalpur Division · former Bahawalpur State · largest district of Punjab by area (24,830 km²) · 4,284,964 people (2023 census)

Bahawalpur District: Quick Answers

Bahawalpur District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, in Bahawalpur Division, with its headquarters at Bahawalpur. It has 6 tehsils: Ahmadpur East, Bahawalpur City, Bahawalpur Saddar, Hasilpur, Khairpur Tamewali and Yazman. The 2023 census counted 4,284,964 people on 24,830 km², and 53.35% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Bahawalpur District

Bahawalpur District has six tehsils: Ahmadpur East, Bahawalpur City, Bahawalpur Saddar, Hasilpur, Khairpur Tamewali and Yazman. Yazman is by far the largest, at 18,374 km² of the district's 24,830 km². Ahmadpur East has the most people (1,307,578 in 2023).

Area and population

At 24,830 km², Bahawalpur is the largest district of Punjab by area in the 2023 census. The next largest, Rajanpur, covers 12,318 km². The census counted 4,284,964 people, 37.79% of them urban. The largest urban locality is the Bahawalpur Metropolitan Corporation (815,202), followed by Ahmadpur East (196,618) and Hasilpur (168,146). Literacy (age 10 and over) was 53.35%.

History

Muhammad Bahawal Khan founded the city in 1748. The nawabs of Bahawalpur originally came from Sindh. They formed a princely state and declared independence in 1802, and the city became a municipality in 1874 (Britannica).

Landmarks and institutions

Two palaces of the nawabs, the Nur Mahal and the Gulzar Mahal, are in the city. So are the Islamia University (founded 1925) and the Quaid-e-Azam Medical College (Britannica).

Two sites in the district are on Pakistan's UNESCO World Heritage Tentative List. One is the tombs of Bibi Jawindi, Baha'al-Halim and Ustead, with the tomb and mosque of Jalaluddin Bukhari, at Uch (UNESCO). The other is Derawar and the desert forts of Cholistan (UNESCO).

Geography: river plain and Cholistan

The city lies just south of the Sutlej River. The land near the river is irrigated by Sutlej inundation canals and grows wheat, cotton and sugarcane. Farther east lies the Rohi, or Cholistan, a barren desert tract (Britannica).

Bahawalpur District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Bahawalpur District?

Bahawalpur District has 6 tehsils: Ahmadpur East, Bahawalpur City, Bahawalpur Saddar, Hasilpur, Khairpur Tamewali and Yazman.

What is the population of Bahawalpur District?

The 2023 census counted 4,284,964 people in Bahawalpur District, up from 3,669,176 in 2017 (annual growth 2.63%). 37.79% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Bahawalpur District?

Bahawalpur District covers 24,830 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 172.57 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Bahawalpur District?

The 2023 census lists 12 urban localities in Bahawalpur District; the ten largest are Bahawalpur (815,202, metropolitan corporation), Ahmadpur East (196,618, municipal committee), Hasilpur (168,146, municipal committee), Uch Sharif (98,852, municipal committee), Bahawalpur Cantonment (88,593, cantonment), Yazman (60,738, municipal committee), Khairpur Tamewali (47,284, municipal committee), Khanqah Sharif (32,739, town committee), Samasatta (29,622, town committee) and Qaim Pur (28,302, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Bahawalpur District?

53.35% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 59.4%, female 47.09%).

Which languages are spoken in Bahawalpur District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Saraiki (67.87% of the 4,264,457 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Punjabi (24.19%), Urdu (5.47%).

Which province is Bahawalpur District in?

Bahawalpur District is in Punjab, in Bahawalpur Division. Its headquarters is Bahawalpur.

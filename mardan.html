Mardan District

Takht-i-Bahi · UNESCO World Heritage Site · headquarters Mardan · 5 tehsils · 2,744,898 people (2023 census)

Mardan District: Quick Answers

Mardan District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Mardan. It has 5 tehsils: Ghari Kapoora, Katlang, Mardan, Rustam and Takht Bhai. The 2023 census counted 2,744,898 people on 1,632 km², and 55.79% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Mardan and its heritage

Mardan lies just north of the Kalpani River and is linked by road and rail with Nowshera and Peshawar. Until 1936 the district was part of Peshawar District; it covers most of the Yusufzai plain. The Rock of Shahbazgarhi, 11 km northeast of Mardan, bears one of the great inscriptions of Emperor Ashoka (3rd century BCE) (Britannica). The Buddhist monastery of Takht-i-Bahi, founded in the early 1st century on a 152-metre hill about 16 km northwest of Mardan, was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1980 with the nearby ruins of Sahr-i-Bahlol (UNESCO).

Tehsils of Mardan District

The 2023 census counted 5 tehsils in Mardan District: Ghari Kapoora, Katlang, Mardan, Rustam and Takht Bhai. Mardan is the most populous, with 1,040,893 people, and Katlang is the largest by area (422 km²). Literacy ranges from 49.98% in Rustam to 61.47% in Katlang.

Population and growth

Mardan District had 2,744,898 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,373,399 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.46%). It covers 1,632 km². Of the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the census, it ranks 2nd by population and 22nd by area. 16.52% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 105.44 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 3 urban localities: Mardan (363,788), Takht Bhai (85,040) and Mardan Cantonment (4,514). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.07%).

Literacy and schooling

55.79% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 12th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 68.31% and female literacy 42.66%; the urban rate was 63.59% and the rural rate 54.19%. The census counted 230,969 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (133,215 girls and 97,752 boys).

Mardan District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Mardan District?

Mardan District has 5 tehsils: Ghari Kapoora, Katlang, Mardan, Rustam and Takht Bhai.

What is the population of Mardan District?

The 2023 census counted 2,744,898 people in Mardan District, up from 2,373,399 in 2017 (annual growth 2.46%). 16.52% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Mardan District?

Mardan District covers 1,632 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 1,681.92 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Mardan District?

The 2023 census lists 3 urban localities in Mardan District: Mardan (363,788, municipal committee), Takht Bhai (85,040, municipal committee) and Mardan Cantonment (4,514, cantonment).

What is the literacy rate of Mardan District?

55.79% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 68.31%, female 42.66%).

Which languages are spoken in Mardan District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.07% of the 2,740,692 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Mardan District in?

Mardan District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Mardan.

Where is Takht-i-Bahi?

In Mardan District, on a 152-metre hill about 16 km northwest of Mardan city. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1980. (UNESCO World Heritage Centre)

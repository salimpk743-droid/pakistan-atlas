Mastung District: Quick Answers

Mastung District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Quetta Division, with its headquarters at Mastung. The 2023 census lists 4 administrative units in it: Dasht, Khad Kocha, Kirdgap and Mastung. The 2023 census counted 313,271 people on 3,308 km², and 45.97% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Mastung District

The 2023 census counted 4 administrative units in Mastung District: Dasht (sub-division), Khad Kocha (sub-tehsil), Kirdgap (sub-division) and Mastung (sub-division). Mastung is the most populous, with 162,319 people, and Dasht is the largest by area (1,047 km²). Literacy ranges from 33.22% in Khad Kocha to 53.7% in Mastung.

Population and growth

Mastung District had 313,271 people in the 2023 census, up from 265,676 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.79%). It covers 3,308 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 15th by population and 28th by area. 12.89% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 113.7 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Mastung (40,374). Brahui was the most common mother tongue (89.05%), followed by Balochi (7.19%) and Pashto (2.12%).

Literacy and schooling

45.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 7th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 57.26% and female literacy 33.27%; the urban rate was 68.28% and the rural rate 42.46%. The census counted 57,702 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (30,094 girls and 27,606 boys).

Mastung District: FAQ

What is the population of Mastung District?

The 2023 census counted 313,271 people in Mastung District, up from 265,676 in 2017 (annual growth 2.79%). 12.89% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Mastung District?

Mastung District covers 3,308 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 94.7 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Mastung District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Mastung District: Mastung (40,374, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Mastung District?

45.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 57.26%, female 33.27%).

Which languages are spoken in Mastung District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Brahui (89.05% of the 312,378 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Balochi (7.19%), Pashto (2.12%).

Which province is Mastung District in?

Mastung District is in Balochistan, in Quetta Division. Its headquarters is Mastung.

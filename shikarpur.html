Shikarpur District

Historic caravan town · headquarters Shikarpur · 4 talukas · 1,386,330 people (2023 census)

Shikarpur District: Quick Answers

Shikarpur District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Shikarpur. It has 4 talukas: Garhi Yasin, Khanpur, Lakhi and Shikarpur. The 2023 census counted 1,386,330 people on 2,512 km², and 43.7% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Shikarpur city

Shikarpur lies 29 km west of the Indus River and is linked by road and rail with Sukkur, Jacobabad and Larkana. It was founded in 1617 on a caravan route through the Bolan Pass into Afghanistan, and was constituted a municipality in 1855. Its great covered bazaar was famous across Central and South Asia. The surrounding alluvial plain is a major rice-growing and sheep- and goat-raising area (Britannica).

Talukas of Shikarpur District

The 2023 census counted 4 talukas in Shikarpur District: Garhi Yasin, Khanpur, Lakhi and Shikarpur. Shikarpur is the most populous, with 421,332 people, and Garhi Yasin is the largest by area (971 km²). Literacy ranges from 29.41% in Khanpur to 50.99% in Shikarpur.

Population and growth

Shikarpur District had 1,386,330 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,233,760 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.97%). It covers 2,512 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 21st by population and 18th by area. 22.99% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 102.2 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 7 urban localities; the largest are Shikarpur (204,938), Lakhi (33,588), Madeji (20,530) and Khanpur (18,052). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (97.14%), followed by Brahui (1.29%).

Literacy and schooling

43.7% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 18th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 52.59% and female literacy 34.65%; the urban rate was 69.36% and the rural rate 35.13%. The census counted 307,001 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (160,867 girls and 146,124 boys).

Shikarpur District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Shikarpur District?

Shikarpur District has 4 talukas: Garhi Yasin, Khanpur, Lakhi and Shikarpur.

What is the population of Shikarpur District?

The 2023 census counted 1,386,330 people in Shikarpur District, up from 1,233,760 in 2017 (annual growth 1.97%). 22.99% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Shikarpur District?

Shikarpur District covers 2,512 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 551.88 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Shikarpur District?

The 2023 census lists 7 urban localities in Shikarpur District: Shikarpur (204,938, municipal committee), Lakhi (33,588, town committee), Madeji (20,530, town committee), Khanpur (18,052, town committee), Garhi Yasin (16,155, town committee), Chak (14,908, town committee) and Rustam (10,567, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Shikarpur District?

43.7% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 52.59%, female 34.65%).

Which languages are spoken in Shikarpur District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (97.14% of the 1,386,330 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Brahui (1.29%).

Which province is Shikarpur District in?

Shikarpur District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Shikarpur.

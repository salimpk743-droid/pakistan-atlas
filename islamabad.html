Islamabad District: Quick Answers

Planned capital

Islamabad stands on the Potwar Plateau, 14 km north-east of Rawalpindi, which served as the interim capital. A commission chose the site in 1959 after Karachi was found unsuitable as the capital, and construction began in 1961. The name means "City of Islam" or "City of Peace". The planned capital area covers 906 km² of terraces and meadows around the city, and a further 3,626 km² of hinterland, the Specified Areas, is also subject to planning controls (Britannica).

Population and growth

Islamabad District had 2,363,863 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,003,368 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.8%). It covers 906 km². 46.91% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 111.81 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Islamabad Metropolitan Corporation (1,108,872). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (50.57%), followed by Pashto (18.21%), Urdu (15.72%) and Hindko (6.17%).

Literacy and schooling

83.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023. Male literacy was 88.23% and female literacy 79.13%; the urban rate was 82.91% and the rural rate 84.88%. The census counted 89,127 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (41,275 girls and 47,849 boys).

Islamabad District: FAQ

What is the population of Islamabad District?

The 2023 census counted 2,363,863 people in Islamabad District, up from 2,003,368 in 2017 (annual growth 2.8%). 46.91% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Islamabad District?

Islamabad District covers 906 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 2,609.12 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Islamabad District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Islamabad District: Islamabad Metropolitan Corporation (1,108,872, metropolitan corporation).

What is the literacy rate of Islamabad District?

83.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 88.23%, female 79.13%).

Which languages are spoken in Islamabad District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (50.57% of the 2,283,244 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Pashto (18.21%), Urdu (15.72%), Hindko (6.17%).

Which province is Islamabad District in?

Islamabad District is in Islamabad Capital Territory. Its headquarters is Islamabad.

When was Islamabad built?

A commission chose the site in 1959 after Karachi was found unsuitable as the capital, and construction began in 1961. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

What does the name Islamabad mean?

City of Islam, or City of Peace. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

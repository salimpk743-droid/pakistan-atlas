Chagai District

Largest district of Pakistan by area · headquarters Dalbandin · 5 census units · 269,192 people (2023 census)

Chagai District: Quick Answers

Chagai District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Rakhshan Division, with its headquarters at Dalbandin. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Chagai, Dalbandin, Nokundi, Taftan and Yak Machh. The 2023 census counted 269,192 people on 44,748 km², and 33.15% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Chagai at a glance

Chagai is the largest district in Pakistan by area. It lies in the north-west corner of Balochistan, and its headquarters is Dalbandin (High Court of Balochistan). At 44,748 km², it is bigger than any other district in the 2023 census tables. With 269,192 people it has about 6 people per km² (PBS Table 1). The district is known for the Saindak and Reko Diq copper-gold projects.

Administrative units of Chagai District

The 2023 census counted 5 administrative units in Chagai District: Chagai (tehsil), Dalbandin (sub-division), Nokundi (tehsil), Taftan (sub-division) and Yak Machh (sub-tehsil). Dalbandin is the most populous, with 122,918 people, and Nokundi is the largest by area (16,092 km²). Literacy ranges from 19.7% in Taftan to 48.57% in Nokundi.

Population and growth

Chagai District had 269,192 people in the 2023 census, up from 226,517 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.93%). It covers 44,748 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 21st by population and 1st by area. 7.45% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 108.24 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Dalbandin (20,054). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (73.77%), followed by Brahui (16.79%) and Pashto (8.93%).

Literacy and schooling

33.15% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 26th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 41.9% and female literacy 23.57%; the urban rate was 57.88% and the rural rate 30.97%. The census counted 68,191 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (34,231 girls and 33,956 boys).

Saindak copper-gold project

The Geological Survey of Pakistan found copper at Saindak in 1961. In 1972–73 it confirmed the deposit by drilling, jointly with the United States Geological Survey. The Resource Development Corporation, now Saindak Metals Limited, was set up in April 1974 to develop it. On 23 October 1990 it signed a turnkey contract with China Metallurgical Construction Corporation (MCC) to build the project. Trial production in 1995 yielded 1,500 tonnes of blister copper. Low copper prices then kept the project idle until the government decided to lease it out (Saindak Metals Limited).

Reko Diq

Reko Diq lies in Chagai near Nok Kundi, just off the Quetta–Zahedan railway and the N-40 highway. The Afghan border is about 35 km to the north and the Iranian border about 55 km to the west (Barrick technical report). It holds one of the world's largest undeveloped open-pit copper-gold porphyry deposits. The project was suspended in 2011 in a dispute over the legality of its licensing. Under a framework agreed on 20 March 2022, Barrick holds 50% and Pakistani stakeholders hold the other 50%. Of the Pakistani half, the government of Balochistan holds a 10% free-carried share and a special-purpose company it owns holds 15%. Federal state-owned enterprises hold the remaining 25%. Barrick is the operator (Barrick).

The 1998 nuclear tests

Pakistan's first nuclear tests, on 28 May 1998, were carried out at Ras Koh in Chagai. The test of 30 May 1998 was held at a separate site in the Kharan Desert, about 100 km away, not in the Chagai Hills (Federation of American Scientists; ISIS).

سیندک کاپر گولڈ منصوبہ

جیولوجیکل سروے آف پاکستان نے 1961 میں سیندک میں تانبے کے ذخائر دریافت کیے، اور 1972–73 میں امریکی جیولوجیکل سروے کے ساتھ ڈرلنگ کے ذریعے ان کی تصدیق ہوئی۔ ریسورس ڈویلپمنٹ کارپوریشن (اب سیندک میٹلز لمیٹڈ) اپریل 1974 میں قائم ہوئی۔ 1995 کی آزمائشی پیداوار میں 1,500 ٹن بلسٹر کاپر حاصل ہوا (سیندک میٹلز لمیٹڈ)۔

ریکو ڈک

ریکو ڈک ضلع چاغی میں نوکنڈی کے قریب واقع ہے؛ افغان سرحد تقریباً 35 کلومیٹر شمال اور ایرانی سرحد تقریباً 55 کلومیٹر مغرب میں ہے (بیرک تکنیکی رپورٹ)۔ یہ منصوبہ 2011 میں لائسنس کے تنازع کی وجہ سے معطل ہوا۔ 20 مارچ 2022 کے فریم ورک کے تحت 50 فیصد حصہ بیرک کا ہے؛ باقی 50 فیصد میں حکومت بلوچستان کا 10 فیصد مفت حصہ، اس کی ملکیتی کمپنی کا 15 فیصد اور وفاقی سرکاری اداروں کا 25 فیصد شامل ہے (بیرک)۔

1998 کے ایٹمی تجربات

پاکستان نے 28 مئی 1998 کو ضلع چاغی کے راس کوہ کے علاقے میں ایٹمی تجربات کیے۔ 30 مئی 1998 کا تجربہ تقریباً 100 کلومیٹر دور خاران کے صحرا میں کیا گیا، چاغی کے پہاڑوں میں نہیں (فیڈریشن آف امریکن سائنٹسٹس)۔

اس حصے کی ہر بات صفحے پر دیے گئے ذرائع سے جانچی گئی ہے (آخری جانچ: 2026-09-30)۔

Chagai District: FAQ

What is the population of Chagai District?

The 2023 census counted 269,192 people in Chagai District, up from 226,517 in 2017 (annual growth 2.93%). 7.45% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Chagai District?

Chagai District covers 44,748 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 6.02 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Chagai District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Chagai District: Dalbandin (20,054, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Chagai District?

33.15% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 41.9%, female 23.57%).

Which languages are spoken in Chagai District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (73.77% of the 268,611 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Brahui (16.79%), Pashto (8.93%).

Which province is Chagai District in?

Chagai District is in Balochistan, in Rakhshan Division. Its headquarters is Dalbandin.

Which is the largest district of Pakistan by area?

Chagai, at 44,748 km² in the 2023 census. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

Where were Pakistan’s 1998 nuclear tests held?

The 28 May 1998 tests were at Ras Koh in Chagai; the 30 May test was in the Kharan Desert, about 100 km away. (Federation of American Scientists)

Who owns Reko Diq?

Barrick holds 50%. The government of Balochistan holds 25% (10% free-carried plus 15% through a company it owns), and federal state-owned enterprises hold 25%. (Barrick)

When was copper discovered at Saindak?

In 1961, by the Geological Survey of Pakistan. (Saindak Metals Limited)

Barkhan District: Quick Answers

Barkhan District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Koh-i-Suleman Division, with its headquarters at Barkhan. The 2023 census lists 1 administrative units in it: Barkhan. The 2023 census counted 210,249 people on 3,514 km², and 33.62% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Barkhan District

The 2023 census counted 1 tehsils in Barkhan District: Barkhan.

Population and growth

Barkhan District had 210,249 people in the 2023 census, up from 171,025 in 2017 (average annual growth 3.51%). It covers 3,514 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 26th by population and 25th by area. 6.86% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 108.95 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Barkhan (14,425). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (98.62%).

Literacy and schooling

33.62% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 25th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 41.63% and female literacy 24.93%; the urban rate was 57.93% and the rural rate 31.63%. The census counted 46,164 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (28,083 girls and 18,077 boys).

Barkhan District: FAQ

What is the population of Barkhan District?

The 2023 census counted 210,249 people in Barkhan District, up from 171,025 in 2017 (annual growth 3.51%). 6.86% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Barkhan District?

Barkhan District covers 3,514 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 59.83 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Barkhan District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Barkhan District: Barkhan (14,425, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Barkhan District?

33.62% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 41.63%, female 24.93%).

Which languages are spoken in Barkhan District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (98.62% of the 210,249 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Barkhan District in?

Barkhan District is in Balochistan, in Koh-i-Suleman Division. Its headquarters is Barkhan.

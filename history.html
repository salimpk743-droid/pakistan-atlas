History

A short spine of the land that became Pakistan — written so a secondary student can follow it.

Deep time

The Indus cities of Mohenjo-daro and Harappa, the Buddhist landscape of Taxila and Swat, and the medieval ports of the Makran and Thatta all sit on today’s district map. History on this site should always name the modern district so a reader can walk from the atlas into the ruin.

The idea of Pakistan

Allama Iqbal’s 1930 address and Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah’s legal-political work are the two portraits on the home banner for a reason. Students should read primary lines — the 11 August 1947 assembly speech on equal citizenship — not only slogans.

After 1947

Capital moved from Karachi to Islamabad. Provinces were merged and un-merged. East Pakistan became Bangladesh in 1971. The 18th Amendment in 2010 sent schools and hospitals to the provinces. FATA merged into Khyber Pakhtunkhwa in 2018. Each of those turns still shows up when you open a district page.

How to add a history note

Pick one place (Rohtas in Jhelum, Makli in Thatta, Baltit in Hunza, Ziarat Residency). Write 600 words with dates and a source list. That is better for readers and for AdSense than a copied general essay titled “History of Pakistan”.

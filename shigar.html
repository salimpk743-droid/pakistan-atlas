Shigar District: Quick Answers

Shigar District is one of the 10 districts for which Gilgit-Baltistan's Planning & Development Department publishes statistics. The 2023 census counted 84,608 people on 4,173 km², and 62.14% of residents were literate.

Population and growth

Shigar District had 84,608 people in the 2023 census, up from 74,540 in 2017, an average annual growth rate of 2.13%. It covers 4,173 km². The census counted 10,368 households, with an average of 8 people each, and 107 males for every 100 females (GB at a Glance 2025).

How Shigar compares within Gilgit-Baltistan

Among the 10 districts in the GB statistics, Shigar ranks 8th by population, 9th by area and 9th by literacy (62.14% in the 2023 census). Gilgit-Baltistan as a whole counted 1,709,049 people on 72,496 km² (GB at a Glance 2025).

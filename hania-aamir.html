Hania Aamir

Hania Aamir is a Pakistani actress whose television work includes several major ARY Digital dramas. ARY's official artist page currently lists Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum, Mere Humsafar, Meri Zindagi Hai Tu, Ishqiya and Mujhe Pyaar Hua Tha among her featured shows.

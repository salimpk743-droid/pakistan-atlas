Hania Aamir: Latest Dramas and Awards

At the 24th Lux Style Awards in Karachi on 11 December 2025, Hania Aamir won the drama best actress award for Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum, in which she plays Sharjeena opposite Fahad Mustafa, who won the matching best actor award. She then led Meri Zindagi Hai Tu (2025–2026) on ARY Digital as Ayra, opposite Bilal Abbas Khan as Kamyar.

Hania Aamir biography and career

Hania Aamir got her break in cinema before television. She was a student at FAST-NUCES when she auditioned for the romantic comedy Janaan (2016), and she later left college to support her family (The Express Tribune). Titli and Phir Wohi Mohabbat (both 2017) followed on television. The second won her the HUM Award for Best TV Sensation (Female) in 2018 (Daily Pakistan).

What sets her apart from her peers is how easily she moves between registers. Anaa (2019) and Ishqiya (2020) are romantic melodramas. Mere Humsafar (2021–22) is a quiet underdog story. In Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum (2024) she is Sharjeena, sharp and funny, opposite Fahad Mustafa, and the role won her the drama best-actress award at the 24th Lux Style Awards (Geo News). In 2025 she made her Indian Punjabi film debut in Sardaar Ji 3 with Diljit Dosanjh (The Express Tribune). In September 2026 Images Dawn reported the release of her first single, "Pablo".

Her birthday is 12 February: Geo News covered her birthday celebrations in February 2025 and February 2026. The year of birth usually quoted (1997) comes from sources we do not rely on, so this page gives no age.

Hania Aamir's biggest hits

Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum (ARY Digital, 2024)

Her role is Sharjeena, not "Shagufta" as some listings say. She plays the quick-tempered, warm-hearted wife of Fahad Mustafa's Mustafa. Both leads won the drama acting awards at the 24th Lux Style Awards on 11 December 2025 (Geo News). The official ARY upload of episode 1 (2 July 2024) has 88.9 million views, and the lyrical OST "Chal Diye Tum Kahan" has 47.4 million (as of 2 October 2026). See our Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum guide.

Mere Humsafar (ARY Digital, 2021–2022)

As Hala, a young woman mistreated by her relatives, opposite Farhan Saeed, she found a huge audience on YouTube. Episode 1 (30 December 2021) has 68.3 million views on ARY's channel.

Meri Zindagi Hai Tu (ARY Digital, 2025–2026)

She plays Dr Ayra opposite Bilal Abbas Khan's Kamyar. Episode 1 (7 November 2025) has 53.7 million views on the official upload. Our Meri Zindagi Hai Tu page covers the cast and story.

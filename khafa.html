Khafa Story

Khafa follows Tazeen (Hira Mani), a caring schoolteacher who has given her life to raising her younger siblings since her father died, putting her own dreams aside to meet every family responsibility.

Mohtashim (Mikaal Zulfiqar), son of Raza, is born into wealth, but his stepmother Shehla and Rayan make his life miserable, and he chooses a simple, humble life over luxury. When Tazeen is falsely accused of theft, she loses her family's trust and the man she loves; Mohtashim marries her to protect her.

Rayan, who secretly loves Tazeen, grows jealous and, driven by revenge, takes a step that sets off secrets, betrayal and twists. The drama asks whether fate will bring Mohtashim and Tazeen together, whether Tazeen can open her heart to him and whether the truth will finally come out.

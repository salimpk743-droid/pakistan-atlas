157,822 people (2023 census) · 8,531 km² · literacy 69.57%

Ghanche District: Quick Answers

Ghanche District is one of the 10 districts for which Gilgit-Baltistan's Planning & Development Department publishes statistics. The 2023 census counted 157,822 people on 8,531 km², and 69.57% of residents were literate.

Population and growth

Ghanche District had 157,822 people in the 2023 census, up from 156,697 in 2017, an average annual growth rate of 0.12%. It covers 8,531 km². The census counted 21,174 households, with an average of 7 people each, and 112 males for every 100 females (GB at a Glance 2025).

How Ghanche compares within Gilgit-Baltistan

Among the 10 districts in the GB statistics, Ghanche ranks 5th by population, 4th by area and 7th by literacy (69.57% in the 2023 census). Gilgit-Baltistan as a whole counted 1,709,049 people on 72,496 km² (GB at a Glance 2025).

headquarters Jauharabad · 4 tehsils · 1,501,089 people (2023 census)

Khushab District: Quick Answers

Khushab District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Jauharabad. It has 4 tehsils: Khushab, Noor Pur, Nowshera and Quaidabad. The 2023 census counted 1,501,089 people on 6,511 km², and 62.52% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Khushab District

The 2023 census counted 4 tehsils in Khushab District: Khushab, Noor Pur, Nowshera and Quaidabad. Khushab is the most populous, with 816,682 people, and Noor Pur is the largest by area (2,500 km²). Literacy ranges from 55.11% in Quaidabad to 70.43% in Nowshera.

Population and growth

Khushab District had 1,501,089 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,280,372 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.69%). It covers 6,511 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 34th by population and 10th by area. 27.9% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.8 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 8 urban localities; the largest are Khushab (139,905), Jauharabad (113,188), Hadali (45,302) and Mitha Tawana (32,870). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (92.38%), followed by Saraiki (2.39%), Pashto (2.05%) and Urdu (1.43%).

Literacy and schooling

62.52% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 20th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 75.59% and female literacy 49.03%; the urban rate was 72.7% and the rural rate 58.6%. The census counted 84,809 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (45,579 girls and 39,228 boys).

Khushab District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Khushab District?

Khushab District has 4 tehsils: Khushab, Noor Pur, Nowshera and Quaidabad.

What is the population of Khushab District?

The 2023 census counted 1,501,089 people in Khushab District, up from 1,280,372 in 2017 (annual growth 2.69%). 27.9% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Khushab District?

Khushab District covers 6,511 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 230.55 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Khushab District?

The 2023 census lists 8 urban localities in Khushab District: Khushab (139,905, municipal committee), Jauharabad (113,188, municipal committee), Hadali (45,302, municipal committee), Mitha Tawana (32,870, municipal committee), Quaidabad (24,520, municipal committee), Girote (24,283, town committee), Noor Pur (20,100, municipal committee) and Nowshera (18,577, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Khushab District?

62.52% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 75.59%, female 49.03%).

Which languages are spoken in Khushab District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (92.38% of the 1,500,835 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Saraiki (2.39%), Pashto (2.05%), Urdu (1.43%).

Which province is Khushab District in?

Khushab District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Jauharabad.

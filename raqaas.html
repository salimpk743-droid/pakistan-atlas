Raqaas Story

Green Entertainment presents Raqaas as an intense psychological tale about the fragile space between love and obsession, freedom and restraint, and memory and reality, where every relationship carries an unspoken weight and every truth may have another side.

Dawn's review sets out the early story: to fulfil her conservative father Dilawar Khan's wish, Urooj (Komal Meer) becomes a doctor and buries her love of art; after the freedom of university she longs to draw and perhaps dance, but her strict family objects even to an anklet. Her easy friendship with her cousin Fawad (Hunain Shahid) changes when she learns her father has chosen Fawad as her husband.

The household hides a dark secret, and a mysterious new neighbour, the musician Hamza (Danyal Zafar), draws Urooj towards him with his haunting music. The reviewer notes that the script hints at a past stained by murder and asks whether Urooj could become the next victim in the name of honour.

Zanjeerain Drama: Cast and Story

Zanjeerain (زنجیریں) is a romance drama on HUM TV, written by Farhat Ishtiaq and directed by Shehzad Kashmiri, starring Sajal Aly, Danyal Zafar.

Zanjeerain Story

Zanjeerain (Chains) is a HUM TV romance drama set against the mountains of Azad Kashmir. Its story is built around love, power and a long feud between rival families, with Sajal Aly as Rabiya, known as Biya, at the centre.

Rabiya is caught between the men and families around her: Danyal Zafar plays Sar Buland, Ameer Gilani plays Mudassir, Ahsan Khan plays Sher Dil and Adnan Siddiqui plays Zafran Khan. Confrontations between the rival families drive the drama, and scenes between Rabiya and Sar Buland drew strong attention online during its run.

Writer, Director and Production of Zanjeerain

The serial is written by Farhat Ishtiaq, whose earlier dramas include Humsafar and Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum, and directed by Shehzad Kashmiri, director of Parizaad. According to published credits it is their second collaboration after Yaqeen Ka Safar (2017). Momina Duraid Productions produced it for HUM TV's 2026 line-up.

Producer / production house: Momina Duraid Productions.

When and Where to Watch Zanjeerain

Zanjeerain airs on HUM TV. It premiered on 29 April 2026. Schedules and repeat timings change, so check the official HUM TV website for the latest episode and timing. Channels also upload episodes to their official YouTube channels.

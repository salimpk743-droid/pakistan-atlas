Nasirabad District: Quick Answers

Nasirabad District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Naseerabad Division, with its headquarters at Dera Murad Jamali. The 2023 census lists 6 administrative units in it: Baba Kot, Chattar, Dera Murad Jamali, Landhi, Mir Hassan Khosa and Tamboo. The 2023 census counted 563,315 people on 3,387 km², and 28.96% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Nasirabad District

The 2023 census counted 6 administrative units in Nasirabad District: Baba Kot (tehsil), Chattar (sub-division), Dera Murad Jamali (sub-division), Landhi (tehsil), Mir Hassan Khosa (tehsil) and Tamboo (tehsil). Dera Murad Jamali is the most populous, with 265,822 people, and Baba Kot is the largest by area (967 km²). Literacy ranges from 15.41% in Baba Kot to 34.93% in Dera Murad Jamali.

Population and growth

Nasirabad District had 563,315 people in the 2023 census, up from 489,779 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.37%). It covers 3,387 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 7th by population and 27th by area. 18.99% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.54 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Dera Murad Jamali (106,952). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (43.21%), followed by Brahui (22.77%), Sindhi (21.16%) and Saraiki (12.15%).

Literacy and schooling

28.96% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 30th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 36.43% and female literacy 21.18%; the urban rate was 48.34% and the rural rate 24.36%. The census counted 151,207 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (78,014 girls and 73,183 boys).

Nasirabad District: FAQ

What is the population of Nasirabad District?

The 2023 census counted 563,315 people in Nasirabad District, up from 489,779 in 2017 (annual growth 2.37%). 18.99% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Nasirabad District?

Nasirabad District covers 3,387 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 166.32 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Nasirabad District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Nasirabad District: Dera Murad Jamali (106,952, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Nasirabad District?

28.96% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 36.43%, female 21.18%).

Which languages are spoken in Nasirabad District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (43.21% of the 563,315 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Brahui (22.77%), Sindhi (21.16%), Saraiki (12.15%).

Which province is Nasirabad District in?

Nasirabad District is in Balochistan, in Naseerabad Division. Its headquarters is Dera Murad Jamali.

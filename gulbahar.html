Gulbahar Drama: Cast and Story

Gulbahar (گل بہار) is a family drama on HUM TV, written by Aliya Bukhari and directed by Mehreen Jabbar, starring Sanam Saeed, Emmad Irfani.

Gulbahar Story

Gulbahar is a new HUM TV family drama about Sara (Sanam Saeed) and Sheheryar (Emmad Irfani), a couple deeply in love whose marriage comes under pressure because Sara cannot have children.

Komal Meer plays Diya, Sara's younger relative and close friend, whose own engagement falls apart. As older women in the family push a second marriage as the only answer, the story sets up rivalry and heartbreak between two women who care for each other. Dawn's reviewer criticised the familiar second-wife trope but praised Mehreen Jabbar's bright, brief and character-focused scenes.

Writer, Director and Production of Gulbahar

Gulbahar is written by Aliya Bukhari and directed by Mehreen Jabbar, one of Pakistan's best-known television directors. It is produced by Momina Duraid Productions for HUM TV.

Producer / production house: Momina Duraid Productions.

When and Where to Watch Gulbahar

Gulbahar airs on HUM TV, Wednesday and Thursday, 8:00 pm (repeat Thursday and Friday, 9:00 am) (Pakistan time). Schedules and repeat timings change, so check the official HUM TV website for the latest episode and timing. Channels also upload episodes to their official YouTube channels.

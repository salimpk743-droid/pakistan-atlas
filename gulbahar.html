Gulbahar Drama: Cast and Story, Episode Guide and OST

Gulbahar (گل بہار) is a family drama on HUM TV, written by Aliya Bukhari and directed by Mehreen Jabbar, starring Sanam Saeed, Emmad Irfani.

ChannelHUM TV
WriterAliya Bukhari
DirectorMehreen Jabbar
YearSeptember 2026

Gulbahar Story

Gulbahar is a new HUM TV family drama about Sara (Sanam Saeed) and Sheheryar (Emmad Irfani), a couple deeply in love whose marriage comes under pressure because Sara cannot have children.

Komal Meer plays Diya, Sara's younger relative and close friend, whose own engagement falls apart. As older women in the family push a second marriage as the only answer, the story sets up rivalry and heartbreak between two women who care for each other. Dawn's reviewer criticised the familiar second-wife trope but praised Mehreen Jabbar's bright, brief and character-focused scenes.

Gulbahar Story So Far (Episodes 1 to 4)

Gulbahar opens as the story of Sara (Sanam Saeed) and Sheheryar (Emmad Irfani), a loving couple who first met at Frere Hall in Karachi and have been married for twelve years without children. The pressure comes mainly from Sheheryar's widowed elder sister Shehnaz, who is like a mother to him and wants an heir for his business; she keeps pushing a second marriage, which Sara refuses, and she dismisses adoption as something the family would never accept.

Running alongside is the story of Diya (Komal Meer), the bright, studious daughter of Sara's neighbours Ahmed and Ruqaiya, who are close to the couple. Diya is expected to marry her cousin Azar, but his mother Samina, Ahmed's sister, looks down on the family and wants a wealthier match. At the silver-jubilee party Diya throws for her parents, Samina humiliates her and ends the engagement.

By Episode 3 tragedy strikes: a gas cylinder explosion kills Ahmed and Ruqaiya, leaving Diya alone. Her aunt's family turns away, Azar leaves for abroad without standing by her, and in Episode 4 Sara and Sheheryar take the grieving, feverish Diya into their home, over Shehnaz's objections. That sets up the triangle the reviews point to, but as of Episode 5 nothing beyond this had been shown on screen.

Gulbahar airs two episodes a week on HUM TV (Wednesday and Thursday at 8:00 pm); HUM TV's promos say each episode is also uploaded to its official YouTube channel at 9:00 pm on the same day. Five episodes had aired by 30 September 2026, and HUM TV released the Episode 6 teaser the same day.

Writer, Director and Production of Gulbahar

Gulbahar is written by Aliya Bukhari and directed by Mehreen Jabbar, one of Pakistan's best-known television directors. It is produced by Momina Duraid Productions for HUM TV.

Producer / production house: Momina Duraid Productions.

When and Where to Watch Gulbahar

Gulbahar airs on HUM TV, Wednesday and Thursday, 8:00 pm (repeat Thursday and Friday, 9:00 am) (Pakistan time). Schedules and repeat timings change, so check the official HUM TV website for the latest episode and timing. Channels also upload episodes to their official YouTube channels.

Gulbahar OST

The Gulbahar original soundtrack is sung by Quratulain Balouch and Hassan Badshah, with composition and lyrics by Hassan Badshah and Fatima Najeeb. HUM TV released it on 16 September 2026, the day of the premiere; the official OST upload had 783,702 views as of 1 October 2026. See the Pakistani drama OSTs guide for more soundtracks.

Gulbahar Premiere, Timing and Episode Count

HUM TV released the first teaser on 30 August 2026 and the drama premiered on Wednesday 16 September 2026. It airs every Wednesday and Thursday at 8:00 pm, with repeats on Thursday and Friday at 9:00 am, and five episodes had aired by 30 September 2026. On YouTube, HUM TV lists Vgo Tel as the digital presenter and Master Paints as the digital sponsor, and describes Gulbahar as a story of emotions, relationships and the truths hidden between the lines.

Gulbahar Last Episode

The last episode aired so far is Episode 5, shown on Wednesday 30 September 2026. HUM TV has not announced the total number of episodes or a date for the final episode in the sources used here, so this page does not give one.

Filming and Production Notes

The official material and the news reports used for this page do not list the filming locations. In the story, Sara and Sheheryar first met at Frere Hall in Karachi. Speaking to The Express Tribune, Mehreen Jabbar described an intense shoot in the summer heat, with air conditioners failing on set. The drama reunites Sanam Saeed and Emmad Irfani, who played Zeba and Jami in Kafeel.

Gulbahar Reception

Reviews so far are mixed. In The News (20 September 2026), Gaitee Ara Siddiqi found the lead pair's chemistry a little disappointing next to Zeba and Jami in Kafeel and called the story a bit clichéd, but singled out the actress playing Diya's paternal aunt as the strongest link and suggested the drama could still surprise viewers, as Aap Ki Izzat did. In Dawn ICON (27 September 2026), Sadaf Haider criticised the “second wife as the only answer to infertility” trope and the way adoption is brushed aside, while praising Mehreen Jabbar's bright, brief, character-focused scenes.

Online interest has been strong: the Episode 1 upload on HUM TV's YouTube channel had passed six million views by 1 October 2026, about two weeks after it went up (see the episode guide above for the figures as of 1 October 2026).

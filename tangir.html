Tangir District

Diamer division · notified as a district in June 2019

Tangir District: Quick Answers

Tangir District is one of four sub-divisions that the Gilgit-Baltistan government notified as separate districts in June 2019. The official 2023 statistics still report 10 districts, with no separate figures for Tangir.

District status

The Gilgit-Baltistan government notified Tangir sub-division, in Diamer division, as a separate district in June 2019, together with Rondu, Gupis-Yasin and Darel. The notification, issued under Section 5 of the Land Revenue Act 1967, kept the same territorial jurisdiction and boundaries it had as a sub-division, and took Gilgit-Baltistan to 14 districts (Dawn).

Official statistics

The GB Planning & Development Department's statistics for the 2023 census still report 10 districts and give no separate figures for Tangir (GB at a Glance 2025). No population or area is shown here until an official figure for the district is published.

Tangir District: FAQ

When did Tangir become a district?

The Gilgit-Baltistan government notified Tangir sub-division as a separate district in June 2019, with unchanged boundaries. (Dawn)

What is the population of Tangir District?

No official figure has been published: the GB statistics for the 2023 census still report 10 districts, without Tangir. (P&DD Gilgit-Baltistan)

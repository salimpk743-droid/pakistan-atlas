Mitti De Baway Drama: Cast and Story, Episode Guide and OST

Mitti De Baway is a rural punjabi drama on Green Entertainment (Green TV), written by Faiza Iftikhar and directed by Syed Wajahat Hussain, starring Mahira Khan, Wahaj Ali.

ChannelGreen Entertainment (Green TV)

DirectorSyed Wajahat Hussain

Mitti De Baway Story

Mitti De Baway is a large-scale Green TV drama set in rural Punjab. It marks Mahira Khan's return to television after several years focused mainly on films, and pairs her with Wahaj Ali.

Mahira Khan plays Billo, a strong and intense woman at the centre of the story, and Wahaj Ali plays Bala, a raw and ruthless character that is a departure from his earlier roles; he underwent a physical transformation for the part. Sohail Ahmed plays an influential figure in Bala's journey, Amar Khan takes on an intense Punjabi character, and parts of the story go back to the 1990s, with Aena Khan and Fazal Hussain as the younger Billo and Bala.

Green TV Entertainment's official episode descriptions give the leads' full names. Mahira Khan plays Bilqees Bano, known as Billo, and Wahaj Ali plays Iqbal Channan, known as Baala (spelt Bala in some reports). The official synopsis describes Bano as a woman from a respected family in Wazirabad who falls in love with Baala, a dhol player from Mitranawali. Bound by family honour, she denies that love. Years later the two meet again in Faisalabad, both married with children, and the synopsis says their story ends in tragedy when an old enemy returns.

On-screen captions in the aired episodes move between three periods: Mitranwali and Wazirabad in 1980, Faisalabad (called Lyallpur by the characters) in 1996, and Kot Lakhpat Jail in Lahore in 1999. Pre-launch coverage of the trailer in Images and The Express Tribune mentioned later dates, but the aired episodes date the younger Billo and Baala's romance to 1980.

Mitti De Baway Drama Story So Far (Episodes 1 to 4)

Mitti De Baway tells one love story across three timelines. The first episode opens at the end: in 1999 Iqbal Channan, known as Baala (Wahaj Ali), walks out of prison a hero to the inmates and is ambushed at a graveyard. The drama then shows how it came to that. In 1996 Faisalabad, Iqbal is a respected cloth merchant with a wife, a son and a family he supports. The pious Bilqees Bano (Mahira Khan), revered as the chosen successor of a pir, has just moved into his neighbourhood as the wife of Nazeer Shah (Junaid Khan).

The flashbacks to 1980 explain why the two cannot simply be neighbours. Baala was the son of a family of musicians from Mitranwali, and Billo was the daughter of an influential Wazirabad family who was about to be married off. She flirted with him, denied him, called him the son of a minstrel and went ahead with her wedding. That wedding turned bloody when a rival suitor's men killed her brother.

By the fourth episode the past has caught up with the present. Bilqees has made Iqbal swear on his son to stay silent, but she then helps the Shahs reject his brother's proposal by exposing Iqbal's low birth. The man Phajja called a hidden villain begins to turn. He speaks of eloping and of breaking his oath, and goes back to where it all began.

Writer, Director and Production of Mitti De Baway

The drama is written by Faiza Iftikhar and directed by Syed Wajahat Hussain, the director of Khaie. Uzma Hassan reunites with him after Khaie.

The official episode descriptions credit Tehreem Chaudhary as producer. The News names Saleem Daad and Zaid Safdar Sandhu as the directors of photography. On YouTube the episodes are digitally presented by VGO TEL, powered by Happilac Paints and associated with Vince shampoo, with VELO as a digital partner.

When and Where to Watch Mitti De Baway

Mitti De Baway airs on Green Entertainment (Green TV), Wednesday and Thursday, 8:00 pm (Pakistan time). It premiered on 23 September 2026. Official schedule: Green Entertainment website.

Each episode has gone up on the official Green TV Entertainment YouTube channel, with English subtitles, on the night it airs. The first four uploads appeared between about 8:45 pm and 9:35 pm Pakistan time, and each runs about 32 to 36 minutes. By 2 October 2026 the official Green Entertainment website did not yet list Mitti De Baway in its schedule, so we do not give repeat timings.

Mahira Khan and Wahaj Ali Drama: The Lead Pair

Mitti De Baway is the first television pairing of Mahira Khan and Wahaj Ali, a point Images and The Express Tribune both made when the trailer came out. Every official episode upload is billed "Wahaj Ali - Mahira Khan". Images described Billo as a lovestruck girl who becomes a cold-hearted spiritual leader, and Baala as the romantic interest of her youth who comes back from jail hardened and handy with a gun. The News review said the two "set the screen alight". Before the premiere, the Tribune reported that Mahira had thanked director Wajahat Hussain for shaping Billo, writing that he "knew her more than I ever could".

Mitti De Baway OST and Music

Music is woven into the story. Baala comes from a family of musicians, and the official clips and episodes use Punjabi folk songs and Sufi poetry. By 2 October 2026, however, Green TV Entertainment had not posted a separate full OST video on its official YouTube channel, and neither the channel nor the outlets used for this page had published the OST singers' credits. We therefore give no OST credits or view counts yet. Credits circulating on unofficial channels are not confirmed, so we do not repeat them. See the Pakistani drama OSTs guide for other soundtracks.

Mitti De Baway Reception

The first review in the outlets used here was positive. Writing in The News on Sunday on 27 September 2026, after two episodes, Gaitee Ara Siddiqi called the result "mind blowing", praised the performances, the Punjabi dialogue and the aerial photography, and described the drama as "Definitely Netflix material". Her verdict was "Worth a watch every Wednesday and Thursday at 8pm." Before the premiere, The Express Tribune reported that actors including Kinza Hashmi, Bilal Abbas, Maya Ali, Iqra Aziz and Yasir Hussain had praised the trailer.

Official figures show the size of the audience online. The Episode 1 upload passed 18 million views on Green TV Entertainment's YouTube channel within nine days, and the four episodes together had more than 36 million views (see the episode guide for exact figures as of 2 October 2026 (read at about 11:55 am Pakistan time)).

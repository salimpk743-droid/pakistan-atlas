Ayeza Khan biography

Born Kanza Khan on 15 January 1991 in Karachi (The News), Ayeza Khan entered television through modelling. Her first part was a supporting role in the Ramadan series Tum Jo Miley (2009), and for most of the next decade she worked almost without a break. Our verified list has 38 serials.

The turning point came in 2013–14 with Pyarey Afzal on ARY Digital. Her Farah, opposite Hamza Ali Abbasi, won the Lux Style Award for Best TV Actress (Daily Times). Mere Meherbaan, Tum Kon Piya and Koi Chand Rakh kept her among the top leads. Then came Meray Paas Tum Ho (2019), in which she played the woman who leaves her husband (Humayun Saeed) for a rich man. It became one of the most-watched and most-debated serials of its time.

She lightened up in Chupke Chupke (HUM TV, 2021), a Ramadan comedy with Osman Khalid Butt that won her a Lux Style Award (Viewers' Choice) and a HUM Award. She has since returned to drama with Mein (2023), as a woman with bipolar disorder, and Jaan-e-Jahan (2023–24), reuniting with Hamza Ali Abbasi.

Ayeza Khan's biggest hits

Meray Paas Tum Ho (ARY Digital, 2019–2020)

Written by Khalil-ur-Rehman Qamar, this is the serial Pakistanis still argue about. As Mehwish, Ayeza played a character written to be disliked, and played her without asking for sympathy. The official OST upload on ARY Digital has 221.5 million views, and episode 1 has 35.8 million (as of 2 October 2026).

Pyarey Afzal (ARY Digital, 2013–2014)

Her Farah, the woman Afzal loves and loses, won the Lux Style Award for Best TV Actress. ARY's 2021 re-upload of episode 1 has 6.5 million views.

Chupke Chupke (HUM TV, 2021)

A Ramadan comedy with Osman Khalid Butt that went on to the season's awards. She won Best TV Actress (Viewers' Choice) at the 21st Lux Style Awards (Business Recorder) and best actress (popular) at the 8th HUM Awards. Episode 1 on HUM TV's channel has 63.8 million views.

Mein and Jaan-e-Jahan (ARY Digital, 2023–2024)

These are her two most recent ARY leads. In Mein, opposite Wahaj Ali, she plays Mubashira, a woman with bipolar disorder, and episode 1 has 33.7 million views. Jaan-e-Jahan reunited her with Hamza Ali Abbasi, and its episode 1 has 21.5 million.

Shaheed Benazirabad District

formerly Nawabshah District · headquarters Nawabshah · 4 talukas · 1,845,102 people (2023 census)

Shaheed Benazirabad District: Quick Answers

Shaheed Benazirabad District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Nawabshah. It has 4 talukas: Daur, Kazi Ahmed, Nawabshah and Sakrand. The 2023 census counted 1,845,102 people on 4,502 km², and 50.86% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

From Nawabshah to Shaheed Benazirabad

Nawabshah taluka was set up in 1907 and upgraded to Nawabshah District in 1912, named after Syed Nawab Shah, who settled in the area in 1881. At its creation the district had seven talukas: Kandiaro, Nausheroferoze, Moro, Sakrand, Nawabshah, Sanjhoro and Shahdadpur. In 2008 the Provincial Assembly of Sindh unanimously passed a resolution renaming it Shaheed Benazirabad in honour of Benazir Bhutto (district government website, archived). The headquarters town is still called Nawabshah. It was originally called Nasrat and is linked by road and rail with Karachi, Hyderabad and Sukkur (Britannica).

Talukas of Shaheed Benazirabad District

The 2023 census counted 4 talukas in Shaheed Benazirabad District: Daur, Kazi Ahmed, Nawabshah and Sakrand. Daur is the most populous, with 532,621 people, and it is also the largest by area (2,210 km²). Literacy ranges from 41.36% in Daur to 62.44% in Nawabshah.

Population and growth

Shaheed Benazirabad District had 1,845,102 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,613,506 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.27%). It covers 4,502 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 12th by population and 13th by area. 32.42% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.18 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 9 urban localities; the largest are Nawabshah (363,138), Sakrand (72,040), Bandhi (36,588) and Daur (35,662). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (85.2%), followed by Urdu (6.17%), Punjabi (3.63%) and Brahui (1.64%).

Literacy and schooling

50.86% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 12th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 61.07% and female literacy 40.29%; the urban rate was 64.91% and the rural rate 43.87%. The census counted 259,216 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (148,059 girls and 111,152 boys).

Shaheed Benazirabad District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Shaheed Benazirabad District?

Shaheed Benazirabad District has 4 talukas: Daur, Kazi Ahmed, Nawabshah and Sakrand.

What is the population of Shaheed Benazirabad District?

The 2023 census counted 1,845,102 people in Shaheed Benazirabad District, up from 1,613,506 in 2017 (annual growth 2.27%). 32.42% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Shaheed Benazirabad District?

Shaheed Benazirabad District covers 4,502 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 409.84 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Shaheed Benazirabad District?

The 2023 census lists 9 urban localities in Shaheed Benazirabad District: Nawabshah (363,138, municipal committee), Sakrand (72,040, town committee), Bandhi (36,588, town committee), Daur (35,662, town committee), Kazi Ahmed (28,610, town committee), Jam Sahib (19,127, town committee), Buchery (18,798, town committee), Daulatpur (14,508, town committee) and Shahpur Jahania (9,649, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Shaheed Benazirabad District?

50.86% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 61.07%, female 40.29%).

Which languages are spoken in Shaheed Benazirabad District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (85.2% of the 1,844,627 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (6.17%), Punjabi (3.63%), Brahui (1.64%).

Which province is Shaheed Benazirabad District in?

Shaheed Benazirabad District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Nawabshah.

What is the old name of Shaheed Benazirabad?

Nawabshah. Nawabshah District, created in 1912 and named after Syed Nawab Shah, was renamed Shaheed Benazirabad in 2008 by a unanimous resolution of the Sindh Assembly. The headquarters town is still called Nawabshah. (District Government Shaheed Benazirabad (archived))

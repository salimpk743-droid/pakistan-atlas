Abbottabad District

Hazara hill district · headquarters Abbottabad · 4 tehsils · 1,419,072 people (2023 census) · highest literacy in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Abbottabad District: Quick Answers

Abbottabad District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Abbottabad. It has 4 tehsils: Abbottabad, Havelian, Lora and Lower Tanawal. The 2023 census counted 1,419,072 people on 1,967 km², and 77.34% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Abbottabad District

Abbottabad District has four tehsils: Abbottabad, Havelian, Lora and Lower Tanawal. Abbottabad tehsil holds most of the people, 1,003,339 of the district's 1,419,072 in 2023, on 1,285 km². Havelian has 256,754, Lora 98,717 and Lower Tanawal 60,262.

Population and literacy

The 2023 census counted 1,419,072 people on 1,967 km², and 23.42% of them lived in urban areas. The largest urban localities are Abbottabad Cantonment (159,683) and Abbottabad municipal committee (74,712).

Abbottabad's literacy rate of 77.34% (age 10 and over) was the highest of any Khyber Pakhtunkhwa district in the 2023 census. Its female literacy rate, 68.42%, was also the highest in the province.

History

The town of Abbottabad was founded in 1853. It is named after Major James Abbott, the first British deputy commissioner of the region (Britannica). The Pakistan Military Academy was set up at Kakul, near Abbottabad, in October 1947, soon after independence (Pakistan Army).

Geography and how to reach Abbottabad

Abbottabad is a hill station at 1,256 metres (4,120 ft). It sits on a plateau at the southern corner of the Rash (Orash) Plain, 61 km northeast of Rawalpindi, and is the gateway to the Kagan Valley. Roads link it to the Indus plain and to Kashmir. The nearest railhead is at Havelian, 16 km to the south (Britannica).

Abbottabad District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Abbottabad District?

Abbottabad District has 4 tehsils: Abbottabad, Havelian, Lora and Lower Tanawal.

What is the population of Abbottabad District?

The 2023 census counted 1,419,072 people in Abbottabad District, up from 1,333,089 in 2017 (annual growth 1.05%). 23.42% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Abbottabad District?

Abbottabad District covers 1,967 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 721.44 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Abbottabad District?

The 2023 census lists 6 urban localities in Abbottabad District: Abbottabad Cantonment (159,683, cantonment), Abbottabad (74,712, municipal committee), Nawan Shehr (40,711, town committee), Havelian Cantonment (37,509, cantonment), Havelian (18,916, municipal committee) and Murree Gallies Cantonment (784, cantonment).

What is the literacy rate of Abbottabad District?

77.34% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 86.2%, female 68.42%).

Which languages are spoken in Abbottabad District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Hindko (87.43% of the 1,397,587 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Pashto (5.85%), Urdu (3.06%).

Which province is Abbottabad District in?

Abbottabad District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Abbottabad.

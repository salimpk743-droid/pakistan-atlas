Bajaur District

Headquarters: Khar · Census 2023 · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Bajaur was a tribal agency on the Afghan border until it became a settled KP district after the 2018 FATA merger.

Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is Khar. The available public data note is Census 2023. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Education

Degree colleges have been expanding steadily since the merger, though the district is still catching up with older KP districts.

Hospitals and healthcare

DHQ Bajaur serves the district's main population centres.

Culture and geography

The valleys are home to Pashtun communities along the border.

Public issues

Building out full provincial services after decades under separate tribal administration remains an ongoing task.

Villages and local places

Nawagai and the Mamund valleys.

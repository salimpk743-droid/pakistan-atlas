Nowshera District

Peshawar Division · Kabul River and Grand Trunk Road · 1,740,705 people in 2023 · three tehsils with almost the same literacy

Nowshera District: Quick Answers

Nowshera District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Nowshera. It has 3 tehsils: Jehangira, Nowshera and Pabbi. The 2023 census counted 1,740,705 people on 1,748 km², and 56.78% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Nowshera district sits on the Kabul River and the Grand Trunk Road east of Peshawar, in Peshawar Division. The 2023 census counted 1,740,705 people on 1,748 km², or 995.83 people per km². That is up from 1,520,995 in 2017, a growth rate of 2.28 percent a year. There are 103.78 men for every 100 women.

Pashto is the mother tongue of about 95 percent of residents: 1,649,524 of the 1,735,689 people in the census mother-tongue table. Hindko comes second with 39,058 speakers, and 24,494 of them live in Jehangira tehsil.

Towns and cantonments

In 2023, 19.64 percent of the district lived in urban areas. The census lists eight urban localities with 341,959 people between them:

  • Nowshera Kalan municipal committee: 88,472
  • Jehangira municipal committee: 57,011
  • Pabbi municipal committee: 52,701
  • Amangarh town committee: 42,159
  • Risalpur Cantonment: 36,074
  • Nowshera Cantonment: 34,481
  • Akora Khattak municipal committee: 29,750
  • Cherat Cantonment: 1,311

Three tehsils, literacy almost flat

The district's literacy rate (age 10 and over) is 56.78 percent: 68.53 percent for men and 44.49 percent for women. It is 68.23 percent in urban areas and 53.90 percent in rural ones. Unusually for KP, all three tehsils fall within a quarter of a point of each other.

Nowshera tehsil: 679 km², 796,226 people, 25.43 percent urban, literacy 56.76 percent. It contains Nowshera Kalan, Amangarh and the Nowshera, Risalpur and Cherat cantonments.

Pabbi tehsil: 351 km², 509,495 people, 10.34 percent urban, literacy 56.70 percent. It is the smallest tehsil by area and the most densely populated, at 1,451.55 people per km².

Jehangira tehsil: 718 km², 434,984 people, 19.95 percent urban, literacy 56.92 percent. It contains Jehangira and Akora Khattak.

The census counted 140,214 out-of-school children aged 5 to 16 in the district. Of these, 85,710 are girls.

Institutions

The Pakistan Air Force Academy at Risalpur was renamed PAF Academy Asghar Khan, after the air force's first Muslim commander-in-chief (APP). Darul Uloom Haqqania, a seminary founded in 1947, stands on the Grand Trunk Road at Akora Khattak, about 60 km east of Peshawar (Dawn, 1 March 2025). Dawn describes the Qazi Hussain Ahmad Medical Complex in Nowshera as the district's main medical teaching institute.

The 2010 flood

At the end of July 2010 the Kabul River burst its banks. Al Jazeera reported on 2 August 2010 that the city of Nowshera, a garrison town on the river, was "completely inundated". Residents waited on rooftops and water tanks for rescue.

Nowshera District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Nowshera District?

Nowshera District has 3 tehsils: Jehangira, Nowshera and Pabbi.

What is the population of Nowshera District?

The 2023 census counted 1,740,705 people in Nowshera District, up from 1,520,995 in 2017 (annual growth 2.28%). 19.64% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Nowshera District?

Nowshera District covers 1,748 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 995.83 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Nowshera District?

The 2023 census lists 8 urban localities in Nowshera District: Nowshera Kalan (88,472, municipal committee), Jehangira (57,011, municipal committee), Pabbi (52,701, municipal committee), Amangarh (42,159, town committee), Risalpur Cantonment (36,074, cantonment), Nowshera Cantonment (34,481, cantonment), Akora Khattak (29,750, municipal committee) and Cherat Cantonment (1,311, cantonment).

What is the literacy rate of Nowshera District?

56.78% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 68.53%, female 44.49%).

Which languages are spoken in Nowshera District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (95.04% of the 1,735,689 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Hindko (2.25%), Punjabi (1.08%).

Which province is Nowshera District in?

Nowshera District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Nowshera.

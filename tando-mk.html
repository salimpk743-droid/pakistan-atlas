Tando Muhammad Khan District: Quick Answers

Tando Muhammad Khan District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Tando Muhammad Khan. It has 3 talukas: Bulri Shah Karim, Tando Ghulam Hyder and Tando Muhammad Khan. The 2023 census counted 726,119 people on 1,423 km², and 34.02% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Tando Muhammad Khan District

The 2023 census counted 3 talukas in Tando Muhammad Khan District: Bulri Shah Karim, Tando Ghulam Hyder and Tando Muhammad Khan. Tando Muhammad Khan is the most populous, with 272,427 people, and Bulri Shah Karim is the largest by area (770 km²). Literacy ranges from 27.15% in Bulri Shah Karim to 42.7% in Tando Muhammad Khan.

Population and growth

Tando Muhammad Khan District had 726,119 people in the 2023 census, up from 677,098 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.18%). It covers 1,423 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 30th by population and 22nd by area. 22.33% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 107.84 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 3 urban localities: Tando Muhammad Khan (114,406), Tando Ghulam Hyder (41,277) and Bulri Shah Karim (6,459). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (93.54%), followed by Hindko (2.01%) and Urdu (1.4%).

Literacy and schooling

34.02% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 28th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 42.34% and female literacy 25.02%; the urban rate was 53.05% and the rural rate 28.22%. The census counted 141,821 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (73,574 girls and 68,246 boys).

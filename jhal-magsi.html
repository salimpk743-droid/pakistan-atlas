Jhal Magsi District: Quick Answers

Jhal Magsi District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Naseerabad Division, with its headquarters at Gandawa. The 2023 census lists 3 administrative units in it: Gandawa, Jhal Magsi and Mirpur. The 2023 census counted 203,368 people on 3,615 km², and 30.14% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Jhal Magsi District

The 2023 census counted 3 administrative units in Jhal Magsi District: Gandawa (tehsil), Jhal Magsi (tehsil) and Mirpur (sub-tehsil). Jhal Magsi is the most populous, with 116,528 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,679 km²). Literacy ranges from 24.86% in Mirpur to 36.35% in Gandawa.

Population and growth

Jhal Magsi District had 203,368 people in the 2023 census, up from 148,900 in 2017 (average annual growth 5.35%). It covers 3,615 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 29th by population and 23rd by area. 11.87% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.49 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Gandawa (24,130). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (75.43%), followed by Sindhi (16.33%), Saraiki (5.76%) and Brahui (2.09%).

Literacy and schooling

30.14% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 29th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 37.89% and female literacy 22.09%; the urban rate was 50.5% and the rural rate 27.15%. The census counted 50,408 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (27,430 girls and 22,976 boys).

Jhal Magsi District: FAQ

What is the population of Jhal Magsi District?

The 2023 census counted 203,368 people in Jhal Magsi District, up from 148,900 in 2017 (annual growth 5.35%). 11.87% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Jhal Magsi District?

Jhal Magsi District covers 3,615 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 56.26 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Jhal Magsi District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Jhal Magsi District: Gandawa (24,130, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Jhal Magsi District?

30.14% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 37.89%, female 22.09%).

Which languages are spoken in Jhal Magsi District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (75.43% of the 203,368 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Sindhi (16.33%), Saraiki (5.76%), Brahui (2.09%).

Which province is Jhal Magsi District in?

Jhal Magsi District is in Balochistan, in Naseerabad Division. Its headquarters is Gandawa.

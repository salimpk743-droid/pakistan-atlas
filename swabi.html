Swabi District

Mardan Division · headquarters Swabi · 1,894,600 people in 2023 · Topi, Lahor and Razar on the same plain

Swabi is the Yusufzai plain between Mardan and the Indus. The town of Swabi is the headquarters. Topi sits toward Tarbela. Lahor is the Indus-facing tehsil. Razar is the largest count. This is farm country and campus country at the same time. Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute at Topi is the degree name the rest of Pakistan already uses. The district is not only that campus.

Pashto is the language of the houses. Topi tehsil’s 2023 row also prints about 21,000 Hindko speakers beside 352,000 Pashto speakers. That is the Tarbela-facing mix.

2023 sheet

Households 278,976. Population 1,894,600. Area 1,543 km². Urban about 340,000. Rural about 1.55 million. Literacy 58.48 percent — 72.34 percent for men, 44.50 percent for women. Under-tens about 520,000 (28 percent). Density is above 1,200 people per km². Site: swabi.kp.gov.pk.

Topi municipal count in city lists is about 74,900. Swabi town is the other urban name. Most of Razar still prints as village.

Four tehsils

Razar — 418 km², 682,303 people, literacy about 56.6 percent. Largest tehsil by population.

Swabi — 389 km², 475,352 people, literacy about 63.2 percent. Headquarters. Highest literacy of the four.

Topi — 418 km², 382,562 people, literacy about 59.8 percent. GIKI and the Tarbela road.

Lahor — 318 km², 354,383 people, literacy about 54.2 percent. Lowest literacy of the four. Indus side.

Guns and dushmani

Swabi has a public reputation for family feud — dushmani — and for keeping weapons in the house as if they were ordinary tools. That reputation is not a compliment. A quarrel over land, a marriage slight, or an old killing is allowed to run across years. Young men are pulled into it. Funerals follow. Schools lose days. Women keep children indoors after dark on streets that should be ordinary.

The gun is treated in too many hujras as izzat. It is not izzat. It is a habit that empties houses. Jirgas sometimes cool a case. Sometimes they freeze it until the next shot. Police files in this district include the same family names more than once. None of that is culture worth teaching a child to copy. Tobacco, sugarcane and a GIKI seat are the district’s better face. The feud is the face that keeps the better face from showing.

This page records the pattern because students already hear it. It does not explain how a feud is armed or how a score is settled.

Campus and hospital

GIKI at Topi is the engineering campus. Government colleges sit in Swabi, Topi and Lahor. DHQ Swabi is the first public hospital. Tarbela’s labour market and the Indus road pay part of Topi. Female literacy at 45 percent is stronger than Shangla; it is still the line that decides whether a girl in Razar finishes school after a shooting on her street.

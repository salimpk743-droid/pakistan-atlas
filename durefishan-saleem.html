Durefishan Saleem biography

Durefishan Saleem has had one of the fastest climbs in recent Pakistani television. She moved to Karachi in 2019 with a law degree and no screen credits. By the end of 2023 she was the female lead of Ishq Murshid, and the first episode of that HUM TV serial has now passed 94 million views on YouTube.

Images Dawn reports that she was born in Lahore to Saleem-ul-Hassan, who directed and produced for PTV in the 2000s, and her mother Shumaila Saleem. She has two brothers and a sister. She studied law (LLB) at University College London before deciding to act. Her date of birth has not been published by any of the outlets we rely on, so this page does not give her age.

Her first role was Erum Jameel, a supporting part in Momina Duraid's Dil Ruba (HUM TV, 2020) alongside Hania Aamir and Mohib Mirza, and it brought a Lux Style Awards nomination for emerging talent. ARY Digital then cast her as Zoya, the lead of Bharaas, which won her the New Emerging Talent (Female) award at the 2021 ARY People's Choice Awards.

Durefishan Saleem's biggest hits

Ishq Murshid (HUM TV, 2023–2024)

As Shibra she plays opposite Bilal Abbas Khan's Shahmeer, and the push and pull between the two drives the serial. The Express Tribune's report on the 10th HUM Awards (October 2025) names Ishq Murshid as the leading winner of the night: Best Drama, Best Actor for Bilal Abbas Khan, Best Director for Farooq Rind (shared), Best OST for Ahmed Jahanzeb, and Best On-Screen Couple for Bilal Abbas Khan and Durefishan Saleem. On HUM TV's official channel, episode 1 (uploaded 8 October 2023) has 94.9 million views, and the HUM Music upload of the OST has 9.4 million (both as of 2 October 2026).

Kaisi Teri Khudgarzi (ARY Digital, 2022)

Many viewers search for this serial as "Yeh Kaisi Teri Khudgarzi", but the title ARY Digital broadcast was Kaisi Teri Khudgarzi (ARY's YouTube uploads spell it "Khudgharzi"). Durefishan plays Mehak Shamsher, a sheltered young woman, opposite Danish Taimoor, in their first project together. It was the role that made her widely known. The official ARY Digital upload of episode 1 (11 May 2022) has 116.9 million views, one of the biggest episode-1 numbers in this guide. Later, in Images Dawn, she said she would not watch or make a drama like it again, which is a frank thing to say about the show that made her name.

Sanwal Yaar Piya (Geo Entertainment, 2025–2026)

Her first serial with Feroze Khan and Ahmed Ali Akbar casts her as Piya Aslam, a college student. Geo premiered it on 15 September 2025, and the episode 1 upload on HAR PAL GEO has 32.3 million views.

Khaie (Geo Entertainment, 2024)

A complete change of register: she plays Zamda Khan, a Pashtun woman in a revenge thriller led by Faysal Quraishi. The serial was also debated. Images Dawn questioned whether it reinforced stereotypes about Pashtuns, a charge Quraishi rejected.

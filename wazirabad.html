Wazirabad District

Gujrat Division · Wazirabad and Alipur Chatha tehsils · 993,412 people in the 2023 census

Administrative status: Dawn, 18 October 2022; Dawn, 22 February 2023; Dawn, 24 December 2024.

Wazirabad District was notified by the Punjab government in October 2022. It has two tehsils: Wazirabad and Alipur Chatha. Alipur Chatha was created by dividing the old Wazirabad tehsil of Gujranwala District, and Gakhar Mandi stayed in Wazirabad tehsil. The notification put 123 revenue estates in Wazirabad tehsil and 139 in Alipur Chatha (Dawn).

The new district was placed in the new Gujrat Division. The caretaker government suspended the division and the district on 16 February 2023, but the Lahore High Court stayed that order (Dawn). The Board of Revenue notification of 18 December 2024 kept the district and named Gujrat as Punjab's 10th division, with Wazirabad in it (Dawn).

Wazirabad in the 2023 census

PBS counted Wazirabad as a single tehsil of Gujranwala District, covering what are now both tehsils: 993,412 people on 1,196 km², 37.15% of them urban, with annual growth of 3.04% since 2017 (PBS Table 1). Literacy was 77.39% (men 79.18%, women 75.62%), and the census counted 45,483 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (PBS Table 12).

Wazirabad District: FAQ

Which division is Wazirabad in?

Gujrat Division. The Punjab notification of 18 December 2024 kept Wazirabad District and confirmed Gujrat as the province’s 10th division. (Dawn)

Which tehsils are in Wazirabad District?

Two: Wazirabad and Alipur Chatha. Alipur Chatha was carved out of the old Wazirabad tehsil in October 2022. (Dawn)

Maya Ali: Dramas, Films and Awards

مایا علی

A former VJ from Lahore who became the lead of Mann Mayal and the star of two of Pakistani cinema's biggest romantic comedies, Teefa in Trouble and Parey Hut Love.

Maya Ali biography

Maya Ali, born Maryam Tanveer Ali in Lahore, worked as a video jockey for news and entertainment channels before she acted. The Express Tribune reported in 2019 that her father opposed her choice of career and did not speak to her for years. They reconciled before he died in 2016. Her date of birth is not given in the outlets we rely on, so this page gives no age.

She made her screen debut in Haissam Hussain's Durr-e-Shehwar (2012) and played the title role in his Aunn Zara (2013). Shanakht (2014) and Mehreen Jabbar's Mera Naam Yousuf Hai (2015) followed. Diyar-e-Dil (2015) won her the HUM Award for Best Actress (Popular), and then came Mann Mayal.

In cinema she starred opposite Ali Zafar in Teefa in Trouble (2018) and Sheheryar Munawar in Parey Hut Love (2019). She came back to television in 2021 with Pehli Si Muhabbat and the historical drama Jo Bichar Gaye, set during 1971, for which Images Dawn praised her work.

Maya Ali's biggest hits

Mann Mayal (HUM TV, 2016)

As Manahil, opposite Hamza Ali Abbasi, she played the outspoken heroine The News called "refreshing". It won her the Lux Style Award for Best TV Actress (The Express Tribune). HUM TV's re-upload of episode 1 has 10.5 million views (as of 2 October 2026).

Diyar-e-Dil (HUM TV, 2015)

A family saga adapted from Farhat Ishtiaq's novel, with Osman Khalid Butt. It won her the HUM Award for Best Actress (Popular) and, with Butt, the jury's on-screen couple award (The News). Episode 1 has 3.2 million views on HUM TV's channel.

Jo Bichar Gaye (Geo Entertainment, 2021–2022)

She plays an art student caught up in the events of 1971, opposite Wahaj Ali. Sadaf Haider praised her performance in Images Dawn.

Maya Ali new drama 2026 and upcoming projects

Aik Mohabbat Aur on Green Entertainment (2026) is her latest serial. She plays a divorced assistant commissioner opposite Ahad Raza Mir. She is also in the cast of Netflix's Jo Bachay Hain Sang Samait Lo, which ARY News announced in 2023 and which is expected in late 2026, though Netflix has not given a date. A Shoaib Mansoor film, Aasmaan Bolay Ga, was announced in 2022 (Images Dawn) but has no release date.

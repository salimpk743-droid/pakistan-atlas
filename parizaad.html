Parizaad — پری زاد

Parizaad (پری زاد) is a HUM TV drama based on Hashim Nadeem's novel, directed by Shehzad Kashmiri and starring Ahmed Ali Akbar.

ChannelHUM TV
WriterHashim Nadeem
DirectorShehzad Kashmiri
LeadAhmed Ali Akbar

Story

Parizaad follows a sensitive, educated young man who moves through different social worlds while confronting prejudice, loneliness, ambition and changing identity. HUM TV's own preview identifies Hashim Nadeem as the novel's writer, Shehzad Kashmiri as director, and Ahmed Ali Akbar among the principal cast. citeturn0search1

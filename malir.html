Malir District: Quick Answers

Malir District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Malir. It has 6 sub-divisions: Airport, Bin Qasim, Gadap, Ibrahim Hydri, Murad Memon and Shah Mureed. The 2023 census counted 2,432,248 people on 2,160 km², and 63.14% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Malir District

The 2023 census counted 6 administrative units in Malir District: Airport (sub-division), Bin Qasim (sub-division), Gadap (sub-division), Ibrahim Hydri (sub-division), Murad Memon (sub-division) and Shah Mureed (sub-division). Ibrahim Hydri is the most populous, with 1,341,638 people, and Gadap is the largest by area (1,104 km²). Literacy ranges from 49.7% in Shah Mureed to 80.94% in Airport.

Population and growth

Malir District had 2,432,248 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,924,346 in 2017 (average annual growth 3.99%). It covers 2,160 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 7th by population and 19th by area. 47.95% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 112.7 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (34.98%), followed by Pashto (17.71%), Urdu (15.79%) and Punjabi (9.94%).

Literacy and schooling

63.14% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 7th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 67.74% and female literacy 57.84%; the urban rate was 70.26% and the rural rate 56.48%. The census counted 291,573 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (143,410 girls and 148,149 boys).

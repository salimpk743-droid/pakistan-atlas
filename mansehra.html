Mansehra District

Gateway to the Kagan Valley · headquarters Mansehra · 5 tehsils · 1,797,177 people (2023 census)

Mansehra District: Quick Answers

Mansehra District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Mansehra. It has 5 tehsils: Baffa Pakhal, Balakot, Darband, Mansehra and Oghi. The 2023 census counted 1,797,177 people on 4,125 km², and 63.79% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Geography and history

Mansehra town lies at the southern end of the Pakhli Plain on the Bhut stream, a tributary of the Siran River, at 1,122 metres, 19 km north of Abbottabad. The Ashokan rock edicts near the town date to the 3rd century BCE. The region extends northwest to the Kagan (Kaghan) Valley, formed by the Kunhar River, which is 154 km long by road and hemmed in by peaks rising to 5,200 metres (Britannica).

Tehsils of Mansehra District

The 2023 census counted 5 tehsils in Mansehra District: Baffa Pakhal, Balakot, Darband, Mansehra and Oghi. Mansehra is the most populous, with 723,325 people, and Balakot is the largest by area (2,376 km²). Literacy ranges from 50.47% in Darband to 68.7% in Mansehra.

Population and growth

Mansehra District had 1,797,177 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,555,742 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.44%). It covers 4,125 km². Of the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the census, it ranks 7th by population and 8th by area. 8.62% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.08 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Mansehra (137,278) and Baffa (17,556). Hindko was the most common mother tongue (66.22%), followed by Pashto (17.97%), Kohistani (2.51%) and Urdu (1.16%).

Literacy and schooling

63.79% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 6th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 75.33% and female literacy 52.02%; the urban rate was 80.12% and the rural rate 62.17%. The census counted 111,292 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (70,220 girls and 41,070 boys).

Mansehra District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Mansehra District?

Mansehra District has 5 tehsils: Baffa Pakhal, Balakot, Darband, Mansehra and Oghi.

What is the population of Mansehra District?

The 2023 census counted 1,797,177 people in Mansehra District, up from 1,555,742 in 2017 (annual growth 2.44%). 8.62% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Mansehra District?

Mansehra District covers 4,125 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 435.68 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Mansehra District?

The 2023 census lists 2 urban localities in Mansehra District: Mansehra (137,278, municipal committee) and Baffa (17,556, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Mansehra District?

63.79% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 75.33%, female 52.02%).

Which languages are spoken in Mansehra District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Hindko (66.22% of the 1,789,624 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Pashto (17.97%), Kohistani (2.51%), Urdu (1.16%).

Which province is Mansehra District in?

Mansehra District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Mansehra.

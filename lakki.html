Lakki Marwat covers the sandy southern plains of KP.

Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is Lakki Marwat. The available public data note is Census 2023. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Education

The University of Lakki Marwat is the district's main higher-education institution.

Hospitals and healthcare

DHQ Lakki serves the district.

Culture and geography

The Marwat Pashtun tribe is dominant across the district's towns.

Public issues

Dryland farming conditions and staffing the district's campuses are ongoing challenges.

Villages and local places

Naurang and Ghazni Khel villages.

Sources: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics census material and public provincial or district sources. Statistics are not presented where the source dataset does not provide them.

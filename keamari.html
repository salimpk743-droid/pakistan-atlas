Keamari District: Karachi's Gateway to the Sea

Karachi Harbour · headquarters Keamari · 4 census units · 2,068,451 people (2023 census)

Keamari District: Quick Answers

Keamari District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, in Karachi Division, with its headquarters at Keamari. It has 4 sub-divisions: Baldia, Keamari, Mauripur and Site. The 2023 census counted 2,068,451 people on 559 km², and 62.07% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Karachi city

Karachi, the capital of Sindh, is Pakistan's largest city and principal seaport, on the Arabian Sea just north-west of the Indus delta (Britannica). Karachi Harbour is sheltered from storms by Kiamari Island, Manora Island and Oyster Rocks, which block most of the harbour entrance in the west (Britannica).

Administrative units of Keamari District

The 2023 census counted 4 administrative units in Keamari District: Baldia (sub-division), Keamari (sub-division), Mauripur (sub-division) and Site (sub-division). Baldia is the most populous, with 948,597 people, and Mauripur is the largest by area (450 km²). Literacy ranges from 52.76% in Mauripur to 68.48% in Site.

Population and growth

Keamari District had 2,068,451 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,829,837 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.07%). It covers 559 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 10th by population and 25th by area. 100% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 113.75 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Pashto was the most common mother tongue (33.14%), followed by Urdu (22.05%), Punjabi (10.84%) and Sindhi (9.25%).

Literacy and schooling

62.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 8th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 66.86% and female literacy 56.51%. The census counted 251,655 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (125,825 girls and 125,817 boys).

Keamari District: FAQ

How many sub-divisions are in Keamari District?

Keamari District has 4 sub-divisions: Baldia, Keamari, Mauripur and Site.

What is the population of Keamari District?

The 2023 census counted 2,068,451 people in Keamari District, up from 1,829,837 in 2017 (annual growth 2.07%). 100% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Keamari District?

Keamari District covers 559 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 3,700.27 people per km².

What is the literacy rate of Keamari District?

62.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 66.86%, female 56.51%).

Which languages are spoken in Keamari District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (33.14% of the 2,066,574 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (22.05%), Punjabi (10.84%), Sindhi (9.25%).

Which province is Keamari District in?

Keamari District is in Sindh, in Karachi Division. Its headquarters is Keamari.

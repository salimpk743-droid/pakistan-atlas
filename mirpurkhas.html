Mirpurkhas District: Quick Answers

Mirpurkhas District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Mirpurkhas. It has 7 talukas: Digri, Hussain Bux Mari, Jhudo, Kot Ghulam Muhammad, Mirpur Khas, Shujaabad and Sindhri. The 2023 census counted 1,681,386 people on 2,925 km², and 45.37% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Mirpurkhas District

The 2023 census counted 7 talukas in Mirpurkhas District: Digri, Hussain Bux Mari, Jhudo, Kot Ghulam Muhammad, Mirpur Khas, Shujaabad and Sindhri. Kot Ghulam Muhammad is the most populous, with 310,142 people, and it is also the largest by area (762 km²). Literacy ranges from 31.99% in Sindhri to 74.63% in Mirpur Khas.

Population and growth

Mirpurkhas District had 1,681,386 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,504,440 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.88%). It covers 2,925 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 18th by population and 15th by area. 29.27% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 110.36 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 8 urban localities; the largest are Mirpur Khas (267,833), Digri (41,774), Tando Jan Muhammad (41,188) and Jhudo (37,379). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (73.7%), followed by Urdu (11.93%), Punjabi (6.27%) and Balochi (1.65%).

Literacy and schooling

45.37% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 17th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 55.04% and female literacy 34.75%; the urban rate was 68.82% and the rural rate 34.81%. The census counted 267,147 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (146,644 girls and 120,498 boys).

میرپورخاص شہر

میرپورخاص لیٹ واہ نہر پر واقع ہے اور ریل اور سڑک کے ذریعے حیدرآباد (تقریباً 65 کلومیٹر مغرب جنوب مغرب) اور سڑک کے ذریعے عمرکوٹ سے ملا ہوا ہے۔ اسے 1806 میں میر علی مراد تالپور نے آباد کیا، اور 1843 میں سر چارلس نیپیئر کی قیادت میں برطانوی فوج کے ہاتھوں شکست تک یہ تالپور حکمرانوں کا دارالحکومت رہا۔ 1900 میں جمڑاؤ نہر کھلنے کے بعد شہر کی اہمیت بڑھی، اور اسے 1901 میں میونسپلٹی کا درجہ ملا۔ آج یہ اناج، کپڑے اور سوتی مصنوعات کی تجارت کا مرکز ہے (بریٹانیکا)۔

اس حصے کی ہر بات صفحے پر دیے گئے ذرائع سے جانچی گئی ہے (آخری جانچ: 2026-09-30)۔

Mirpurkhas District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Mirpurkhas District?

Mirpurkhas District has 7 talukas: Digri, Hussain Bux Mari, Jhudo, Kot Ghulam Muhammad, Mirpur Khas, Shujaabad and Sindhri.

What is the population of Mirpurkhas District?

The 2023 census counted 1,681,386 people in Mirpurkhas District, up from 1,504,440 in 2017 (annual growth 1.88%). 29.27% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Mirpurkhas District?

Mirpurkhas District covers 2,925 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 574.83 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Mirpurkhas District?

The 2023 census lists 8 urban localities in Mirpurkhas District: Mirpur Khas (267,833, municipal committee), Digri (41,774, town committee), Tando Jan Muhammad (41,188, town committee), Jhudo (37,379, town committee), Naukot (30,296, town committee), Kot Ghulam Muhammad (30,088, town committee), Mirwah (23,048, town committee) and Hingorno (20,569, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Mirpurkhas District?

45.37% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 55.04%, female 34.75%).

Which languages are spoken in Mirpurkhas District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (73.7% of the 1,680,980 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (11.93%), Punjabi (6.27%), Balochi (1.65%).

Which province is Mirpurkhas District in?

Mirpurkhas District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Mirpurkhas.

Qilla Saifullah District: Quick Answers

Qilla Saifullah District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Zhob Division, with its headquarters at Qilla Saifullah. The 2023 census lists 6 administrative units in it: Badini, Kan Mehtarzai, Killa Saifullah, Loiband, Muslim Bagh and Shinki. The 2023 census counted 380,200 people on 6,831 km², and 32.96% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Qilla Saifullah District

The 2023 census counted 6 administrative units in Qilla Saifullah District: Badini (sub-tehsil), Kan Mehtarzai (sub-tehsil), Killa Saifullah (sub-division), Loiband (tehsil), Muslim Bagh (tehsil) and Shinki (sub-tehsil). Killa Saifullah is the most populous, with 152,516 people, and Shinki is the largest by area (2,445 km²). Literacy ranges from 14.93% in Shinki to 53.65% in Kan Mehtarzai.

Population and growth

Qilla Saifullah District had 380,200 people in the 2023 census, up from 342,932 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.74%). It covers 6,831 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 11th by population and 15th by area. 16.88% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 109.91 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Killa Saifullah (35,043) and Muslim Bagh (29,132). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.52%).

Literacy and schooling

32.96% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 27th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 43.93% and female literacy 21.24%; the urban rate was 50.9% and the rural rate 29.07%. The census counted 85,325 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (48,966 girls and 36,358 boys).

Qilla Saifullah District: FAQ

What is the population of Qilla Saifullah District?

The 2023 census counted 380,200 people in Qilla Saifullah District, up from 342,932 in 2017 (annual growth 1.74%). 16.88% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Qilla Saifullah District?

Qilla Saifullah District covers 6,831 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 55.66 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Qilla Saifullah District?

The 2023 census lists 2 urban localities in Qilla Saifullah District: Killa Saifullah (35,043, municipal committee) and Muslim Bagh (29,132, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Qilla Saifullah District?

32.96% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 43.93%, female 21.24%).

Which languages are spoken in Qilla Saifullah District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.52% of the 380,200 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Qilla Saifullah District in?

Qilla Saifullah District is in Balochistan, in Zhob Division. Its headquarters is Qilla Saifullah.

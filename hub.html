Administrative status: Dawn, 4 September 2022.

Hub was notified as Balochistan's 35th district in September 2022, carved out of Lasbela. It has three sub-divisions: Hub, Dureji and Sonmiani (Winder). Lasbela kept Bela, Uthal and Kanraj, with its headquarters at Uthal (Dawn). The Balochistan cabinet had approved the new district in June 2022 (Quetta Voice).

2023 census rows

PBS counted these units under Lasbela District (PBS Table 1). Hub sub-division: 233,443 people on 868 km², 83.82% urban. Dureji sub-division: 52,236 people on 2,813 km². Sonmiani/Winder tehsil: 67,991 people on 2,616 km². The Gaddani, Lakhra and Liari census rows are not assigned to either district in the sources used, so no district total is given.

Popular Pakistani Dramas

There is no single objective popularity ranking. This guide groups widely searched and culturally recognised Pakistani dramas by era and type.

Widely Searched Dramas

2025 Google Search Interest

Google's Pakistan Year in Search included Sher, Judwaa, Aas Paas, Naqaab, Mohra, Iqtidar, Parwarish, Meri Zindagi Hai Tu, Dayan and Dunyapur.

Modern Reference Titles

Humsafar, Zindagi Gulzar Hai, Yakeen Ka Safar, Parizaad, Tere Bin, Mere Paas Tum Ho and Kabhi Main Kabhi Tum.

What This Page Is Not

It is not a paid ranking, ratings table or claim that one drama is universally “the best.” Search interest, ratings and cultural impact are different measures.

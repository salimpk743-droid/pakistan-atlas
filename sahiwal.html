Sahiwal District: Quick Answers

Sahiwal District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Sahiwal. It has 2 tehsils: Chichawatni and Sahiwal. The 2023 census counted 2,881,811 people on 3,201 km², and 64.77% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

From Montgomery to Sahiwal

Sahiwal city was founded in 1865 and named Montgomery after Sir Robert Montgomery, then lieutenant governor of the Punjab. It became a municipality in 1867 and took its present name in 1969. It lies on the Indus plain between the Sutlej and Ravi rivers, is linked by rail and road with Lahore and Multan, and is an important cotton centre. Harappa, one of the major sites of the Indus civilization, lies southwest of the city (Britannica).

Tehsils of Sahiwal District

The 2023 census counted 2 tehsils in Sahiwal District: Chichawatni and Sahiwal. Sahiwal is the most populous, with 1,725,833 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,610 km²). Literacy ranges from 64.44% in Sahiwal to 65.25% in Chichawatni.

Population and growth

Sahiwal District had 2,881,811 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,513,011 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.31%). It covers 3,201 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 20th by population and 26th by area. 26.29% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 102.8 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (96.95%), followed by Urdu (2.28%).

Literacy and schooling

64.77% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 16th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 71.06% and female literacy 58.29%; the urban rate was 78.6% and the rural rate 59.69%. The census counted 209,761 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (113,382 girls and 96,368 boys).

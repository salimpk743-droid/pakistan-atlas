Faisalabad District

Formerly Lyallpur · headquarters Faisalabad · 6 tehsils · 9,075,819 people (2023 census)

Faisalabad District: Quick Answers

Faisalabad District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, in Faisalabad Division, with its headquarters at Faisalabad. It has 6 tehsils: Chak Jhumra, Faisalabad City, Faisalabad Sadar, Jaranwala, Sammundri and Tandlian Wala. The 2023 census counted 9,075,819 people on 5,857 km², and 73.41% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

From Lyallpur to Faisalabad

Faisalabad lies in the Rechna Doab between the Chenab and Ravi rivers. When it was founded in 1890 it was named after Sir Charles James Lyall, lieutenant governor of the Punjab; it became the headquarters of the Lower Chenab canal colony and a municipality in 1898. The University of Agriculture, Faisalabad, was established in 1961. Wheat, cotton and sugarcane are the chief crops of the surrounding plain, which is irrigated by the Lower Chenab Canal (Britannica). Britannica gives the city's former name as Lyallpur (Britannica, Sargodha).

Tehsils of Faisalabad District

The 2023 census counted 6 tehsils in Faisalabad District: Chak Jhumra, Faisalabad City, Faisalabad Sadar, Jaranwala, Sammundri and Tandlian Wala. Faisalabad City is the most populous, with 3,691,999 people, and Jaranwala is the largest by area (1,811 km²). Literacy ranges from 52.83% in Tandlian Wala to 81.59% in Faisalabad City.

Population and growth

Faisalabad District had 9,075,819 people in the 2023 census, up from 7,882,444 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.38%). It covers 5,857 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 2nd by population and 13th by area. 48.4% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 108.92 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (94.13%), followed by Urdu (4.49%).

Literacy and schooling

73.41% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 9th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 77.34% and female literacy 69.11%; the urban rate was 81.03% and the rural rate 66.02%. The census counted 479,098 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (232,183 girls and 246,881 boys).

Faisalabad District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Faisalabad District?

Faisalabad District has 6 tehsils: Chak Jhumra, Faisalabad City, Faisalabad Sadar, Jaranwala, Sammundri and Tandlian Wala.

What is the population of Faisalabad District?

The 2023 census counted 9,075,819 people in Faisalabad District, up from 7,882,444 in 2017 (annual growth 2.38%). 48.4% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Faisalabad District?

Faisalabad District covers 5,857 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 1,549.57 people per km².

What is the literacy rate of Faisalabad District?

73.41% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 77.34%, female 69.11%).

Which languages are spoken in Faisalabad District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (94.13% of the 9,065,306 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (4.49%).

Which province is Faisalabad District in?

Faisalabad District is in Punjab, in Faisalabad Division. Its headquarters is Faisalabad.

What was the old name of Faisalabad?

Lyallpur, after Sir Charles James Lyall, lieutenant governor of the Punjab when the city was founded in 1890. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

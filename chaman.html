Created 2021 from Qilla Abdullah · headquarters Chaman · 2 census units · 466,218 people (2023 census)

Chaman District: Quick Answers

Chaman District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Pishin Division, with its headquarters at Chaman. The 2023 census lists 2 administrative units in it: Chaman City and Chaman Saddar. The 2023 census counted 466,218 people on 1,341 km², and 39.97% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

How Chaman District was created

In June 2021 the Balochistan Board of Revenue split Qilla Abdullah into two districts. The new Chaman District took Chaman city and Saddar Chaman tehsil, while Qilla Abdullah, Gulistan and Dobandi tehsils stayed in Qilla Abdullah District (Dawn).

Administrative units of Chaman District

The 2023 census counted 2 administrative units in Chaman District: Chaman City (sub-division) and Chaman Saddar (sub-division). Chaman Saddar is the most populous, with 336,079 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,319 km²). Literacy ranges from 35.18% in Chaman Saddar to 51.36% in Chaman City.

Population and growth

Chaman District had 466,218 people in the 2023 census, up from 434,561 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.18%). It covers 1,341 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 9th by population and 32nd by area. 27.91% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 127.4 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Chaman (130,139). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.37%).

Literacy and schooling

39.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 14th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 47.73% and female literacy 30.6%; the urban rate was 51.36% and the rural rate 35.18%. The census counted 117,899 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (59,466 girls and 58,428 boys).

Chaman District: FAQ

What is the population of Chaman District?

The 2023 census counted 466,218 people in Chaman District, up from 434,561 in 2017 (annual growth 1.18%). 27.91% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Chaman District?

Chaman District covers 1,341 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 347.66 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Chaman District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Chaman District: Chaman (130,139, municipal corporation).

What is the literacy rate of Chaman District?

39.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 47.73%, female 30.6%).

Which languages are spoken in Chaman District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.37% of the 466,218 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Chaman District in?

Chaman District is in Balochistan, in Pishin Division. Its headquarters is Chaman.

When did Chaman become a district?

In June 2021, when the Balochistan Board of Revenue split Qilla Abdullah District and created Chaman District from Chaman city and Saddar Chaman. (Dawn)

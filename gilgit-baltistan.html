Gilgit-Baltistan گلگت بلتستان

Gilgit-Baltistan, formerly the Northern Areas, had 1,709,049 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,492,924 in 2017, on 72,496 km². Its government notified 14 districts in 2019; official statistics are still published for 10.

About Gilgit-Baltistan

Gilgit-Baltistan, formerly known as the Northern Areas, is administered by Pakistan and forms the northern part of the larger Kashmir region (Government of Gilgit-Baltistan).

Culture and Geography of Gilgit-Baltistan

Meeting point of Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush. Shina, Balti, Burushaski and Wakhi languages. Polo at Shandur and apricot culture of Hunza.

Education and Health in Gilgit-Baltistan

Karakoram International University Gilgit and growing college network.

DHQ Gilgit and Skardu; specialist care often requires travel to Islamabad.

Public Issues in Gilgit-Baltistan

Glacial lake floods, road closures in winter, and tourism carrying capacity.

Notable Places in Gilgit-Baltistan

Gilgit, Skardu, Hunza, Chilas

Gilgit-Baltistan: FAQ

What is the population of Gilgit-Baltistan?

The 2023 census counted 1,709,049 people, up from 1,492,924 in 2017 (annual growth 2.28%). (P&DD Gilgit-Baltistan)

What is the area of Gilgit-Baltistan?

72,496 km² according to the GB Planning & Development Department; the GB government portal gives 72,971 km². (P&DD Gilgit-Baltistan)

How many districts does Gilgit-Baltistan have?

14 since the June 2019 notification of Gupis-Yasin, Rondu, Darel and Tangir; the official 2023 statistics still report 10. (Dawn)

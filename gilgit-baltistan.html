Gilgit-Baltistan گلگت بلتستان

Meeting point of Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush. Shina, Balti, Burushaski and Wakhi languages. Polo at Shandur and apricot culture of Hunza.

Gilgit-Baltistan facts

Capital: Gilgit · Population: 1,492,924 (2023 census) · Area: 72,971 km² · Districts: 14.

Full list: districts of Gilgit-Baltistan → · All provinces and territories →

Culture and geography

Meeting point of Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush. Shina, Balti, Burushaski and Wakhi languages. Polo at Shandur and apricot culture of Hunza.

Education and health

Karakoram International University Gilgit and growing college network.

DHQ Gilgit and Skardu; specialist care often requires travel to Islamabad.

How many districts does Gilgit-Baltistan have?

14 districts in 3 divisions, as of September 2026. Gilgit-Baltistan government notification of June 2019 (four new districts, 14 in total).

Sources: Dawn, 2019-06.

Public issues

Glacial lake floods, road closures in winter, and tourism carrying capacity.

Kharan District

Desert district on the Baddo River · headquarters Kharan · 4 census units · 260,352 people (2023 census)

Kharan District: Quick Answers

Kharan District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Rakhshan Division, with its headquarters at Kharan. The 2023 census lists 4 administrative units in it: Kharan, Patkain, Sar Kharan and Tohmulk. The 2023 census counted 260,352 people on 14,958 km², and 41.07% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Kharan town and its desert

Kharan town lies about 10 km from the Baddo River. Long a caravan depot, it still trades in salt, millet, wheat, dates, melons, carpets and baskets. The surrounding area is mostly desert, with cultivated tracts that depend on flood irrigation along the foothills and the Baddo and Mashkel rivers. Camel, goat and sheep breeding are important (Britannica).

Administrative units of Kharan District

The 2023 census counted 4 administrative units in Kharan District: Kharan (sub-division), Patkain (sub-tehsil), Sar Kharan (sub-division) and Tohmulk (sub-tehsil). Kharan is the most populous, with 104,035 people, and Tohmulk is the largest by area (6,347 km²). Literacy ranges from 34.81% in Tohmulk to 48.63% in Kharan.

Population and growth

Kharan District had 260,352 people in the 2023 census, up from 162,766 in 2017 (average annual growth 8.17%). It covers 14,958 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 22nd by population and 9th by area. 31.04% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 115.79 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Kharan (80,806). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (91.24%), followed by Brahui (8.5%).

Literacy and schooling

41.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 12th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 51.78% and female literacy 29.36%; the urban rate was 51.18% and the rural rate 36.21%. The census counted 61,622 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (29,962 girls and 31,656 boys).

خاران شہر اور صحرا

خاران شہر دریائے بڈو سے تقریباً 10 کلومیٹر دور ہے۔ یہ طویل عرصے تک قافلوں کا پڑاؤ رہا اور آج بھی نمک، باجرہ، گندم، کھجور، خربوزہ، قالین اور ٹوکریوں کی تجارت ہوتی ہے۔ اردگرد کا علاقہ زیادہ تر صحرا ہے؛ پہاڑوں کے دامن اور بڈو اور ماشکیل دریاؤں کے ساتھ سیلابی آبپاشی سے کاشت ہوتی ہے۔ اونٹ، بکریاں اور بھیڑیں پالنا اہم ہے (بریٹانیکا)۔

ریاست خاران سے ضلع تک

ریاست خاران تقریباً 1697 میں ریاست قلات کی باج گزار ریاست کے طور پر قائم ہوئی۔ 17 مارچ 1948 کو خاران نے پاکستان سے الحاق کیا، 3 اکتوبر 1952 کو یہ بلوچستان اسٹیٹس یونین میں شامل ہوئی اور 14 اکتوبر 1955 کو مغربی پاکستان کے قیام پر ریاست ختم ہو گئی۔ 1970 میں مغربی پاکستان کے خاتمے کے بعد سابق ریاست کے علاقے کو ضلع خاران بنا دیا گیا۔ پاکستان کا دوسرا ایٹمی تجربہ 30 مئی 1998 کو خاران کے صحرا میں کیا گیا (بلوچستان ہائی کورٹ)۔

Kharan District: FAQ

What is the population of Kharan District?

The 2023 census counted 260,352 people in Kharan District, up from 162,766 in 2017 (annual growth 8.17%). 31.04% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Kharan District?

Kharan District covers 14,958 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 17.41 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Kharan District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Kharan District: Kharan (80,806, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Kharan District?

41.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 51.78%, female 29.36%).

Which languages are spoken in Kharan District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (91.24% of the 260,352 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Brahui (8.5%).

Which province is Kharan District in?

Kharan District is in Balochistan, in Rakhshan Division. Its headquarters is Kharan.

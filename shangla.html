Shangla District: Quick Answers

Shangla District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Alpuri. It has 5 tehsils: Alpuri, Bisham, Chakisar, Martung and Puran. The 2023 census counted 891,252 people on 1,586 km², and 33.74% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Shangla District

The 2023 census counted 5 tehsils in Shangla District: Alpuri, Bisham, Chakisar, Martung and Puran. Alpuri is the most populous, with 366,772 people, and it is also the largest by area (582 km²). Literacy ranges from 24.01% in Martung to 38.26% in Alpuri.

Population and growth

Shangla District had 891,252 people in the 2023 census, up from 759,609 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.71%). Among the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the PBS 2023 census tables, it ranks 20th by population and 22nd by area (1,586 km²). PBS counted no urban population in the district. There were 106.65 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Pashto was the most common mother tongue (95.4%), followed by Kohistani (1.03%).

Literacy and schooling

33.74% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 27th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 48.82% and female literacy 17.82%. The census counted 163,716 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (95,004 girls and 68,711 boys).

Shangla District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Shangla District?

Shangla District has 5 tehsils: Alpuri, Bisham, Chakisar, Martung and Puran.

What is the population of Shangla District?

The 2023 census counted 891,252 people in Shangla District, up from 759,609 in 2017 (annual growth 2.71%). 0% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Shangla District?

Shangla District covers 1,586 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 561.95 people per km².

Is Shangla District urban or rural?

Entirely rural: the 2023 census counted no urban population in Shangla District, so all 891,252 residents were enumerated in rural areas.

What is the literacy rate of Shangla District?

Which languages are spoken in Shangla District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (95.4% of the 890,846 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Kohistani (1.03%).

Which province is Shangla District in?

Shangla District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Alpuri.

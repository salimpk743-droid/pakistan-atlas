Malakand District: Quick Answers

Malakand District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Batkhela. It has 2 sub-divisions: Sam Rani Zai and Swat Rani Zai. The 2023 census counted 826,250 people on 952 km², and 61.66% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Malakand District

The 2023 census counted 2 administrative units in Malakand District: Sam Rani Zai (sub-division) and Swat Rani Zai (sub-division). Swat Rani Zai is the most populous, with 472,959 people, and it is also the largest by area (672 km²). Literacy ranges from 59.13% in Sam Rani Zai to 63.57% in Swat Rani Zai.

Population and growth

Malakand District had 826,250 people in the 2023 census, up from 717,806 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.38%). It covers 952 km². Of the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the census, it ranks 21st by population and 33rd by area. 8.9% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.8 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Pashto was the most common mother tongue (98.31%).

Literacy and schooling

61.66% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 7th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 71.91% and female literacy 51.05%; the urban rate was 63.08% and the rural rate 61.52%. The census counted 54,983 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (29,810 girls and 25,169 boys).

How many sub-divisions are in Malakand District?

Malakand District has 2 sub-divisions: Sam Rani Zai and Swat Rani Zai.

Which languages are spoken in Malakand District?

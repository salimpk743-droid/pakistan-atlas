Malakand District

Headquarters Batkhela · old Protected Area · 952 km² · 826,250 people in 2023

Malakand District: Quick Answers

Malakand District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Batkhela. It has 2 sub-divisions: Sam Rani Zai and Swat Rani Zai. The 2023 census counted 826,250 people on 952 km², and 61.66% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Malakand is the short district on the pass between the Peshawar plain and Swat–Dir country. It used to be a Protected Area, not an ordinary settled district. That legal history still sits in the name. The pass is why trucks, students and fruit loads all use the same climb. Batkhela is the headquarters. Dargai is the other town people name without thinking. Thana is the third place-name on local tongues.

The district is small — 952 km² — and crowded for its size (about 868 people per km²). It is not Lower Kohistan and it is not Lower Dir. It is a gate.

Malakand: 2023 sheet

Households 113,118. Population 826,250. Urban 73,590. Rural 753,260. Literacy 61.66 percent — 71.91 percent for men, 51.05 percent for women. That female figure is high next to Bajaur or Lower Kohistan. Under-tens about 245,000 (30 percent). Sex ratio about 105 men per 100 women.

Batkhela municipal count is about 73,525 in 2023 city lists — almost the whole urban row of the district. Dargai does not take that municipal slot in the same list.

Malakand: Two printed tehsils, one extra name

Swat Ranizai (Batkhela side) — 672 km², 472,959 people, literacy about 63.6 percent. Headquarters tehsil. Batkhela bazaar is the long market people describe as having no proper junction for kilometres. Treat that as a local description, not a tourist slogan.

Sam Ranizai (Dargai side) — 280 km², 353,291 people, about 1,262 per km², literacy about 59.1 percent. Smaller land, denser. Dargai is the town name.

Thana Baizai appears as a third tehsil title on some maps. The 2023 population line for it is still blank in the same extract. Name Thana. Do not invent a total.

Malakand: College and hospital

Government colleges sit in Batkhela and Dargai. University of Malakand is the regional campus name students use; the main site is at Chakdara on the Dir side of the river, not inside Batkhela. DHQ Malakand / Batkhela is the first hospital. Dargai keeps THQ-style cover. Female literacy at 51 percent is why this page can talk about colleges without pretending the work is finished.

Sources: PBS 2023; tehsil literacy rows for Swat Ranizai and Sam Ranizai; Batkhela municipal series. September 2026.

Malakand District: FAQ

How many sub-divisions are in Malakand District?

Malakand District has 2 sub-divisions: Sam Rani Zai and Swat Rani Zai.

What is the population of Malakand District?

The 2023 census counted 826,250 people in Malakand District, up from 717,806 in 2017 (annual growth 2.38%). 8.9% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Malakand District?

Malakand District covers 952 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 867.91 people per km².

What is the literacy rate of Malakand District?

61.66% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 71.91%, female 51.05%).

Which languages are spoken in Malakand District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (98.31% of the 825,377 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Malakand District in?

Malakand District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Batkhela.

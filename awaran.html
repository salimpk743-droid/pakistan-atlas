Awaran District

headquarters Awaran · 5 census units · 178,958 people (2023 census)

Awaran District: Quick Answers

Awaran District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Lasbela Division, with its headquarters at Awaran. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Awaran, Gishkore, Jhal Jao, Korak Jhao and Mashkai. The 2023 census counted 178,958 people on 29,510 km², and 36.34% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Awaran District

The 2023 census counted 5 administrative units in Awaran District: Awaran (sub-division), Gishkore (tehsil), Jhal Jao (tehsil), Korak Jhao (tehsil) and Mashkai (tehsil). Mashkai is the most populous, with 45,938 people, and Awaran is the largest by area (13,075 km²). Literacy ranges from 26.62% in Jhal Jao to 42.9% in Awaran.

Population and growth

Awaran District had 178,958 people in the 2023 census, up from 121,821 in 2017 (average annual growth 6.64%). It covers 29,510 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 33rd by population and 4th by area. 26.17% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 104.93 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Awaran (28,780) and Gujar Mashkai (18,056). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (98.58%), followed by Brahui (1.17%).

Literacy and schooling

36.34% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 23rd of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 44.28% and female literacy 27.95%; the urban rate was 44.93% and the rural rate 33.23%. The census counted 33,451 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (16,767 girls and 16,683 boys).

Awaran District: FAQ

What is the population of Awaran District?

The 2023 census counted 178,958 people in Awaran District, up from 121,821 in 2017 (annual growth 6.64%). 26.17% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Awaran District?

Awaran District covers 29,510 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 6.06 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Awaran District?

The 2023 census lists 2 urban localities in Awaran District: Awaran (28,780, municipal committee) and Gujar Mashkai (18,056, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Awaran District?

Which languages are spoken in Awaran District?

Which province is Awaran District in?

Awaran District is in Balochistan, in Lasbela Division. Its headquarters is Awaran.

Kolai-Palas District: Quick Answers

Kolai-Palas District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It has 2 sub-divisions: Battaira and Palas. The 2023 census counted 280,162 people on 1,410 km², and 18.8% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Kolai-Palas District

The 2023 census counted 2 administrative units in Kolai-Palas District: Battaira (sub-division) and Palas (sub-division). Battaira is the most populous, with 142,660 people, and Palas is the largest by area (1,240 km²). Literacy ranges from 15.93% in Battaira to 23.08% in Palas.

Population and growth

Kolai-Palas District had 280,162 people in the 2023 census, up from 274,923 in 2017 (average annual growth 0.32%). It covers 1,410 km². Of the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the census, it ranks 33rd by population and 28th by area. PBS counted no urban population in the district. There were 103.65 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Kohistani was the most common mother tongue (85.19%), followed by Shina (7.75%) and Pashto (2.81%).

Literacy and schooling

18.8% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 35th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 24.24% and female literacy 13.14%. The census counted 80,333 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (40,458 girls and 39,874 boys).

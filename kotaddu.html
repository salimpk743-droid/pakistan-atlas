Kot Addu District: FAQ

When did Kot Addu become a district?

Kot Addu was approved as a district on 14 October 2022 and notified as a district of Dera Ghazi Khan Division in December 2024. (The Nation)

Which tehsils are in Kot Addu District?

Two: Kot Addu and Chowk Sarwar Shaheed, both formerly in Muzaffargarh District. (The Nation)

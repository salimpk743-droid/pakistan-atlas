New Pakistani Drama OSTs 2026

  • Nashtar (Geo): lyrics by SK Khalish; vocals and composition by Shani Arshad.
  • Mohabbat (Geo): sung and written by Shani Haider, with music arranged together with Aliyan.
  • Uswah (Geo): music by Azaan Zair Baga with vocals by Afshan Fawad; the OST's poetry, composition and voice are by Sahir Ali Bagga.

Beyond drama OSTs, the 24th Lux Style Awards named Coke Studio's Jhol (Maanu and Annural Khalid) Song of the Year and Hamza Malik feat. Laiba Khurram (Loco) Artist of the Year.

Sources: The News (Geo premiere reports, August and September 2026); Dawn, 13 December 2025.

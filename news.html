News & public updates

Write short briefings. Always name the original department or newspaper.

Method

How this page should work

Do not scrape headlines. Twice a week, add 300–600 words on one public fact: a new district notification, a university admission calendar, a PBS release, or a documented flood / drought briefing. Link the source. That original writing is what AdSense reviewers look for.

Baseline

2023 census remains the population backbone

Until PBS publishes a newer national count, every district card on this site should show the 2023 figure and the year in brackets. When a province splits a district, add a footnote rather than guessing the new population.

Education

Watch official admission windows

Provincial boards and HEC announce sessions on their own sites. Reprint dates only after you open the circular. Students use this page if they can trust the clock.

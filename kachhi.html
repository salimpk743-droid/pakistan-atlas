Kachhi District: Quick Answers

Kachhi District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Sevi Division, with its headquarters at Dhadar. The 2023 census lists 6 administrative units in it: Balanari, Bhag, Dhadar, Khattan, Mach and Sanni. The 2023 census counted 442,674 people on 5,682 km², and 30.2% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Kachhi District

The 2023 census counted 6 administrative units in Kachhi District: Balanari (sub-tehsil), Bhag (sub-division), Dhadar (sub-division), Khattan (sub-tehsil), Mach (sub-division) and Sanni (sub-division). Sanni is the most populous, with 150,821 people, and it is also the largest by area (2,011 km²). Literacy ranges from 15.58% in Sanni to 45.1% in Dhadar.

Kachhi: Population and growth

Kachhi District had 442,674 people in the 2023 census, up from 308,000 in 2017 (average annual growth 6.25%). It covers 5,682 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 10th by population and 19th by area. 18.17% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 111.73 males for every 100 females.

Kachhi: Towns and languages

The census lists 3 urban localities: Mach (44,542), Bhag (18,634) and Dhadar (17,276). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (58.88%), followed by Sindhi (17%), Saraiki (12.29%) and Brahui (10.25%).

Kachhi: Literacy and schooling

30.2% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 28th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 36.47% and female literacy 23.29%; the urban rate was 52.27% and the rural rate 25.21%. The census counted 140,355 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (67,502 girls and 72,838 boys).

Kachhi District: 2023 Census in Detail

The 7th Population and Housing Census (2023) by the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) counted 442,674 people in Kachhi District — 233,584 males and 209,056 females (sex ratio 111.73 males per 100 females). The 2017 census had counted 308,000, so the district gained 134,674 people in six years (difference calculated from the two PBS totals), an average annual growth rate of 6.25%. The district covers 5,682 km², giving a density of 77.91 people per km²; 18.17% of residents live in urban areas and the average household has 8.8 members.

Kachhi District: FAQ

What is the population of Kachhi District?

The 2023 census counted 442,674 people in Kachhi District, up from 308,000 in 2017 (annual growth 6.25%). 18.17% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Kachhi District?

Kachhi District covers 5,682 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 77.91 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Kachhi District?

The 2023 census lists 3 urban localities in Kachhi District: Mach (44,542, municipal committee), Bhag (18,634, municipal committee) and Dhadar (17,276, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Kachhi District?

Which languages are spoken in Kachhi District?

Which province is Kachhi District in?

Kachhi District

Sevi Division · Balochistan

Kachhi District: Quick Answers

Kachhi District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Sevi Division, with its headquarters at Dhadar. The 2023 census lists 6 administrative units in it: Balanari, Bhag, Dhadar, Khattan, Mach and Sanni. The 2023 census counted 442,674 people on 5,682 km², and 30.2% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Kachhi District: FAQ

What is the population of Kachhi District?

The 2023 census counted 442,674 people in Kachhi District, up from 308,000 in 2017 (annual growth 6.25%). 18.17% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Kachhi District?

Kachhi District covers 5,682 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 77.91 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Kachhi District?

The 2023 census lists 3 urban localities in Kachhi District: Mach (44,542, municipal committee), Bhag (18,634, municipal committee) and Dhadar (17,276, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Kachhi District?

30.2% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 36.47%, female 23.29%).

Which languages are spoken in Kachhi District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (58.88% of the 442,674 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Sindhi (17%), Saraiki (12.29%), Brahui (10.25%).

Which province is Kachhi District in?

Kachhi District is in Balochistan, in Sevi Division. Its headquarters is Dhadar.

Tando Allahyar District: Quick Answers

Tando Allahyar District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Tando Allahyar. It has 3 talukas: Chamber, Jhando Mari and Tando Allahyar. The 2023 census counted 922,012 people on 1,554 km², and 39.8% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Talukas of Tando Allahyar District

The 2023 census counted 3 talukas in Tando Allahyar District: Chamber, Jhando Mari and Tando Allahyar. Tando Allahyar is the most populous, with 421,923 people, and Jhando Mari is the largest by area (626 km²). Literacy ranges from 29.96% in Chamber to 46.83% in Tando Allahyar.

Population and growth

Tando Allahyar District had 922,012 people in the 2023 census, up from 838,527 in 2017 (average annual growth 1.6%). It covers 1,554 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 27th by population and 21st by area. 30.99% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.31 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (85.34%), followed by Urdu (7.25%), Punjabi (2.49%) and Balochi (1.14%).

Literacy and schooling

39.8% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 23rd of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 47.82% and female literacy 31.48%; the urban rate was 54.9% and the rural rate 32.39%. The census counted 170,489 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (90,619 girls and 79,867 boys).

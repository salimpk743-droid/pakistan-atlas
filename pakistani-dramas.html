Pakistani Dramas — Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I check the latest Pakistani drama episode?

Use the official broadcaster's episode page when available. MyBook.Pk will focus on reference information and link readers toward official listings rather than copying episodes.

What are the most famous Pakistani dramas?

There is no single objective list because popularity can mean television ratings, digital views, cultural influence or long-term recognition. MyBook.Pk therefore groups classic and modern titles without assigning a universal ranking.

Which Pakistani dramas are airing in 2026?

Schedules change. Current official listings checked for this page include HUM TV titles such as Mohabbat Mukhtasir, Zehar E Mohabbat, Gulbahar and Khushi, plus Geo listings such as Kaisa Yeh Ishq, Nashtar, Uswah and Tapish.

Editorial note: Current programme names and episode information can change quickly. This page was reviewed in September 2026. Official broadcaster pages are treated as the primary source for live schedules and episode listings.

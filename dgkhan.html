Gateway between southern Punjab and the Sulaiman mountains.

Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is D.G. Khan. The available public data note is Census 2023 — see PBS. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Education

Ghazi University and medical college; public colleges in tehsils.

Hospitals and healthcare

DHQ and teaching hospital D.G. Khan.

Culture and geography

Saraiki and Baloch tribal neighbourhoods, foot-hills culture.

Public issues

Floods on hill torrents and access into tribal tehsils.

Villages and local places

Taunsa belt (now partly a separate district), Fort Munro road villages

Sources: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics census material and public provincial or district sources. Statistics are not presented where the source dataset does not provide them.

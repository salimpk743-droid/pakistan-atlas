Tehsils of Quetta District

The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in Quetta District: City (sub-division), Kuchlak (sub-division), Saddar (sub-division), Sariab (sub-division) and Panjpai (sub-tehsil). City is the most populous, with 1,360,600 people in the 2023 census, and Saddar is the largest by area (1,283 km²).

Quetta District administrative structure

Balochistan, Quetta Division at the 2023 census. Each card gives the 2023 census population, area, urban share and literacy of one tehsil.

These figures are for the undivided Quetta District as enumerated in the 2023 census, before it was divided into Quetta East and Quetta West.

How the tehsils compare

City sub-division has the most people (1,360,600) and Panjpai the fewest (21,371). Saddar covers the largest area (1,283 km²). City is the most urban (91.24% of residents in urban areas). Literacy among people aged 10 and over ranges from 37.67% in Panjpai to 63.97% in City.

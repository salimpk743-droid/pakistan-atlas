Khuzdar District

On the Kalat–Karachi road · headquarters Khuzdar · 10 census units · 997,214 people (2023 census)

Khuzdar District: Quick Answers

Khuzdar District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Khuzdar Division, with its headquarters at Khuzdar. The 2023 census lists 10 administrative units in it: Aranji, Gresha, Karakh, Khuzdar, Moola, Nal, Ornach, Saroona, Wadh and Zehri. The 2023 census counted 997,214 people (2023 boundaries) on 35,380 km², and 38.59% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Khuzdar town

Khuzdar lies along the Kolachi River at the head of a narrow valley, at 1,237 metres. It is on an old caravan route to the Arabian Sea and on the main road between Kalat and Karachi. The town is a market centre for wool, barite deposits are worked nearby, and its baked-brick fort was built in 1870. The surrounding area is mostly mountainous and semi-arid (Britannica).

Administrative units of Khuzdar District

The 2023 census counted 10 administrative units in Khuzdar District: Aranji (sub-tehsil), Gresha (sub-tehsil), Karakh (sub-division), Khuzdar (sub-division), Moola (sub-tehsil), Nal (sub-division), Ornach (sub-division), Saroona (sub-tehsil), Wadh (sub-division) and Zehri (sub-division). Khuzdar is the most populous, with 359,358 people, and Aranji is the largest by area (7,456 km²). Literacy ranges from 12.11% in Aranji to 52.68% in Moola.

Population and growth

Khuzdar District had 997,214 people in the 2023 census, up from 798,896 in 2017 (average annual growth 3.78%). It covers 35,380 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 3rd by population and 2nd by area. 36.54% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 116.84 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 4 urban localities: Khuzdar (218,112), Zehri (70,910), Nal (48,481) and Wadh (26,875). Brahui was the most common mother tongue (78.93%), followed by Balochi (18.71%) and Sindhi (1.22%).

Literacy and schooling

38.59% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 17th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 44.91% and female literacy 31.42%; the urban rate was 53.74% and the rural rate 29.76%. The census counted 234,905 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (112,144 girls and 122,752 boys).

خضدار شہر

خضدار شہر پب کے پہاڑی سلسلے کی ایک تنگ وادی کے سرے پر دریائے کولاچی کے کنارے، سطح سمندر سے 1,237 میٹر کی بلندی پر واقع ہے۔ یہ بحیرۂ عرب کی طرف جانے والے ایک قدیم قافلہ راستے پر ہے اور باغات میں گھرا ہوا ہے۔ شہر اون کی منڈی ہے، قریب ہی بیرائٹ کے ذخائر سے کان کنی ہوتی ہے، اور یہاں کا پکی اینٹوں کا قلعہ 1870 میں تعمیر ہوا۔ خضدار قلات اور کراچی کے درمیان مرکزی شاہراہ پر واقع ہے (بریٹانیکا)۔

تاریخ

عرب دور میں یہ علاقہ توران کا صوبہ تھا اور خضدار اس کا دارالحکومت اور ایک اہم چھاؤنی تھا۔ شہر کے قریب عربوں کے بنائے ہوئے ایک پرانے قلعے کے کھنڈرات ہیں۔ بعد میں خضدار ریاست قلات کا حصہ بنا، اور 1869 میں یہاں جھالاوان کے لوگوں اور خانِ قلات کے درمیان جنگ ہوئی جس میں خان کو فتح ملی (بلوچستان ہائی کورٹ)۔

خضدار پہلے ضلع قلات کا حصہ تھا اور یکم مارچ 1974 کو اسے الگ ضلع بنایا گیا (بلوچستان ہائی کورٹ)۔ نومبر 1992 میں خضدار کی آواران سب ڈویژن کو الگ ضلع آواران بنایا گیا (بلوچستان ہائی کورٹ، آواران)۔

اس حصے کی ہر بات صفحے پر دیے گئے ذرائع سے جانچی گئی ہے (آخری جانچ: 2026-09-30)۔

Khuzdar District: FAQ

What is the population of Khuzdar District?

The 2023 census counted 997,214 people in Khuzdar District (2023 boundaries), up from 798,896 in 2017 (annual growth 3.78%). 36.54% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Khuzdar District?

Khuzdar District covers 35,380 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 28.19 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Khuzdar District?

The 2023 census lists 4 urban localities in Khuzdar District: Khuzdar (218,112, municipal corporation), Zehri (70,910, municipal committee), Nal (48,481, municipal committee) and Wadh (26,875, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Khuzdar District?

38.59% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 44.91%, female 31.42%).

Which languages are spoken in Khuzdar District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Brahui (78.93% of the 997,214 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Balochi (18.71%), Sindhi (1.22%).

Which province is Khuzdar District in?

Khuzdar District is in Balochistan, in Khuzdar Division. Its headquarters is Khuzdar.

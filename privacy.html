Privacy Policy

MyBook.Pk (“we”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what little data a static educational site may collect once hosting and advertising are switched on.

Information we may process

  • Messages you send through the contact form (name, email, message).
  • Standard server logs kept by the host (Vercel or similar): IP address, browser type, pages viewed.
  • Cookies and advertising identifiers if Google AdSense is approved and enabled.

Google AdSense and cookies

If ads are shown, Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit. Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads on this site. Google’s use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Children

The site is written for students but does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Do not send a child’s private details through the form.

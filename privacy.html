Privacy Policy

What data is processed when you visit MyBook.Pk, and your choices.

MyBook.Pk is a static educational website. It has no user accounts, sign-up forms or comment boxes, and we do not ask you for personal information in order to read it.

Information processed when you visit

  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Fonts and images. Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and images from Wikimedia Commons. Your browser connects to those services, which receive your IP address.
  • Email. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to act on corrections.

Advertising and cookies (Google AdSense)

MyBook.Pk shows advertising from Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's My Ad Center (Ads Settings). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and how it uses cookies in advertising at Advertising – Google Privacy & Terms. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site (for example which pages are read most and how people find them). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as the pages you visit, your device and browser type, and your approximate location; Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. We also use Google Search Console, which reports aggregated search data (for example, how often pages appear in Google results) and does not place cookies on the site.

Children

The site is written for students and a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not send a child's personal details by email.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and its "Last updated" date will change. Privacy questions can be sent to zainkhanpk742@gmail.com.

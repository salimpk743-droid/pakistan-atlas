Gwadar District

Arabian Sea port · part of Oman until 1958 · headquarters Gwadar · 5 census units · 305,160 people (2023 census)

Gwadar District: Quick Answers

Gwadar District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Makuran Division, with its headquarters at Gwadar. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Gwadar, Jiwani, Ormara, Pasni and Suntser. The 2023 census counted 305,160 people on 12,637 km², and 50.3% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Gwadar town and port

Gwadar stands on the sandy Nuh headland, which juts south into the Arabian Sea, and is a fishing and trade centre. It became part of the Sultanate of Muscat and Oman in 1797; Oman gave up the town and its hinterland to Pakistan in 1958. Roads connect Gwadar with Pasni to the east and Turbat to the northeast (Britannica).

Administrative units of Gwadar District

The 2023 census counted 5 administrative units in Gwadar District: Gwadar (sub-division), Jiwani (tehsil), Ormara (tehsil), Pasni (sub-division) and Suntser (sub-tehsil). Gwadar is the most populous, with 147,673 people, and Pasni is the largest by area (4,822 km²). Literacy ranges from 31.69% in Suntser to 59.71% in Pasni.

Population and growth

Gwadar District had 305,160 people in the 2023 census, up from 262,253 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.56%). It covers 12,637 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 16th by population and 10th by area. 52.12% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 111.33 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 5 urban localities; the largest are Gwadar (70,852), Pasni (43,494), Jiwani (25,332) and Ormara (16,573). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (97.66%).

Literacy and schooling

50.3% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 4th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 57.62% and female literacy 42.19%; the urban rate was 57.12% and the rural rate 42.48%. The census counted 33,621 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (17,497 girls and 16,119 boys).

گوادر شہر اور بندرگاہ

گوادر ریتلی نوح ہیڈلینڈ پر واقع ہے جو جنوب کی طرف بحیرۂ عرب میں نکلی ہوئی ہے۔ یہ ماہی گیری (سارڈین اور شارک) اور تجارت کا اہم مرکز ہے؛ یہاں مچھلی کی پروسیسنگ کا کارخانہ ہے اور سمندری پانی سے نمک حاصل کیا جاتا ہے۔ سڑکیں گوادر کو مشرق میں پسنی اور شمال مشرق میں تربت سے ملاتی ہیں (بریٹانیکا)۔

عمان سے پاکستان تک

گوادر 1797 میں سلطنتِ مسقط و عمان کا حصہ بنا، اور 1958 میں عمان نے یہ شہر اور اس کا ملحقہ علاقہ پاکستان کے حوالے کیا (بریٹانیکا)۔ بلوچستان ہائی کورٹ کے مطابق یکم جولائی 1977 کو نیا ضلع گوادر بنا اور گوادر شہر اس کا صدر مقام قرار پایا؛ ضلع کی ساحلی پٹی تقریباً 600 کلومیٹر لمبی ہے (بلوچستان ہائی کورٹ)۔

اس حصے کی ہر بات صفحے پر دیے گئے ذرائع سے جانچی گئی ہے (آخری جانچ: 2026-09-30)۔

Gwadar District: FAQ

What is the population of Gwadar District?

The 2023 census counted 305,160 people in Gwadar District, up from 262,253 in 2017 (annual growth 2.56%). 52.12% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Gwadar District?

Gwadar District covers 12,637 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 24.15 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Gwadar District?

The 2023 census lists 5 urban localities in Gwadar District: Gwadar (70,852, municipal committee), Pasni (43,494, municipal committee), Jiwani (25,332, municipal committee), Ormara (16,573, municipal committee) and Ormara Cantonment (2,784, cantonment).

What is the literacy rate of Gwadar District?

50.3% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 57.62%, female 42.19%).

Which languages are spoken in Gwadar District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (97.66% of the 302,831 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Gwadar District in?

Gwadar District is in Balochistan, in Makuran Division. Its headquarters is Gwadar.

When did Gwadar become part of Pakistan?

In 1958, when Oman gave up the town and its hinterland to Pakistan. Gwadar had been part of the Sultanate of Muscat and Oman since 1797. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

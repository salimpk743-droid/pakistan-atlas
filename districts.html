Districts & villages

Pakistan has roughly 160–170 districts depending on the latest gazette. This page starts with signature districts. Add the rest in data/provinces.json.

To add a village photo: save it as images/villages/district-village.jpg and mention the file on that district card. Keep people’s faces and private homes out of photos unless you have permission.

How a full district page should look

When you expand the database, every district page should carry the same headings so students can compare places fairly:

  • Headquarters, area, 2017 and 2023 population, tehsils.
  • Public universities, degree colleges, medical and engineering campuses.
  • DHQ / THQ hospitals and major private hospitals.
  • Languages, crafts, urs or festivals, and two or three named villages.
  • Documented local issues: water, schools for girls, roads, floods or drought — written without insulting any community.

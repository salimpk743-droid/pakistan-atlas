Ziarat District: Quick Answers

Ziarat District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Loralai Division, with its headquarters at Ziarat. The 2023 census lists 2 administrative units in it: Sinjawi and Ziarat. The 2023 census counted 189,535 people on 3,301 km², and 43.37% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Ziarat District

The 2023 census counted 2 administrative units in Ziarat District: Sinjawi (sub-division) and Ziarat (sub-division). Sinjawi is the most populous, with 110,623 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,812 km²). Literacy ranges from 35.88% in Sinjawi to 54.2% in Ziarat.

Population and growth

Ziarat District had 189,535 people in the 2023 census, up from 160,095 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.86%). It covers 3,301 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 31st by population and 29th by area. 26.06% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.02 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Sinjawi (45,539) and Ziarat (3,863). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (98.85%).

Literacy and schooling

43.37% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 9th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 53.06% and female literacy 33.49%; the urban rate was 47.6% and the rural rate 41.83%. The census counted 43,568 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (22,782 girls and 20,781 boys).

Ziarat District: FAQ

What is the population of Ziarat District?

The 2023 census counted 189,535 people in Ziarat District, up from 160,095 in 2017 (annual growth 2.86%). 26.06% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Ziarat District?

Ziarat District covers 3,301 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 57.42 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Ziarat District?

The 2023 census lists 2 urban localities in Ziarat District: Sinjawi (45,539, municipal committee) and Ziarat (3,863, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Ziarat District?

43.37% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 53.06%, female 33.49%).

Which languages are spoken in Ziarat District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (98.85% of the 189,535 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Ziarat District in?

Ziarat District is in Balochistan, in Loralai Division. Its headquarters is Ziarat.

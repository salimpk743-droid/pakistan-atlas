Danish Taimoor: New Drama Nashtar (2026)

In 2026 Danish Taimoor leads Nashtar on Geo Entertainment as Shah Dawood, the proud heir of a powerful landowning family, opposite Anmol Baloch as Bareera Aijaz. Written by Maha Malik and directed by Seraj ul Haq for 7th Sky Entertainment, it premiered on 21 August 2026 and airs on Friday and Saturday at 8:00 pm.

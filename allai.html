Allai District: Quick Answers

Status
Allai is a tehsil of Battagram District. A January 2023 notification giving it district status was not implemented; KP chief ministers announced district status again in May 2025 and February 2026, and in August 2026 it was still described as a tehsil to be upgraded. MyBook.Pk does not count it as a district until a notification is issued.
2023 census
The census counted Allai tehsil under Battagram District: 218,149 people on 804 km². No separate district total was published.

Allai is the high valley west of the Indus climb from Thakot. It is a tehsil of Battagram District in Hazara Division. A Khyber Pakhtunkhwa notification of 22 January 2023 was to raise it to district status, but it was not implemented: the chief minister announced district status again in May 2025, said in February 2026 that the notification would be issued, and in August 2026 still described Allai as a tehsil to be upgraded. Banna is the tehsil headquarters. This page gives Allai’s own 2023 tehsil figures rather than Battagram’s combined count.

PBS 2023 for Allai (tehsil table): 218,149 people, area 804 km², density about 271 per km². Urban column: zero. Literacy 35.71 percent overall — 52.16 percent for men, 18.43 percent for women. Under the 2017 census the same belt was listed near 180,000. The road from Thakot to Banna is about two hours when it is open. Most villages after Banna are still jeep tracks or walking paths.

One tehsil, eight union councils

Public lists name these eight councils:

Banna, Bateela, Batkul, Biari, Jambera, Pashto, Rashang, Sakargah.

Banna is the administrative name people use for the headquarters. Pashto village is not “the Pashto language” — it is one of the eight councils. The valley is bounded north and east by Kohistan, south by the Kaghan / Nandihar side, and west by the Indus.

Pashto is the main house language on the 2023 tongue table. Older notes also list Hindko and Kohistani speech on the edges. Do not treat a tourist pamphlet as a fresh PBS row.

How the district was made

Allai was a small princely holding under its own Khans until it acceded to Pakistan. It later sat inside Battagram as a tehsil. The January 2023 notification was meant to make it a district so the valley would have its own deputy commissioner instead of sending every file down to Battagram bazaar. Public lists still name Prince Muhammad Nawaz Khan as chief of Allai Valley. That is a local title, not a substitute for the district administration.

Schools and the hospital

Female literacy at 18 percent is the education fact for this page. Degree students leave for Battagram, Mansehra or Abbottabad. A district hospital at Banna is the local demand. Until wards and staff are complete, Battagram DHQ remains the first public hospital many families still use. A maternity case carried on a wooden stretcher to the nearest jeep road is ordinary administration here, not a rare headline.

The 8 October 2005 earthquake hit this belt hard. Reports from the valley speak of thousands dead and of the road and the old cable way over the Indus being cut for days. Winter snow and monsoon slides still close the climb from Thakot. That date is how older people mark time, not a tourist line.

Buner District: Quick Answers

Buner District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Daggar. It has 6 tehsils: Chagharzai, Daggar, Gadezai, Gagra, Khado Khel and Mandanr. The 2023 census counted 1,016,869 people on 1,865 km², and 43.75% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Buner District

The 2023 census counted 6 tehsils in Buner District: Chagharzai, Daggar, Gadezai, Gagra, Khado Khel and Mandanr. Gadezai is the most populous, with 197,466 people, and it is also the largest by area (472 km²). Literacy ranges from 38.27% in Chagharzai to 47.57% in Daggar.

Population and growth

Buner District had 1,016,869 people in the 2023 census, up from 895,460 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.15%). Among the 35 Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts in the PBS 2023 census tables, it ranks 19th by population and 18th by area (1,865 km²). PBS counted no urban population in the district. There were 99.9 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Pashto was the most common mother tongue (95.91%), followed by Hindko (2.07%).

Literacy and schooling

43.75% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 19th of the 35 districts in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Male literacy was 60.61% and female literacy 27.4%. The census counted 123,628 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (79,433 girls and 44,193 boys).

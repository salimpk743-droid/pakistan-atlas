headquarters Bhakkar · 4 tehsils · 1,957,470 people (2023 census)

Bhakkar District: Quick Answers

Bhakkar District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Bhakkar. It has 4 tehsils: Bhakkar, Darya Khan, Kalur Kot and Mankera. The 2023 census counted 1,957,470 people on 8,153 km², and 55.68% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Bhakkar District

The 2023 census counted 4 tehsils in Bhakkar District: Bhakkar, Darya Khan, Kalur Kot and Mankera. Bhakkar is the most populous, with 809,789 people, and it is also the largest by area (2,427 km²). Literacy ranges from 51.09% in Darya Khan to 58.56% in Bhakkar.

Population and growth

Bhakkar District had 1,957,470 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,647,852 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.92%). It covers 8,153 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 26th by population and 7th by area. 18% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 108 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 8 urban localities; the largest are Bhakkar (131,658), Darya Khan (68,622), Kalur Kot (34,319) and Kotla Jam Dagger (27,726). Saraiki was the most common mother tongue (79.42%), followed by Punjabi (9.73%), Urdu (7.5%) and Pashto (2.41%).

Literacy and schooling

55.68% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 29th of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 66.81% and female literacy 43.87%; the urban rate was 71.67% and the rural rate 52.04%. The census counted 154,352 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (82,438 girls and 71,909 boys).

Bhakkar District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Bhakkar District?

Bhakkar District has 4 tehsils: Bhakkar, Darya Khan, Kalur Kot and Mankera.

What is the population of Bhakkar District?

The 2023 census counted 1,957,470 people in Bhakkar District, up from 1,647,852 in 2017 (annual growth 2.92%). 18% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Bhakkar District?

Bhakkar District covers 8,153 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 240.09 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Bhakkar District?

The 2023 census lists 8 urban localities in Bhakkar District: Bhakkar (131,658, municipal committee), Darya Khan (68,622, municipal committee), Kalur Kot (34,319, municipal committee), Kotla Jam Dagger (27,726, town committee), Hyderabad (25,319, town committee), Dullewala (25,276, municipal committee), Jandanwala (21,280, municipal committee) and Mankera (18,234, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Bhakkar District?

55.68% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 66.81%, female 43.87%).

Which languages are spoken in Bhakkar District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Saraiki (79.42% of the 1,956,971 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Punjabi (9.73%), Urdu (7.5%), Pashto (2.41%).

Which province is Bhakkar District in?

Bhakkar District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Bhakkar.

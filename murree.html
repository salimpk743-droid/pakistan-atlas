Murree District is the hill district of Rawalpindi Division. It was approved by Chief Minister Parvez Elahi on 14 October 2022 with four other new Punjab districts (Dawn), and it consists of two tehsils, Murree and Kotli Sattian, both formerly part of Rawalpindi District (Dawn). In February 2023 the caretaker government withdrew the notification and both became tehsils of Rawalpindi again (Dawn). The Board of Revenue notification of December 2024 made Murree a full district again, in Rawalpindi Division (ProPakistani; Daily Pakistan).

Murree and Kotli Sattian in the 2023 census

The census was taken while both tehsils were still in Rawalpindi District, so PBS publishes no Murree District total. Together the two tehsil rows count 372,947 people on 738 km² (PBS Table 1). Murree tehsil: 434 km², 252,526 people, literacy 84.79%. Kotli Sattian tehsil: 304 km², 120,421 people, literacy 88.2%.

Across the two tehsils, 85.90% of people aged 10 and over were literate: 92.13% of men and 79.52% of women (PBS Table 12). That is higher than any district row in Punjab's 2023 census; the highest, Rawalpindi at 83.22%, still included Murree and Kotli Sattian. PBS publishes mother-tongue figures by district only, so there is no separate language count for Murree.

The hill station

Murree town stands on a ridge of the Murree Hills at 7,517 feet (about 2,290 m). The site was chosen in 1850, soon after the British annexed the Punjab. Troops were first housed there in 1851 and permanent barracks were built in 1853. Until 1877 it was the usual summer seat of the Punjab government, which then moved to Simla (Imperial Gazetteer of India). The 1911 Britannica describes it as the starting point for Kashmir, with views of the snow peaks of Kashmir (Britannica 1911).

Murree District: FAQ

When did Murree become a district?

Murree was approved as a district on 14 October 2022. The notification was withdrawn in February 2023, and Murree was made a full district again in the Punjab notification of December 2024. (ProPakistani)

Which tehsils are in Murree District?

Two: Murree and Kotli Sattian, both formerly part of Rawalpindi District. (Dawn)

What is the population of Murree District?

The 2023 census counted 372,947 people in the Murree (252,526) and Kotli Sattian (120,421) tehsil rows, then part of Rawalpindi District. PBS published no separate district total. (Pakistan Bureau of Statistics)

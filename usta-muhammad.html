Usta Muhammad District: Quick Answers

2023 census
The census counted Usta Muhammad sub-division under Jafarabad District: 210,870 people on 399 km². No separate district total was published.

Administrative status: Quetta Voice, 6 June 2022.

The Balochistan cabinet approved Usta Muhammad as a new district, along with Hub and Karezat, in June 2022 (Quetta Voice). It was carved out of Jafarabad District.

2023 census

PBS counted Usta Muhammad as a sub-division of Jafarabad District: 210,870 people on 399 km², 30.65% urban, with literacy of 38.17% (PBS Table 1).

headquarters Khanewal · 4 tehsils · 3,364,077 people (2023 census)

Khanewal District: Quick Answers

Khanewal District is one of the 41 districts of Punjab, with its headquarters at Khanewal. It has 4 tehsils: Jahanian, Kabirwala, Khanewal and Mian Channu. The 2023 census counted 3,364,077 people on 4,349 km², and 60.97% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Khanewal District

The 2023 census counted 4 tehsils in Khanewal District: Jahanian, Kabirwala, Khanewal and Mian Channu. Kabirwala is the most populous, with 1,119,229 people, and it is also the largest by area (1,804 km²). Literacy ranges from 54.13% in Kabirwala to 65.65% in Jahanian.

Population and growth

Khanewal District had 3,364,077 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,920,233 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.39%). It covers 4,349 km². Of the 36 Punjab districts in the census, it ranks 17th by population and 19th by area. 21.31% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 106.48 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 8 urban localities; the largest are Khanewal (281,890), Mian Channu (140,112), Kabirwala (91,932) and Abdul Hakim (67,501). Punjabi was the most common mother tongue (70.41%), followed by Saraiki (19.41%), Urdu (8.05%) and Pashto (1.09%).

Literacy and schooling

60.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 23rd of the 36 districts in Punjab. Male literacy was 69.26% and female literacy 52.18%; the urban rate was 77.56% and the rural rate 56.27%. The census counted 270,019 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (137,897 girls and 132,096 boys).

Khanewal District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Khanewal District?

Khanewal District has 4 tehsils: Jahanian, Kabirwala, Khanewal and Mian Channu.

What is the population of Khanewal District?

The 2023 census counted 3,364,077 people in Khanewal District, up from 2,920,233 in 2017 (annual growth 2.39%). 21.31% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Khanewal District?

Khanewal District covers 4,349 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 773.53 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Khanewal District?

The 2023 census lists 8 urban localities in Khanewal District: Khanewal (281,890, municipal committee), Mian Channu (140,112, municipal committee), Kabirwala (91,932, municipal committee), Abdul Hakim (67,501, town committee), Jahanian (50,318, municipal committee), Tulamba (35,069, town committee), Makhdoompur Pahoran (29,065, town committee) and Sarai Sidhu (20,899, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Khanewal District?

60.97% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 69.26%, female 52.18%).

Which languages are spoken in Khanewal District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Punjabi (70.41% of the 3,363,589 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Saraiki (19.41%), Urdu (8.05%), Pashto (1.09%).

Which province is Khanewal District in?

Khanewal District is in Punjab. Its headquarters is Khanewal.

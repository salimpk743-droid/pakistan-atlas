Sherani District: Quick Answers

Administrative units of Sherani District

The 2023 census counted 1 administrative units in Sherani District: Sherani (sub-division).

Population and growth

Sherani District had 191,687 people in the 2023 census, up from 152,952 in 2017 (average annual growth 3.84%). It covers 4,310 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 30th by population and 20th by area. PBS counted no urban population in the district. There were 122.02 males for every 100 females.

Languages

Pashto was the most common mother tongue (99.87%).

Literacy and schooling

23.86% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 33rd of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 31.53% and female literacy 15.02%. The census counted 55,915 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (25,645 girls and 30,268 boys).

Sherani District: FAQ

What is the population of Sherani District?

The 2023 census counted 191,687 people in Sherani District, up from 152,952 in 2017 (annual growth 3.84%). 0% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Sherani District?

Sherani District covers 4,310 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 44.47 people per km².

Is Sherani District urban or rural?

Entirely rural: the 2023 census counted no urban population in Sherani District, so all 191,687 residents were enumerated in rural areas.

What is the literacy rate of Sherani District?

23.86% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 31.53%, female 15.02%).

Which languages are spoken in Sherani District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.87% of the 191,673 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Sherani District in?

Sherani District is in Balochistan, in Zhob Division. Its headquarters is Sherani.

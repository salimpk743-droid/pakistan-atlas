Zhob River valley · Apozai · Fort Sandeman · Pashtun country

Zhob District: Quick Answers

Zhob District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Zhob Division, with its headquarters at Zhob. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Ashwat, Kakar Khurasan, Kashatoo, Sambaza and Zhob. The 2023 census counted 355,692 people on 15,987 km², and 36.62% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Zhob town

Zhob lies on an open plain just east of the Zhob River. Originally called Apozai, a name still used locally, it was renamed Fort Sandeman after Sir Robert Sandeman in 1889 and kept that name until the 1970s. On a ridge to the north stands the Castle, the political agent's residence under British rule. Zhob is linked by road with Quetta, Bannu and Peshawar (Britannica).

Administrative units of Zhob District

The 2023 census counted 5 administrative units in Zhob District: Ashwat (sub-tehsil), Kakar Khurasan (sub-division), Kashatoo (sub-tehsil), Sambaza (sub-tehsil) and Zhob (sub-division). Zhob is the most populous, with 284,620 people, and it is also the largest by area (9,322 km²). Literacy ranges from 9.64% in Kashatoo to 41.27% in Zhob.

Population and growth

Zhob District had 355,692 people in the 2023 census, up from 310,354 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.3%). It covers 15,987 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 13th by population and 7th by area. 13.21% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 117.57 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Zhob (44,251) and Zhob Cantonment (2,725). Pashto was the most common mother tongue (97.65%), followed by Saraiki (1.49%).

Literacy and schooling

36.62% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 19th of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 45.24% and female literacy 26.81%; the urban rate was 70.18% and the rural rate 31.33%. The census counted 73,649 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (36,651 girls and 36,995 boys).

Zhob District: FAQ

What is the population of Zhob District?

The 2023 census counted 355,692 people in Zhob District, up from 310,354 in 2017 (annual growth 2.3%). 13.21% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Zhob District?

Zhob District covers 15,987 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 22.25 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Zhob District?

The 2023 census lists 2 urban localities in Zhob District: Zhob (44,251, municipal committee) and Zhob Cantonment (2,725, cantonment).

What is the literacy rate of Zhob District?

36.62% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 45.24%, female 26.81%).

Which languages are spoken in Zhob District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (97.65% of the 355,556 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Saraiki (1.49%).

Which province is Zhob District in?

Zhob District is in Balochistan, in Zhob Division. Its headquarters is Zhob.

What was the old name of Zhob?

Fort Sandeman, after Sir Robert Sandeman, from 1889 until the 1970s; the original local name is Apozai. (Encyclopaedia Britannica)

Sanghar District

headquarters Sanghar · 6 talukas · 2,308,465 people (2023 census)

Sanghar District: Quick Answers

Sanghar District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Sanghar. It has 6 talukas: Jam Nawaz Ali, Khipro, Sanghar, Shahdadpur, Sinjhoro and Tando Adam. The 2023 census counted 2,308,465 people on 10,728 km², and 43.66% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Sanghar town

Sanghar is a market town linked by road with Hyderabad, Karachi and Sukkur. The surrounding land is mostly semi-arid, part of the Thar Desert, with cropped areas irrigated by the Mithrao Canal system; rice, wheat and cotton are the chief crops (Britannica).

Talukas of Sanghar District

The 2023 census counted 6 talukas in Sanghar District: Jam Nawaz Ali, Khipro, Sanghar, Shahdadpur, Sinjhoro and Tando Adam. Shahdadpur is the most populous, with 525,164 people, and Khipro is the largest by area (5,933 km²). Literacy ranges from 31.67% in Jam Nawaz Ali to 50.81% in Tando Adam.

Population and growth

Sanghar District had 2,308,465 people in the 2023 census, up from 2,049,873 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.01%). It covers 10,728 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 9th by population and 4th by area. 27.32% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 103.25 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 15 urban localities; the largest are Tando Adam (174,291), Shahdadpur (113,342), Sanghar (74,112) and Khipro (58,307). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (76.13%), followed by Urdu (8.69%), Punjabi (5.38%) and Balochi (2.05%).

Literacy and schooling

43.66% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 19th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 53.11% and female literacy 33.85%; the urban rate was 65.13% and the rural rate 34.84%. The census counted 418,695 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (231,919 girls and 186,759 boys).

Sanghar District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Sanghar District?

Sanghar District has 6 talukas: Jam Nawaz Ali, Khipro, Sanghar, Shahdadpur, Sinjhoro and Tando Adam.

What is the population of Sanghar District?

The 2023 census counted 2,308,465 people in Sanghar District, up from 2,049,873 in 2017 (annual growth 2.01%). 27.32% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Sanghar District?

Sanghar District covers 10,728 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 215.18 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Sanghar District?

The 2023 census lists 15 urban localities in Sanghar District; the ten largest are Tando Adam (174,291, municipal committee), Shahdadpur (113,342, municipal committee), Sanghar (74,112, municipal committee), Khipro (58,307, town committee), Shahpur Chakar (42,365, town committee), Jatia (41,993, town committee), Sinjhoro (22,637, municipal committee), Berani (20,946, town committee), Jhol (19,364, town committee) and Sarhari (18,351, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Sanghar District?

43.66% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 53.11%, female 33.85%).

Which languages are spoken in Sanghar District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (76.13% of the 2,307,831 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Urdu (8.69%), Punjabi (5.38%), Balochi (2.05%).

Which province is Sanghar District in?

Sanghar District is in Sindh. Its headquarters is Sanghar.

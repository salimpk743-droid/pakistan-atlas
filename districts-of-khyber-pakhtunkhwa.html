Districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has 38 districts (September 2026). An alphabetical list with headquarters, 2023 census population and tehsils where the census publishes them.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts, A–Z

Abbottabad

Abbottabad District

Population: 1,419,072 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Abbottabad, Havelian, Lora and Lower Tanawal

Abbottabad is a hill city on the approach to the Karakoram Highway, known for its cool climate and dense concentration of colleges and universities.

Khar

Bajaur District

Population: 1,287,960 (2023 census)

7 tehsils: Bar Chamer Kand, Barang, Khar Bajaur, Mamund, Nawagai, Salarzai and Utman Khel

Bajaur was a tribal agency on the Afghan border until it became a settled KP district after the 2018 FATA merger.

Bannu

Bannu District

Population: 1,357,890 (2023 census)

Bannu is a southern KP city on the route toward Waziristan, historically an important trading and garrison town.

Matta

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District

Population: 1,130,354 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) was notified as a separate district in January 2026, following a provincial cabinet decision of 19 December 2025. Its headquarters is Matta and it has three tehsils: Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela.

Battagram

Battagram District

Population: 554,133 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Allai and Batagram

Battagram sits on the Karakoram Highway in Hazara Division, with a cool hill climate around its market-town headquarters.

Daggar

Buner District

Population: 1,016,869 (2023 census)

6 tehsils: Chagharzai, Daggar, Gadezai, Gagra, Khado Khel and Mandanr

Buner is a district of green valleys tucked between Swat and Mardan, with Daggar as its headquarters.

Charsadda

Charsadda District

Population: 1,835,504 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Charsadda, Shabqadar and Tangi

Charsadda covers the fertile Hashtnagar plain just north of Peshawar, one of the province's most productive farming belts.

D.I. Khan

Dera Ismail Khan District

Population: 1,829,811 (2023 census, pre-split)

Dera Ismail Khan is a Saraiki-Pashtun city on the Indus in southern KP; it lost its northern Paharpur and Paniala tehsils to a new district in October 2025.

Hangu

Hangu District

Population: 528,902 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Hangu and Tall

Hangu is a small district west of Kohat, with a mostly rural upland population.

Haripur

Haripur District

Population: 1,174,783 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Ghazi, Haripur and Khanpur

Haripur is a Hazara district that combines farmland with industry at Hattar and proximity to Tarbela Dam.

Karak

Karak District

Population: 815,878 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Banda Daud Shah, Karak and Takht-e-Nasrati

Karak is an oil-and-gas producing upland district south of Kohat.

Landi Kotal / Jamrud

Khyber District

Population: 1,146,267 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Bara, Jamrud, Landi Kotal and Mulla Gori

Khyber district takes its name from the historic Khyber Pass and was a tribal agency until the 2018 FATA merger.

Kohat

Kohat District

Population: 1,234,661 (2023 census)

Kohat is a southern KP city built around a cantonment, with a long-established medical college.

Kolai-Palas District

Population: 280,162 (2023 census)

2 sub-divisions: Battaira and Palas

Kolai-Palas is one of KP's newest districts, formed from the left bank of the Indus in the Kohistan region.

Parachinar

Kurram District

Population: 785,434 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Central Kurram F.R, Lower Kurram and Upper Kurram

Kurram is a high valley district toward the Afghan border, long known for its apple orchards and its history as a tribal agency.

Lakki Marwat

Lakki Marwat District

Population: 1,040,856 (2023 census)

Lakki Marwat covers the sandy southern plains of KP.

Chitral

Lower Chitral District

Population: 320,407 (2023 census)

2 sub-divisions: Chitral and Drosh

Lower Chitral covers the Kalash valleys and Chitral town, sitting in the shadow of Trich Mir, Pakistan's highest peak outside the Karakoram.

Timergara

Lower Dir District

Population: 1,650,183 (2023 census)

4 sub-divisions: Adenzai, Lal Qila, Samarbagh and Temergara

Lower Dir covers the Panjkora valley below Upper Dir, with Timergara as its headquarters.

Pattan

Lower Kohistan District

Population: 340,017 (2023 census)

Lower Kohistan is an Indus gorge district on the Karakoram Highway, with Pattan as its headquarters.

Batkhela

Malakand District

Population: 826,250 (2023 census)

2 sub-divisions: Sam Rani Zai and Swat Rani Zai

Malakand district sits at the historic Malakand Pass, the gateway between the Peshawar valley and Swat.

Mansehra

Mansehra District

Population: 1,797,177 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Baffa Pakhal, Balakot, Darband, Mansehra and Oghi

Mansehra is the Hazara gateway to the Kaghan valley and the Karakoram Highway.

Mardan

Mardan District

Population: 2,744,898 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Ghari Kapoora, Katlang, Mardan, Rustam and Takht Bhai

Mardan is KP's second-largest city, built on the sugar and farm economy of the Yousafzai plain.

Ghalanai

Mohmand District

Population: 553,933 (2023 census)

7 tehsils: Ambar Utman Khel, Halim Zai, Pindiali, Prang Ghar, Safi, Upper Mohmand and Yake Ghund

Mohmand district lies north of Peshawar and was a tribal agency until the 2018 FATA merger.

Miran Shah

North Waziristan District

Population: 693,332 (2023 census)

9 tehsils: Datta Khel, Dossali, Gharyum, Ghulam Khan, Mir Ali, Miran Shah, Razmak, Shewa and Spinwam

North Waziristan was a tribal agency in western KP, home to the mountain towns of Miran Shah and Mir Ali, before becoming a settled district in 2018.

Nowshera

Nowshera District

Population: 1,740,705 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Jehangira, Nowshera and Pabbi

Nowshera is a Kabul River and Grand Trunk Road district east of Peshawar, with cantonments at Nowshera, Risalpur and Cherat.

Kalaya

Orakzai District

Population: 387,561 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Central, Ismail Zai, Lower and Upper

Orakzai is a hill district in Kohat Division. It was Orakzai Agency from 1973 until the 2018 merger with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Paharpur

Paharpur District

Paharpur became a new KP district in October 2025, carved out of Dera Ismail Khan's Paharpur and Paniala tehsils.

Peshawar

Peshawar District

Population: 4,758,762 (2023 census)

Peshawar is the provincial capital and one of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the region, blending the historic Qissa Khwani bazaar with new housing developments.

Alpuri

Shangla District

Population: 891,252 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Alpuri, Bisham, Chakisar, Martung and Puran

Shangla is a high district sitting between Swat and the Indus River, with Alpuri as its headquarters.

Wana

South Waziristan Lower District

South Waziristan Lower was created alongside South Waziristan Upper in April 2022, with Wana as its headquarters.

Spinkai Raghazai

South Waziristan Upper District

South Waziristan Upper was created in April 2022, when the former South Waziristan district was divided in two, with Spinkai Raghazai as its headquarters.

Swabi

Swabi District

Population: 1,894,600 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Lahor, Razar, Swabi and Topi

Swabi sits on the Indus and Kabul river plains, bordering the Gadoon industrial zone and the edge of Tarbela Dam.

Gulkada (Babuzai tehsil)

Swat District

Population: 1,557,030 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot

Swat is the former princely state on the Swat River, known for its Buddhist archaeology and mountain valleys. Since January 2026 it has four tehsils (Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot), with its headquarters at Gulkada in Babuzai.

Tank

Tank District

Population: 470,293 (2023 census)

Tank is a small southern district sitting at the edge of Waziristan.

Judba

Torghar District

Population: 200,445 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Daur Mera, Judba and Khander

Torghar is a small mountain district of Hazara Division, created out of Mansehra.

Booni

Upper Chitral District

Population: 195,528 (2023 census)

1 sub-division: Mastuj

Upper Chitral covers the high valleys toward the Wakhan Corridor, with Booni as its headquarters.

Dir

Upper Dir District

Population: 1,083,566 (2023 census)

4 sub-divisions: Dir, Larjum, Sharingal and Wari

Upper Dir covers the high country toward the Lowari Pass and the Kumrat valley.

Dassu

Upper Kohistan District

Population: 422,947 (2023 census)

Upper Kohistan covers the Indus gorge around the Dasu Dam project.

How many districts does Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have?

38 districts in 7 divisions, as of September 2026. 35 districts in the PBS 2023 census tables (South Waziristan counted as one), plus the 2022 split of South Waziristan into Upper and Lower South Waziristan, Paharpur (notified 26 November 2025) and Bar Swat (notified 27 January 2026).

Press totals disagree (The News: 37 after Bar Swat; MM News: 40). Allai is not counted: a January 2023 notification was never implemented, and KP chief ministers announced district status again in May 2025, February 2026 and August 2026 without a new notification.

Sources: APP, 2025-11-26; The News, 2026-01-28; Dawn, 2026-01-28; APP, 2025-05-23; Dawn, 2026-02-25; The Public Purview, 2026-08-29.

announced, not yet notified

Allai District

Allai is a tehsil of Battagram District. A January 2023 notification giving it district status was not implemented; KP chief ministers announced district status again in May 2025 and February 2026, and in August 2026 it was still described as a tehsil to be upgraded. MyBook.Pk does not count it as a district until a notification is issued.

Sources and editorial method

Population, area and tehsil figures are taken from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Table 1. Districts created after the census are marked, and no figure is shown where no official number exists.

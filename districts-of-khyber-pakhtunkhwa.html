Districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has 38 districts (September 2026). An alphabetical list with headquarters, 2023 census population and tehsils where the census publishes them.

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa districts, A–Z

Abbottabad

Abbottabad District

Population: 1,419,072 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Abbottabad, Havelian, Lora and Lower Tanawal

Hill district of Hazara whose headquarters, Abbottabad, was founded in 1853 and named after Major James Abbott; home of the Pakistan Military Academy at Kakul.

Khar

Bajaur District

Population: 1,287,960 (2023 census)

7 tehsils: Bar Chamer Kand, Barang, Khar Bajaur, Mamund, Nawagai, Salarzai and Utman Khel

Former FATA tribal agency, a district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Malakand Division) since the 2018 merger.

Bannu

Bannu District

Population: 1,357,890 (2023 census)

District on the Kurram and Tochi rivers in southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; Bannu town was founded in 1848 as a military base by Herbert Edwardes.

Matta

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District

Population: 1,130,354 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela

Bar Swat (Upper Swat) District, notified in January 2026 from the Matta, Bahrain and Khwazakhela tehsils of Swat; headquarters Matta.

Battagram

Battagram District

Population: 554,133 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Allai and Batagram

Battagram District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 554,133 people in the 2023 census, 2 tehsils, headquarters Battagram.

Daggar

Buner District

Population: 1,016,869 (2023 census)

6 tehsils: Chagharzai, Daggar, Gadezai, Gagra, Khado Khel and Mandanr

Buner District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 1,016,869 people in the 2023 census, 6 tehsils, headquarters Daggar.

Charsadda

Charsadda District

Population: 1,835,504 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Charsadda, Shabqadar and Tangi

Farming district of the Peshawar Valley; the Bala Hisar mound at Charsadda is identified with Pushkalavati, a one-time capital of Gandhara.

D.I. Khan

Dera Ismail Khan District

Population: 1,829,811 (2023 census, pre-split)

District on the west bank of the Indus in southern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; its headquarters town was named after Ismail Khan, son of the 15th-century Baloch chief who founded it.

Hangu

Hangu District

Population: 528,902 (2023 census)

2 tehsils: Hangu and Tall

Hangu District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 528,902 people in the 2023 census, 2 tehsils, headquarters Hangu.

Haripur

Haripur District

Population: 1,174,783 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Ghazi, Haripur and Khanpur

Haripur District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 1,174,783 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Haripur.

Karak

Karak District

Population: 815,878 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Banda Daud Shah, Karak and Takht-e-Nasrati

District south of Kohat that became separate on 1 July 1982; known for its rock salt, gypsum, oil and gas.

Landi Kotal / Jamrud

Khyber District

Population: 1,146,267 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Bara, Jamrud, Landi Kotal and Mulla Gori

Former FATA agency on the Khyber Pass, a district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Peshawar Division) since the 2018 merger.

Kohat

Kohat District

Population: 1,234,661 (2023 census)

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa district whose headquarters stands at the entrance to the Kohat Pass; Darra Adam Khel is one of its sub-divisions.

Kolai-Palas District

Population: 280,162 (2023 census)

2 sub-divisions: Battaira and Palas

Kolai-Palas District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 280,162 people in the 2023 census, 2 administrative units, headquarters Kuz Paro / notified HQ.

Parachinar

Kurram District

Population: 785,434 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Central Kurram F.R, Lower Kurram and Upper Kurram

Former FATA tribal agency, a district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Kohat Division) since the 2018 merger.

Lakki Marwat

Lakki Marwat District

Population: 1,040,856 (2023 census)

Lakki Marwat District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 1,040,856 people in the 2023 census, 4 administrative units, headquarters Lakki Marwat.

Chitral

Lower Chitral District

Population: 320,407 (2023 census)

2 sub-divisions: Chitral and Drosh

Lower Chitral District, created when Chitral was divided in 2018; its headquarters is Chitral town on the Kunar (Chitral) River.

Timergara

Lower Dir District

Population: 1,650,183 (2023 census)

4 sub-divisions: Adenzai, Lal Qila, Samarbagh and Temergara

Lower Dir District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 1,650,183 people in the 2023 census, 4 administrative units, headquarters Timergara.

Pattan

Lower Kohistan District

Population: 340,017 (2023 census)

District created on 15 January 2014 when Kohistan was divided; its headquarters is Pattan.

Batkhela

Malakand District

Population: 826,250 (2023 census)

2 sub-divisions: Sam Rani Zai and Swat Rani Zai

Malakand District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 826,250 people in the 2023 census, 2 administrative units, headquarters Batkhela.

Mansehra

Mansehra District

Population: 1,797,177 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Baffa Pakhal, Balakot, Darband, Mansehra and Oghi

Hazara district whose headquarters lies on the Pakhli Plain; it includes the Kagan Valley and the Ashokan rock edicts near Mansehra town.

Mardan

Mardan District

Population: 2,744,898 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Ghari Kapoora, Katlang, Mardan, Rustam and Takht Bhai

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa district on the Yusufzai plain; home to the Takht-i-Bahi Buddhist ruins and the Ashokan rock edicts at Shahbazgarhi.

Ghalanai

Mohmand District

Population: 553,933 (2023 census)

7 tehsils: Ambar Utman Khel, Halim Zai, Pindiali, Prang Ghar, Safi, Upper Mohmand and Yake Ghund

Former FATA tribal agency, a district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Peshawar Division) since the 2018 merger.

Miran Shah

North Waziristan District

Population: 693,332 (2023 census)

9 tehsils: Datta Khel, Dossali, Gharyum, Ghulam Khan, Mir Ali, Miran Shah, Razmak, Shewa and Spinwam

Former FATA tribal agency, a district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (Bannu Division) since the 2018 merger.

Nowshera

Nowshera District

Population: 1,740,705 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Jehangira, Nowshera and Pabbi

Nowshera is a Kabul River and Grand Trunk Road district east of Peshawar, with cantonments at Nowshera, Risalpur and Cherat.

Kalaya

Orakzai District

Population: 387,561 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Central, Ismail Zai, Lower and Upper

Orakzai is a hill district in Kohat Division. It was Orakzai Agency from 1973 until the 2018 merger with Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Paharpur

Paharpur District

District created from Dera Ismail Khan in November 2025, made up of Paharpur and Paniyala tehsils with its headquarters at Paharpur.

Peshawar

Peshawar District

Population: 4,758,762 (2023 census)

Capital district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa; Peshawar, once capital of Gandhara, lies near the Khyber Pass.

Alpuri

Shangla District

Population: 891,252 (2023 census)

5 tehsils: Alpuri, Bisham, Chakisar, Martung and Puran

Shangla District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 891,252 people in the 2023 census, 5 tehsils, headquarters Alpuri.

Wana

South Waziristan Lower District

Ahmadzai Wazir-majority district formed in October 2022 from South Waziristan; headquarters Wana, four tehsils.

Spinkai Raghazai

South Waziristan Upper District

Mehsud-majority district formed in October 2022 from South Waziristan; headquarters Spinkai Raghazai, seven tehsils.

Swabi

Swabi District

Population: 1,894,600 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Lahor, Razar, Swabi and Topi

Swabi District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 1,894,600 people in the 2023 census, 4 tehsils, headquarters Swabi.

Gulkada (Babuzai tehsil)

Swat District

Population: 1,557,030 (2023 census)

4 tehsils: Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot

Former princely state on the Swat River; since January 2026 a district of four tehsils (Babuzai, Kabal, Charbagh and Barikot) with its headquarters at Gulkada.

Tank

Tank District

Population: 470,293 (2023 census)

Tank District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 470,293 people in the 2023 census, 2 administrative units, headquarters Tank.

Judba

Torghar District

Population: 200,445 (2023 census)

3 tehsils: Daur Mera, Judba and Khander

Torghar District, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa: 200,445 people in the 2023 census, 3 tehsils, headquarters Judba.

Booni

Upper Chitral District

Population: 195,528 (2023 census)

1 sub-division: Mastuj

Upper Chitral District, notified in November 2018 with its headquarters at Booni, made up of the Mastuj and Torkhow/Mulkhow sub-divisions.

Dir

Upper Dir District

Population: 1,083,566 (2023 census)

4 sub-divisions: Dir, Larjum, Sharingal and Wari

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa district in the upper Panjkora valley, part of the former princely state of Dir; its headquarters is Dir town.

Dassu

Upper Kohistan District

Population: 422,947 (2023 census)

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa district created in January 2014 when Kohistan was divided into Upper and Lower Kohistan.

How many districts does Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have?

38 districts in 7 divisions, as of September 2026. 35 districts in the PBS 2023 census tables (South Waziristan counted as one), plus the 2022 split of South Waziristan into Upper and Lower South Waziristan, Paharpur (notified 26 November 2025) and Bar Swat (notified 27 January 2026).

Press totals disagree (The News: 37 after Bar Swat; MM News: 40). Allai is not counted: a January 2023 notification was never implemented, and KP chief ministers announced district status again in May 2025, February 2026 and August 2026 without a new notification.

Sources: APP, 2025-11-26; The News, 2026-01-28; Dawn, 2026-01-28; APP, 2025-05-23; Dawn, 2026-02-25; The Public Purview, 2026-08-29.

announced, not yet notified

Allai District

Allai is a tehsil of Battagram District. A January 2023 notification giving it district status was not implemented; KP chief ministers announced district status again in May 2025 and February 2026, and in August 2026 it was still described as a tehsil to be upgraded. MyBook.Pk does not count it as a district until a notification is issued.

Sources and editorial method

Population, area and tehsil figures are taken from the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics 2023 census, Table 1. Districts created after the census are marked, and no figure is shown where no official number exists.

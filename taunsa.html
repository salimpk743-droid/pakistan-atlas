Taunsa District: Quick Answers

2023 census
The census counted Taunsa tehsil under Dera Ghazi Khan District: 796,777 people on 2,769 km². No separate district total was published.
Headquarters
Taunsa Sharif
Province / territory
Punjab

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, Census 2023, Table 1.

Separated from D.G. Khan. Shrine town and the road toward Fort Munro.

Population and headquarters

The district headquarters is Taunsa Sharif. The available public data note is Newer district — confirm latest PBS row. Check the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics and the relevant provincial department for updated official tables.

Administrative status: Dawn, 14 October 2022.

South Waziristan Lower is the Ahmadzai Wazir-majority half of the former South Waziristan tribal district. The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa cabinet approved dividing South Waziristan on 12 April 2022 (Dawn). The formal notification, under the Land Revenue Act 1967, came in October 2022, and it took Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to 36 districts (Dawn). The notification gave Lower one sub-division, Wana, which is also the headquarters, and four tehsils: Wana, Shakai, Tolkhela (Toi Khulla) and Birmal (Dawn).

2023 census rows

PBS counted the whole of South Waziristan as one district, with no urban population (PBS Table 1). The three census tehsils that are now in Lower add up to 400,237 people on 3,805 km². Shakai has no separate row. Wana tehsil: 2,315 km², 184,645 people, literacy 31.29%. Birmil tehsil: 923 km², 112,757 people, literacy 23.06%. Toi Khulla tehsil: 567 km², 102,835 people, literacy 7.81%.

Across these three tehsils, 22.99% of people aged 10 and over were literate. Toi Khulla's 7.81% was the lowest tehsil rate in the old district: 10.64% for men and 4.69% for women (PBS Table 12). For the combined figures and the Upper district, see South Waziristan.

South Waziristan Lower District: FAQ

When was South Waziristan divided?

The KP cabinet approved the division on 12 April 2022; the notification creating South Waziristan Upper and Lower came in October 2022. (Dawn)

What is the headquarters of South Waziristan Lower?

Wana. (Dawn)

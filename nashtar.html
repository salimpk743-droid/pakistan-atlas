Nashtar Drama: Cast and Story

Nashtar (نشتر) is a romantic drama on Geo Entertainment (Har Pal Geo), written by Maha Malik and directed by Seraj ul Haq, starring Danish Taimoor, Anmol Baloch.

Nashtar Story

Nashtar (also spelt Nishter) is a Geo Entertainment drama about Shah Dawood, the proud heir of a powerful landowning family who is used to getting his way, and Bareera Aijaz, a principled university student who refuses to be intimidated by his status.

Their first public clash turns into a battle of egos, and circumstances then force them into an unexpected marriage. Living together sharpens their differences, but resentment slowly turns into respect. Their bond is threatened by Tamseel, who has always loved Shah Dawood and will not accept Bareera. Geo describes it as a tale that moves from hatred towards love and passion.

Writer, Director and Production of Nashtar

Nashtar is written by Maha Malik and directed by Seraj ul Haq (also spelt Siraj-ul-Haq), the director of Tere Bin. It is a 7th Sky Entertainment presentation produced by Abdullah Kadwani and Asad Qureshi.

Producer / production house: Abdullah Kadwani and Asad Qureshi (7th Sky Entertainment).

Nashtar OST

The OST lyrics are by SK Khalish, with vocals and composition by Shani Arshad. See the Pakistani drama OSTs guide for more soundtracks.

When and Where to Watch Nashtar

Nashtar airs on Geo Entertainment (Har Pal Geo), Friday and Saturday, 8:00 pm (Pakistan time). It premiered on 21 August 2026. Schedules and repeat timings change, so check the official Geo Entertainment website for the latest episode and timing. Channels also upload episodes to their official YouTube channels.

Zindagi Gulzar Hai — زندگی گلزار ہے

Zindagi Gulzar Hai (زندگی گلزار ہے) is a landmark HUM TV drama associated with Sanam Saeed and Fawad Khan, exploring class, ambition, relationships and self-discovery.

ChannelHUM TV
WriterUmera Ahmed

Story

Zindagi Gulzar Hai follows Kashaf and Zaroon and explores class differences, gender expectations, education, career ambition and relationships.

Larkana District

Moenjodaro · UNESCO World Heritage Site · headquarters Larkana · 4 talukas · 1,784,453 people (2023 census)

Larkana District: Quick Answers

Larkana District is one of the 30 districts of Sindh, with its headquarters at Larkana. It has 4 talukas: Bakrani, Dokri, Larkana and Ratodero. The 2023 census counted 1,784,453 people on 1,948 km², and 55.58% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Moenjodaro

The ruins of Moenjodaro lie on the right bank of the Indus River, 28 km from Larkana city in Larkana District. It was the metropolis of the Indus civilization and was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1980 (UNESCO). Its archaeological importance was first recognised in 1922, a year after the discovery of Harappa, and later excavations showed it had been the largest city of the Indus civilization (Britannica).

Talukas of Larkana District

The 2023 census counted 4 talukas in Larkana District: Bakrani, Dokri, Larkana and Ratodero. Larkana is the most populous, with 873,868 people, and Ratodero is the largest by area (562 km²). Literacy ranges from 46.38% in Bakrani to 58.93% in Ratodero.

Population and growth

Larkana District had 1,784,453 people in the 2023 census, up from 1,521,786 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.7%). It covers 1,948 km². Of the 30 Sindh districts in the census, it ranks 13th by population and 20th by area. 44.73% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 109.5 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 7 urban localities; the largest are Larkana (551,716), Ratodero (81,935), Naudero (52,846) and Badah (43,756). Sindhi was the most common mother tongue (98.94%).

Literacy and schooling

55.58% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 11th of the 30 districts in Sindh. Male literacy was 65.34% and female literacy 44.96%; the urban rate was 65.46% and the rural rate 47.1%. The census counted 252,953 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (134,774 girls and 118,172 boys).

Larkana District: FAQ

How many talukas are in Larkana District?

Larkana District has 4 talukas: Bakrani, Dokri, Larkana and Ratodero.

What is the population of Larkana District?

The 2023 census counted 1,784,453 people in Larkana District, up from 1,521,786 in 2017 (annual growth 2.7%). 44.73% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Larkana District?

Larkana District covers 1,948 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 916.04 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Larkana District?

The 2023 census lists 7 urban localities in Larkana District: Larkana (551,716, municipal corporation), Ratodero (81,935, municipal committee), Naudero (52,846, municipal committee), Badah (43,756, town committee), Dokri (27,125, town committee), Arija (26,047, town committee) and Garello (14,726, town committee).

What is the literacy rate of Larkana District?

55.58% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 65.34%, female 44.96%).

Which languages are spoken in Larkana District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Sindhi (98.94% of the 1,784,453 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Larkana District in?

In which district is Moenjodaro?

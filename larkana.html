Larkana District: Quick Answers

Population: Census 2023 records 1,784,453 people in Larkana District. Larkana city itself recorded 551,716 residents in the 2023 census.

Where is Larkana District? Larkana lies in north-western Sindh in the lower Indus plain. Its agricultural landscape and proximity to the ancient settlement at Mohenjo-daro make it important both economically and historically.

Headquarters and talukas: Larkana city is the district headquarters. The four talukas are Larkana, Bakrani, Dokri and Ratodero.

What is Larkana famous for? Larkana is internationally associated with Mohenjo-daro and nationally with the Bhutto family's political and historical legacy. It is also an important rice-growing and agricultural-trading district.

History and geography: The district sits within the ancient Indus civilisation landscape. Mohenjo-daro near Dokri provides one of South Asia's most important archaeological windows into the Bronze Age, while modern Larkana developed as an agricultural and administrative centre.

Education and healthcare: Chandka Medical College and its associated teaching hospital form an important part of the district's healthcare and medical-education network. Larkana also has universities, colleges and a wide school system.

Important places and local areas: Larkana city, Dokri, Ratodero, Bakrani, Mohenjo-daro and Garhi Khuda Bakhsh are key reference points. The district page separates archaeological heritage from the more recent political history of the Bhutto family.

Notable people: Larkana has documented associations with Zulfikar Ali Bhutto, Benazir Bhutto and other members of the Bhutto family. The page keeps these connections tied to specific places and historical records.

Larkana and Sindh: Larkana is a major heritage and agricultural centre of Sindh. Continue to the Sindh province guide for the wider regional picture.

Larkana is one of the most historically and politically significant districts of Sindh. Its identity is shaped by two very different periods of history: the Bronze Age civilisation represented by Mohenjo-daro and the modern political history associated with the Bhutto family.

Between these two famous stories lies a living district of farmers, traders, students, doctors, teachers, rice mills, fruit orchards, towns and villages. The district is part of the fertile right-bank agricultural landscape of the Indus and is particularly known for rice production and related agro-processing.

Larkana is also an important centre for higher education and healthcare in northern Sindh. Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University grew from Chandka Medical College, an institution founded during the government of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Larkana in the 2023 Census

The 2023 census recorded a population of 1,784,453 for Larkana District, compared with approximately 1.52 million in 2017.

The district has four talukas: Larkana, Bakrani, Dokri and Ratodero.

Larkana city is by far the largest urban centre. The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics recorded a population of 551,716 for Larkana Municipal Corporation in the 2023 census. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

The Four Talukas

Larkana Taluka

Larkana taluka contains the district headquarters and the main urban centre. The city serves as the administrative, commercial, educational and medical hub for much of the surrounding region.

Larkana city had a 2023 population of 551,716. Its large urban population supports markets, hospitals, educational institutions, government offices, transport businesses and agricultural trade.

Bakrani Taluka

Bakrani is predominantly connected with rural agriculture and the villages of the Indus plain. Arija is one of the recognised urban localities in the taluka.

The local economy depends on farming, irrigation, agricultural labour, livestock and markets serving nearby villages.

Dokri Taluka

Dokri has an exceptional place in world history because the archaeological landscape of Mohenjo-daro lies in this part of Larkana District.

The town of Dokri is also part of the district's agricultural network. Badah is another significant urban locality within the taluka.

Ratodero Taluka

Ratodero is especially important for understanding the modern history of the Bhutto family. Naudero and Garhi Khuda Bakhsh are located in this part of the district.

Ratodero town recorded a 2023 population of 81,935, while Naudero recorded 52,846. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

Mohenjo-daro: Larkana's Ancient World

Larkana District is home to Mohenjo-daro, one of the world's most important archaeological sites and a defining symbol of the Indus Valley Civilisation.

The ancient city flourished more than four thousand years ago. Its planned streets, drainage system, brick architecture, wells, residential structures and public buildings demonstrate an advanced urban society.

Mohenjo-daro is particularly famous for the Great Bath, the organised street plan and sophisticated drainage infrastructure.

The archaeological site provides evidence of a civilisation that developed extensive urban planning long before many later cities of South Asia.

For Larkana, Mohenjo-daro is not merely a tourist attraction. It is one of the strongest reasons the district has international cultural significance.

The Archaeological Landscape Around Dokri

Mohenjo-daro is part of a much wider archaeological story. The Indus plain around Larkana contains numerous ancient settlement sites and remains that help researchers understand how communities interacted with the river, agriculture and trade.

The landscape has changed dramatically over thousands of years, but the relationship between settlement and the Indus remains one of the central themes in the history of the region.

The Bhutto Family and Larkana

No modern profile of Larkana is complete without discussing the Bhutto family. The family's history is deeply tied to Larkana District, particularly Garhi Khuda Bakhsh, Naudero and the Larkana city area.

The Bhutto family's branch in Sindh became closely established in the Larkana region over generations. The family developed landholdings and residences in the district, and Larkana became the political and social centre from which one of Pakistan's most influential political families emerged. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

The modern political story begins prominently with Sir Shah Nawaz Bhutto, a landowner and politician who was born at Garhi Khuda Bakhsh and later served as the Dewan of Junagadh. He was the father of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto. :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

Zulfikar Ali Bhutto

Zulfikar Ali Bhutto was born on 5 January 1928 at Al-Murtaza in Larkana. He became one of the most consequential political figures in Pakistan's history. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

He founded the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) in 1967, served as President of Pakistan from 1971 to 1973 and then became Prime Minister from 1973 to 1977.

His political legacy remains strongly connected with Sindh and especially Larkana. Supporters remember him for populist politics, while historians and political observers continue to debate many aspects of his government and political legacy.

His connection with Larkana was also reflected in development and educational projects. Chandka Medical College was established in Larkana in 1973 during his government. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

Benazir Bhutto

Benazir Bhutto, daughter of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto, became the first woman to serve as Prime Minister of Pakistan and the first woman to lead a democratic government in a Muslim-majority country in the modern era.

Although Benazir was born in Karachi, Larkana and Garhi Khuda Bakhsh became central to her family's political and personal identity. Her connection with the district remained strong throughout her political career. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

After her assassination in 2007, Benazir Bhutto was buried at the family mausoleum in Garhi Khuda Bakhsh.

Garhi Khuda Bakhsh and the Bhutto Mausoleum

Garhi Khuda Bakhsh is a village in Ratodero Taluka and the ancestral graveyard of the Bhutto family.

The Sindh Tourism Development Corporation identifies the mausoleum as the resting place of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto, Nusrat Bhutto, Benazir Bhutto, Mir Murtaza Bhutto and Mir Shahnawaz Bhutto. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

The mausoleum has become one of the most recognisable political and cultural landmarks in Sindh. Large gatherings take place there on important anniversaries associated with the Bhutto family.

The site also illustrates how family memory, Sindhi political identity and national history intersect in one location.

Mir Murtaza Bhutto

Mir Murtaza Bhutto, son of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto and brother of Benazir Bhutto, was another prominent member of the family.

He founded the Pakistan Peoples Party (Shaheed Bhutto) and remained a controversial and influential figure in Sindh's political history.

Murtaza Bhutto is buried at the family mausoleum in Garhi Khuda Bakhsh. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}

Mir Shahnawaz Bhutto

Mir Shahnawaz Bhutto, another son of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto, was also a member of the politically prominent family.

His grave is located at Garhi Khuda Bakhsh alongside other members of the Bhutto family. :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

Nusrat Bhutto

Begum Nusrat Bhutto, wife of Zulfikar Ali Bhutto and mother of Benazir, Murtaza and Shahnawaz, played an important role in Pakistan Peoples Party politics, particularly after the removal and death of her husband.

Her grave is also part of the Bhutto family mausoleum at Garhi Khuda Bakhsh. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}

Other Bhutto Family Connections

The wider family has continued to play a major role in Pakistani public life. Fatima Bhutto, daughter of Murtaza Bhutto, is known as a writer and journalist. Bilawal Bhutto Zardari, son of Benazir Bhutto, became chairman of the Pakistan Peoples Party and has served in national government.

Aseefa Bhutto Zardari and Bakhtawar Bhutto Zardari are also prominent members of the next generation of the family.

These individuals should not all be described as being born in Larkana: their connection to the district comes primarily through family ancestry, political activity, residence and the Bhutto mausoleum. This distinction is important when documenting the history of the district.

Chandka Medical College and Modern Healthcare

One of the most important educational legacies associated with Zulfikar Ali Bhutto's period in government is Chandka Medical College.

The college began functioning on 20 April 1973. In 2008 it was upgraded into Shaheed Mohtarma Benazir Bhutto Medical University, with the university formally becoming operational in subsequent years. :contentReference[oaicite:16]{index=16}

Today the institution is one of the most important centres for medical education and healthcare in upper Sindh.

Education in Larkana

The Sindh Annual School Census 2023–24 records 1,197 schools in Larkana District. Of these, 1,134 were functional.

The same census records approximately 286,339 students and 9,193 teachers. :contentReference[oaicite:17]{index=17}

Functional schools include primary, elementary, middle, secondary and higher-secondary institutions. Primary schools form the largest part of the district's school network.

Agriculture and the Rice Economy

Agriculture remains one of the foundations of Larkana's economy. Irrigation from the Indus system supports rice, wheat, sugarcane and other crops.

Rice is particularly important. Larkana has developed a large rice-milling and agricultural-processing sector, linking farmers with local markets and buyers in other parts of Sindh and Pakistan.

Karak is Khattak country on the dry plateau between Kohat and Bannu. Older papers still say Teri for the old tehsil. The town of Karak is the headquarters. Takht-e-Nasrati and Banda Daud Shah are the other two poles. Oil and gas wells are part of the modern economy; they do not turn the district into a city.

Pashto is the language of almost every household in the tehsil tongue tables (Banda Daud Shah’s 2023 row is 176,885 Pashto speakers out of 177,744 people). That is a different picture from Haripur’s Hindko majority or Dera’s Saraiki–Pashto mix.

A rural count

PBS 2023: 815,878 people, 95,997 households. Urban 58,065. Rural 757,813. Sex ratio about 107 men per 100 women. Under-tens about 242,000 (30 percent). Literacy 65.36 percent overall — 84.12 percent for men, 45.60 percent for women. The male figure is high. The female figure is the gap that actually describes school life outside the town.

Karak municipal council is essentially the whole urban row. The other tehsils print as rural in the household tables.

Three tehsils, three densities

Karak tehsil — 1,299 km², 339,983 people, about 262 per km², literacy about 67.8 percent. Headquarters and the only sizeable municipal town. About 58,000 of this tehsil is urban; the rest is village.

Takht-e-Nasrati — 607 km², 298,151 people, about 491 per km², literacy about 69.6 percent. Smallest area, highest density of the three. This is not an empty desert tehsil.

Banda Daud Shah — 177,744 people in 2023 (155,482 in 2017). Literacy on the district tehsil list about 54 percent. The compiled table lists the tehsil as all rural. Named for the town of Banda Daud Shah.

Wells, colleges, DHQ

Hydrocarbon work is why outsiders know the name Karak. Local employment from wells and related contracts sits beside ordinary wheat and livestock. When a well site is quiet, the town still has to live on shops and government pay.

Government colleges and a university campus presence are in Karak town. DHQ Karak is the first hospital. THQ-style cover at Takht-e-Nasrati and Banda Daud Shah is what the farther villages use. Khushal Khan Khattak is the poet this belt claims; that is literature class, not a festival paragraph.

Sources: PBS 2023 district and tehsil compilations; Banda Daud Shah tongue row; KP literacy table for Karak and Takht-e-Nasrati. September 2026.

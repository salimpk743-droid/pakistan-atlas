Karak District

South of Kohat on the Indus Highway · district since 1 July 1982 · 3 tehsils · 815,878 people (2023 census)

Karak District: Quick Answers

Karak District is one of the 38 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, with its headquarters at Karak. It has 3 tehsils: Banda Daud Shah, Karak and Takht-e-Nasrati. The 2023 census counted 815,878 people on 3,371 km², and 65.36% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Tehsils of Karak District

Karak District has three tehsils: Banda Daud Shah, Karak and Takht-e-Nasrati. Karak tehsil, with the district headquarters, has 339,983 people. Takht-e-Nasrati has 298,151 and Banda Daud Shah 177,744 (2023 census).

Population and literacy

The census counted 815,878 people on 3,371 km², and only 7.12% of them lived in urban areas. Literacy (age 10 and over) was 65.36%. Male literacy was 84.12%, among the highest in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Female literacy was 45.6%.

History

Karak became a separate district on 1 July 1982; before that it was part of Kohat District. The Khattak tribe makes up most of the population (District Administration Karak).

Location, minerals and energy

Karak lies south of Kohat District. It borders Bannu, Lakki Marwat, Mianwali and Waziristan, and the Indus Highway runs through it, about 123 km from Peshawar. Hills of the Salt Range extend into the district from Punjab. There are salt mines at Jatta Ismail Khel, Garh Jawal Khel and Bahadar Khel, and gypsum deposits in the Jatta valley and Garh Jawal Khel. The district is also rich in oil and gas (District Administration Karak).

Karak District: FAQ

How many tehsils are in Karak District?

Karak District has 3 tehsils: Banda Daud Shah, Karak and Takht-e-Nasrati.

What is the population of Karak District?

The 2023 census counted 815,878 people in Karak District, up from 705,362 in 2017 (annual growth 2.46%). 7.12% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Karak District?

Karak District covers 3,371 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 242.03 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Karak District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Karak District: Karak (58,065, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Karak District?

65.36% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 84.12%, female 45.6%).

Which languages are spoken in Karak District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (99.78% of the 815,150 people in the mother-tongue table).

Which province is Karak District in?

Karak District is in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Its headquarters is Karak.

When did Karak become a district?

Karak became a separate district on 1 July 1982. Before that it was part of Kohat District. (District Administration Karak)

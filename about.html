About this project

A civic learning site, not a political campaign.

Pakistan Atlas is being built so students can open one calm website and find the shape of their country: provinces, districts, languages, universities, hospitals and the ordinary problems a place is trying to solve.

The first public version is a complete frame. You will add official tables, village photographs and new articles over time. That slow work is the product.

Editorial promise

  • Population numbers come from PBS or another named official source.
  • No hate speech, no community bait, no copied Wikipedia dumps.
  • Photos of living people need consent. Government buildings and landscapes are preferred.
  • Ads will never cover the text or look like news.

Who can help

Teachers, students and local readers can send corrections through the contact page. A wrong district name is more serious here than a missing decoration.

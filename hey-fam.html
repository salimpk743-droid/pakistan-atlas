Hey Fam Drama: Cast and Story

Hey Fam is a drama about social media and viral fame on ARY Digital, written by Awais Ahmad Joyia and directed by Zeshan Ansari, starring Zaviyar Nauman Ejaz, Zarrar Khan.

Hey Fam Story

Hey Fam is an ARY Digital drama about viral fame, social media and online personas, aimed at a younger audience. It stars Zaviyar Nauman Ejaz, Zarrar Khan and Dua Zahra.

In September 2026 the drama drew attention for a ten-minute scene filmed as one continuous take, without cuts or edits, which film producer Abdullah Seja described as rare for Pakistani television. He praised director Zeshan Ansari for the technically demanding shot and said the ratings showed younger viewers engaging with fresh storytelling.

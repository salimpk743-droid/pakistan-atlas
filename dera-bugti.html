Sui gas field

Sui, north-east of Jacobabad, rose to importance in 1950–51 with the discovery of one of the world's largest natural-gas deposits. Operations began in 1955 with pipelines to Hyderabad, Karachi and Sukkur, later extended to Multan (1958), Lahore, Faisalabad and Hattar (1970) (Britannica).

Administrative units of Dera Bugti District

The 2023 census counted 8 administrative units in Dera Bugti District: Baiker (sub-tehsil), Dera Bugti (sub-division), Loti (sub-tehsil), Malam (sub-tehsil), Pir Koh (sub-tehsil), Qadirabad (sub-division), Sangsillah (sub-tehsil) and Sui (sub-division). Sui is the most populous, with 126,725 people, and it is also the largest by area (3,858 km²). Literacy ranges from 12.12% in Pir Koh to 33.75% in Dera Bugti.

Population and growth

Dera Bugti District had 355,274 people in the 2023 census, up from 313,110 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.13%). It covers 10,160 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 14th by population and 11th by area. 30.52% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 121.1 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 2 urban localities: Sui (79,567) and Dera Bugti (28,880). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (98.94%).

Literacy and schooling

24.07% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 32nd of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 34.4% and female literacy 11.88%; the urban rate was 41.7% and the rural rate 15.89%. The census counted 92,428 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (40,831 girls and 51,595 boys).

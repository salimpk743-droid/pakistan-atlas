Lasbela District

headquarters Uthal · 9 census units · 680,977 people (2023 census)

Lasbela District: Quick Answers

Lasbela District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Lasbela Division, with its headquarters at Uthal. The 2023 census lists 9 administrative units in it: Bela, Dureji, Gaddani, Hub, Kanraj, Lakhra, Liari, Sonmiani/Winder and Uthal. The 2023 census counted 680,977 people (pre-split boundaries) on 15,153 km², and 36.47% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Lasbela District

The 2023 census counted 9 administrative units in Lasbela District: Bela (sub-division), Dureji (sub-division), Gaddani (tehsil), Hub (sub-division), Kanraj (sub-division), Lakhra (tehsil), Liari (sub-tehsil), Sonmiani/Winder (tehsil) and Uthal (sub-division). Hub is the most populous, with 233,443 people, and Dureji is the largest by area (2,813 km²). Literacy ranges from 15.31% in Lakhra to 48.57% in Gaddani.

Population and growth

Lasbela District had 680,977 people in the 2023 census, up from 576,271 in 2017 (average annual growth 2.83%). It covers 15,153 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 5th by population and 8th by area. 48.55% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 105.04 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 6 urban localities; the largest are Hub (195,661), Uthal (37,071), Winder (35,245) and Bela (29,380). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (75.32%), followed by Sindhi (9.96%), Brahui (8.09%) and Pashto (2.56%).

Literacy and schooling

36.47% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 21st of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 45.86% and female literacy 26.48%; the urban rate was 45.94% and the rural rate 27.03%. The census counted 127,144 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (67,917 girls and 59,223 boys).

Lasbela District: FAQ

What is the population of Lasbela District?

The 2023 census counted 680,977 people in Lasbela District (pre-split boundaries), up from 576,271 in 2017 (annual growth 2.83%). 48.55% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Lasbela District?

Lasbela District covers 15,153 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 44.94 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Lasbela District?

The 2023 census lists 6 urban localities in Lasbela District: Hub (195,661, municipal committee), Uthal (37,071, municipal committee), Winder (35,245, municipal committee), Bela (29,380, municipal committee), Gaddani (17,540, municipal committee) and Dureji (15,688, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Lasbela District?

36.47% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 45.86%, female 26.48%).

Which languages are spoken in Lasbela District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (75.32% of the 680,816 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Sindhi (9.96%), Brahui (8.09%), Pashto (2.56%).

Which province is Lasbela District in?

Lasbela District is in Balochistan, in Lasbela Division. Its headquarters is Uthal.

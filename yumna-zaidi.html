Yumna Zaidi: Every Drama, Every Lux Style Award

Yumna Zaidi biography

Yumna Zaidi's career rewards patience. She spent the 2010s taking supporting parts, second leads and difficult women other actresses passed on. In the 2020s those choices paid off: she won the Lux Style Award for Inkaar, then for Pyar Ke Sadqay, then for Bakhtawar, and then for Tere Bin.

Her first screen role was a supporting part in ARY Digital's domestic drama Thakan (2012). Leads followed in Meri Dulari and Ullu Baraye Farokht Nahi (both 2013). Playing the troubled wife in Farooq Rind's Rishtay Kuch Adhooray Se brought her first HUM Awards best-actress nominations. The News later described her Dar Si Jaati Hai Sila (2017), in which she plays a victim of sexual harassment, as a role she "pulled off effortlessly".

She is unusually private for an actress of her standing. We found no verified birth date or marriage in the outlets we rely on, so this page gives neither. What is on record is the work, and it is listed in full below.

Yumna Zaidi's biggest hits

Tere Bin (Geo Entertainment, 2022–2023)

As Meerab, opposite Wahaj Ali's Murtasim, Yumna reached her biggest YouTube audience yet. The official HAR PAL GEO upload of episode 1 (28 December 2022) has 109.4 million views, and the OST has 153.1 million (as of 2 October 2026). At the 23rd Lux Style Awards (2025) Tere Bin won Best TV Play (Viewers' Choice) and she won Best TV Actress (Viewers' Choice), according to The News. Our guide to Tere Bin covers the cast and story.

Bakhtawar (HUM TV, 2022)

She plays a young woman who works as a delivery rider to support her family. At the 22nd Lux Style Awards in October 2023 the role won her both the Viewers' and Critics' Choice trophies, and The Express Tribune reported that these made her the most-awarded female actor in the LSAs' TV category. Episode 1 on HUM TV's channel has 18.4 million views.

Pyar Ke Sadqay (HUM TV, 2020)

This romantic drama, opposite Bilal Abbas Khan, showed a lighter side of her acting and won her both Best TV Actress awards at the 20th Lux Style Awards, according to The News.

Kohlu District: Quick Answers

Kohlu District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Koh-i-Suleman Division, with its headquarters at Kohlu. The 2023 census lists 5 administrative units in it: Grisani, Kahan, Kohlu, Mawand and Tamboo. The 2023 census counted 260,220 people on 7,610 km², and 28.53% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

Administrative units of Kohlu District

The 2023 census counted 5 administrative units in Kohlu District: Grisani (sub-tehsil), Kahan (sub-division), Kohlu (sub-division), Mawand (sub-division) and Tamboo (sub-tehsil). Kahan is the most populous, with 107,840 people, and it is also the largest by area (3,754 km²). Literacy ranges from 19.87% in Kahan to 49.76% in Kohlu.

Population and growth

Kohlu District had 260,220 people in the 2023 census, up from 213,933 in 2017 (average annual growth 3.33%). It covers 7,610 km². Of the 34 Balochistan districts in the census, it ranks 23rd by population and 13th by area. 7.29% of residents were counted in urban areas. There were 109.91 males for every 100 females.

Towns and languages

The census lists 1 urban locality: Kohlu (18,978). Balochi was the most common mother tongue (95.61%), followed by Pashto (3.97%).

Literacy and schooling

28.53% of people aged 10 and over were literate in 2023, 31st of the 34 districts in Balochistan. Male literacy was 32.65% and female literacy 23.93%; the urban rate was 65.11% and the rural rate 25.62%. The census counted 69,516 children aged 5 to 16 out of school (32,939 girls and 36,572 boys).

Kohlu District: FAQ

What is the population of Kohlu District?

The 2023 census counted 260,220 people in Kohlu District, up from 213,933 in 2017 (annual growth 3.33%). 7.29% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Kohlu District?

Kohlu District covers 7,610 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 34.19 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Kohlu District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Kohlu District: Kohlu (18,978, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Kohlu District?

28.53% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 32.65%, female 23.93%).

Which languages are spoken in Kohlu District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Balochi (95.61% of the 260,220 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Pashto (3.97%).

Which province is Kohlu District in?

Kohlu District is in Balochistan, in Koh-i-Suleman Division. Its headquarters is Kohlu.

2 September 2026 — 2023 census is still the population backbone

Pakistan Bureau of Statistics has not issued a newer national census than the 2023 digital count. District pages on this atlas therefore keep the 2023 household and population rows, including Islamabad Capital Territory at 2,363,863 and the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa district figures already printed on those pages. When a province notifies a new district, the old row stays until PBS publishes a split table.

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, 7th Population and Housing Census 2023.

2 September 2026 — Two Waziristan districts on one historic name

South Waziristan was split on 13 April 2022 into Lower and Upper districts. Readers still search the old name. This site keeps one long page for the combined 2023 count of 888,675 and lists both sets of tehsils. Tank remains the first settled stop on that road.

Source: KP district notification 13 April 2022; PBS 2023 tehsil rows.

2 September 2026 — School calendars belong on board websites

Admission dates change by circular. This atlas records campuses and literacy. For the live date of a board exam or an HEC schedule, open the board or HEC site that issued the notice.

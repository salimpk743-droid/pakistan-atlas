How to use this page

Write 400–800 words, twice a week

AdSense reviewers and students both need original sentences. Do not copy a TV ticker. Open the official circular, write what it means for a district, and add the date.

Census

2023 census is still the population backbone

Until Pakistan Bureau of Statistics publishes a newer national count, every district page should show the 2023 figure and the year. When a province notifies a new district, add a footnote instead of guessing the new population.

Education

Watch official admission calendars

Boards and universities change dates. Reprint a closing date only after you open the circular on the institution website.

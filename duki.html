Duki District

Loralai Division · district since 1 August 2017 · coal-mining district of northern Balochistan · 205,044 people (2023 census)

Duki District: Quick Answers

Duki District is one of the 41 districts of Balochistan, in Loralai Division, with its headquarters at Duki. The 2023 census lists 4 administrative units in it: Duki, Luni, Talao and Thal Chutyali. The 2023 census counted 205,044 people on 4,233 km², and 44.18% of residents aged 10 and over were literate.

How Duki became a district

Chief Minister Nawab Sanaullah Khan Zehri announced in October 2016 that Duki would be made a district (Balochistan Voices). The Revenue Department notified the change in July 2017, under Section 6 of the Balochistan Land Revenue Act, 1967, with effect from 1 August 2017. The new district was carved out of Loralai District, covered the area of its Duki sub-division and had its headquarters at Duki (Daily Azadi, 18 July 2017). When Zhob Division was split in June 2021, Duki became part of the new Loralai Division, together with Loralai and Musakhail (Dawn, 30 June 2021).

Administrative units

The 2023 census lists four units in Duki District: Duki, Luni and Thal Chutyali sub-divisions and Talao sub-tehsil. The census table above gives their population and area.

Population and literacy

The census counted 205,044 people on 4,233 km², and 4.77% of them lived in urban areas. Literacy (age 10 and over) was 44.18%: 53.91% for males and 33.43% for females.

Coal mining

Coal mining is central to Duki's economy. On 11 October 2024, gunmen attacked coal mines in Duki and killed 21 miners (Reuters).

Duki District: FAQ

What is the population of Duki District?

The 2023 census counted 205,044 people in Duki District, up from 152,977 in 2017 (annual growth 5.02%). 4.77% of the population lived in urban areas.

What is the area of Duki District?

Duki District covers 4,233 km² according to the 2023 census, a density of 48.44 people per km².

What are the main towns and cities in Duki District?

The 2023 census lists 1 urban locality in Duki District: Duki (9,783, municipal committee).

What is the literacy rate of Duki District?

44.18% of people aged 10 and over were literate in the 2023 census (male 53.91%, female 33.43%).

Which languages are spoken in Duki District?

The most common mother tongue in the 2023 census was Pashto (93.27% of the 205,044 people in the mother-tongue table), followed by Balochi (4.85%).

Which province is Duki District in?

Duki District is in Balochistan, in Loralai Division. Its headquarters is Duki.

When did Duki become a district?

The Balochistan government notified Duki as a district in July 2017, taking effect on 1 August 2017. It was formed from the Duki sub-division of Loralai District, with its headquarters at Duki. (Daily Azadi, Quetta)

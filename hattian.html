Jhelum Valley District: Quick Answers

Jhelum Valley District is one of the 10 districts of Azad Jammu and Kashmir, in Muzaffarabad Division. The 2017 census counted 226,496 people on 854 km², in 3 tehsils: Hattian, Chikar and Leepa.

Tehsils of Jhelum Valley District

The 2017 census tables published by the AJ&K Bureau of Statistics list 3 tehsils in Jhelum Valley District: Hattian, Chikar and Leepa (Statistical Year Book 2023, Table 15.15). Hattian is the most populous, with 161,424 people, and it is also the largest by area (530 km²). AJK at a Glance 2024 counts 32 tehsils across Azad Kashmir in 2023, against 30 in the 2017 census tables, so tehsil boundaries may since have changed (AJK at a Glance 2024).

Population and area

Jhelum Valley District had 226,496 people in the 2017 census, with average annual growth of 1.64% since 1998, 7.53% of them in urban areas and an average household of 6.08 people (Table 15.6). It covers 854 km² (Table 15.1). Of the 10 AJK districts it ranks 8th by population and 7th by area. The AJ&K Bureau of Statistics projects about 0.250 million people for 2023 (AJK at a Glance 2024); these are projections, not census counts.
